How is it possible to get an unsatisfied judgment against another motorist?
================
Do you mean you want to get an unsatisfied judgment against a motorist because of a motor vehicle accident, collision, or crash?
Yes that is exactly what I mean.
You will have to file a Supporting Affirmation SR-60 and other documents listed on the affirmation SR-60.
================
What do I need to do to remove a suspension related to an unsatisfied judgment?
You would have to contact the attorney or court that issued the judgment and the info you find on the suspension order will advise you of the name of the court and the court index number for the judgment; and the DMV cannot clear the suspension without the proof of the court.
================
Will the DMV suspend my driver license and all of the registrations if I have an unsatisfied judgment against me?
The answer is yes the DMV will suspend the license and registrations if you fail to pay or satisfy a judgment of more than $1,000 and it is related to a motor vehicle accident, collision or crash which causes property damage, bodily injury, or death.
What else will the DMV suspend?
The DMV will also suspend all vehicle registrations of a company and the privilege to register another vehicle.
================
How do I know which court to contact?
You can call the NY State DMV Insurance Services Bureau or see contact us below if you are not sure which court to contact.
Do I need to resolve the judgment to remove a suspension related to an unsatisfied judgment?
Yes, you must resolve the judgment first.
