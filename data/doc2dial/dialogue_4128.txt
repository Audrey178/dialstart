When receiving a closed school loan discharge you have to be aware of several points
================
What happens if you did not complete your study program?
Please note that the fact that the school has awarded you a diploma or certificate does not disqualify you for discharge.
================
What can I do if I received loans for several different study programs at the same school, but could not complete any of the programs. Can all loans be canceled?
Only loans you received for a program you were unable to complete due to school closure are eligible for discharge.
Suppose I initially enrolled in Program A, but decided not to continue with that program and transferred to Program B, what can I do?
only the loans you received for Program B could be repaid.
================
If my school closes and then I enroll in a comparable program at another school, can I receive a loan write-off that I received at the closed school?
It all depends on whether you have completed or are in the process of completing a comparable study program.
