How do I know if I have to do a Driver Responsibility Assessment?
================
Have you been convicted of a DWI or DWAI?
No, no convictions.
Have you refused to submit to a chemical test?
Yes, I refused a test.
Yes, you will have to pay a driver responsibility assessment.
================
When will my license be suspended?
It begins at your hearing or sentencing.
Is there a way I can still drive to work?
You might be eligible for a conditional license if you participate in the Impaired Driver Program.
How do I enroll in IDP?
The Order of Revocation they send you will show close by offices you can enroll at.
Is your license always suspended if you get convicted?
Yes, if convicted, your license will be suspended or revoked.
