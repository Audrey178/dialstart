I've already gone through the basics. My questions are a bit more specific than that. For example, where do I find the number of a document?
The document number is 8 or 10 characters, and is printed on the lower right corner of your DMV photo document, or on the back for documents issues after January 29, 2014. The 8 character format is all numeric.
Good to know. I was also asked for my SSN, why is that?
Entering the last 4 digits of your SSN is used only to help identify you for the transaction.
================
What if I already have a residence address on file with the DMV?
In that case you are not required to change it when you change your mailing address, but you can change the residence address if you want to.
================
OK. And what should I do for bulk manual orders for public or private organizations?
Simply use the online DMV Manual Order transaction. For public organizations there is no fee for bulk orders. For private organizations there is a charge of $ 1.00 per manual for bulk orders and a minimum order of 25 manuals.
