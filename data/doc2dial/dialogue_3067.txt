I'm from outside the U.S. and I need student aid. What can you do for me?
First of all you should never assume you can't get aid just because you're not a citizen.
================
Great. So I can get student aid even if I'm not an eligible non-citizen?
Yes, there may be some scholarships and other aid you can get.
================
When am I considered an eligible non-citizen then?
When you are a U.S. national, for example. This includes natives of American Samoa or Swains Island.
================
What if the expiration date on my documents has passed?

That doesn't necessarily mean that your status as a legal permanent resident has expired.
Can I just renew it somehow?
Yes, you might just need to renew the card.
