What are the fees and taxes for passenger vehicles?
You will use both the registration and county use tax charts to determine you registration fee and county use tax.
Is this the original registration of your vehicle?
Yes it is.
================
I am outside New York City, how do I get those fees?
You will have to ask the city or county where you reside.
================
Do I have to pay a vehicle use tax?
Residents of New York City and several counties in New York State must pay a vehicle use tax when they register or renew a passenger vehicle registration.
What if I am exempt from the vehicle use tax?
You will complete form UT-11C.
================
How much are the fees?
You must pay the fee for your vehicle plate which is $25 and your title certificate fee of $50.
