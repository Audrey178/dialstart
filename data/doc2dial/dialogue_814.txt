Hello, I would like to know what happens after I send my retirement application?
================
Once we receive your request, we will review it and contact you if we have any questions. We may request additional documents from you before we can process your request.
================
What do I do if I can't check my status online?
If you can't verify your status online, you can call us at 1 - 800 - 772 - 1213 TTY 1 - 800 - 325 - 0778 from 8:00 a.m. at 5:30 p.m., Monday through Friday.
================
and by what means will they give the answer to the request?
You will receive a letter in the mail with our decision.
Did you include information about other family members when you applied?
yes
We will let you know if they can receive benefits from your request.
================
And how is it done in the event that someone wants to withdraw their application?...
in that case You have up to 12 months to withdraw your application, if you change your mind. You will be asked to repay any benefits you have already received.
