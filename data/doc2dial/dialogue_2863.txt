What is the General and Federal Guidelines and what do they do?
================
The General and Federal Guidelines of the IT Standards Library highlights federal organizations involved in establishing and coordinating the implementation of federal laws, regulations, standards and guidelines for information and information technology management for all federal agencies and departments.
