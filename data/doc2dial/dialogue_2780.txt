If I have an out of state vehicle, do I need to register it?
================
Do you live outside of New York State and want to know if you need to register your vehicle
Yes, I am an out of state resident not from New York State.
It depends on a few things.
================
My out of state title is held by my lienholder, how do I register in New York?
All you need is a copy of the title certificate that has your name from the lienholder.
================
What if I am not a resident of New York?
Actually, you registration from your state is valid in New York.
How many days do I have to register my vehicle in New York state?
You have 30 days to register your vehicle after becoming a resident.
================
What if there is a lien on my out of state title certificate?
The NYS DMV will record the lien on your title record and on your  title certificate in NYS
================
What if my vehicle does not meed California emissions standards?
You can read the information about California emissions standards to determine whether there is or not an exemption for your vehicle.
