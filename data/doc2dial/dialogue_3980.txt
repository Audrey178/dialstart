Is there a listing of the TOS for a MyDMV account?
There is a listing of 48 Terms of Service for a MyDMV account.
Are you registering for an account on MyDMV?
I am trying to register for an account but I don't see where to enter a password.
When you register for an account you will be sent a temporary password by e-mail.  You must use that temporary password to complete the rest of the registration process.
Who created MyDMV?
It was created by the New York State Department of Motor Vehicles DMV in cooperation with the New York State Office of Information Technology Services.
Can I gain access to MyDMV using my passwords for other NYS agencies?
If you have registered to gain access to use secure web applications at another NYS agency, you can simply sign in using the same Username and Password.
Why does MyDMV validate my identity?
That process is designed to protect the security of your identifying personal information.
What happens if MyDMV is unable to verify my identity?
If MyDMV is unable to verify your identity you account will be deactivated and thereafter access to applications and on-line services will be denied and the account will be locked.
What do I need to do to be permitted access to use DMV's secure web applications?
You will be permitted access upon validation of your identity.
