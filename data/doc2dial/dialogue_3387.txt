Is there really student aid scams I need to avoid?
Yes, you should avoid student aid scams and protect your identity while looking for scholarships and FAFSA.
================
Is there a way to identify student loan scams?
Some of the scams that have been reported and should be looked out for are phone calls, emails, letters, text offering relief from federal student loans and warning them the forgiveness ends soon.
================
What should be reported if information gets stolen?
You should quickly report all lost or stolen IDs to the issuer or even to the police if appropriate.
================
What should I do if my FSA ID or password has been shared?
It is best to log in to your account using your username and password and once logged in change your account information so no one else can access it.
