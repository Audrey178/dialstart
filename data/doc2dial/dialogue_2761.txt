Can you tell me about driver responsibility assessment?
================
You want to know more about paying the driver responsibility assessment?
Yes, I would like to know about the driver responsibility assessment.
I am able to tell you that the driver responsibility assessment is a fee that is paid to the DMV over a three year period if you are convicted of certain traffic offenses in NY State or when you accumulate 6 or more points on your driving record within 18 months.
================
What happens if i am convicted of an alcohol or drugged driving-related offense or if I refuse to take a chemical test?
The minimum annual assessment is $250 and the total for three years will be $750.
What if I have 6 points on my driver record  for violations committed for a span of 18 months?
You would pay the $100 minimum annual assessment and the total of $300 for three years.
================
Can I pay off the assessment at one time?
Yes, you actually can pay the assessment for the three years when you receive your first statement.
================
What can I use for payment at the DMV office?
You can bring a check or money order for at least the minimum amount on your statement to the local DMV.
