I need to know if I'm eligible for a discharge of my federal student loans.
I'll be happy to help you find out if you qualify for the unpaid refund discharge.
Thank you. I've withdrawn from school and I received my loan under the FFEL Program. Do I qualify?
In this case, your school may be required to return part or all of your loan money to the loan servicer.
What if my school failed to return the loan funds after I withdrew?
This may make you eligible for a discharge of the loan money that your school failed to return.
================
What if my parents received a Direct PLUS loan for my education? Does the loan still qualify?
yes. If your school did not return the loan money, that amount will be discharged.
Did the school refuse to make a required return of your parent's PLUS loan funds?
No.
================
How do I find out who my loan servicer is?
You can find this information by logging into your My Federal Student Aid account.
