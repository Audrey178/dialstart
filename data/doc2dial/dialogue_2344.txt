Hello I need information about How to renew a registration
You must renew your registration before it expires. Your new registration documents will arrive in the mail in approximately 10 days. Early renewal will not change the new expiration date of your new registration.
================
What if my address changed?
Your email address on DMV files must be current. You cannot change it during the transaction. First change your address online and then renew.

and can i renew by mail?
Send the bottom of your MV-3 or OP-3 renewal notice to the address on the notice. Include a personal check or money order made payable to the 'Commissioner of Motor Vehicles' for the fee.
What if I don't have my MV-3 or OP-3?
If you don't have your MV-3 or OP-3, use the Duplicate Registration / Renewal Application PDF MV-82D
