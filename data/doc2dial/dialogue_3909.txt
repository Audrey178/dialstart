Is it now required by law to have a REAL ID?
================
The new REAL ID is optional and is not needed for either being licensed to drive or to vote or register for voting

How do I go about getting a REAL ID?
You can apply for a REAL ID at any time. You need to go to a DMV office. You cannot avail of a REAL ID online, by phone or by way of mail
================
Are there any documents that are already REAL ID compliant?
The enhanced driver licenses, permits or non-driver IDs along with valid US Passports are already REAL ID compliant
================
How do I get a REAL ID if I have lost my Social Security Card?
If you have mislaid your Social Security Card, you must get a replacement card before applying for a REAL ID. Further information on how to get a duplicate card is available on the Social Security Administration website.
================
What if I don't want to get a REAL ID at this moment in time?
If you have decided not to get a REAL ID, please be advised that any license, permit or ID card issued after October 30, 2017 will be marked Not for Federal Purposes
