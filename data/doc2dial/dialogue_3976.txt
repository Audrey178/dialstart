I don't have the proofs of name identification that I need to bring to DMV.
================
I can assist you with this issue. Most people have more proof documents already available than they think.
================
Can I use a proof of residence document that is in my wife's name?
Do you and your wife have the same last name?
Yes, we do.
Great. You can use your wife's proof of residence documents.
================
Can I use any documents that I received at my Community College while I attended?
If you received a GED certificate while attending the college, you can contact them for a certified copy to be used as proof of Name Identification.
I was going to use my Union card, but I lost it. What do I do?
You can call you union and ask them for a duplicate.
================
Do I need to show anything else if I use my wife's proof of residence documents?
Yes, you will need to show documents that prove your relationship, such as a marriage certificate.
