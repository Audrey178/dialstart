I need info about VA programs for substance abuse
================
The VA health care program covers services to treat substance use problems .
Do you want to know how to access these services?
yes, please
To access these services you must first apply for VA health care .
where can I get help if I'm not familiar with the VA?
You can find a Substance Use Disorder SUD program near you, you can call our general information hotline at 800 - 827 - 1000 , or contact your local VA medical center
================
what kind of help do you give?
The services we offer you depend on your specific needs .We provide many options for Veterans seeking treatment for substance use,  problems ranging from unhealthy alcohol use to life - threatening addiction .
================
But what if someone didn't have VA benefits?
You may still be able to get care if you served in a combat zone, get free private counseling , alcohol and drug assessment , and other support at one of our 300 community Vet Centers .
================
and as for me I just go to my VA care provider?
If you have a VA primary care provider, your provider can help you get screened for substance use problems and related issues like PTSD or depression and can offer treatment and support as needed .
