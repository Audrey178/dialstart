Do you need to  complete the entrance counseling?

================
What do I need to complete this counseling?
You will need a verified FSA ID.
================
How long will the entrance couseling take me to complete?
Actually, the process should be completed in one session and usually takes around 20-30 minutes of you time.
================
Does each school have the same entrance counseling requirements to complete?
Every school may have something different for entrance counseling to complete and it is smart to check with your school's financial aid office to be sure the counseling available on this Website will satisfy the requirements.
Who is the one that requires me to complete the entrance counseling?
It is the Federal Government that requires you to complete the counseling to make sure you understand the responsibilities and obligations that come along with loans.
