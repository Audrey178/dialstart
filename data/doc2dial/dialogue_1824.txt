What out of state insurance documents can I give to DMV?
================
We can't accept any out of state insurance documents of any type.
What can I do to get an acceptable insurance if my vehicle is registered in New York?
Since we will not accept any out of state insurance documents, your vehicle must have New York State auto liability insurance coverage.
So, since my vehicle is registered in New York, does that mean that I have to get a New York-based insurance?
Yes, your vehicle must have New York State auto liability insurance coverage.
Do I need to have insurance coverage for a motorcycle?
Yes, if you are going to use your motorcycle on a public road or highway, you must have insurance coverage.
================
What should I take into consideration for my liability insurance coverage?
Your liability insurance coverage must remain in effect for as long as your registration is valid, there are exceptions for motorcycles.
