What are the things that I need to organise before I am eligible to take my road test?
Firstly, you will need a learner permit, and please remember that you must obey all rules and conditions that are applicable to being a learner driver. You must also have completed a Pre-Licensing Course OR a high school/college Driver Education Course
================
Are there age restrictions involved regarding the amount of supervised practice driving that I should have
In order for you to be eligible to take the road test, you must have a minimum of 50 hours of supervised driving practice completed
================
Are there particular rules regarding proceeding in a vehicle when stopped or when selecting a position in a lane on the highway
When the vehicle is stopped, you must not proceed until you can clearly see in both directions, you must also be very vigilant when choosing a lane on the highway
================
Is there a particular place where I can find the necessary information regarding the Pre-Licensing course
You can search online for the course or alternatively, contact a local Driving Instructor from your telephone directory
================
If I was to change my license from one class to another, is there a necessity for a Pre-Licensing Course Certificate
To amend the class on your license, there is no need to have a Pre-Licensing Course Certificate, provided you have a valid NY State driver license
================
What proof of supervise driving practice do I need to bring with me to the driver test
You must present a Certificate of Supervised Driving MV-262 to the license examiner. This Certificate is completed by your parent of guardian which certifies that you have the required amount of supervised driving practice
