Who is allowed to operate an ATV?
================
Anyone over 16 can operate an ATV.  People under 16 can with restrictions.
Are you between 10 and 15?
My son is.  WHat restrictions will he have?
He would need to be supervised by an adult.
================
When do I need to register my ATV?
You must register your ATV before you take delivery.
================
Are there regsistration fees?
There is a plate fee of $12.50, then a registration fee of $12.50 per year.
================
Are there special restrictions for riding an ATV?
There are laws regarding ATV riding.
================
Do ATVs need to be registered?
Yes, they have to be registered with the DMV.
================
Can an ATV be operated anywhere?
No, they may not be operated on public land, except designated areas.
