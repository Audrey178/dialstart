Hello, I came here to learn more about replacing IRP registration items.
================
Of course. Have both of your apportioned plates been lost or stolen?
Yes, I'm afraid that is the case.
Then you should get the form Report of Lost or Stolen Motor Vehicle Items MV-78B from a police agency. Also get form MV-78B if your items were lost because of a crime. Your items will be replaced at no fee if form MV-78B shows the items were lost because of a crime.
================
Can I also apply by phone?
Unfortunately, you can't apply to replace IRP registration items by phone.
================
Oh. Can I do it by fax then?
Yes, you can apply to replace IRP registration items by fax at 518-486-6579.
================
And can I also do it in person, at the DMV office?
Yes, you can apply to replace IPR registration items at the DMV.
================
Can you suggest a random topic?
Motor Carriers.
================
My final question would be if I can also apply by mail. Can I?
Yes. You can apply to replace IRP registration items by mail. Follow the instructions on this page and Schedule A&C (IRP-6).
