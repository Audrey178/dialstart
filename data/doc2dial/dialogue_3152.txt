Hello, I would like to know what I must do to continue receiving federal student aid
Of course You must continue to meet the basic eligibility criteria, make satisfactory academic progress, and complete the FAFSA every year.
================
Should I continue to meet the basic eligibility criteria?
yes Remember the basic eligibility criteria that allow you to get federal student aid continue to apply the entire time you receive aid, not just when you first fill out the FAFSA and receive aid.
================
and Must I have satisfactory academic progress to continue receiving federal student aid?
You must make satisfactory academic progress to continue receiving federal student aid. In other words, you have to get good grades and complete enough classes (credits, hours, etc.)
and is it indispensable?
yes You must make satisfactory academic progress to continue receiving federal student aid.

and for how long is the form you fill out valid?
When you complete the FAFSA, you are applying for help for a specific year. To get help next year, you'll need to submit next year's FAFSA form.
