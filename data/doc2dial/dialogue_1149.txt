Hi, I need to file a claim but am uncertain how to go about doing it. Is there help for someone like me?
Do you need help filing a claim or an appeal?
No I don't need help filing a claim, thank you.
================
I have an accredited representative helping me with my claim but I feel the charges are too high, what can I do?
If you feel that the representative's fees are too high you can challenge the fees. You can find more information in the How to Challenge a Fee guide.
================
I would like to become an accredited representative or a VSO what qualifications are required for this?
If you would like to become an accredited representative or a VSO you will need to pass an exam and a background check and take continuing education courses to make sure you are providing the most up-to-date information.
================
Who is the best person to help me with the filing of my appeal?
If you need help you may want to work with an accredited attorney, or a claims agent, or a Veterans Service Officer (VSO).
================
What is a VSO?
Veterans Service Officers work for the Veterans Service Organizations both are called VSOs.
================
Will a VSO help service member's survivors?
Yes VSOs work on behalf of both Veterans and service members and their dependents and survivors.
