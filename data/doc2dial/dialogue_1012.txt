I would like to find out if I'm eligible for VA Home Loan Benefits.
================
We can assist you in learning how VA direct and VA-backed home loans work and also finding which loan type may be right for you.
================
I'm about to lose my apartment and will have nowhere to live. Is there a service that I can call for help?
Yes you can connect with a caring and qualified Veterans Crisis Line responders - it's private, free, and available 24/7.
================
After I get back on my feet, can you help me get information about getting assistance in paying my rent?
If you are a Veteran or Service member with a service - connected disability, we can tell you how to apply for a housing grant.
================
We also have other benefits and services that might interest you, would you like to know about them?
Not for now thank you.
================
I'm applying for a VA-backed home loan grant,  what types are there?
You can apply for either a VA direct or VA-backed home loan or Specially Adapted Housing grant.
================
How do I find out what the progress on my application is?
It is possible to track the status of an open claim for a Specially Adapted Housing grant through our service.
