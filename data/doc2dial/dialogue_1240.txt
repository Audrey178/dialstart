I need some information about where to go for VA healthcare.
================
There are several places where you may go to receive care, including a VA outpatient clinic and mobile Vet centers.
Do you live near a VA medical center?
No, I don't think so.
================
What do the VA medical centers offer?
VA medical centers offer a wide range of services to cover your healthcare needs.
What if I have to use VA centers at different locations? Will they have my medical records on file?
Yes. With our electronic medical record system, your medical records will be available no matter what location you use.
================
I was thinking about moving to France with my wife. Do you have any programs for those who live internationally?
Yes. You can sign up for our Foreign Medical Program. This program will pay for certain needed health care services received in a foreign country.
Can I receive health care at an urgent care clinic?
Yes, but you will need to be signed up with our VA health care program and have received care with us within the last 2 years.
