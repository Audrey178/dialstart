What happens to my personalized plates when I turn them in to the DMV?
Fortunately, the DMV will store them unto you come to reinstate them.
What happens if I surrender my standard plates?

What do I do with my personalized plates?
The DMV actually stores them for you until you go back to the same DMV you surrendered them to to get reinstated.
