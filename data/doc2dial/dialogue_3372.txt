I want to get a federal work-study job, so I wanted to get some information about it.
Of course. Undergraduate and graduate students with work-study jobs will work part-time on or off campus while enrolled.
Alright. What else can you tell me about federal work-study jobs?
They are administered by schools participating in the Federal Work-Study Program.
================
Does this mean I can work as many hours as I want?
No. The amount you earn can't exceed your total Federal Work-Study award.
Are you wondering how work hours are assigned?
Yes, please explain.
In order to do so, your employer or your school's financial aid office will consider your class schedule and your academic progress.
================
How can I find out if my school takes part in the work-study program?
Check with your school's financial aid office to find out if your school participates.
================
I'll do that. What about jobs that are off campus?
If you work off campus, your employer will usually be a private nonprofit organization or a public agency, and the work performed must be in the public interest.
