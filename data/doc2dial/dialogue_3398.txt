I have questions, questions about closed school loan discharges. Can you answer them, please?
================
Of course. Are you wondering what benefits you have by receiving a closed school loan discharge?
Yes, among other things.
You'll have several advantages by receiving a closed school loan discharge.
================
What if I have completed a program of study at another school, doing a teach-out?
In that case you are not eligible for a closed school discharge, by transferring academic credits or hours from the closed school, or by any other comparable means.
But am I still eligible for a discharge at least?
You may be eligible for discharge if you completed or are in the process of completing a comparable program at another school.
================
What if I initially enrolled in a program, but decided not to continue with that program and transferred to another program?
If you were also unable to complete Program B because the school closed, only loans you received for Program B could potentially be discharged.
================
I've received a certificate without completing the program. Do I qualify for a loan discharge in that case?
No, even though some schools may issue a diploma or certificate to students who did not complete the program of study.
