How do I provide proof of my SSI?
================
Do you want to prove you receive SSI?
Yes, I need to prove I receive SSI.
Please bring in one of the original documents not photocopied to the DMV.
What if I want to exchange in an office and keep my standard document type?
Then we have some steps you need to follow.
================
Is there a topic I can locate online?
Yes look under the topic ID Card.
================
Will they accept my standard passport?
As of October 1, 2020 they will not accept a standard passport and you should come to the DMV to upgrade to an Enhanced or REAL ID.
================
What if I do not have a Enhanced or REAL ID?
You will be able to use a standard passport up until October, 2020.
