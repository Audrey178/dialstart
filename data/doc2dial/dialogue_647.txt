Can you tell me anything about the disability benefits application process for a child?
================
Of course. Are you planning to apply for a child?
No.
================
Do you want me to walk you through the steps of an application?
Yes, that'd be great.
First you should review the Child Disability Starter Kit. This kit answers common questions about applying for Supplemental Security Income (SSI) benefits for children, and includes a worksheet that will help you gather the information you need.
What if I need additional help with the process?
No problem. You can always call us toll-free at 1-800-772-1213.
================
Great. And for the SSI application I need an appointment, correct?
Correct. Please contact us by phone or in person to schedule an appointment to complete the SSI application. We will help you in person or by phone.
