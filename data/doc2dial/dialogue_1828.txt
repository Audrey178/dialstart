In order for me to register my vehicle, do I have to have an insurance policy in place on that vehicle
================
For you to register a vehicle in New York, it is a requirement of the DMV that you have auto liability insuranc
================
I have a question in relation to my vehicle registration application. Does my name on that application form have to match my Insurance Identification Card
Yes, your name as it appears on your insurance card must match the name that appears on your vehicle registration application
================
Are there any restrictions in place regarding out-of-state insurance and what are the implications of not having these in order
You cannot drive your motorcycle on public highways without having insurance in place. If your name differs from your insurance to your registration, your insurance coverage may lapse and become invalid, which could result in the DMV suspending your driver license and registration
================
I will have a problem in providing my original Insurance Identification Card. Will the DMV be able to accept a copy or a fax?
Once the DMV barcode reader can read scan the barcode on your Insurance Identification Card, this will be acceptable
================
What type of policy do I need to have in place before I can register a vehicle?
Auto insurance liability is a pre-requisite before you can register a vehicle in New York
