What information needs to be reported on the FAFSA form about dependent students?
I can help you with that question. Dependent students must report their parents as well as their own information. They must find out who counts as their parent when they file the form.
What do I need to do if I am a dependent student?
You will need to provide information about your legal parent on the application.
================
What if I have a step mom?
Is your step mom married to the legal parent whose information you are reporting?
No, they are not married.
================
What do I do if my parents are unwilling to provide their information on the FAFSA form?
We can provide you with an explanation of what we consider special circumstances.
What if I agree to my parents not supporting me?
You may submit your FAFSA form without the information about your parent.
