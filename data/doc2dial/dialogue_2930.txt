Can you explain what a co-signer is for an Income-Driven Repayment Plan application?
A co-signer for an Income-Driver Repayment plan request is the spouse of the applicant.
Did they explain to you that you are not obligated as a spouse to repay the loan by co-signing?
Yes, that was explained to me.
What if the applicant completed an IDR plan request that was not part of a Direct Consolidation Loan Application?
He or she will click on "Income-Driven Repayment Plan Requests" under "My Loan Documents" to find and provide you with the required Reference Number.
================
Who should complete the co-signer form?
If you are the spouse of the applicant, then you should complete the Income-Drive Repayment Plan Request.
================
Where do I go to click for co-sign code?
You should click on "Direct Consolidation Loan Applications" under "My Loan Documents" to locate the code.
================
What do I need to do?
You must be logged in with your own unique FSA ID in order to Co-Sign the applicant's IDR Request.
