Are there many differences between federal and private student loans, and where would I get all the relevant information about these borrowings?
The government makes federal loans available, the terms and conditions of which are set out by the law. You can avail of a lot of benefits with a federal loan, such as fixed interest rates and income-driven repayments, which you won't find with private student loans. Private loans are offered by financial institutions whose terms and conditions are governed by the financial institution themselves
================
What type of funding is included if I apply for student financial aid?
It's quite likely that your school would include a student loan as part of your financial aid package, but it is important to understand the differences between federal loans and private student loans
================
What is the process required for getting a federal loan for my college attendance?
The first step of the application process is to complete the Free Application for Federal Student Aid FAFSA form. This form will also give you further information about applying for aid
================
Does my school choose one type of student loan over another in respect of any financial aid package that I might be offered?
It's likely that they will include student loans as part of the package, but the advice is to be certain that the loan that is offered to you is the best choice for you personally
