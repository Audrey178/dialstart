Hello, I am here to figure out how I can get disability benefits.
================
In order to get these benefits, you'll need to file a claim for disability compensation. When you file, you'll have to show that the added disability happened because of VA medical care or a VA program designed to help you find, get, or keep a job.
Do you want to file a claim?
No, at least not now.
================
I've read that if my disability was caused by unexpected complications, it can qualify me for a benefit. Is that correct?
Yes. One example would be if VA carelessness or negligence led directly to an added disability or to your injury or disease getting worse.
What about a mistake during a surgical treatment? Does that count?
Yes, this also applies when VA medical or surgical treatment led directly to an added disability or to your injury or disease getting worse.
================
And who's covered?
Veterans are covered.
