Are their repercussions for retiring early?
================
While you can retire between age 62 and full retirement age, there can be penalties for starting benefits early.
Are you planning on starting your benefits early?
No, I am not wanting to start my benefits at an earlier age.
================
Are there any rewards for delaying my benefits until after full retirement age?
Yes, you may be eligible for delayed retirement credits that would increase your monthly benefit.
================
What happens to my benefits if I decide to start receiving them early?
If you receive your benefits early they will be reduced a fraction of a percent for each month before your full retirement age.
================
What is an advantage for taking your benefits before your full retirement age?
The advantage is that you get to collect benefits over a longer period of time.
================
What is the earliest age that I can start receiving benefits?
You can receive benefits as early as 62 years old.
================
What do I need to select to find out how much my benefit will be reduced if I retire between 62 and full retirement age?
You need to select your year of birth.
