Hello there. I came to get advice on how to transfer an IRP registration and have several questions about the process.
Do you want to transfer an IRP registration and apportioned plates?
Yes, I do.
In that case you can request that the IRB transfer the registration when you buy or register the new vehicle.
================
Can I apply for a IRP registration transfer online?
Yes, you can apply to transfer an IRP registration online through the New York State One Stop Credentialing and Registration system OSCAR.
================
I see. What if I send someone else to hand in my application?
For this scenario, see the requirements for a second-party registration application.
================
I could also do it by fax. Can you transfer an IRP registration by fax?
Yes, for this you simply need to fax us the items described above to the IRB at518-486-6579.
================
Can I also transfer an IRP registration at the Customer Service Counter in Albany?
Of course. Just bring the required items you'll find listed on our website with you.
