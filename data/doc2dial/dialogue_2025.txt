I have been placed on a problem driver restriction, how long will this restriction be for, can i have it removed early?
================
may i ask if this was due to an alcohol-related incident?
yes, how long will the restriction be?
it remains in effect for 5 years from the date you receive your drivers licence.
is there any way i can have the restriction removed early?
no, it can not be removed earlier
================
How does it work if i am supposed to have a DMV mandated ignition interlock device?
you may have to install the device on any and all vehicles you own and/or operate, and you are also subject to the problem driving restrictions.
================
am i able to drive through new york state with my out of state CDL license if my new york state record has the problem driver restriction?
no, a driver who has been issued with a problem driver restriction on their new york state record can not operate a vehicle in new york state even if an out of state licence was issued.
================
what happens if i have another violation before i obtain my driver's license with the problem driver restriction?
your drivers license or permit with the problem driver restriction will be withdrawn if you are convicted of any violations such as tailgating or speeding.
================
