How do I estimate registration fees and taxes?
Is this the first time you are registering your vehicle?
Yes this is the first time.
You must pay the registration fee and vehicle plate fee as well as some other fees.
================
Are boat and trailer fees the same?
No boat and trailer fees are calculated differently and can't be estimated online.
================
Can I transfer fees from one vehicle to another?
If you transfer the registration and plates from another vehicle, you don't need to ay the plate fees or MCTD fee and you may be able to transfer the registration fees to your new vehicle.
What happens if I just need to transfer from one vehicle to another?
You will not have to pay the plate fees or MCTD fee and you may be able to transfer the registration fees to your new vehicle.
================
Can I estimate fees and taxes online?
The online estimate does not include the sales tax.
================
How can I estimate boat and trailer fees?
You can't use the online service to estimate them.  You will need to see register a trailer or register a boat for how to estimate these registration fees.
