Hello my vehicle plates were stolen, what do I do? Please help me.
================
You will need to surrender your registration if the plates have been stolen.
No someone might have taken them but I'm going to go look for them later.
If I do determine that they have been stolen, how do I get new plates?
You will need to report that the plates have been stolen and surrender their registration. The DMV can then issue a new registration and plates.
================
If I find the plates can my brother-in-law bring them in to prove that I got them back?
Yes but only if he intends to surrender the plates for you.
Maybe I don't trust him so much. Can my little brother some in instead?
Yes as long as he will surrender the plates for you.
================
He's probably too young to understand forms and stuff. Can I fill in some of the paperwork online?
Yes there is the Complete the PD-7 application that is available as a PDF online.
Can I submit that online or do I have to print it up and give it to him to bring in?
Yes you can submit the form online.
