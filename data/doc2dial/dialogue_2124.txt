I need to change my address
================
Are you reporting a change of address for your business?
Yes
You must complete a Statement of Ownership and/or Permission to Use Place of Business pdf VS-19
Where can I get the form
You can also call the DMV Office of Vehicle Safety and Clean Air at 1 - 518 - 474 - 4653 to get the form , or call 1 - 518 - 474 - 0919 to get more information
================
What do I do if I also have an Inspectation Station License?
please see Inspection Station Cap Information
What happens if the new location was previously registered as a repair shop?
You must provide the Facility number or name of that shop. This may be accepted as proof of zoning
================
