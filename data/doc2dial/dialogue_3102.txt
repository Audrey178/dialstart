What exactly is a TEACH Grant that is available through the FSA?
This type of grant is for students who are completing coursework in preparation for a career in teaching. The candidates need to agree to work in an elementary school, secondary school or educational service agency that serves students from a low-income family for at least four years

Students that complete this form are those who are in receipt of a TEACH Grant and who have also completed TEACH Initial and Subsequent Counseling for the same Award Year
