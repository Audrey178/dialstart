Do I qualify for a discharge of my federal student loan?
You can qualify if you are totally or permanently disabled.
Are you totally and permanently disabled?
Yes I am.
To qualify you must complete and submit a TPD discharge application, along with various documentation.
================
What if I submit VA documentation?
We will notify you that your obligation has been discharged based on VA documentation.
================
Through the document, who does the term "we", "our", and "us" refer to?
The US Department of Education.
What other documents will I need?
You will need documents that show that you meet our requirements for being considered totally and permanently disabled.
================

Is your disability expected to result in death?
Sadly, yes.
I am terribly sorry to hear that. You can have your doctor certify on the TPD discharge application that you are unable to engage in any substantial gainful activity due to a physical or mental impairment.

Does a TPD discharge mean that I won't have to repay my loan back?
Yes. You will not have to pay your loan back.
