I need some help with retirement benefits.
================
Are you needing help with benefits on your record or your spouse's record?
Yes, i'm the spouse.
We can help you with that.  Many people who have never worked under Social Security still qualify for benefits under their spouse.
Can a divorced spouse get benefits?
You may still be eligible to receive benefits even if you are divorced.
================
Can my children receive benefits?
Other members of your family may be eligible for benefits.  You can go to "Benefits For Your Family"  If you never married or had children, then go to "How You Apply For Retirement Benefits Or Medicare"
================
Can you qualify for your own benefits and spousal benefits?
Yes, it is possible to qualify for both.  Your benefits are paid first.
================
How do I apply for benefits?
You can apply online with the online Social Security Retirement/Medicare Benefit Application for your benefits, spousal, divorced spouse or Medicare.
Can I apply if I don't live in the U.S.?
Yes, you can contact the nearest U.S. Social Security office, U.S. Embassy or consulate to apply.
