Can you tell me about my driving school instructor certificate?
================
Did you pass all tests?
Yes, I passed all my tests.
Then the Bureau of Driver Training Programs will issue a Driving School Instructor Certificate MV-524 and mail it to the driving school where you are employed.
Do I need to show a customer or DMV employee my certificate if they ask for it?
Yes, you must have your certificate on hand to show them.
================
What happens when my Driving School Certificate needs to be renewed?
Before it can be renewed you must complete a course in driver training and traffic safety which is approved by the Commissioner of at 30 hours.
================
What if my driver license is from another state besides NY?
A certified copy of your driving record from the state agency that issued your license must be included.
================
What should the owner do with the application?
The owner should mail the application.
