How do I appeal to a veterans law judge?
================
Download VA For 10182 Board Appeal PDF
================
Do you need to request a board appeal?
yes I do
You can request a Board Appeal after an initial claim , Supplemental Claim , or Higher - Level Review decision. You can t request two Board Appeals in a row.
================
When can I request a higher-level review?
You can request a Higher - Level Review of an initial claim or Supplemental Claim decision.  This option isn t available after a Higher - Level Review or Board decision .
================
What happens next?
If VA schedules exams for you be sure not to miss them
================
Can I get a new look from a senior reviewer?
A senior reviewer will take another look at your case and determine whether the decision can be changed based on a difference of opinion or an error .
================
What if I disagree with the board and have new evidence?
you can file a Supplemental Claim . You can also appeal to the U.S. Court of Appeals for Veterans Claims .
