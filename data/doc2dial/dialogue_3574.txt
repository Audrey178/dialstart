Tell me about the increase for delayed retirement.
================
Have you already reached full retirement age?
Not yet.
Can I decide to delay my retirement?
You can, but be sure to sign up for just Medicare at age 65.
when would I start receiving benefits after reaching full retirement age?
You can choose to start receiving the benefits from the month before you apply.
================
What would be the best time to start receiving my retirement benefits?
We do not recommend starting at age 62, your full retirement age, age 70, or any age in between.
How could I decide?
You can get more information to help you decide by reading the sections Early or Late Retirement, Retirement Information for Medicare Beneficiaries, to Consider and Other things.
