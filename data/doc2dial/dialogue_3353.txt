Absolutely, but the maximum period that you can receive Direct Subsidized Loans is three years.
================

Yes, this would be a good choice for you. Direct Subsidized Loans are also great for those who enter a period of deferment of their loan payments.
Who is eligible for a Direct Subsidized Loan?
These loans are for undergraduate students with financial need.
