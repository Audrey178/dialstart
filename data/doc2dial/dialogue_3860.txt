What should I do if my vehicle is recovered?
================
If your vehicle is recovered, make sure to notify the police so they can cancel the stolen vehicle alarm and list the recovery on your title.
Will my title be revised?
Yes, your title will be revised to show that your vehicle was recovered.
If my vehicle and plates are recovered before I file a MV-78B form, is my registration still active?
Yes, your registration will still be active as long as you make sure to cancel the stolen vehicle alarm.
Would I need to apply for new plates?
You would need to apply for new plates if your vehicle was recovered after you filed form MV-78B.
What should I do if my plates are missing?
In the case of missing plates, you would need to bring your copy of form MV-78B to a DMV office to apply for new plates and reinstatement of your registration.
Who should I contact if my recovered vehicle was damaged or totaled?
You would need to contact your auto insurance company.
If my plates were recovered after I filled out a form, will I have to surrender my old plates?
Yes, the old plates will have to be surrendered and you will have to register for new ones.
When should I not apply for plates?
You should not apply for new plates until after you cancel the police alarm for a stolen vehicle.
