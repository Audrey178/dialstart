What if the vehicle has an odometer reading of more than 7,500 miles?
In that case your vehicle is exempt from the California emissions standard
What if the vehicle meets the California emissions standard?
Even if your vehicle meets the California emissions standard, it is still subject to New York State emissions inspection and safety requirements.
