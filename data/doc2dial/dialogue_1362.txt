Good evening. I have to organize a burial, but I fear that I won't qualify for burial benefits. What will happen if I get rejected?
================
I'll gladly explain. We'll send you a denial decision letter explaining the reasons or basis of our denial. We'll also send you a form called Your Rights to Seek Further Review of Our Decision VA Form 20-0998 along with your denial letter. This form explains your rights to appeal or to request another review, and how to submit more evidence.
================
Are you wondering how much time you have to appeal the decision or request another review?
No, I am familiar with the deadlines.
================
Would you like to know if your family member or authorized representative can still apply for my burial in a VA national cemetery at the time of need?
Yes, can they?
Of course. Laws and personal circumstances may change earlier eligibility decisions.
================
That's good to know. And how can I update my information?
You can update your information at any time. Call our toll-free hotline at 877-222-8387. We're here Monday through Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. ET.
================
Can you tell me more about appeals?
You can learn more about appeals and reviews of VA claims on our page.
================
Do you also have information about burials on your page?
Yes, you can also learn more about planning a burial for a family member.
================
And when will you decide if I qualify for a burial?
We'll make the final decision of whether you qualify for burial at the time of need.
