Could you give me a summary of what I need to bring to the DMV to register a moped?
Firstly you will need to complete a Registration/Title Application form PDF (4) MV-82
Is there any way for me to speed up the service of registering a moped?
Yes there is
To speed up the registration process, you can make a reservation online for any of the offices located in New York City, Westchester, Nassau, Suffolk, Rockland, Onondaga, Monroe or Albany
