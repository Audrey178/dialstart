I'm enquiring about out of state insurance docs
================
We will never accept out - of - state vehicle insurance coverage of any type
Is your vehicle registered in New York?
Yes
it must have New York State auto liability insurance coverage.Your motorcycle must have insurance coverage any time it is operated on a public road or highway. Unlike most other motor vehicles , motorcycles are registered for one year rather than two
================
Do I need insurance to register my vehicle?
Yes the DMV requires auto liability insurance to register a vehicle in New York
How long do I have to register?
You must register your vehicle at the DMV within 180 days of the effective date on your insurance ID card
================
Where can I find more information?
The New York State Department of Financial Services website has more information about liability insurance and insurance companies licensed in New York : http://www.dfs.ny.gov
================
What kind of proof of insurance do I need?
 Your insurance company will issue proof of insurance in two ways: It will give you two original NY State Insurance ID Cards or provide you with access to your digital electronic NY State Insurance ID Card
