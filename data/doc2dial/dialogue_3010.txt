Would you like to know more about Exit Counseling?
yes please
Exit counseling provides information to prepare you to repay your federal student loan.
Do I need the counseling for any loan?
If you have received a subsidized, unsubsidized or PLUS loan under the Direct Loan Program or the FFEL Program, you must complete exit counseling when certain events occur.
================
will I need to do it?
If you have received a subsidized, unsubsidized or PLUS loan under the Direct Loan Program or the FFEL Program, you must complete exit counseling each time you drop below half-time enrollment, graduate, or leave school.
================
how much time does this take up?
You must complete the process in a single session that lasts around 20-30 minutes.
================
what will I need to bring with me?
You will need a verified FSA ID
