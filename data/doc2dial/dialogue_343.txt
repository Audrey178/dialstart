Can my family qualify for benefits on my record even if I didn't qualify for benefits on someone else's record?
================
Yes, some members of your family may still qualify for benefits on your record.
Would you like to learn more information about this?
No, thank you I don't need more information.
Is there more information regarding benefits if you were never married?
Yes, you can visit "How You Apply For Retirement Benefits Or Medicare."
================
Do you have to be a certain age to get a spouse's retirement benefits?
Yes, you must be at least 62 years of age.
================
Is there someone I can contact about benefits for my spouse if I don't live in the U.S.?
Yes, you can contact the nearest U.S. Social Security Office, a U.S. Embassy, or a consulate.
================
Will the benefits I receive decrease my spouse's retirement benefit?
No, it will not.
================
At what age can I qualify for Medicare?
You can qualify for Medicare at age 65.
================
At what ages will my benefits be permanently reduced?
Between age 62 and your full retirement age.
