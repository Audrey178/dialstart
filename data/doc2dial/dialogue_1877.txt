Anything I need to do before taking the road test?
Once you get your learner permit, you have to obey the restrictions on where and when you are allowed to drive. You should also complete a 5-hour pre-licensing course or a high school or college driver education course.
================
How long it lasts the pre-licensing course certificate?
The pre-licensing course certificate lasts for 1 year from the date it is issued, and the student certificate of completion lasts for two years.
================
anything else I need to do before the road test?
apart from obeying the learner permits restrictions and completing a 5-hour pre-licensing course, you should get a good amount of supervised driving practice.
================
please give me more recommendations about practicing and lessons for my road test.
It is recommended a minimum of 50 hours of supervised driving for drivers of all ages and that you take a driver education course or professional driving lessons.
================
I'm under 18, what I need to do before taking the road test?
You have to wait for at least six months from the date you received your learner permit to schedule a road test.
================
What should I do in case I lost my certificate?
You must contact the school that issued the certificate, they will give you a replacement.
