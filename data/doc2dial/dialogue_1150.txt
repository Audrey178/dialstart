How do I set it up for a VSO to help me on my behalf?
To have a VSO help you, you will need to fill out an Appointment of Veterans Service Organization as Claimants Representative VA Form 21-22 and you can download the form online.
================
Who can represent a Veteran?
Recognized organization and individuals can legally represent a Veteran and non-recognized organizations and individuals can provide information, but are not allowed to be representative.
Can a dependent of mine be covered under recognized organizations?
Yes, a dependent can be covered under the same rules.
================
What is the actual cost to be represented by an accredited representative or a VSO?
They are not allowed to charge you to just file your initial application; but they may charge you for unusual expenses.
================
How do I get a claims agent or attorney help me?
You would need to fill out an Appointment of Individual as Claimant's Representative VA Form 21-22a and you can download it.
