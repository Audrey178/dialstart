Why would someone have to have an ignition interlock device?
================
They may be ordered for people with a history of alcohol-related violations.
I don't have a history of it, just the one time.
================
If I have a violation before I get my restricted license or permit, what would happen?
Your license will be revoked for 30 days.
================
If you have an interlock device, are there any other restrictions?
If you are required to install an ignition interlock device, you will be subjected to problem driving restrictions.
Is there a fee to install the device?
Yes, there is an installation fee.  A complete listing of fees is on the Ignition Interlock page.
================
When am I allowed to drive if I have a problem driving restriction?
To and from your place of employment during the hours of your employment.
Can I still drive to school?
Yes, you can drive to and from the school at which you are enrolled.
