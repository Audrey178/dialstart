How do I go about getting customized plates?
================
The I LOVE NY Adventure Custom Plates viewable here [ 2 ] are available to individuals that hold a currently valid NYS Department of Environmental Conservation DEC issued Sporting License and/or a NYS Office of Parks , Recreation and Historic Preservation Parks issued Empire Passport .
