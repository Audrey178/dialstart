Hello? Please, i'm a non citizen adult and i'm looking to get a replacement for a SSN card. Can i apply? Need documents for that?
================
Hi. Indeed, you can apply. As immigrant, first thing to do is to prove your immigration status to us. As such, you must present your current immigration document.
Can you prove your U.S immigration status?
Yeah, i can prove that, i have the proper documentation.
Perfect. Then you must show us one of the next documents: Form I-551 Immigrant Visa or I-766 EAD work permit.
No problem. I can do that. Please, i want to know something. All non citizens can apply for a SSN?
Sorry, but that would be a no. Just those non citizens who have work permit from DHS can apply for a Social security number.
I see. And what about if i'm a foreign worker? Can i apply too?
If that's the case. You'll just need to show us form I-94, Arrival or Departure Record or the admission stamp in your valid foreign passport showing a class of admission permitting to work. In some cases, workers must show form I-766 from DHS.
Um, ok, thanks. Just to be sure about an earlier point, only non citizens with work permit can apply for SSN, correct?
That's correct. Only those with work permit from DHS can apply.
Thanks. Look, i don't have this condition, but i wanted to know what i would need to show in case i need a social security number with tax purposes?
Sure, i can give you that information. If you have that case, you can apply for an individual Taxpayer identification number from the U.S IRS. You need to call the IRS toll free number to get more info.
Ok, got it. Last question, what about if i'm a student?
Well, if that' the case, aside the earlier documents, you'll need to show us your form I-20.
