What is available for me to estimate my student aid?
Are you not ready to apply for federal student aid, but want an estimate for future reference?
Yes, I am not ready and just would like an estimate.
Then you are fortunate enough to get an estimate with FAFSA4caster.
Is the FAFSA4caster free?
Yes, the FAFSA4caster is free and gives you an estimate of your eligibility for federal student aid and once you are ready to apply fill out your FAFSA form.
================
Am I allowed to compare schools?
You are certainly able to compare schools by changing the cost of attendance, deleting state aid if you will be an out-of-state student, or modifying the amount of aid available from the school, among others.
================
Will FAFSA4caster help me understand my options?
Yes, it is a helpful tool for each person who is not yet ready to fill out a FAFSA form and really is intended for juniors or even younger so parents can start saving up for funding.
================
Is info provided by FAFSA4caster able to help apply for aid?
It is basically just information to show what you could receive in the future and is not an application for aid.
