I have a family member passed and need help with driver license permit and ID.
================
Please mail a copy of the death certificate and a copy of their license to the DMV.
This does not apply to me.
Will this prevent identity theft?
Yes.
================
Why is this neccessary?
They often leave outstanding issues.
================
Who do I need to contact?
Please call 518 - 474 - 0902 the DMV Revenue Accounting Unit
What about registration refund?
It may be possible and would be refunded to the name of the estate.
What if the estate is settled?
Call the DMV Revenue Accounting Unit
