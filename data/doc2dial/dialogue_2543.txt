I'm removing my truck from the road for a while and I need to know what to do with my professional plates.
================
You can keep your professional plates and have them stored for you at most DMV offices.
Do you plan to register your vehicle again in the future?
Yes. I will be registering it again later on.
Great. When you are ready to register your vehicle again, you need to go to the DMV office that has your plates stored. Be sure to bring your receipt form FS-6 T and any other documents you need to register your vehicle.
================
What about my custom plate fees? My vehicle was only registered for a year.
Since you've only had the registration for a year, you qualify for a refund for your custom plate fee. To get your refund, you need to submit Request for refund or fee paid for motor vehicle registrations and driver licenses MV-215. You will be charged a $1.00 processing fee.
================

The prices of professional plates vary. You can browse the available plate styles and view detailed pricing information on our website.

These plates can be purchased for an additional fee, but their prices vary. You can find pricing information for specific plates on our website.
