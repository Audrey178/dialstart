Can you answer a few questions about registering a vehicle, please? I plan to visit the DMV office next week.
================
Of course. Please bring a completed Vehicle Registration/Title Application (MV-82) or Application for Title (MV-82TON) with you.
Would you like to know what other documents instead of those two you can use to register?
No, thank you. I already have these two documents ready.
================
What other documents do you need, a proof of my payment of the sales tax?
Yes, we accept an FS-6 T receipt issued by DMV or a Dealer Retail Certificate of Sale MV-50 completed by a dealer located in New York State.
Where should I send all these documents?
To the NYS DMV Title ServicesDepartment of Motor Vehicles6 Empire State PlazaAlbany, NY 12228-0331.
Alright. What else should I put inside in the envelope?
The New York State title or other acceptable proof of ownership.
================
My dealer requested information about removing a lien. Where can he find it?
See the section "Remove or add a lienholder from a title" on our website.
================
By the way, do I need to include buyer and seller information with the title?
Yes. The title must have completed buyer and seller information, both the leasing company and the buyer must provide their signatures, dates of transfer, and addresses.
================
I understand. What else should I include?
You should also include a completed statement of transaction sale or gift of the vehicle in question.
