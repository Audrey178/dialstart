I need help with a vehicle that was owned by a family member who recently passed away.
================
There's a checklist that you can use to get started.
How do I transfer ownership?
You must complete an affidavit.
It should automatically transfer to you depending on the value.
================
What documents do I need?
The previous registration.
================
What if there's more than one?
They will become part of the estate.
================
How do I file?
Go to the department of transportation.
================
Who has to sign the transfer?
The owner.
