What do I do if I want to get a revocation?
You must first apply for and receive approval from the DMV IUD Driver Improvement Unit before you can apply for a new driver's license at a DMV office
================
What to do if you have two or more convictions for drugged driving?
You must complete an alcohol evaluation and / or treatment within one year from the date of the final review. Your provider must electronically notify the DMV of its successful completion. Paper evidence submitted by you will not be accepted.
