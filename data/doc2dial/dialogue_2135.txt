What are rules for ATVs on private land?
================
Will you be operating on your own private land or someone else's?
On someone else's land.
You must have permission of the land owner or lessee.
What else do I need from the land owner?
You must know the boundaries of the property and respect any special restrictions or request of the land owner or lessee.
================
How old do you have o be to operate an ATV?
A parent or guardian may not knowingly permit or authorize his or her child under age 16 to operate a ATV.
================
What are rules for operating an ATV?
You may not operate an ATV other than while seated on the permanent, regular seat and only carry passengers if the ATV is designed to carry a passenger.
================
What documentation do I need to have when operating an ATV?
You should carry your ATV safety training course certificate when operating any ATV.
================
Is there a law about who can operate an ATV?
Yes, no one inn possession of an ATV can allow any one under the age of 16 to operate an ATV.
================
What do I do if I have an accident and damage property but the owner is not around?
You must provide the name and address of the ATV owner as well as the plate number as well as proof of insurance to the police as soon as possible.
