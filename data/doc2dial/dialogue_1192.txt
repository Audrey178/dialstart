I need MGIB active duty info
The Montgomery GI Bill Active Duty can help you pay for education and training programs
Have you served at least 2 years on active duty?
I've done more than 2 years
Okay, we can help you find out if you qualify for the MGIB-AD program
================
how do I qualify?
You qualify if you were honorably discharged and meet the requirements
================
what benefits are available if I do qualify?
You may get up to 36 months of education benefits. The benefits will be payed monthly. How much you receive will depend on several factors
================
what are the requirements?
Depends on the category of the MGIB-AD program you will be in?
================
how do I apply?
You have to complete the Application for VA Benefits
================
and if I get benefits I can study what I want?
Yes, but if you want to enroll in a program that we haven’t approved, you’ll need to ask the school to request approval. We can’t take any action until an official from your school requests approval.
