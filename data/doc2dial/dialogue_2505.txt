Hello, I would like to know what happens if I receive a traffic ticket in another state?
================

Your New York State driver's license will be suspended if you do not respond to a ticket for a traffic violation in any state except Alaska, California, Michigan, Montana, Oregon, or Wisconsin. Your license will remain suspended until you respond to the ticket.
Does the New York State DMV record convictions out of state?
The New York State DMV does not record out-of-state convictions for traffic violations by New York State noncommercial licensed drivers, except for traffic offenses committed in the provinces of Ontario and Quebec in Canada.
================
What happens if the offender is under 21 years old?
If you are under the age of 21 when you commit an out-of-state alcohol or drug related driving violation and are convicted of the violation, your New York State driver's license will be revoked for at least one year
What if you have a previous alcohol or drug related driving conviction?
Your license will be revoked for at least one year or until the age of 21, whichever is longer.
================
And what happens to the license when there are convictions for criminal negligence, murder or assault?
The New York State DMV records out-of-state convictions of any New York driver for criminal negligence, manslaughter, or battery, resulting from the operation of a motor vehicle and resulting in death. As a result of any of these convictions, your New York State driver's license will be revoked.
