How do I get a ticket of a TVB ticket?
================
Are you able to get online?
Yes I can.
Was your TVB ticket lost or destroyed?
Yes it was lost.
You can print a substitute online.  The ticket you are replacing must have the words Traffic Violations Bureau at the bottom.
================
Can I print substitute tickets for all my open TVB tickets?
Yes, once you provide information for one open TVB traffic ticket you will be able to print any open TVB traffic ticket you have.
================
What can I do if my traffic ticket isn't available to print?
If it is not available to print, the transaction will give you instructions on what you can do.
================
Are the printed substitute tickets the same as tickets printed at a TVB office?
Yes, a substitute ticket that you print has exactly the same information as tickets printed at a TVB office.
================
Do I need my driver license number?
You will need either your DMV ID Number Client ID Number from your New York State driver License, learner permit or non-driver photo ID card.
