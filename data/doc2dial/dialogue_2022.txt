If I have a problem driver restriction on my license, how long does this restriction last and is there anything I can do to remove this restriction early?
================
Was your license revoked due to an alcohol-related incident?
Yes it was
In this instance, the restriction is in place for five years from the date that you received your permit or license
================
If there are alcohol-related violations associated with my driver history, does this affect problem driver restrictions with a DMV Mandated Ignition Interlock  Device?
If this is the case, you may have to install an ignition interlock device. You will be subject to problem driving restrictions, and these restrictions will indicate that you are required to have an ignition interlock device
================
What happens if there is a moving violation conviction received before receiving a driver licence with a problem driver restriction?
If you have been convicted of any moving violations including tailgating or speeding, the approval that you received for a driver license or permit will be withdrawn
================
Where can I find more information regarding installation fees for Mandated Ignition Interlock Devices?
This information can be found at the Ignition Interlock page at the NYS Division of Criminal Justice Services website
What happens if I am convicted of an offence while driving out of restriction?
In this instance, your license will be revoked
