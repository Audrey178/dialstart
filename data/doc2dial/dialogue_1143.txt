How do I find an accredited representative or a VSO?
You can find an accredited representative or a VSO in 1 to 2 ways:  Go to eBenefits to find a local representative that includes a recognized VSO, attorney, or claims agent by state / territory, zip code, or organization name.

What are VSOs and what do they do?
VSOs work on behalf of veterans and service members, as well as their dependents and survivors
