I need to report a problem with a DMV regulated automotive business.
================
Are you sure it is a DMV regulated business?
No, I am not.
================
Do you need to know how to file a complaint against a DMV regulated automotive business?
No I do not.
================
How do I contact?
If you have questions about the complaint process you can contact the DMV Complaint Unit at 1-518-474-8943.
What are the hours I can call?
Office hours are Monday-Friday, 8:15am to 4:15pm.
================
What is regulated by the DMV?
The DMV regulates all repair shops, inspection stations, dealers, junk and salvage vendors, and other automotive related businesses in New York State.
================
How can I get information on the investigation and hearing process?
For additional information on the investigation and hearing process, please see our What to Expect if You File a Complaint Against a DMV Regulated Business.
