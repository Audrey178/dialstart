Yes. What new CDL restriction changes have been made?
There are new CDL restrictions may apply to CLP holders who partake in skills tests in vehicles that do not have full airbrakes or a manual transmission.
What vehicle requirements could cause a restriction on my CDL?
There are several incidents which would result in a restriction on your license. For example, if the vehicle you are driving has an automatic transmission and this is your first road test in a CMV, there will be a E restriction on your license.
================
What new revocations could effect my ability to obtain a CLP or CDL?
Your commercial license will be revoked for one year if you are found guilty or were convicted of falsifying a CDL application or misrepresented your self certification driving type.
