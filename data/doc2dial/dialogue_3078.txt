What is a grant repayment?
Your school will notify you if you have repay part of your grant and from that point, you will have 45 days to either pay that portion of the grant back in full or enter into a satisfactory repayment arrangement
What happens if I enter into a satisfactory repayment arrangement?
In that case,  the school may assign the debt to ED for collection or may keep the debt and allow you to make payments directly to the school
