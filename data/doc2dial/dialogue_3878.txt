What happens if I don't comply with the restrictions?
You run the risk of having your learner permit suspended or revoked for 60 days.
What junior driver license classes are restricted under the Graduated License Law?
In addition to a junior learner permit, junior driver license classes DJ, MJ, and DJMJ are restricted under the Graduated License Law.
