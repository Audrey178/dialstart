Can you please give me some information on the Direct PLUS Loan application for graduate students?
================
Do you need to know what the Direct PLUS Loan application for graduate students is?
I do.
This application is a request for supplemental information in connection with your application for a Federal Direct PLUS Loan Direct PLUS Loan through the Willam D. Ford Federal Direct Loan Direct Loan Program.  Some schools may have different processes for obtaining the additional information needed to process your application.
Have you placed a security freeze on your credit file?
I have placed a freeze on my credit file.  Will that be an issue?
If you have placed a freeze on your credit file, you must life or remove it at each credit bureau before you can continue with this process.  Your application will not be processed if you have a security freeze.
================
Can I apply for a PLUS Loan online?
You can, and as with anything else on the web, you just go to the website and log in to start and then you would start the demo.
================
What must I do before I can receive a Direct PLUS Loan?
Before you can receive a Direct PLUS Loan, you must complete a Direct PLUS Loan Master Promissory Note Direct PLUS Loan MPN, which explains all of the terms and conditions of the loan and constitutes your legally binding agreement to repay all loans that you receive under the Direct PLUS Loan MPN.
What happens with the information that I enter for the application?
The information that you provide will be sent to the school you select to use to determine your eligibility for a Direct PLUS Loan and to process your application.
