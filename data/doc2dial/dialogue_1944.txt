Hi, I'm looking for information regarding replacement of a title certificate
================
Can I replace a title certificate online?
================
Can I order the replacement certificate online?
To order a replacement title certificate online, you must be the owner of the vehicle, you must have a current UD address on file with the DMV and the vehicle in question must be registered and titled in New York State
================
What happens if I apply for a replacement title certificate at a DMV office?
You won't receive your replacement title certificate at the DMV office, they are printed at an off-site facility and are mailed to the owner
================
Where is the replacement certificate sent if the registration for the vehicle is in my name?
The re[placement is mailed to the address that is held on file for you. If you wish to amend this address, please do this online before replacing your title certificate
================
I need further information about replacing a title certificate only
For a title certificate only replacement, you must use the Application for Title Only PDF (6) MV-82TON and follow the instructions carefully
