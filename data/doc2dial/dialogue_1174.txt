No I don't have any new evidence to support my claim.
================
How do I apply for a Decision Review?
You can ask a regional benefit office for a copy of Form 10182 and fill it out or you can call the VA toll-free hotline at 800-827-1000 and request a copy of the form.
================
I am not in agreement with the Board's decision and have further relevant evidence that supports my case what should I do to have my case reassessed?
If you have new or further relevant evidence that supports your case you can file a Supplemental Claim. You can also appeal to the U.S. Court of Appeals for Veterans Claims.
================
I am not able to continue with the claim on my own where can I get help with this?
You can get help requesting a decision review by using the services of a Veterans Service Organization or VA-accredited attorney or agent.
================
What information do I have to give to the appeal board?
You will have to make a list of the issues you disagree with and the VA decision date for each of the issues.
================
If I am not happy with the decision regarding my claim is it possible to request a Board Appeal?
Yes you can. You can request a Board Appeal after an initial claim, Supplemental Claim, or Higher-Level Review decision, however you cannot request two Board Appeals in a row.
