I would like to ask about motorcycle and trailer plates.
================
What do you want to know?
Do i need to surrender my motorcycle plates if it is not insured?
No. It is not compulsory but if you ride the motorcyle, you need to insured it.
================
What if i lost it?or if it is stolen?
If that happens, then you need to surrender the registration and report the lost, stolen or destroyed plates. New registration and plates will be issued by the DMV.
================
When i go to the DMV Office to surrender my plates, what the first thing that i need to do?
You will need to first remove the plates and stickers.
================
I heard that liability insurance are not required by some trailers. So do i need to surrender the plates if the trailer is not insured?
Yes however, if the trailer does not have a valid registration, you need to surrender the plates regardless of whether the trailer required or not to have a liability insurance.
================
After i remove the plates and stickers at the DMV Office to surrender my plates, what should i do?
You will need to check the plates. Plate attached with plate frames and fasteners will not be accepted.
================
For lost, stolen or destroyed plates, can i get a new one using the old registration?
No. You need to surrender the registration and a new registration and plates will be issued after that by the DMV.
