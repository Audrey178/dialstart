What happens if I am on disability benefits and reach full retirement age? Do I receive disability payments and retirement benefits?
If you are receiving Social Security disability benefits when you reach full retirement age your disability benefits automatically convert to retirement benefits but the amount remains the same.

Are there any special rules for the blind?
You are considered legally blind under Social Security rules if your vision cannot be corrected to better than 20/200 in your better eye or if your visual field is 20 degrees or less, even with a corrective lens.
