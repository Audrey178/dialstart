Hi. I need more information about your medical benefits package
================
In our package we cover preventive care services, such as:  Health exams, including gender-specific exams  Health education, including nutrition education
What finger to do to use these services?
In order to use these services, you must be enrolled in VA health care, and you must have received care from us within the past 24 months. Be sure to tell the urgent care provider that you are using the VA urgent care benefit when you arrive.
