Could you please give me some information on how to apply for benefits if I am not currently living in the U.S.?
================
You would need to contact the Federal Benefits Unit for your country of residence if you live outside the U.S. or a U.S. territory and wish to apply for retirement benefits.
================
Are you attempting to begin a retirement account?
I am not yet.  I am just seeking information to prepare myself for my retirement as the time draws nearer.
================
What are the alternatives to checking my status online?
We have 1-800 telephone and TTY numbers that you will be able to use to check your status once your application has been submitted.
================
Can you give me any information about finalizing the application process and signing the online application?
Before you sign the online application, you will have a chance to review your answers and make any necessary changes.  Once you have filled everything out, we will ask you to confirm that your answers are true to the best of your knowledge and then you can sign the application and submit.
================
Can I include information with my application for other family members at the same time?
If you have included information about other family members when you applied, we will let you know if they may be able to receive benefits from your application.
================
What is the question that I will be asked before I electronically sign the application?
We will ask you to confirm that your answers are true to the best of your knowledge.
