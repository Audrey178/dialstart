When I'm filing a supplemental claim with new evidence to VA, what issues do I include in the claim?
In your claim, you can include all of the issues that VA decided upon or just some of them. Any of the issues that you disagree with must be listed along with the VA decision date
================
What exact information needs to be included in a supplemental claim with new evidence to the VA?
The new evidence that is required is classed as information that was not already submitted to the VA when they made a decision. Information is deemed to be relevant evidence if it proves or disproves something that is contained in your case
================
What forms must be completed when filing a supplemental claim with new evidence to VA?
You must complete a Decision Review Request: Supplemental Claim VA Form 20-0995, this form can be downloaded from the website
================
