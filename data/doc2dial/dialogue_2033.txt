If I don't respond, there will be issues?
Yes, there will be many issues. Your license will not be valid. You could be charged with a violation of the Vehicle and Traffic Law VTL if you operate a commercial motor vehicle.
What if I am charged and convicted?
Your commercial drivers license may be suspended.
Why would I be charged?
You could be charged if you operate a motor vehicle and are not medically certified or in the possession of the proper license to operate the vehicle.
What are sections 509 in reference to?
