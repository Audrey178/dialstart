How much does one have to pay for this REAL ID I've been hearing about?
================
There is no additional cost to get a REAL ID compliant driver license, permit or ID card. All normal transaction fees still apply.

Is there really a need to get a REAL ID if I already have a passport?
In terms of flying a U.S. Passport can still be used for identification, boarding a plane for domestic and international flights, and entry into a federal building or military base.

Can I apply for a REAL ID online?
No, you can not get a REAL ID online. You will need to go to a DMV Office.

If I'm under 18 years old do I need a REAL ID?
If you are traveling with a companion within the United States, the TSA does not currently require children under 18 years old to provide identification.
