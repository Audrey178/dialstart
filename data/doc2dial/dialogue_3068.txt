Do non-US citizens ever qualify for federal student aid?
Yes, many non-citizens get financial aid.
================
Will I need to complete a FAFSA form if I have been granted DACA?
Always check with your high school counselor or financial aid office to see whether completing a FAFSA form is the way to apply for state and college student aid.

What happens if I were a citizen of the Federated States of Micronesia, the Republic of the Marshall Islands, or the Republic of Palau?
If this is the case then you may be eligible for only certain types of federal student aid.
What if I am a parolee?
If a parolee then you have to be paroled for no less than a year and must provide evidence from the USCIS that you are in the US for other than a temporary purpose with the intention of becoming a US citizen or permanent resident.
================
If I am not an eligible noncitizen can I still get some type of aid to study in the US?
You can ask the college you plan to attend if they could offer you some type of aid.
