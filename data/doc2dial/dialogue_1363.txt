How will I know if I do not qualify for burial benefits?
================
We will send you a denial decision letter telling why you were denied burial benefits and we will also send you a form called Your Rights to seek Further Review of Our Decision VA Form 20-0998 which explains your rights to appeal.
================
Do you know how much time you have to appeal?
Yes, I know how much time I have to appeal.
It just depends on which option you use to request a review or appeal.
================
What will happen if I change my mind and really don't want to be buried in a VA national cemetery?
Your family will be able to make arrangements for your burial in a private cemetery even if we have all ready sent you a pre-need decision letter.
================
What happens when my family member requests a burial for me?
We will need to confirm your pre-need eligibility determination based on the laws at the time.
What will they need at the time of need burial?
We will keep a copy of your pre-need application, decision letter, and supporting documents in our system.
================
Can my family member still apply for my burial in a VA national cemetery at the time of need?
Yes, laws and personal circumstances may change earlier eligibility decisions and we will make a final decision.
