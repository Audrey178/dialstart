What information should i see on my lifetime driving record?
You should see all your license information that currently stored by the DMV on you.
What about my driving license abstract?
A standard ones should only contains information that is still required to keep by the DMV. Meanwhile, only 4 years records of most recent suspensions and revocations are shown on a standard driving record abstract.
================
What should i do if i suspect that my driving recod abstract contains wrong information?
You need to contact the DMV Call Center.
