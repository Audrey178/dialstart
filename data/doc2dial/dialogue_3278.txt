I think I'm ready to do it now, how can I access the form?
Just go to the website and click the Fill Out the FAFSA button on the home page.
================
I have a brother that also needs to fill his FAFSA form, is there a way to reuse some of our common info from my form to his?
Yes. At the end of the submitting process, you'll get a confirmation page and there you'll see an option for the parent information in your FAFSA form that you can use to transfer this information automatically into another student's application.
================
So we can also use an app?
Yes, you can find the 2020-21 and 2019-20 FAFSA forms on the myStudentAid app, available for android and IOS on their respective app stores.
