Could you please give me information as to why I would need to contact a national cemetery directly?
================
You would have to contact a national cemetery directly to schedule a burial for an active-duty service member or to cancel or reschedule a burial.
Are there any other reasons why I would have to contact them?
Over and above the reasons already mentioned you will also have to contact a national cemetery directly to change any information you already provided to the scheduling office.
