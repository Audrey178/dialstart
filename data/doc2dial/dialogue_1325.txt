what is a PACT?
A Patient Aligned Care Team or PACT is made up of you, those who support you like your family members and caregivers, and your health care providers.
================
can I use other medical care while enrolled in VA health care?
If you’re enrolled in VA health care, we can find out if you may be able to receive care from a provider in your local community through community care
what if the place I am getting looked at is no longer working with the VA?
There is a plan in place if your primary care provider leaves VA. We will make sure you always have access to a qualified primary care provider so that you keep getting the ongoing care you need.
what are some of the things the team will do for me?
build a partnership, provide care and coordinate care
