I'd like information on how to apply for a Veterans Housing Assistance Grant please.
================
Yes we have information applying for a housing grant as a Veteran or Servicemember with a service - connected disability.
================
Would you also like information about our other services?
Do you have anything about Vocational Rehabilitation and Employment VR&E?
Yes we will show you how to file a claim for Disability Compensation Pay for conditions related to your military service.
================
How does it impact my home loan with VA?
If you have a VA direct or VA-backed home loan or Specially Adapted Housing grant, we can show you how to get help.
Are there any additional charges implied?
You will be able to find out if you need to pay a VA Loan Funding Fee fee when using your home loan benefits.
================
I'm in rehab and will need to look for a job soon, can you help me?
Some of the VR&E benefits and services we offer include exploring employment options and getting more training if required.
================
Where do I call to get one on one help?
You can all the National Call Center for Homeless Veterans to talk in private with a trained VA counsellor for free. The number is 877-424-3838 for help 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
