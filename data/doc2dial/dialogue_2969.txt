Can I get a discharge of my student loan?
You could if you are eligible due to an unpaid refund by your school after you withdraw from school.
 Did you withdraw from school after receiving a direct federal loan or an FFEL Program loan?
I did, yes.
Then your school may have been required under federal regulations to return some or all of the loan money to your servicer, if they didn't then you may be eligible for a discharge of your loan.
How much of mu loan could be discharged?
Just the portion that your school failed to return.
================
Just that?
Yes, as I said, only the portion of the loan that the school failed to return is eligible for a discharge. You can contact your loan servicer for more information.
================
So, how can I apply for an unpaid refund discharge? My school it' still operating.
First, you should contact your school to try and resolve the issue with them before applying for an unpaid refund discharge.
And what could I do in case the school is suddenly closed down?
Then you should determine if you are eligible for a closed school discharge instead.
