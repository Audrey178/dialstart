Once I complete the course and receive my certificate, will I see a reduction in insurance costs if I present my certificate to the insurance company within 90 days after getting it?
Yes. You will see an immediate reduction in your liability and collision premium.
================
How can I verify that the course completion has been put on my driving record?
You can request a copy of your driving record from the DMV.
Does completing the Point and Insurance Reduction Program have any affect on my Driver Responsibility Assessment?
Completing the Point and Insurance Reduction Program does not prevent or reduce the calculation of points affecting your Driver Responsibility Assessment.
