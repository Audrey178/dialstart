Yes I would like some information about getting a Social Security card for my daughter who is not a citizen.
As a rule, only noncitizens who have permission to work from the DHS (Department of Homeland Security) can apply for a Social Security number.
Does she need a card for another purpose?
She does not have permission to work from DHS but needs a card anyway, is it possible?
We have a section called " If your child does not have permission to work " where you will find more information on the subject.
================
What if she needs a number in order to qualify for benefits?
In that case, you must bring us a letter from the government agency in question. The letter must be on letterhead stationery and it must specifically identify your child as the applicant.
Can it be a photocopy?
No form letters or photocopies will be accepted.
What other documents do I need to bring?
You must show us your child 's current U.S. immigration document, such as a Lawful Permanent Resident Card (I-551).
Anything else?
Social Security will need to see her current DHS document.
Is her passport from Jordan acceptable?
It can be shown along with an I-94 Arrival / Departure Record.
