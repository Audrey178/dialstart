Hi, i'm about to start the process to replace my license. I was wondering if there is a fee involved?
================
Sure, there is one. For replacing, the fee is $17.50.
Ok, i understand. Which payment options do i have when i apply both mail and going to DMV's office?
Well, DMV puts a wide array of payment options when you apply via mail or in person. For those, you can use credit / debit card, check or money order made out to Commissioner of Motor Vehicles. On the contrary, only debit / credit card will be available for payment if you apply online.
================
Wow, is good to know that. Oh, what about if  i get robbed my license, can i get a replacement?
Well, in fact you can get a free replacement. You must go to a police agency, and ask for the form MV-78B. I must remark that you will only get that form from police, not from DMV.
================
In that case, could i just waive the fee, being my license lost due to a crime?
If that what you want to do, then you must apply for that in aDMV office, showing a MV-78B form along with your application.
================
Ok, i'm taking notes about it. Now. a last question, It is possible to get my license delivered to another address not being listed?
It is indeed possible. Just add a note and print the alternate address in a clear way. Don't use the form you are using for application to write the address. That must remain as is listed on your DMV record.
