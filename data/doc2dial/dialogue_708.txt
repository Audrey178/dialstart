Will do. And what can you tell me about this direct express card I've read about?
The Direct Express card is a debit card you can use to access your benefits. And you don't need a bank account. With the Direct Express card program, we deposit your federal benefit payment directly into your card account.
Sounds great. How can I sign up for such a card?
It's quick and easy to sign up for the card. Call the toll-free Direct Express hotline at 1-800-333-1795. Social Security can help you sign up.
