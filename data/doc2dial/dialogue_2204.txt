Will you be registering your vehicle in another state?

================
My vehicle is registered in New York and I would like to know if out-of-state insurance is acceptable for this vehicle?
Unfortunately out-of-state insurance is not acceptable for a vehicle registered in NY.
So what you are telling me is that only New York insurance coverage is allowed?
Yes you must have New York insurance coverage for any type of vehicle registered in New York.
================
If I want to register my vehicle in another state what must I do with my NY plates?
You are required to surrender your vehicle registration and plates to the New York DMV.
================
I no longer life in NY but received a letter from the DMV about a lapse of insurance on my vehicle, what should I do to resolve this problem?
To solve this matter please send a copy of your out of state registration in your name along with a copy of your out of state insurance to NYS Department of Motor Vehicles Financial Security Bureau PO BOX 2725 Albany NY 12220 - 0725
================
I have out of state insurance on my vehicle, do I still have to obtain NY coverage as well?
Yes NYS does not accept out of state insurance coverage, you must have NY insurance coverage.
