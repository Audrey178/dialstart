Does your Mom have any driving restrictions?
No, she has a clean record.

What are the global restrictions I face?
I suggest you check with national and interstate legislation for this.

You license can be revoked if you violate any terms of the prohibition.

You may be able to have restrictions removed early if  you contact your State licensing Office with your circumstances.
