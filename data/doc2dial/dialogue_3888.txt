Give me some info about how to replace the inspection sticker
================
Sure. You need to order a replacement sticker using the form Request for Replacement Inspection Sticker PDF VS-118. Fill out the form and mail it to the address on the form. Inspection stations electronically send a record of all emissions inspections to the DMV

What if I don't have the inspection sticker?
The fine would be between $50 and $100. A mandatory state surcharge of $88 - $ 93 in town and village courts is added to the fine. You can receive a parking ticket if your vehicle is parked without an inspection sticker or with an expired inspection sticker.
