I need to know if I have to get insurance to register my vehicle.
================
Yes, this is a requirement to register a vehicle in New York, you have to have auto liability insurance.
================
Will the DMV accept a fax of my Insurance identification card?
Yes, but make sure it's clearly legible. If the DMV barcode reader can't scan the barcode your card will be rejected.
================
How much last the registration for a motorcycle?
It lasts for 1 year.
================
Do the names on my vehicle registration application and insurance identification card have to match?
Yes, the names must be the same in both documents.
================
will the insurance company give insurance proof after getting the insurance?
yes, the insurance company will issue proof of insurance in two ways.
