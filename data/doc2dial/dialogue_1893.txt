How to appeal it by mail?
To appeal, you can use Appeal Form AA-33A to appeal a DMV determination other than a TVB conviction or penalty. You can mail your appeal and fee to DMV Appeals Board, P.O. Box 2935 Albany, NY 12220-0935.
