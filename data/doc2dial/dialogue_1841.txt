I need to know how much is the fee for my vehicle.
You must check your county use tax charts to determine your registration fee and county use tax.
================
What kind of vehicles can I see in the registration fee chart?
Commercial vehicles that weigh less than 18,000 lbs, Diesel-powered commercial vehicles that weigh 8,501 pounds or more require an additional fee.
================
I live in New York and I am exempt from the NYC vehicle use tax, what should I do?
You need to fill the form UT-11 at DMV office or a DMV Call Center.
And what should I do if I live outside of New York and I am also exempt from the vehicle use tax in my county?
In that case, the form you must fill out is the UT-11C.
I am going to register my vehicle and I don't know if I have to pay a vehicle use tax or where to pay. Can you help me?
You must pay a vehicle use tax when you register a commercial vehicle or renew a commercial vehicle registration if you are residents of New York City. The DMV is responsible for collecting all taxes in the city and its counties.
================
How can I know the fee I must pay?
Add 3.25% to the fee listed in the chart and change the result to the nearest $0.25.
