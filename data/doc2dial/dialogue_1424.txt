You can find out if you can get more money to help pay for school through the Tuition Assistance Top - Up program .
================
what do I need to qualify for tuition assistance?
You have to be approved for federal tuition assistance and you must qualify for Montgomery GI Bill Active Duty MGIB - AD or Post-9/11 GI Bill benefits, and the cost of the course and fees must be more than tuition assistance will cover .
================
and if all of that fits my situation then what?
Then you can find out if you can get more money to help pay for school through the Tuition Assistance Top - Up program .
================
who is covered by this financial assistance?
Veterans who meet the requirements
================
