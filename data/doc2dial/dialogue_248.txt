================
Are you divorced or remarried?
Yeah, divorced.
Right. Then your spouse can qualify for benefits on your record.
================
What about benefits for children over 18?
Normally, those benefits stops when child reach 18, but if the child is a full time student, benefits can continue until he graduates or two months after reaching 19.
================
What can do my ex-spouse to qualify?
Well, need to comply with some requirements, such as been married with you for at least ten years and to be at least 62 years old.
================
And what about the children?
In the case of children, the child must be unmarried, under 18 o be 18-19 but full time student or to have a disability.
And if is me who have the disability, my child can receive benefits too?
Sure. The child can receive benefits in that scenario. The child doesn't need to be biologically yours, it can be adopted or a stepchild. A dependent grandchild may also qualify.
