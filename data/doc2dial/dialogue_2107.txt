I need to reinstate my surrendered plates.
================
You should go to a DMV office and ask for the reinstatement of your registration and receive your vehicle's plates. The first document you'll need is the completed Application for Registration or Title form (PDF MV-82)
Are you looking to reinstate your registration within 60 days before the expiration of your registration?
Yes, that's correct.
Then you can renew your registration and pay the renewal fees.
================
In what office should I apply for the reinstatement of the registration?
It should be done at the same DMV office you surrendered your personalized plates.
what is the fee for getting a new set of vehicle plates?
you can get charged up to 25$ by the DMV office for issuing a new set of vehicle plates.
================
when does the new registration expires?
It expires the same date as the registration you surrendered.
================
Can I surrender my plates temporarily?
Yes, you can do it at a local DMV office and reinstate the registration later.
