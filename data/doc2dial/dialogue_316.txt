I was curious if I could mail my documents in for disability benefits?
================
Yes you can, is that what you want to do?
Yes
Ok, you have to be sure to include your social security number, so that we can match it to the correct application.  Also, please make sure you do not write anything on the original documents-use a separate piece of paper to write your SSN.
================
If I don't want to do that, what are other ways to apply?
You can also apply by phone at 1-800-772-1213 from 7 am to 7 om Monday-Friday.
What if I an hard of hearing?
You can then call us at TTY 1-800-325-0778 or make an appointment and visit your Social Security Office.
================
What work information is needed?
You need Worker' Comp, Black Lung Benefits, or Longshore and Harbor Worker's Comp.
================
What are the advantages of applying online?
Doing it online offers several advantages bu starting your claim immediately.
