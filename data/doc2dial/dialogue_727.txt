What if my income has gone down?
Just contact us to explain that you have new information and may need a new decision about your income-related monthly adjustment amount.
================
What if I disagree with the decision you made about my adjustments?
You always have the right to appeal and the quickest and easiest way to file an appeal of your decision online.
Do I need to file an appeal if I am requesting a new decision?
Actually you will not need to file an appeal if you are requesting an new decision because you experienced your income going down or if you have shown us the info we used was wrong.
