What is involved in the hearing process surrounding complaints and charges regarding repair and body shops?
================
You are entitled to bring an attorney of your choice to the hearing. In cases like this, the DMV is the petitioner and the complainant may be called as a witness
What happens as a result of one of these hearings?
The results vary, in some instances, the ALJ are ordered to pay all, some or none of the charges. There may also be no action, or a warning, or a suspension handed out, or a revocation applied and/or civil penalties issued
What is I want to appeal a decision made at a hearing?
In order to appeal the decision made at a hearing, you have 60 days from the date of decision to file an Appeal From DMV Hearing Determination, and this must be filed along with the appropriate fee
What if the ALJ at the hearing imposes civil penalties?
If this is the case, you will receive a letter shortly after the hearing. To prevent the loss of your operating privileges, you will be required to pay all fines and penalties imposed
What happens if I decide to pay my fine and any penalties imposed or serve out any period of suspension?
After complete compliance with the findings of the hearing, you will be entitled to resume your business
