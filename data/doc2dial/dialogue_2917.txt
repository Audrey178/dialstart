Can you explain to me what the Student Aid Report is?
It is a review of your FAFSA information to make sure it is correct and complete.
================
Can I use my email to get a SAR from FAFSA?
Yes, you will receive an email if you provide a valid email address with instructions on how to access an online copy of your SAR.
================
What information does my SAR contain if my application is complete?
If you application is complete, your SAR will have an Expected family Contribution will display in the upper-right hand corner.
If my application is incomplete what will my SAR report show?
If incomplete your SAR report will not show the EFC but will tell you what you need to do to resolve issues.
================
