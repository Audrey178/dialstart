Can I be eligible for Medicare at age 65?
You can, and if you are, your initial enrollment period begins three months before you turn 65, including the month you turn 65 and ends three months after that birthday.
What can I do after applying for Medicare?
You can sign up for A Hospital Insurance and Part B Medical Insurance. You can turn down Part B coverage if you don't want to pay a premium.

How can I be sure that my identity is safe?
Medicare will send you a Medicare card to help protect your identity. This card will have a unique Medicare number instead of your Social Security number.
