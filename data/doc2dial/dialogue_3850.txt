What do I need to know about renewing my ID card?
================
Are you over the age of 21 or will you be 21 soon?
Well I am 20 but I turn 21 next month. Does that make a difference?
Since you are within 40 days of your 21st birthday, you won't have to do anything different. Your new ID won't have "Under 21" printed on it.
OK so my friend is 2 months away from turning 21. Will she have to do anything different?
If they renew it more than 41 days before their 21st birthday, it will still have "Under 21" on that so if they can wait, they should wait until less than 40 days until their birthday.
================
Thanks. Can I go online to renew my ID or do I have to go to the DMV to get it?
Either one is fine. You can renew online and your ID will be just as the same as if you went to the DMV.
================
I've been hearing about enhanced IDs but I don't know if mine is one of those. How do I know?
If yours was issued before October 30, 2017, then yours is not enhanced. It is known as a Standard ID.
