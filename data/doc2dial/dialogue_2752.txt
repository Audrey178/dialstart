What do I need to do if I am a buyer?
================
You need to carefully examine the current owner's Certificate of Title and be sure the info is correct and that the title certificate identifies the motor vehicle being sold.
Do you have a motor vehicle that is 10 years or older?
Yes, I have a vehicle that is 10 years or older.
Make sure your odometer reading, your name and address as a buyer, and the date are printed clearly in the spaces for TRANSFER BY OWNER on the reverse side.
================
What is proof of ownership?
Proof of ownership is a Manufacturers Certificate of Origin or the vehicles Certificate of Title, included with the dealer's Certificate of Sale.
================
What about the Garagemen Lien procedure?
An applicant that is using the Garageman Lien procedure must present ownership documetation that show a Notice of Lien and Sale and a Garageman Affirmation and Bill of Sale.
================
What must I mail to the DMV Title Bureau to apply for original NYS certificate of title?
You need to mail to the DMV Title Bureau the notice of lien satisfaction, the original out of state issued title certificate, and a letter explaining your circumstances.
================
What about a vehicle 8 years old or newer?
Always make sure the seller has completed the right damage disclosure statement because it indicates whether or not the vehicle's new title would be branded as a Rebuilt Salvage.
