What would happen if my TPD discharge request would be denied?
================
Has your request been denied?
It has.
Then you already know that Nelnet has notified you of the reason for the denial of your discharge application and has explained that you may ask for reevaluation of your discharge application if you provide new information that supports your eligibility for discharge within 12 months of the date you were notified that your discharge application had been denied.
================
Would approval of my discharge have done anything about my TEACH Grant service obligation?
A TPD discharge relieves you from having to complete a TEACH Grant service obligation.
================
If I want to reapply, where can I find more information about TPD discharge?
Do you have internet access?
I do.
You can visit the website at disabilitydischarge.com.
================
If I reapply with better documentation and can get my loan discharged due to TPD, would I have to pay taxes on the discharged loan amount?
That information can be found by visiting irs.gov.
