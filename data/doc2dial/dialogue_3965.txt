Look, If somebody anyone wants to get a driving permit there, what should be done?
Well, you should apply for a conditional or restricted use license in person at a DMV Office. You can do this at almost any DMV Office.
================
This restricted license can be issued to those whose license has been suspended or revoked because alcohol or drug?
In fact, yes, they can get a restricted license. In any case, the driver must attend a Impaired Driver Program approved by the DMV.
================
You said that this license can be requested in any DMV office, right?
Not all of the DMV offices. You should contact your nearest DMV office to know if you can apply there.
================
And that license is only conditional, isn't?
Correct. As said before, is a special kind of license, which can be issued to those driver whose license has been suspended or revoked by alcohol or drug related situations.
