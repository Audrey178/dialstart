For any document you don't have or can't get within 10 days, we'll ask you for some other document. Any document submitted must have your name, your birth date, identifying information and a recent photograph.

You can show any document that proves you lived abroad for a long time and you don't have a social security number. You could use your previous or current passport, school or employment records, etc.
