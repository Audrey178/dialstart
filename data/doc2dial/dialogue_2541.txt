I've been wondering about something. Should I choose to remove my vehicle from the road, am I allowed to keep my professional plates?
================
Yes, you are. You can store your picture or professional plates at most DMV offices.
Do you want to register a vehicle again, or to use your stored picture or professional plates to register another vehicle?
Yes. At a later point of time.
In that case, you must visit the office that has your vehicle plates. Make sure that you bring the receipt form FS-6 T and the other documents required to register your vehicle.
Let's say I want to surrender my plates. Do I get something in exchange to proof that I did?
Yes, you will receive a receipt form FS-6 T when you surrender your plates. Verify the information listed on the receipt, make sure that the receipt displays the words "Stored at Local Office", and keep this receipt. We will indicate on the registration record that the vehicle plates have been stored.
================
Do you have any information about my new picture plate available?
Of course. A detailed information packet about the custom plate picture plate development program, including the Request for the Development of Custom Plates (MV-415) is available here. Professional plates do not entitle you to any special parking privileges unless the city, town or village where you park your vehicle has granted such privileges. For more information, contact your local police or parking enforcement agency, or the agency that is responsible for parking regulations.
What if my registration is in the first year of a two-year registration?
In that case you must submit a Request for refund or fee paid for motor vehicle registrations and driver licenses (MV-215) to apply for a refund of the balance of the fee. A processing fee of $ 1.00 is subtracted from your refund. You will not receive a refund if the registration expires in less than one year.
================
