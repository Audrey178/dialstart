Does my record show on my PRIP?
================
If it needs to, it will. You can request confirmation at a local branch.
I think it does; will it be there?
Yes, if needed, it will be.
Can I also get a copy of my record sent to me?
Of course you can, you can do it at a local branch.
================
What effects do points have on my driving record?
You can see that using a referenced document or at a local branch.
But what if I got a revoked status?
You can check to see if point reduction will remove said revoked status, or you can go to a local branch.
================
Can I get them reduced?
Yes, via a point reduction class or taking the road test over again.
================
Will it hit my record before the 10 weeks it says it will?
Perhaps. It all depends on the reporting agency.
