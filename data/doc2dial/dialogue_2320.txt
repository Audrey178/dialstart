I need to replace my vehicle registration, but I recently changed my last name.
================
When you apply for a vehicle registration replacement, you will not be able to change your last name during the transaction. Therefore, your new registration document will have your previous name on it.

In addition to the name change, I've also recently moved to a new home. Will this be a problem when I try to replace my registration?
Yes. Your mailing address must be current on your DMV files. I recommend that you first change your address online, then apply for a replacement registration.
