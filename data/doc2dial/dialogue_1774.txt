What if I qualify for the GI Bill after 9/11? Can I get education benefits?
Yes, you may be able to get benefits through the Post-9/11 GI Bill if you've served on active duty for at least 90 days, whether continuous all at once or interrupted for shorter periods over time, after September 10, 2001.
