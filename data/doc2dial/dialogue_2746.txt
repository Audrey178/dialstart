I just bought a new truck and need help with getting the title.
================
If you didn't automatically apply when you first registered your truck, you can use the form Vehicle Registration/Title Application to get a title.

Due to NY State laws, it could take several weeks for the title application process to complete.
================
That's not good. What is the importance of a title?
If you decided to sell your truck down the road, having a title allows you to transfer ownership of that vehicle to another person.
================
How would I get a duplicate title, in case, I lost the original?
You can apply for a duplicate title online or at any DMV office.
What do I do if I go to DMV to get one?
You'll need to complete and submit an Application for Duplicate Certificate Of Title, provide proof of identity, and proof of address. The duplicate title is normally processed in 72 hours and will be mailed to you for a $20 fee.
================
Can I apply for a title at DMV?
Absolutely. You can submit a Vehicle Registration/Title Application at any DMV office.
