Can my relative or authorized representative still request my burial in a VA national cemetery at the time of need?
Yes Laws and personal circumstances may change previous eligibility decisions. We will make the final decision as to whether you qualify for burial at the time of need.
================
What if I change my mind and don't want to be buried in a VA National Cemetery?
Your family members can make arrangements for your burial in a private cemetery, even if we have already sent you a decision letter prior to need. There is no need to contact VA about this change of plans.
