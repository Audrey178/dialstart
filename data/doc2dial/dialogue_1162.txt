We can help you find out which VA insurance programs may be right for you as a Veteran , service member , spouse , or dependent child
================
can I find out which options are suited for me so I don't waste my time on things I'm not qualified for?
We can help narrow it down for you, different programs cover Veterans, service members, and family members. There is also short-term financial coverage through TSGLI to help you recover from a traumatic  injury. And if you are disabled because of an injury or illness caused or made worse by your active service you may be able to get coverage through S-DVI to continue your life insurance beyond 2 years after you leave the military.
are you ending your military tour of duty soon?
yes
You should act quick to make sure there is no break in your coverage. In some cases you must find insurance within 120 days of leaving the military so you are not left uninsured

which one is the insurance that we get when serving?
SGLI, Servicemembers Group Life Insurance offers insurance while you are serving

S-DVI, Service-Disabled Veterans Insurance and it continues your life insurance beyond 2 years after you leave the military
