How does work study work for me as a Veteran?
Work study programs as a Veteran can help you earn money while being enrolled in college, a vocational school, or professional training program.
Is there any special place I need to be enrolled?
You would have to be enrolled college courses, a vocational school, or professional training program.
================
What about other financial assistance?
All you need to do is contact your school's financial office to find that out.
================
Am I able to take part in the Veterans work-study program?
You will have to meet certain requirements to take part in the Veterans work-study program.
What must be true for me to get Veteran's work study?
Are you enrolled at least three quarters time in a college degree, vocational, or professional program?
No I am not enrolled for at least three quarters time in a college degree, vocational or professional program.
Then you are not eligible for the Veteran's work-study program.
