Hi, I got the news that the New York State DMV started issuing REAL ID licenses, permits, and ID cards on October 30, 2017. How do I get mine?
================
You can get a REAL ID at any time, but you must go to a DMV office. You cannot obtain a REAL ID online, by phone, or by mail.
