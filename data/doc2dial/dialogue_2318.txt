For what kind of vehicles can't I replace registration documents online?
You can't replace registration documents online for vehicles older than model year 1973, boats, snowmobiles or all-terrain vehicles (ATVs).

That's what I thought. But what about a temporary registration?
You can download and print a temporary registration in PDF format that is valid for 10 days. We also attach a copy to your confirmation email. You will get your new registration document by mail within 10 days.
