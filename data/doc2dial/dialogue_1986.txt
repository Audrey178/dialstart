I just passed my test and I want to know what is next.
================
You will receive an interim license that will allow you to drive and that you must to keep it with your photo learner permit. You will receive your photo driver license in the mail  in about 2 weeks.
What should I do after passing my road test if I am 16 years old and I have a Class DJ driver license?
You must obey the restrictions for junior drivers. For more information, I invite you to see the Graduated Driver License GDL Law and restrictions on drivers under age 18.
================
What happens if I fail my test?
No problem if you fail your test.
What happens if I fail my non-commercial license road test?
Your learner permit remains valid until the expiration date.
What if my learner permit expires before my next road test?
In that cse, you must get a new learner permit. For more information, see "Get a learner permit".
