I am looking to make a Student Loan Payment, is this an easy process and what is involved?
This is indeed an easy process, you make a payment to your Student Loan at any time, you need to contact your federal loan servicer to get this process started
================
Do you know where to send payments to regarding your student loan?
I think I do, but is there anything else I should know?
Your Student Loan is handled by your student loan servicer. They will help you if you wish to make a payment to the loan
================
Is there any advantage to having a Direct Loan in relation to making payments?
If you have a Direct Loan, you can avail of a 0.25% interest rate deduction if you make payments by way of an automated direct debit
Is that a bonus that is unique to Direct Loans?
Yes, as mentioned, this bonus is there for those who make payments to their Direct Loan by way of automated direct debits
================
Is the process for making payments different if I have a Great Lakes or Nelnet loan?
The process is basically the same as any loan, payments are now being accepted for Great Lakes and Nelnet loans. You can find more information on this on your account dashboard
Is there any other way for me to make a payment to my Great Lakes or Nelnet loan?
It could happen that we are unable to accept a payment from you, due to your account status. You can make payments on your servicer's website, to do this you will need to log in to your Great Lakes or Nelnet account
