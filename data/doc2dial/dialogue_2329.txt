Hello I need information about the Sales Tax Payment Proof
================
did you pay new york state sales tax to the leasing company?
Yes
in that case please provide your lease stating that you paid New York State sales tax and complete the Sales Tax Exemption Claim {Sales Tax Form} PDF [5] at the Department of New York State Tax and Finance DTF-803.
================
and where do I have to make that request?
Take your completed registration application and additional documents to a local DMV office
================
and what things do i need?
New York State Driver's License and Permit
================
and How do I register a leased vehicle?
The dealership normally registers the vehicle for you by providing all the evidence and your registration request to the DMV. In this case, you don't need to do anything else
================
What happens if I don't pay New York State sales tax to the leasing company?
in that case please provide your lease, and the complete Statement of Transaction {Sales Tax Form} PDF in the New York State Department of Taxation and Finance DTF-802. You must pay New York State sales tax at the DMV office on any lease payments if the leasing company or dealer did not collect the sales tax.
What happens if I don't pay New York State sales tax to the leasing company?
In that case, please provide your lease and the full PDF of the Transaction Statement {Sales Tax Form} at the New York State Department of Taxation and Finance DTF-802. You must pay New York State sales tax at the DMV office on any lease payments if the company or the leasing dealer did not collect the sales tax.
