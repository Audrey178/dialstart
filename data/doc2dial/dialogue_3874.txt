I need refund information about cancelling an I-PIRP course.
================
Was the cancellation due to the sponsor being unable to conduct the course?
Yes, it was cancelled.
In that case, they must give you the option of a full refund of all course fees. If the course is available later on, the sponsor can also give you the option of rescheduling.
================
What happens if I don't have a NY state DMV ID NUmber Client ID Number?
That would mean that the ADM course completion could not be applied to your license record. You may also be required to file a Request for Driver and/or Vehicle Record Information form, before you are allowed to take another ADM course.
================
Do I get the same benefits completing an I-PIRP course as I would a classroom PIRP course?
Yes, you would receive the same benefits. This includes being able to reduce your driver violation point total by a maximum for 4 points and entitles you to save 10% on your auto liability  and collision insurance premiums.
================
When do I get my course completion certificate?
The sponsor will mail your certificate after completion.
================
How secure are the I-PIRP websites?
Very secure. DMV requires sponsors and their delivery agents to provide secure websites that protect your personal information.
