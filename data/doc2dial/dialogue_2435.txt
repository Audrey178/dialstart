I'm a veteran and I need information about obtaining a REAL ID license.
================
Have you yet applied for a REAL ID?
No, I haven't yet.
================
Will I have to pay to have the Veteran indicator added to my license?
No, you are not required to pay a fee. If, by chance, you are charged a document fee, you are entitled to a refund.
================
Can I change my license to a REAL ID and add my veteran status online?
No, this has to be done at a DMV office.
================
If I'm charged the fee for putting my veteran status on my license, how do I get the refund?
If you are charged a fee, you will receive a letter and a refund check from NY state.
================
Where will the veteran indicator be placed on my license?
The Veteran indicator will be placed on the upper left hand corner of your license.
