How do I register as a licensed retail distributor?
To Register as a New York State Department of Motor Vehicle Licensed Retail Dealer   consult the Regulations of the DMV Commissioner for dealers and transporters of motor vehicles pdf CR-78   download and read Installation Requirements - Distributors / Carriers pdf

What if I don't have my Certificate of Authority?
Don't submit an application with the DMV yet!    The authority certificate and sales tax identification number are issued by the New York State Department of Taxation and Finance DTF. You can apply by visiting the NY State DTF website. Be sure to record your sales tax ID on the original Installation Request.
