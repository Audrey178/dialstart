Are loan simulators accurate?
================
Do you want the most realistic results?
Yes, I want the most realistic results.

What is the loan simulator?
The loan simulator is a new tool to help you make decisions about your student loans and whether or not you should decide to consolidate.
================

Are the results of the loan simulator just used for informational purposes?
Yes, results of the loan simulator are just for informational purposes and is not real financial advice.  As always, you should review all available options and make decisions that are right for you.
