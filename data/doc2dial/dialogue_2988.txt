Can I get a teacher loan forgiveness?
================
Are you currently in default on a loan?
No, I'm not.
================
But in case I apply for a teacher loan forgiveness, what should I do if my loan is with a different loan servicers?
If that's the case, you should submit a separate form to each servicer.
================
Is the PLUS loan for parents eligible for teacher loan forgiveness?
No, PLUS loans for parents aren't eligible for this type of forgiveness.
================
What's the maximum amount I can get in loan forgiveness as a full-time secondary education teacher?
If you didn't teach mathematics, science, or special education, you may receive up to 5000$ in loan forgiveness.
================
And what if I'm an AmeriCorps Program volunteer, there's anything I should be aware of when applying for a teacher loan forgiveness?
Just that the period of teaching that qualifies you for a benefit through the AmeriCorps Program can't be counted toward the required period of teaching for the Teacher Loan Forgiveness Program.
