I need to know more about refunds
================
Do you want an example to show how the refund amount is determined?
Yes, please show me the example.
Let's say you paid 137.50$ to register your vehicle for a period of 2 years. In the amount paid, there is included a 62.50$ for the two years or the registration, 32.25$ per year, 50$ for the title certificate and 25$ for the vehicle's plates. In this case, the amount to refund would be of 30.25$
================
And if I changed my name, can I still get a refund?
yes, you can. First, you have to surrender the vehicle plates at a DMV office and then request a transfer receipt, not a refund receipt. Secondly, you have to send some items to the address on the Request for Refund MV-215 form.
================
I have my unused registration sticker and stop and I got a refund for the second year of registration, can I get a refund for the first year?
Yes, you may be eligible to apply for the first year of the registration fee refund. You have a maximum 60 days after the date you got issued the registration to apply for the first year of the registration fee refund.
================
If the registrant died is it possible to get a refund?
Yes, in that case, the DMV will issue the refund to the name of the state. You'd have to surrender the vehicle plates at a DMV office and ask for a transfer receipt.
