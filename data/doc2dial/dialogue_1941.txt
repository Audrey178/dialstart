I want to know how I can replace my title certificate.
================
What is the express mail?
It's about sending you a replacement by express mail. This will cost extra and you will need to sign for the delivery.
================
Where should I deliver the requirements?
You must bring to a DMV office a completed Application for Duplicate Title MV-902.
================
How do I request a title certificate?
Use the Application for Title Only MV-82TON and follow the instructions carefully.
What should I do if I am about to move?
You need to learn how to change your address.
================
What items should I send to the DMV office?
Send a completed form "Application for Duplicate Title MV-902".
