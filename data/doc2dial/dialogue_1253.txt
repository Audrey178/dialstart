Hi, I would like to know how to apply for Pre-Need Eligibility for Burial in a VA Cemetery?
================
You can apply right now online or you can also apply by filling out an application and mailing it.
================
I don't like applying online but am willing to apply by mail. What forms do I have to completed to apply by mail?
================
If you want to apply by mail you will have to download the necessary forms, are you able to download forms?
Yes I am able to download forms on my computer.
You will have to download VA Form 40-10007 in PDF format. Once you have downloaded the form you can complete it, print it and mail it.
Is the person you are applying for able to sign the necessary form?
Yes he is able to sign the form.
If he is unable to sign the form for if the person is under 18 years old, or mentally unable to make decisions for himself or physically unable to sign the form a spouse, parent, or court-appointed representative may sign the form.
================
What if I have a question or need help while filling out the form?
If you need help or have a question you can call our toll-free number at 877-222-8387, Monday through Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m ET.
================
What happens after I have applied?
After you have applied we will let you know that we have received your application and how long it will take to process it.
