I just became disabled and I need to know how long I have to wait to apply for benefits.
I can certainly assist you with any questions you have. You should apply for disability benefits as soon as possible after becoming disabled.
================
Oh, that is good to know! How do I apply?
You can either apply over the phone at 1800-772-1213, or through our website.
================
So since I have already become disabled, I should be able to apply right away?
Yes, since it takes time to get approved, the sooner you apply, the better.
How much time does it take?
Social Security benefits become available after the sixth full month of disability.
Do you have any more questions about the waiting period?
If I became disabled a year ago but apply this month, do I still have to wait six months?
================
Is there anything I can read on my own to help me with all of this?
The Disability Starter Kit can help you get ready for a disability interview or the online application process.
