Why do information rates not allow assumptions to be made?
The 2011 Disclosure Rates are not intended to form a basis for making general or specific assumptions or determinations about specific GE Programs, or for projections of future years' rates.
And what happens when the programs do not meet the debt?
These informative rates were prepared before the years that programs that fail to meet debt measures must provide debt warnings and may lose eligibility after failing three of four consecutive years.
Which loans were calculated for disclosure purposes?
The Medium Private Loans, Medium Institutional Loans, and Medium Title IV Loans were calculated for disclosure purposes and are NOT the median used in the Redemption Rate or Debt-to-Income Ratio calculations.
