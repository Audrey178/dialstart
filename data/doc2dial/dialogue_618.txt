What exactly is a Social Security benefit verification letter and why do I need one?
The verification letter serves as proof of  your retirement, disablity, Supplemental Security Income (SSI) or Medicare benefits. It also serves as proof that you have applied for benefits or that you have never received Social Security benefits or SSI.
