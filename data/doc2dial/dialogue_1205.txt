Can I qualify if my father died in 2005?
If your parents died in the line of duty before August 1, 2011, you may qualify for both the Fry Grant and the DEA Educational Assistance Program for Survivors and Dependents. But you can only use one program at a time. We limit the combined benefits to 81 months of full-time training.
