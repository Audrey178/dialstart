What if I need to take a larger amount in private loans than at another?
You should pay extra attention to the terms and conditions on private loans so you will understand what obligations you would have and also read about federal versus private loans.
What if the school does not list the cost of attendance on their aid offer?
Ask the financial aid office for the amount and make sure it includes amounts you will pay to the school directly such as tuition and fees.
Will any transportation fees be included?
You should also ask them about transportation fees because sometimes they are included.
