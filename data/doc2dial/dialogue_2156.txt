I need help with diesel opacity emmissions.
================
The Diesel Opacity Emissions inspection is not required in other areas of NY State or for vehicles registered out-of-state.
Have you failed a roadside inspection?
Yes and was issued a ticket.
Yes a traffic ticket can be issued if a vehicle fails the roadside inspection.
================
What do I do if I have questions?
You can call 1-518-474-0919.
================
What are the stipulation of diesel powered vehicles and inspections?
All model year 1996 and older diesel powered vehicles registered anywhere in New York State with a gross vehicle weight rating GVWR less than 8501 pounds are not subject to the OBDII emissions inspection, the low enhanced emissions inspection or the diesel emissions inspection.
================
What is that number to call for questions?
It is 1-518-474-0919
================
Where can I get my vehicle tested?
The NY State Dept of Environmental Conservation, the NY State Dept of Transportation or the NY State Police can test any diesel powered vehicle that has a MGW of more that 8500 lbs.
