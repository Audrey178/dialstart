I need help uploading evidence to support my disability claim.
Have you filed a claim for disability compensation?
Yes I have filed a claim.
You can upload evidence for up to one year on your claim such as medical test results, doctor reports and other records.
================
Is there a way to speed up the process?
Yes, by using Disability Benefits Questionnaires.
================
What kinds of documents should I upload to support my disability claim?
copies of your military medical and personnel records
================
Anything else to upload?
you'll also want to upload statements that support your claim.  These should be from people who know about or who you have talked to about your claimed medical condition and how and when it occurred.
================
What about the Disability Benefits Questionairre?
These forms ask for specific information we need to determine a disability rating for different medical conditions.  You can help make sure our rating specialist have exactly what they need to make a decision by having your VA or non-VA doctor fill out a DBQ
================
What if my claim isn't in my military record?
You'll want to upload statements that support your claim.  These should be from people who know about or who you've talked to about your claimed medical condition and how and when it occurred.
