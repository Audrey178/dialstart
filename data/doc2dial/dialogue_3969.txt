I lost my my original U.S. birth certificate, what can I do?
The Vital Records office of the New York State Department of Health can help you obtain vital records in the state. Additionally, you can request vital records online from New York and many other states through an independent partner, VitalChek Network Inc., at vitalchek.com

I lost my Union card, what can I do?
Don't worry, you can ask the union for help in obtaining a duplicate. Even if you no longer have that union job, you can be a member for life.
What should I do if I don't have a bill on paper?
You must print out a copy of a paperless bill, like a phone bill, electricity/gas bill, water bill or cable bill.
How do i get a certified copy of my diploma if i went to a community college?
In that case you may have received a General Educational Development GED certificate. You can contact the college for a certified copy for sure.
