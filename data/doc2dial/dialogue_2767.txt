You could tell me if I can access accident reports online. I've purchased some reports recently. So, can I?
================
Yes, you can.
Have you purchased accident reports online within the last 7 calendar days?
Not within the last 7 days, no.
================
Can you suggest a topic that might interest me?
DMV Records.
================
Why do you ask, though? What if had purchased the reports in the last 7 days?
In that case you could access the reports again.
And what would I do to access those reports?
You would enter the Report Confirmation Number displayed during the report purchase transaction and emailed to you in the transaction confirmation.
================
What conditions do I need to fulfill to order a copy of a accident report?
You must be named on the accident report or represent a person named on the report, or be a party to a civil action that arose from the accident.
