Can I apply for a personalized license plate?
Personalized plates up to six characters with the Universal Symbol of Access are available for an additional charge
================
Where can people with disabilities park in New York City?
There are no reserved spaces set aside for people with disabilities on the streets in New York City. Reserved spaces are available off-street, in parking lots. The vehicle plates for people with disabilities or a state permit for people with disabilities can be used to avail of these off-street reserved spaces
================
Can I apply for a temporary parking permit?
You may be able to avail of a temporary permit which is valid for six months
================
Where do I go if I need information regarding applying for a parking permit for people with disabilities?
Please refer to the Parking for People with Disabilities section of the website
================
Are there specific requirements needed to apply for a permanent parking permit?
You must live in New York State but do not need to be the licensed driver or own a vehicle, applications are made in the locality in which you live
Who issues parking permits?
Every city, town and village has an agent that issues these permits. Contact your local clerk to find out which local agency issues the permits. There is no fee applied for receiving a permit
