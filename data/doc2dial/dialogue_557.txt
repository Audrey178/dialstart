Can I eventually go back to work if I'm receiving disability benefits?
================
Most people do prefer to work instead of getting staying with a disability benefit. Is that your case?
Not really, but I'm just curious about it.
================
For how long can I continue to get the disability benefits?
In most cases, for as long as you are disabled.
Are there any exceptions?
You could stop getting the benefits under certain circumstances, like if your health improves to the point that you are no longer considered disabled and can go back to work.
================
Can you recommend me publications to read more about disability beneftis?
Yes sure. You can read the publications Disability Beneftis, What you Need to Know When you Get Social Security and How we Decide if you Still Have a Qualifying Disability.
================
And about Social Security work incentives?
You can read Working While Disabled and How We Can Help.
