What do I need to renew my Driving School license?
================
It needs to be done 30-60 days prior to expiration.
Do you have updated information about your school?
Yes, everything is updated.
Then you need your Driving School Renewal Application, Proof of Workers Compensation form, and Disability Benefits Coverage form.
What if we provide the Pre-licensing course?
Then you need to attach a Signature Authorization List- Pre-licensing Course.
What about if we use vehicles for instruction?
You need to attach a List of Driving School Vehicles.
Which form is that?
MV-278 6
================
What if my license expired?
You must stop operating.
================
Is there anything else?
Make sure you attach the required documents.
