Sure you can. In any case, you need to go and check under special procedures to surrender your plates temporarily for a season.

No. You need to remove some things from the plates first before surrender. You have to remove any plate frames and fasteners, as well as any sticker you may have attached on it. Besides, you need destroy the registration and inspection stickers you have attached into your windshield.
================

Yes. Along with that, you nee to complete the Plate Surrender Application form PD-7 for each set of plates you present to surrender. When you complete that, you can proceed to mail both the application and the plates.
