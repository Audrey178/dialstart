Can i get support for a small business as a veteran?
Yes, there is a Veteran-owned small business support and all you need to do is register.
================
What happens if my school closed or program ended because of a change in VA regulations?
You would need to apply to restore your GI Bill benefits.
So all I need to do is apply to get my GI Bill benefits restored?
Yes, just apply again to restore your GI Bill benefits if your school closed.
================
Are their any stories of how veterans and service members use the GI Bill that I can see?
You can see stories of how Veterans and service members use GI Bill and it will show you how they advanced in their career.
================
How do I know if I am enrolled in an approved school?
You can use our online verification system called "Wave" to make sure you are enrolled in an approved school and keep getting your benefits.
