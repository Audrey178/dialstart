Can you tell me about the phase 2 of the implementation of the CDL permit rule?
================
Dp ypou want to know about the medical certification changes?
Yes, please.
In this phase, the New York State DMV sended CLP holders a notification if their medical certification status appeared as "Not Certified", after which the CLP was canceled if the CLP holder didn't re-certify within 55 days.
================
Can the NY State reject my application if I have an open suspension in another state?
Yes. With the CDL Permit Rule the NY State can check for open suspensions and revocations in other states and reject any application if it finds open suspensions.
To what applications applies the CDL Permit Rule?
It applies to the CLP and CDL license applications.
================
What should I do to make sure to receive the notification for when the medical certification status becomes "Not Certified"?
We can send employers the notification of the changes if they subscribe to the NY State License Event Notification Service (LENS).
================
Can I get the L restriction on my CDL upgraded?
If you do the test in a vehicle with air over hydraulic brakes, your restriction will be upgraded from an L to a Z.
================
Now tell me about the sanctions for CLP holders.
It is now required that the NY State applies federally mandated commercial suspensions and revocations to CLP holders.This federally mandated commercial suspensions and revocations that affect a driver's commercial privilege will be applied consecutively one after the other with any other open commercial suspension or revocation on record.
