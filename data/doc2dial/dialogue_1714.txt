I'm looking for information regarding the Veterans Educational Assistance Program
Under VEAP, you may be able to use part of your military pay to help cover the cost of schooling and continue your education.
================
If I put money into a VEAP account, can I get that money refunded if needs be?
To get a refund of this money, you need to complete an Application for Refund of Educational Contributions VA Form 22-5281. This form can be downloaded from the website
================
Is there a long list of pre-requisites that are needed to be fulfilled before I qualify for VEAP benefits?
The first thing required is that you entered military service for the first time between January 1, 1977 and June 30, 1985

Is there any other information that I could use to apply for VEP benefits?
Further information is provided through the Veterans Educational Assistance Program VEAP on the website
================
Is there a minimum that I have to have in my VEAP fund?
You must have at least 3 months contributions in your VEAP account
