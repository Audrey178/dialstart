Why do I need Exit Counseling?
================
Do you know what Exit Counseling is for?
Yes, I know what it is for.
Then you are aware that it provides important information to get you ready to pay back your federal student loan.
I have a subsidized PLUS loan do I need Exit Counseling?
Yes, it is required for you to complete Exit Counseling because you have a subsidized PLUS loan.
What if I drop below half-time enrollment, do I have to complete the Exit Counseling?
Yes, you would have to complete the Exit Counseling.
What if I decide to leave school?
Then you are required to take Exit Counseling.
Do I have to take it once I graduate?
Yes, if you have the Direct PLUS loan or FFEL loan you would have to take Exit Counseling.
