What can I do if my parents will not supply their information on my FAFSA form?
I will give you the process for filling out the FAFSA form online or on myStudentAid app
================
What if I don't have parental information on my FAFSA form?
Your form will be submitted but will not be fully processed. You will not receive an EFC and you must contact the financial aid office immediately upon starting at the school or college that you plan to attend
================
Can I submit my FAFSA form without parental information if I agree to the conditions that are attached to non-disclosure of this information?
Yes, you can submit your FAFSA form
