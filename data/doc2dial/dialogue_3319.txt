Are statistics useful in comparing schools?
Yes. Statistics can help you evaluate schools.
================
What can a college fair offer me?
College fairs are a fantastic way to learn more about colleges. They allow you to interact with representatives from several colleges and career schools.
================
What if I receive federal student aid and end up having to withdrawal from college? How do refund policies work?
A college's return of aid policy differs from school to school, but it's possible that some of that money might have to be given back to the source by the school or yourself.
================
Why should I consider applying to multiple schools?
Financial aid packages will differ from school to school. Of course, some packages will be more attractive than others, so applying to multiple schools is the best way to go.
