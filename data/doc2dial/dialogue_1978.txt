Okey, thanks. Just to be sure I own a moped and not a normal motorcycle, what is exactly what DMV consider a moped?
New York law (not DMV specifically), defines a moped/motor scooter as "limited use vehicles with two or three wheels". But generally speaking a moped is a small motorcycle with bicycle pedals.
