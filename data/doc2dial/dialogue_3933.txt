I need help with exchanging my New York driver license for a non-driver ID card.
I'll be happy to assist you with that. You can exchange your NY driver license for a non-driver ID card if the driver license is current or has not been expired for more than 2 years.
================
Can I do this by mail?
Definitely. You will need to complete an Application for Permit, Driver License, or Non-Driver ID Card.
================
Can I upgrade to a REAL ID?
Yes. While a REAL ID isn't required, you can obtain these at a DMV office.
Do you currently have a Standard Driver License?
No, I don't.
Do you plan on travelling in the near future?
Yes, I'll be travelling to Canada.
It's recommended that you come to DMV now, so that you can avoid a second office visit later.
================
Why is this important?
It makes boarding a domestic flight more convenient.
I thought you could just use a Standard driver license for identification?
Starting October 1st 2020, a Standard Driver License will not be accepted as proof of identification when boarding domestic flights, unless you have a Passport.
