Who can use the work-study program?
It is for undergraduate and graduate students to work part time on or off campus.
================
Who would I work for under the work study program?
Most of the time you would be employed by your school.
Would job be available on and off campus?
Most jobs are on campus but it could be both on or off campus.
Will you be working on campus?
Yes, that is what I would be doing.
Then you would normally be employed by the school you are attending.
================
What is my work-study award based on?
It is based on when you apply and your level of financial need.
================
Am I able to work as many hours as I want?
The amount you earn cannot be greater than your Federal Work-Study award.
