Do you have a list of voter registration application FAQs?
================
Do you need to know list of how you are eligible to register to vote in New York State?
Yes, I need the list of what I need to know if I am eligible to register to vote in NY State.
You must be a U.S. Citizen and be 18 years old by the end of this year to be eligible to register vote in NY State.
================
What topic online do I have to look under?
You should look under the topic "More Info".
================
Does the DMV approve or deny voter registration?
At this time the DMV only send the application to the County or City Board of Elections for their review.
What happens if the DMVs records do not reflect my current name or address or date of brith or gender?
Then you should first correct the information before going forth with an online Voter Registration Application.
================
I have registered in the past to vote, do I need to update my registration again?
You would only have to update information that has changed like your preference for political party or your name has changed.
