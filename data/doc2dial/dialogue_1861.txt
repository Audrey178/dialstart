No problem if you are out of the country, you can renew by mail.

You can use an Application for Permit, Driver License or Non-Driver ID Card, form MV-44 or or you can come to a DMV office when you can.

You must follow the steps that I will indicate and complete the renewal notice MV-2 we mailed you.
