Can I use insurance documents from other states?
================
We can't accept any out-of-state vehicle insurance coverage of any kind.
My vehicle was registered in New York. what insurance should I use?
All vehicles registered in New York must have New York State auto liability insurance coverage.
I own a motorcycle, do I need to get insurance for it?
If you're going to use your motorcycle on any public road or highway you must have insurance coverage. Motorcycle registrations are only valid for one year and expire on April 30.
What if I use my motorcycle only on the outskirts of the city?
Any public road counts so you have to get insurance coverage.
================
With how many names can I register a vehicle?
There're only 1 or 2 names allowed on a registration registrant. Both, the primary registrant and the co-registrant must sign the vehicle registration and provide proof of identity and date of birth.
