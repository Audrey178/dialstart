Can i know what happens if i don't qualify for burial benefits?
================
We'll send you a denial decision letter explaining the reasons of our denial. We'll also send you a form called Your Rights to Seek Further Review of Our Decision VA Form 20-0998. This form explains your rights to appeal or to request another review.
================
