Where can I find the FSA Virtual Assistant Aidan?
================
The Aidan service is currently only available to a group of pilot users
Do you know how to find out if you are a member of that pilot group?
Yes, I have checked

In that case, you are advised to check back in the near future as this service is rolled out to new users regularly

Yes, it records all conversations, which are used to improve quality assurance, keep an accurate log of your requests and identify new skills for the Aidan service to learn
