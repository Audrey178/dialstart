================
Yes I can. First off we need to prove her citizenship with an official document.
Do you have your child 's foreign birth certificate?
Yes I will soon.
If it is not available other documents such as your child 's passport or a document issued by DHS as evidence of your child 's age could be considered.
Do I parent need to prove that I'm her parent?
Yes you must show us a U.S. driver 's license or a State - issued non - driver identification card.
================
Do I need the original birth certificate?
Yes an original document or a copy certified by the agency that issued them.
