Winter's coming around and I'd like to register my new snowmobile. Why can't I enter my address on the web form?
================
There is a limit of 20 characters for the street address and 14 characters for the city in our snowmobile registration system.
Is your your street address or city name is too long?
Yes.
You can shorten it by using numbers digits instead of words for the house number.
================
That might work. Also why is my name shortened on my registration?
Due to the limited space in the DMV registration system, your name may be shortened to fit on the document but it remains valid with the shorter name.
================
Besides the usual documentation, what information do I need to provide about my snowmobile?
Basic information includes model year, make or brand and color.
================
If I don't have a current driver's license can I use another document as proof of who I am?
Yes you must provide other kinds of proof.
================
