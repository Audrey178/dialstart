Thanks. I've never worked under Social Security. Can I still qualify for benefits?
Yes. If you are at least 62 years of age and your spouse receives retirement or disability benefits, you may qualify for spouse's retirement benefits.
================
I'm deaf. Do you all have a TTY number I can call, if I have more questions?
Absolutely. You can call us at TTY 1-800-325-0778.
Does it matter that my wife will be applying for her retirement benefits at the same time as I do?
In that case, we would also check your eligibility for spousal benefits.
What if she starts receiving her benefits before I get mine?
We would still check your eligibility for spousal benefits and if you are eligible, your application would automatically serve as a request for these benefits.
================
What are the next steps after I apply?
You could find out if members of your family are eligible for benefits. Are you interested in this information?
Yes, this information would be helpful.
This information can be very important for you, because members of your family could qualify for benefits on your record, even if you do not qualify for benefits on someone else's record.
================
At what do you qualify for Medicare?
You can qualify for Medicare at age 65.
