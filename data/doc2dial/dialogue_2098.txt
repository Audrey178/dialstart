================
What is the CDL Permit Rule?
The CDL Permit Rule is a regulation amended for CDL standards by the The Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration(FMCSA).
What is a CLP and how is it different from a CDL?
CLP or Commercial Learner Permit are permits, whereas CDL(Commercial Driver License) is the actual license.
================
Can a CLP be suspended or revoked like a CDL?
Yes. According to New York, they are required to apply federally mandated commercial suspensions and revocations.
================
Where can I find more information about the CDL Permit Rule?
Information on the CDL Permit Rule can be found in 49 CFR Parts 383, 384, and 385. Information is also posted on the Federal Register.
