What items should I bring to the DMV if I want to register my vehicle?
================
You will need to bring at least one of the following: a copy of the sale, the make, year, and VIN number of the vehicle, or a copy of the newspaper ad containing the aformentioned items.
Can I bring a copy of the newspaper ad or does it have to be the actual newspaper?
You may bring in a copy of the newspaper ad if you don't have the original.

To register a vehicle in New York, do I have to be a United States citizen?
You do not have to be a United States citizen to register a vehicle in New York as long as you meet certain registration requirements.

Do I need to notify the owner of a car I bought at a marshal auction?
Yes, if the car was bought in New York City, the Marshal or Sheriff will have proof of statement that they attempted to notify the owner of the vehicle.
================
Will the VIN number of my vehicle be shared?
Yes, your VIN can be shared but it is in order to protect you and other consumers.
