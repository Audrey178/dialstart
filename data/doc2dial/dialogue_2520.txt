How do I make payments?
You can submit your payment at the DMV Revenue Accounts Drop box. Be advised that any check or money order dropped off after 3:00pm will not be entered into your escrow account until the next day.
================
How do I change my escrow amount?
You need to send a request to DMV Revenue Accounts on your letterhead. It must be signed, dated, and include your account number.
Why is it saying my account is disabled?
If your account balance is zero or a negative amount, your account will be disabled.
================
How does an escrow account work?
An escrow accounts acts as an alternative to sending checks or money orders for DMV transactions.
