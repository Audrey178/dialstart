Can you explain to me how my benefits could change from year to year?
================
Your benefits may not change; but we require a Yellow Ribbon Program agreement each year.
What if my school has an agreement with you and continues to participate in the program?
Then as long as the continue to offer the Yellow Ribbon benefits to you next year then your benefits will not change.
What happens if I decide to take fewer courses?
If you take fewer courses then you won't get as much toward tuition and fees and your school will pay for less.
What happens if the school still offers the program?
Then your school must continue to give you the money as long as you meet all the requirements for the Yellow Ribbon Program.
What requirements are needed for the funding?
Have you made acceptable progress toward completing your program according to the school you attend?
No, I have not made enough progress according to my school.
Then the school will not give you the funding you need for the Yellow Ribbon Progrram.
