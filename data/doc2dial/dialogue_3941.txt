If I have a driver license from another country and I have a NY State learner's permit, can I drive without a supervising driver?
================
Yes, you can.  Your valid foreign driver license allows you to drive on any street, road, highway, bridge or tunnel without a supervising driver.
Is you driver's license printed in the English language?
It is not written in English.  Is there something extra I need to do to get my NY State driver's license?
If it is not printed in English, you must bring an International Driving Permit or a certified translation of your driver's license to the road test.  That translation must be certified by a consulate, the US Department of State, or another official agency.
================
What do I need to do to register a vehicle that I purchased in Delaware in New York State?
The registration requirements are generally the same as to register and title the vehicle in New York with a few exceptions for out-of-state and imported vehicles.
================
Where am I permitted to drive within the state of NY if I have a driver's license from another country and I have a NY State learner permit, if I do not have a supervising driver?
You can drive on any street, road, highway, bridge, or tunnel, except in a DMV road test area.
================
How long to I have to exchange my out-of-state driver's license?
You must get a New York State driver's license within 30 days.
================
What do I need to know about the certified translation that is required to exchange my foreign driver's license for the New York State license?
If you do not have that foreign license, then NY State law will require you to have that supervising driver who must be at minimum age 21 and have their own valid driver's license to operate the vehicle that you drive.
================
Do I need to be a US citizen to get my NY license to drive?
Although citizenship is not a requirement for your license, you must have US Department of Homeland Security DHS documentation that supports your legal status in the country.  The expiration date from your DHS document will be added to your NY State permit, driver license or ID card.
