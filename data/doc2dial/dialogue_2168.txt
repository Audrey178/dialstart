I want to know about the ignition interlock device.
================
this is a device used to measure the alcohol content in the breath of a driver. The device connects to the vehicle ignition system and prevents the vehicle from starting if the results are not satisfactory.
did a court ordered you to use an ignition interlock?
No, it didn't.
================
How can I remove an ignition interlock restriction from my driver license?
You have to ask your ignition interlock monitor to give you a form that states that you no longer have to install the device, then submit that form to the DMV to get you a new document without the restriction.
================
Can you tell me about the Leandra's law?
This is a law created in November 2009, in honor of Leandra Rosado who was a child that died in a car accident in a vehicle drove by the intoxicated mother of one of her friends.
================
Please tell me about the different parts of Leandra's Law and the penalties for conviction.
The law states that no person shall operate a motor vehicle under the influence of alcohol or drugs while having any kids 15 or younger as passengers.
Did the law established any new class of felony?
yes, the law established a new Class E Felony.
tell me some of the penalties for conviction.
Two of the penalties are, the conviction of the person to either a period of probation or to a conditional discharge, and the installation by court order of an ignition interlock device. in any vehicle operated by the convicted person.
