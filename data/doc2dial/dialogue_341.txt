Are there steps I need to take to get members of my family on my benefits?
================
Yes, because even if you don't qualify, some of your family members may still be eligible for benefits on your record.
Are you interested in learning more about this?
Yes I am, where would I go for more information?
================
Is there someone I can contact if I don't live in the U.S.?
Yes, you can contact your nearest U.S. Social Security Office or a U.S. Embassy or Consulate.
================
Where should I go to find more information?
For more information, please visit "Benefits For Your Family."
================
Will a U.S. Embassy be able to help me if I don't live in the U.S. anymore?
Yes, a U.S. Embassy or Consulate can provide you with more help on the matter.
================
Even if I don't qualify for benefits on someone else's record, can a family member still qualify for benefits on my record?
Yes, they may still be eligible to receive benefits from your record.
