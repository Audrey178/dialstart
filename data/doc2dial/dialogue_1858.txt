Can you give me any additional information about renewing my non-driver ID card?
================
Like, in the case of overseas renewal.
When you are out of the country you can renew your non-driver ID card via mail.
================
What are the time limits to renew the non-driver ID card?
The limits are: 1 year before and 2 years after your non-driver ID card expires.
================
What documents can I submit as proof of SSI?
As prove of SSI we accept the Notice of Award notifying you that you are eligible for SSI benefits.
================
What if I renew more than 41 days before turning 21?
in that case, you'll receive your ID with an "Under 21" message written on it.
================
Are there any fees for renewing online?
You'll just have to pay the regular fee for renewing your non-driver ID card.
so, for renewing online the fee is?
Just the normal fees paid for a non-driver ID card renewal.
