Hi! I am not a New York State resident. How can I get a temporary snowmobile record right away?
================
Is your snowmobile registered in its home state?
Yes
If your snowmobile is registered in your home state, you can use NY State's online registration for out-of-state snowmobiles to get a 15-day temporary registration to NY State and operate your snowmobile here immediately. If you have previously registered your snowmobile in New York, you can renew your registration. We will mail your regular registration to you.
================
What do I need for the snowmobile registration application?
proof of ownership and deed of sale - non-residents can use proof of ownership photocopies and aren't required to pay NY state sales tax if the snowmobile is registered in another state
================
What if I am not a New York resident?
If you are not a New York resident, you can get a temporary snowmobile registration right away.
================
What if I don't have one of these documents?
If you do not have one of these documents, you must provide other types of proof
================
What if I don't have proof from NYSSA?
If you don't have proof from NYSSA, the fee is $ 100.
