Can you tell me how to access the accident reports I bought online?
================
Of course.
Have you purchased accident reports online within the last 7 calendar days?
Yes, I did. That's why I'm asking.
In that case you can access the reports again. Enter the Report Confirmation Number displayed during the report purchase transaction and emailed to you in the transaction confirmation.
================
And how much does a search cost me?
Search fees are required by law, see Vehicle and Traffic Law Section 202 on our website for a list of fees.
================
I'll have a look at that later. Say, what do I need to provide to order an accident report by mail?
You need the plate number of a vehicle involved in the crash/accident, and/or the driver license number of a person who was involved in the crash/accident.
================
Can you suggest a topic that might be interesting for me?
DMV Records.
================
Do I have to pay a fee just to find out if my report is available?
You can do a free limited search to see if your report is available. The system provides a list of accidents by date and county, and you can search for the plate number to see if the report is available.
