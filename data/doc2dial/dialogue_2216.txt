And what is the cost of an I-PIRP course?
The cost is established by the course sponsor and the fee varies from one course to another. Its cost is clearly indicated before registering for the course. It is an advantage for you to compare the courses and their fees.

What happens if the person who enrolls in the course is not the same person who takes it?
It is important to know that anyone who attempts to defraud the system or who accepts a certificate of completion of the course and its associated benefits without having personally completed the entire course has committed a crime and may be subject to prosecution, fines and imprisonment.
And is there any problem if I don't have a DMV identification number?
If you do not have a New York State DMV identification number Your New York State Driver's License Client Identification Number, completion of the ADM course may not apply to your license record. You may need to submit the PDF Driver and / or Vehicle Registration Information Request Form [2] MV-15 before attempting to take an ADM course.
