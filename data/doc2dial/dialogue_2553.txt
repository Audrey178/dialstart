I've been wondering what happens if I fail the written tests. Can you explain this, please?
================
Sure. Have you failed a written test or the background checks?
Yes, unfortunately.
In that case the DMV will issue a CDL that does not have a HazMat endorsement.
================
What do I have to do to pass the test? Can I repeat it?
You must answer 80 % or more of the questions correctly to pass the test. You can take the test as many times as necessary until you pass the test.
================
How long do background checks usually take?
It normally takes at least 30 days from the date you submit your fingerprints to complete the background check. However, the time period can be longer than 30 days.
================
That's good to know. And how can I prepare myself for the HazMat test?
You can study the hazardous materials section of the NYS Commercial Driver License Manual (CDL-10), or study the Hazardous Materials Manual (CDL-11).
================
