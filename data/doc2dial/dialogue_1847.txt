Can you tell me what to do to surrender New York State plates?
================
Can I do it by mail?
Yes, to do it by mail you first need to remove plates and stickers,
================
What do I have to do if my plates were destroyed?
You have to report your destroyed plates to the DMV and surrender the registration. The DMV can issue you a new registration and plates.
================
What else do I need to do after removing the plates?
Remove the plates and the stickers.
================
My plates were destroyed.
================
What should I do about it?
I can't surrender my plates.
Just report the destroyed plates and surrender the registration. Later the DMV will give you a new registration and plates.
