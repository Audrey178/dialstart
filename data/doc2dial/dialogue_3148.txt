Why are Master Promissory Notes necessary?
An MPN is necessary because it is a legal document in which you promise to repay your loan plus accrued interest and fees to the U.S. Department of Education and it explains the terms and conditions of your loan.
================
Do I need a MPN for a Direct PLUS Loan?
Yes, you would use a MPN for a Direct PLUS Loans available to eligible graduate students.

Can I receive more than one loan under an MPN?
Yes, you might receive more than one loan under an MPN over a period of 10 years to pay for your educational costs; but you must be at a school that is authorized.
================
What types of loans use MPN?
Direct Unsubsidized Loans available to eligible graduate students and sometime for Direct PLUS Loans use MPN.
