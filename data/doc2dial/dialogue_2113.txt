Hello I would like to renew my driver's license, when is the right time to do so?
================
Renewal of a Driving School License must be performed between 30 and 60 days before the expiration date as seen on your license.

What if my Driving School License is already expired? Can I still renew or will I get into trouble?
The license can be renewed up to 6 months after the previous license expires BUT operating the school without a new License will be an infraction that could result in a $1000 fine and/or future denial of a Driving School license.
