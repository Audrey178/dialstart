What should I do if a complaint is filed against me?
You must first know that the New York State Department of Motor Vehicles regulates all repair shops, inspection stations, dealers, vehicle dismantlers, and other related businesses in New York State.
What do I have to do to be able to legally operate as a business?
You must register with the DMV. Once your application is approved you will receive a certificate from the DMV.
================
What if I choose to appeal?
If you choose to appeal, you have 60 days from the date of the administrative judge's decision to file the Administrative Appeal Form [2] AA-33A, which must be accompanied by the applicable filing fee.
================
What happens if I lose my license?
The license, registration or certification of your facility allows you to operate in New York State. Complaints filed and charges filed against you may result in the loss of your privilege to operate as a business in New York State.
