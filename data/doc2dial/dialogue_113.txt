Are other documents good?
Other documents like a Employee identification card may be acceptable.
What type of information needs to be on it?
The card must not be expired and it has to show your name, date of birth or age and preferably a recent photograph.
What do I need to show to prove that I am who I say I am?
We accept a U.S. driver 's license, aState - issued non - driver identification card or a U.S. passport.
================
Can I show my passport?
You must present a current original document or copies certified by the agency that issued them. No photocopies or notarized copies will be accepted.
================
I have an up to date passport, will that do?
An acceptable document must not be expired and show your name , date of birth and preferably have a recent photograph.
