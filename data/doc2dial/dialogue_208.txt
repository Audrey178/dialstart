Do I need original documents to get a replacement social security card for a noncitizen child?
================
Yes, you will need original documents to verify their immigration status.
Are you able to prove your child's immigration status?
No, I am not able to supply this information.
================
Can you accept photocopies of the documents?
No, we can only accept the original documents, no photocopies or notarized copies.
================
Can my child get a social security card if they don't have permission to work?
If they don't have permission to work, they may only apply for a card if they meet certain requirements.
When must I present my work permit I-766?
You must present the work permit if you are an F-1 student.
Can a child who does not have permission to get work still get benefits?
Lawfully admitted noncitzens who can't work can obtain private health insurance, apply for school lunch programs, and subsidized housing.
What types of documentation prove immigration status?
You would need original documents such as a work permit or lawful permanent resident card.
