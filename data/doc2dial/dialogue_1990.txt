I would like to know what happens after passing my road test.
================
You will receive from our license examiner an interim license that allows you to drive. Please, keep the interim license with your photo learner permit. You will receive your photo driver license in the mail in about 2 weeks.
What are the steps if I am 17 years old?
If you are 17 years old and you have a Class DJ, MJ or DJMJ driver license, you must obey the restrictions for junior drivers. For more information you can see "Graduated Driver License GDL Law and restrictions on drivers under age 18".
================
How can I get a Pre-Licensing Course Certificate MV-278?
For more information about it, see "Prepare for your road test". The fee for up to two road tests for a non-commercial license is included in your $10 driver license application fee. The earliest date is within three to five weeks.
What if I fail my road test?
Don't worry about it, you can pay for additional tests online or by phone.
================
My learner permit will expire 2 weeks before my road test, is that a problem?
Yes, you must get a new learner permit. For more information about it, please, see "Get a learner permit".
