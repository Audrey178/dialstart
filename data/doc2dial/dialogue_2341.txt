How are you doing today? I came here to learn more about getting a learner permit. Hopefully you can help me with the questions I have.
Of course. Getting a permit is the first step to get your driver license. Most people apply for a Class 'D-Operator' permit. You must be 16 or older and must apply in person at a DMV office.
================
Do I need to bring my proofs to the DMV office?
Yes, you need to bring along your proofs.
================
And how should I prepare for the tests?
You can study the NY State Driver's Manual and take the practice tests.
================
Can you suggest a topic that might interest me?
Driver license.
================
What should I know in order to prepare for my road test?
You must have supervised driving practice and you must take a pre-licensing course or a driver education course before you take your road test.
