What types of employment qualify me for the PSLF Program?
Those who work for a government organization at any level(state, federal, local, tribal) qualify for the PSLF Program.

Since you were unable to find the information that you need on the FAQ page, your next step will be to contact FedLoan Servicing at 1-855-265-4038.
================
Which repayment plans qualify for this program?
All income-driven repayment IDR plans and plans that base your monthly payment on your income qualify for the program.
================
I have a FFEL Program loan. Am I eligible for the program?
Loans from the FFEL Program and Federal Perkins Loan Program do not qualify for this program. However, if you were to consolidate your loan into a Direct Consolidation Loan, you could be eligible.
