I need help with transferring a credit.
================
Do you need to transfer your remaining registration to another vehicle?
No, I don't need that.
================
How can I get my refund ?
The DMV issues a receipt FS-6 that you can use to transfer the registration to different vehicles.
================
How do I apply for a full 2-year refund if the registration was never used?
If the sticker was attached to the windshield or vehicle plate, you cannot remove it and apply for a full refund.
Can I turn in the plates?
Yes you can do that.
================
How do I turn in the plates?
When you surrender them, your receipt FS-6 indicates if you are eligible to receive a refund.
================
If the sticker was attached to the windshield or vehicle plate of a registrant who has died, can we get a refund?
You cannot remove the sticker and apply for a refund.
