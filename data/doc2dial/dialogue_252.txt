I am looking for information regarding what benefits my children may qualify for?
================
Do you qualify for Social Security disability benefits?
No, not at this time
What are the requirements for my children to qualify for benefits from my Social Security record?
If you qualify for Social Security disability benefits, your children may also qualify for benefits. Eligible children are classified as your biological child, adopted child or stepchild. Dependent grandchildren may also qualify
What are the rules governing when children can receive benefits under my Social Security record?
The first rule is that your child must be unmarried
================
Are there any publications that I can view regarding benefits?
You can view the following publications; Disability Benefits, Benefits for Children and also What You Need To Know When You Get Social Security Disability Benefits
================
Is there any way that a child can receive benefits after the age of 18?
If a child is till in full-time education at a secondary or elementary school at the age of 18, benefots for the child will continue until the child graduates or until two months after the child turns 19, whichever is first
