Hello, I would like to know how to send PSLF forms
================
Submit the completed form, with your employer's certification, to FedLoan Servicing, the federal loan servicer for the United States Department of Education for the PSLF Program.

What if I am in AmeriCorps or the Peace Corps, or the United States Department of Defense is paying off my loans?
There are special rules that allow borrowers who are AmeriCorps or Peace Corps volunteers to use their Segal Education Award or Peace Corps transition payment to make a one-time payment that can count up to 12 qualified PSLF payments.
================
What if I am employed in more than one qualified part-time job at the same time?
You will be considered full time if you work a combined average of at least 30 hours per week with your employers.
