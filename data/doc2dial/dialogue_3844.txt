Do I need insurance to register my vehicle?
================
Yes. The DMV requires auto liability insurance to register a vehicle in New York
Do you have insurance?

When do I need to register the vehicle with the DMV?
You must register your vehicle at the DMV within 180 days of the effective date on your insurance ID card. Bring one copy or form of your Insurance Identification Cards with you. The DMV office will keep the paper card
================
