What happens if my SSI application is denied?
================
If your application is denied, you can then appeal it.
Has your application been denied?
Yes, it has.
Sorry to hear that. Your next step will be to file an SSI appeal online.
================
How do I educate myself more about the process it takes to get Disability Benefits?
Our Disability Benefit page can help you with that.
================
Can I file an appeal now?
Absolutely.
How can I find out more information about appeals and why my application was denied?
You can learn more about appeals by reading "how to appeal a decision" on our website.
================
How do I know if I'm eligible for Social Security and Retirement Benefits, as well as SSI?
If you have worked long enough you may qualify for these additional benefits.
