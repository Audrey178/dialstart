What is a on-site investigation when I am opening a dealership?
================
Is an automotive facilities inspector coming to inspect your facility?
No, not yet.
================
How would I get a Surety Bond?
You would be able to get once from a surety insurance company and have them fill out Dealer Bond Under NY State Vehicle and Traffic Law Section 415 6-B and be sure the business name and mailing address match 100% with your application.
================
What about an official dealer sign?
You can purchase signs from all kinds of sign shops and automotive supply stores because the DMV does not provide them.
================
What if I am using an Assumed Name for my business?
You must submit a copy of your Business Certificate of Assumed Name and you would need to contact your county clerk to obtain your business certificate.
================
What vendors can I purchase starter kit compenents from?
You can purchase them through Automotive Dealer Supplies at 518-465-9900.
