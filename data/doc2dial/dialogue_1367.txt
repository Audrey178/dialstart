Where can a Veteran turn to for continued assistance?
You can find ongoing support through VA to help you get your life back:Our specially trained suicide prevention coordinators available at every VA medical center across the country can help you get the advice and services you need.   Our Veterinary Centers can help you and your family readjust to life at home after you have returned from serving in a combat zone. Our Veterans Benefits Administration offices can help you access benefits for monthly disability compensation payments, job training, home loans, and more.

I want to help a veteran adjust to life at home, but I don't know how. Can I get support?
If you are a family member or friend of a veteran who is having trouble adjusting to home life, we can help. Through our national Coaching Into Care program, our licensed psychologists and social workers will speak to you on the phone, free of charge, to help you navigate the VA system and discover the best way to help the veteran that matters to you.
