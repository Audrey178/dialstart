Is it possible to sign up for direct deposit by calling social security?
Yes, you can call Social Security toll-free at 1-800-772-1213 TTY 1-800-325-0778 to sign up for direct deposit.
Can I also sign up directly with my bank?
Yes, you'll have to contact your bank, credit union or savings and loan association.
And what if I don't have a bank account?
In that case you must open an account before you can sign up for Direct Deposit. You should shop around in your area to find an account that has the features you want at a price you can afford.
