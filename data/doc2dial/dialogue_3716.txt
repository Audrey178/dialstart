How long will it take for me to get my first disability payment?
================
Does your decision notice show at least a 10% disability rating?
Yes, it show a disability rating above 10%
That means you should get your first payment within 15 days.
================
What should I do if I need to make changes to my information?
You should definitely contact the VA with any changes such as marital status, have a baby, adopt a child, or experience any other life changes that could affect your payment or rating.
What if I have a disability rating of 30% or more?
You may be able to add eligible dependents to your VA disability to get a higher payment or benefit rate.
================
What happens if I don't get a payment within 15 days?
You should call the veterans Help Line at 800-827-1000 TTY :800-829-4833 monday through friday, 8 a.m to 9 p.m., ET.
================
Can I change my address through my VA.gov profile?
Yes, you can sign in to VA.gov and change your address and other contact information.
================
If I am a survivor of a Veteran or service member can i see past history of VA payments?
Yes, you will see your past VA payments for survivors pension benefits and survivors and dependents educational assistance benefits.
