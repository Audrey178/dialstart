Hello, I would like to know more about reporting a death. Can you help me out?
================
Of course. Are you currently receiving benefits on your spouse's or parent's record?
Yes, I do. Why?
In this case you generally will not need to file an application for survivors benefits. We'll automatically change any monthly benefits you receive to survivors benefits after we receive the report of death. We may be able to pay the Special Lump-Sum Death Payment automatically.
Oh, so you'll do it automatically?
Correct. You don't need to worry about anything.
================
Alright then. And which documents do I need to apply?
First you need to select the benefit you will be applying for from the list below to see what information and documents you may need when you apply. For example a widows/widowers or surviving divorced spouse's benefits or a child's benefits.
================
Gotcha. Is there a possibility I can do this over the phone? I feel more comfortable talking to a person.
Of course. You can call 1-800-772-1213 or TTY 1-800-325-0778 and speak to a Social Security representative between 7 a.m. and 7 p.m. Monday through Friday. You can also visit your local Social Security office.
================
Well, I don't have the documents yet. Should I still apply?
Yes, please don't delay applying for Social Security, even if you don't have all documents you need.
