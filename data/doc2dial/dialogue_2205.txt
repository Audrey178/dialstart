And what happens if I register my vehicle in another state?
If you do that, you will have to surrender your vehicle registration and plates to the dmv.

Yes, then you will be free to get an insurance from the state where you do your registration and you must meet the requirements of that state.
And what will happens wit my registration and license?
Your valid insurance coverage will lapse and we will suspend your registration and your driver license may be suspended too.
