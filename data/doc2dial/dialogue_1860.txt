Yes, I need to know more about how to renew my non-driver ID card
You must know that you can only renew your non-driver ID card if it's between the time limits of 1 year prior and 2-year post its expiration date.
What if it's expired for more than 2 years?
Then you can't renew it, no exceptions.
================
Who can opt for the reduced fee or "No Fee" non-driver IDs?
In case you receive Supplemental Security Income and are under 62 years old, your fee would be 6.50$
================
Is there any alternative to renew a non-driver ID card that's expired for over 2 years?
No, there is no exception to this.
================
So what can I do with my over 2 years expired non-driver ID card?
You have to get a new original non-driver ID card.
================
I need to renew but I'm planning on going on a trip soon.
We recommend you to renew before your trip so you can get an Enhanced or REAL ID, and avoid another visit to the office later.
