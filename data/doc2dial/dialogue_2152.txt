Do I need a Diesel Opacity Emission for my out of state registered vehicle? It is a model year 1996.
================
No, you don't need an inspection, however please note if your vehicle is greater than 8,500 lbs, the NY State Police or the NY Departments of Transportation and Environmental Conservation may request an inspection.
What happens if it fails inspection?
Then a traffic ticket may be issued.
How much is the ticket?
That information is not provided.
================
What do I need to buy to become a diesel emissions inspection station?
You would need to purchase a smokemeter that is approved by the DMV.
Then I could perform inspections?
As long as you are licensed for that specific type of vehicle.
Do I need to submit proof that I bought the smokemeter?
Yes, you would mail in a copy of your paid invoice.
================
My emissions certificate expired. What do I need to do?
Do you have an existing certificate on the vehicle?
Yes, and it is expired.
================
My vehicle is over 8,500 pounds. Do I need to have it inspected annually?
No, but please note that you may be subject to a random insection at any time.
