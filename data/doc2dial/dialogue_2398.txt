Where do I dispute an allegation that you violated a toll regulation?
To dispute an allegation that you violated a toll regulation or did not pay the toll, fees, or other charges, contact the toll authority listed in the Notice of Suspension of Registration.
How can I respond to a toll authority for non-payment of tolls?
You can contact the Customer Service Center and speak to a customer service specialist. Each toll authority has a Customer Service Center and can provide you with information about your pending violation.
================
What happens If you request a hearing with the DMV and your affidavit does not appear or file?
In that case your registration will be suspended immediately.
Should I appear for the hearing?
You can request an in-person hearing with the DMV or you can request a hearing by affidavit, this is a paper hearing. The Notice of Suspension of Registration provides instructions on how to file the affidavit with the DMV.
