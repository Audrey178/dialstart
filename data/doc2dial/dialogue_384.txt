You must know that Social Security sends emails from no-reply@ssa.gov, subscription.service@subscriptions.ssa.gov, and ThankYou@ssa.gov. If you get an email from any email address that does not end in .gov you should be careful before opening attachments or clicking any links in the email.

Please, give me more tips about security when dealing with suspicious emails.
Always verify the legitimacy of the information given in the email, before clicking anything in the email. Verify if the phone numbers, website or any other information present in the mail are valid by searching that information in a search engine. Also, do not respond to any email requesting personal information.
