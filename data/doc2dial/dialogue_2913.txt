I have some questions about Student Aid Reports.
The Student Aid Report summarizes your FAFSA form, so it's very important that your information is correct.
================
What will be on my SAR report once I complete my FAFSA form?
Once you complete your application, you will see an Expected Family Contribution(EFC) displayed in the upper right hand corner of your SAR.
================
How will I receive my SAR?
You will receive your SAR on paper. If you included an email in your application, you can also receive an online copy.

What if there is a mistake on my FAFSA form?
Once you correct the mistake and correction has been processed, you will get a copy of your SAR.
================
What is the DRN used for?
Your DRN is needed if you choose to allow your college to change information on your FAFSA form.
