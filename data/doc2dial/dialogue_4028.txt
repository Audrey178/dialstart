What are the next steps involved after I submit my FAFSA form for federal student aid?
Firstly, you will need to check that all the information that you supplied is correct and completed in full as it appears on your Student Aid Report (SAR)
================
How do I receive my SAR after submitting my FAFSA form?
By providing us with a valid email address, you will receive an email from noreply@FAFSA.gov. This will give you instructions as to how you access your SAR online
What if I have no email address, how do I receive my SAR in this case?
With no email address, you will receive a SAR or a SAR acknowledgment via postal mail
================
How do I get a copy of my SAR if I am unable to log into the fafsa.gov website?
You can contact the Federal Student Aid Information Center at 1-800-4-FED-AID 1-800-433-3243. You request a copy of your SAR through this phone service and a copy will be sent to you via the mail, please allow 10-14 days for delivery
================
When looking at my SAR, I noticed a mistake. What can I do to rectify this?
Have you reviewed your SAR fully and found there to be errors present?
