I think I may be eligible for debt forgiveness of my federal student loan due to a law violation carried out by my school. How do I pursue this?
The type of debt forgiveness you are talking about is called borrower defense to repayment, or borrower defense for short. There is further information on this particular item on the website
Does the description of my issue come under borrower defense, do you know?
There are sections on the website dedicated to borrower defense and they will go through all questions and queries you may have
================
Does the ED do anything in particular for those who have successful borrower defense claims based on ED's CCI findings?
Yes, the ED have set up a process for the review of such cases for job placement rates asserted by borrowers enrolled in the covered programs at CCI schools during the eligibility period
================
If I receive a notification regarding the Sweet v. DeVos Lawsuit, and if I agree with the proposed settlement, what happens next?
In this case, you don't have to do anything. You of course have the right to attend the fairness hearing, but you are not obliged to do so, it is entirely a personal decision
================
