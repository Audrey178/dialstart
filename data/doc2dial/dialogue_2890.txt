An educational program must lead to a degree at a non-profit or public institution or it must prepare students for "gainful employment in a recognized occupation" in order for said program to be eligible for funding under the Higher Education Act Title IV student assistance programs.

And how is the Annual Earnings Rate calculated?
The numerator is the calculated annual loan payment amount an estimate of the annual loan repayment amount based on the median educational debt of the members of the sample cohort. The denominator is the higher of the cohort's mean or median earnings obtained from the Social Security Administration SSA.
