I'm not from New York and I need help getting a temporary snowmobile registration.
================
Has your snowmobile been registered in your home state?
Yes, it is.
Great. You can get a temporary 15 day New York State registration to operate your snowmobile here immediately, by using the New York State Registration Out of State Snowmobiles online service.
What are the requirements to use this service?
You need to be a resident of a U.S. state except for New York Canadians.
================
How much would it cost to register my snowmobile in New York?
To register your snowmobile in New York, there will be a $100 registration fee and sales tax.
================
It's asking for a NYSSA Snowmobile Club voucher ID number, but I don't know what that is. What do I do?
No worries. If you don't have a voucher ID number, you can just skip that section.
================
Do I need to pay the NY State sales tax since my snowmobile is registered in another state?
No, you won't be required to pay the sales tax.
