I need information about SSI.
Sure. The SSI or Supplemental Security Income program is used to pay benefits to adults and children who have limited income and resources.
================
Can I appeal if my SSI application is denied?
Yes, you can.
Was your SSI application recently denied?
Yes, it was.
You can file an appeal online. You can read more on how to appeal the decision.
================
Can I file an online application from the Northern Mariana Islands?
Yes, you can if you meet the rest of the requirements.
And to do it in a local social security office do I have to schedule an appointment first?
Yes, you can do it by calling to 1-800-772-1213 or to our TTY (if needed) 1-800-325-0778 from 7 a.m. to 7 p.m and from Monday through Friday.
================
Can people older than 65 get SSI benefits?
Yes, people 65 and older without disabilities can get SSI benefits if they meet the financial requirements.
================
Is there a phone number for people 65 or older that want to apply for SSI?
Yes, there is. We have the number 1-800-772-1213.
