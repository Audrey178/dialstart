Hello there, I came here looking for information on how to register a snowmobile. Can you answer a few questions about that?
================
Of course. Be advised that the snowmobile registration system only allows for 20 characters for the street address and 14 characters for the city.
Is your street address or city name too long to fit in the field?
Yes, I had some issues with that.
In that case please follow our guidelines until the information fits within the field. First, use numbers (digits) instead of words for the house number.
What about abbreviations? If I can abbreviate my street name, should I do that?
Yes, use an accepted abbreviation. You can abbreviate or omit the street type.
