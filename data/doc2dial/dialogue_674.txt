Yesterday I received a suspicious call from someone who said he was working for social security. What should I do in such a situation?
You should hang up and then report details of the call to the Office of the Inspector General. You can do this by submitting a report online.
================
Alright, I'll follow your advice. So what benefits am I entitled to?
Help ensure you get the retirement benefits you should by reviewing the basics like how we determine your benefit amount and who else in your family might be eligible. Visit our Learn About Retirement Benefits page to make sure you know the basics of retirement benefits.
================
What should I do after applying for retirement benefits?
Successfully filing for retirement benefits isn't the end of your Social Security journey. We have resources to save you time on important retirement tasks and keep you in control of your Social Security account benefits through a free my Social Security account.
================
What if I already receive social security benefits and something has changed?
You can update much of your information online with your personal my Social Security account.
