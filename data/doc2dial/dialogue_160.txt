what will I need to get my social security card?
================
are you an American citizen?
If we don't have you in our system yet then you will have to provide proof of U.S. citizenship
I am an American citizen
We will need your U.S. birth certificate or a U.S. passport as proof of citizenship
Do you take any other documents?
For proof of citizenship we would need either a U.S birth certificate or a U.S. passport
We need proof of identity too, do you have a U.S. driver's license, State - issued non - driver identification card or a U.S. passport?
No passport, but my birth certificate and driver's license will do just fine right?
Yes, as long as the license is a U.S. drivers license or a State - issued non - driver identification card
What would happen if I didn't have a license or state ID?
We would ask for other current not expired documents that  show your name , identifying information date of birth or age and preferably a recent photograph.  such as you employee identification card or other similar documents
