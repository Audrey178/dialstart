I need some information about the terms of service for MyDMV Account.
I'll be happy to assist you with any questions that you may have.
Have you registered for an account?
Yes, I registered today.
Excellent. You should have been emailed a temporary password to use to complete your online registration process.
Can I use the same username and password that I have with other NY.gov agencies?
Yes, you can sign in using the same username and password.
How secure is my identity information that I give to NY.gov?
Your information is very secure. We use a secure directory called the NY.gov that is maintained by New York State Office of Information Technology Services.
Why do I need to input identity information?
The identity information helps us validate your identity, which allows you to access DMV's secure web applications.
Why might NY.gov deactivate my account?
Your use of MyDMV account is dependent on the validation of your identity by NY.gov, so if they are unable to do so, your account will be deactivated.
When will I be asked for my identity information?
You will be asked to provide this information when you sign up.
