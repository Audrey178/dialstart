hello, please tell me about the qualifying process for the disability benefit.
Do you mean Social Security disability benefit?
yes
first, the person who applies must have worked in jobs covered by Social Security. Then the person must have a medical condition that meets Social Security's definition of disability.
================
What would qualify as a severe medical condition?
A condition is considered severe if it limits your ability to do basic-work related activities.
What if it doesn't?
Then you won't be considered disabled.
================
What else is it needed to be qualified as disabled if I already can't work because of it?
Your disability must also be expected to last for at least a year or result in death.
oh my, isn't that too much?
Well yes, this is a strict definition of disability. We assume that working families already have access to other resources to provide support during short-term periods of disabilities.
