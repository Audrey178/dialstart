I need help with your site
================
Are you looking for help on a particular area like health care?
Yes, health care
On the site you can fill and track your prescriptions, Send a secure message to your health care team, Schedule and view your appointments , and other tasks.
================
I need help with my records too, like my ID and my benefits documents so can you do that too?
Yes, you can get Veteran ID cards, access your VA medical records, and download benefit letters
================
and it's the place to go for disability issues too right?
Yes, you can do such tasks as check your claim or appeal status, view your payment history,  or upload evidence to support your claim
================
is there a place to go for survivor benefits?
Yes, you can apply for survivor and dependent benefits .
================
what about other benefits for family?
You can learn about the benefits you may qualify for as a spouse , dependent , or survivor . Also, find out what you are eligible for as a family member caring for a Veteran with disabilities .
================
so would my benefits letter that I need be under benefits or records?
You can download your benefit letters on the Records section.
