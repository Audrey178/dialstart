Thank you, I suppose. Now how can I get my vehicle title record abstract?
You can order your title record abstract by using the request form for DMV Records (MV-15).
================
Can I send it to you by mail?
Yes, you can order your vehicle or title record abstracts by mail using the Request for DMV Records (MV-15).
================
Say, while I'm here, how can I get another person's records? Is that even possible?
Yes. You must submit a Request for DMV Records (MV-15) and certify that you have a permissible use under the Driver's Privacy Protection Act DPPA to see the personal information of another person on DMV records.
