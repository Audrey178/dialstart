How doI apply?
================
You can apply online for Medicare even if you are not ready to retire .
Can I apply for both?
 When you apply for Medicare ,you can sign up for Part A Hospital Insurance and Part B Medical Insurance
================
what happens when I finish?
When you are finished  just select Submit Now to send your application to Social Security .
================
Am I eligible at 65?
your initial enrollment period begins three months before your 65th birthday , includes the month you turn age 65 , and ends three months after that birthday
================
What is medicare?
Medicare is our country s health insurance program for people age 65 or older
================
what happens after I apply?
 Once we receive your application  we wills   Review your application and contact you if we need more information or if we need to see your documents
