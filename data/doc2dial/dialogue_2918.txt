what's next after I send the FAFSA application?
You'll get a Student Aid Report and you will need to review it to make sure it is correct and complete.
================
what else happens when the report is ready?
The school you listed on your FAFSA form will use your information to determine your eligibility for federal and possibly nonfederal financial aid
what do I do if everything is correct after I look it over?
You keep it for your records
================
what if something was wrong on the report?
Did you find a mistake when you reviewed your SAR?
No, just wondering
================
where would I go to make corrections if they were needed?
You would need to make any corrections at fafsa.gov
