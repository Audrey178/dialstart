Do you want a Direct PLUS loan application for Parents?
Yes, that is the Direct Plus Loan for Parents.
Parents must be logged into using their own account.
What if I have questions regarding my loan eligibility and about the next steps in the processing of my loan?
You should definitely get in contact with your school's financial aid office and they will help you with any questions.
================
Do I need to complete a Direct PLUS Loan Master Promissory Note?
Yes, you must complete a Direct Loan Master Promissory Note before you can receive a Direct PLUS Loan because it explains all the terms and conditions your your loan and is the legal binding agreement to repay the loan.
What does the Direct PLUS Loan request allow me to do?
It allows you as a parent borrower to designate whether the school pays any credit balance to the student or to you; request a deferment while student is in school.
