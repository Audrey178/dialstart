hi i need help about Am I eligible for VA DIC as a surviving spouse or dependent?
================
You can meet VA or surviving spouse compensation requirements if you meet the requirements below. You must also present evidence with your claim that shows one of the descriptions below is true for the Veteran or service member. Evidence may include documents such as military service records, medical reports, and medical examination results.
What requirements do I need to meet?
Were you married to the veteran or service member before January 1, 1957?
do not
Were you married to the veteran or service member within 15 years of your discharge from the period of military service during which the qualifying illness or injury began or worsened?
do not
Were you married to the Veteran or service member for at least 1 year?
Yes
