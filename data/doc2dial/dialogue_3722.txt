can I take classes at 2 schools at the same time if I still have benefits leftover?
Yes, you can take classes at more than one school under the GI Bill if the classes at both schools count toward your degree. Also, the school granting your degree has to consider the classes at the second school to be required in order to get your degree
================
how much will I be getting so I can budget?
Your payment amounts will be based on the number of classes you go to and the hours spent in those classes
================
can a dependent also use my benefits to go to community college or another school?
Yes, and we can help you find out if you can use GI Bill benefits for college as a Veteran, service member, or qualified dependent. We can help you learn more about payment rates for undergraduate and graduate degree courses at institutions of higher learning like a 4-year university, community college, or graduate school.
