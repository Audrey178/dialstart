What happens after I submit my FAFSA form?
You receive a Student Aid Report, which is a summary of the information that you gave in the application. You should review this and ensure that it is correct and completed in full
What is my Student Aid Report (SAR)?
This report gives information about your eligibility for student aid. It also goves answers to any FAFSA questions that you may have

How long does it take to get a SAR and how do I receive it?
You can log in at fafsa.gov to view any SAR information. This is regardless of whether you filled out the FAFSA form online, on the myStudentAid app or on a FAFSA PDF or whether you provided an email address or not
What information is listed on my SAR?
All of your FAFSA information is listed along with spaces provided for you to make corrections. You will receive a paper form of your SAR if you filed a paper FAFSA form and didn't provide an email address
