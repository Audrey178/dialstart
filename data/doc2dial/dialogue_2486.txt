I need address entry information for DMV records
================
New York State law requires that you report a change of residence to the DMV within 10 days.
Have you submitted a change of postal address online, and do not have a residence address registered with the DMV?
yes
You must enter a residence address. Your residence address cannot be a post office box
================
If I'm not sure how my name appears?
Follow these rules to enter your name. names in DMV records appear in UPPER CASE and upper case in online transactions and enter only letters
================
if I already have a residence address registered with the DMV
You are not required to change it when you change your email address, but you can change your residence address if you wish.
================
I need details of the Document number
Document number. The document number is 8 or 10 characters, and is printed in the lower right corner of your DMV photo document, or on the back for document issues after January 29, 2014, The 8 character format is all numerical.
================
What should I use for manual bulk orders for public or private organizations?
use the online manual order transaction from the DMV.For public organizations, see definition below, no bulk order feeFor private organizations, see definition below, there is a $ 1.00 per manual charge for bulk orders and a minimum order of 25 manuals
