No problem. For applying, you must submit a Form VS-1 also known as Original Facility Application, plus a copy of your complete original, sealed and signed Surety Bond.

In any case, can you just tell more about that certificate?
Sure. The certificate of authority and sales taxes ID number are issued by the NYS Department of Taxation and Finance. To get one, you may apply visiting the DTF website. After you complete the process, be sure to record your Sales Tax ID on to the Original Facility application.
================
All this in via online, isn't? There's nothing like mail or something involved, right?
Sorry, but there's some mail involved. You must submit a hard copy of your application, associated documents and fee to the NYS DMV Bureau of Consumer & Facility Services Application Unit at P.O Box 2700 Albany NY 12220-0700.
================
