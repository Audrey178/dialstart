Hello there, can you tell me how I view my medical records online?
Of course. Our online tools can help you view, organize, and share your VA medical records and personal health information.
================
What can you tell me about VA Blue Button?
================
I mean, what can VA Blue Button do to help me with my data?.
VA Blue Button is a feature of the health management portal within My HealtheVet. It lets you view, print, save, download, and share information from your VA medical record and personal health record.
================
Ok. And what if I want to add information to my health record?
Simply click on Track Health in the blue navigation menu at the top of the page. You'll go to a new page where you can choose to record information like your vital signs, health history, goals, and food and exercise efforts.
================
