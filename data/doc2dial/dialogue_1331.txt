How does the VA make payments for the GI Bill for classes?
================
The VA make payments after each months classes, so you would get your November classes payment in December.
Are you part of the Post-9/11 GI Bill?
No I am not part of the Post-9/11 GI Bill.
================
Can I find out if I can use my GI Bill benefits for undergraduate and graduate degrees?
Yes, you can find out if you can use your GI Bill benefits for college as a Veteran, service member, or even a qualified dependent.  You can find payment rates for undergraduate and graduate degree courses at institutions of higher learning.
================
Is it even possible to take classes at more than one school at a time?
You it is possible to take classes at more than one school under the GI Bill as long as classes at both schools will count toward your degree and the school giving you your degree considers the classes to be required to get your degree.
================
What about left over money from GI Bill?
Any left over GI Bill Benefits can be used to pay for classes and another degree.
================
So if I am taking classes at a second school that help my first degree then I can take them?
Yes, you will be able to take the classes at the second school if the school granting your degree requires them.
================
Do the tuition and fee go directly to the school?
Yes, we pay the tuition and fees directly to the school and money for books are taken care of at the beginning of the term.
