How do I renew my license?
================
Your application for renewal of a Driving School License must be submitted between 30 and 60 days before the license expires.
Are you in the renewal process?
Yes, I am.
You must provide updated information about your driving school including Driving School Renewal Application form MV-522, proof of Worker's Compensation form C-105.2 or U-26.3/
================
What if my Driving School License has expired?
You must immediately stop operating your driving school business.  Your school will not be licensed to operate.
================
What are Driving School License?
Driving School Licenses are issued to businesses that qualify to operate a driving school.  They are renewed for 2 years.  The license must be renewed before the expiration date shown on the license if the driving school wants to continue to operate the business.
================
What if my school uses vehicles for instruction?
You must attach a list of Driving School Vehicles, MV-527.
================
Can I renew if my license has expired?
Driving School Licenses can be renewed up to six months after they expire, but the Driving School business can not operate unless the license is valid.
