================
How long will I be required to pay this?
If you have been convicted of certain traffic offenses in NY or have accumulated 6 or more points on your record within 18 months, you will have to pay DMV over a three year period.
================
Can I just pay my entire balance?
Yes, but you will be informed that there are additional assessments if you pay the balance of an initial assessment that didn't include the additional assessments.
================
What happens if I don't pay the assessment?
Your driver license may be suspended if you don't pay, but it will be removed once your payment is processed.
================
Where can I find more information about driver responsibility assessments?
Online at our website. You can also pay by mail. You will need to send the minimum amount on your DRA statement before the payment date.
