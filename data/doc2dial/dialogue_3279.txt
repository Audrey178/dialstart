what will I be asked about when I fill it out?
The FAFSA questions ask for your name, date of birth, address, and about your financial situation.
Would you like to know what other information it may ask?
yes
It may also ask you whether you’re a U.S. citizen or what tax form you used
================
what will I enter if I get a FSA id and do the fafsa online?
You will be given the option to enter your FSA ID or to enter your “identifiers” like your name, date of birth, and SSN.
I do the 'I am the student' part only?
The student is the one applying for financial aid, so be sure it’s the student entering his or her FSA ID in the “I am the student” tab. Do not start the FAFSA form by supplying the parent’s FSA ID.
