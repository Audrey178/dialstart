Then you can request a Board Appeal after an initial claim, supplemental claim, or higher-level review decision. You can't request two Board Appeals in a row.

What if I'm one of multiple persons claiming the same benefit?
In this rare case, you have 60 days.
================
That's good to know. So what should I do after a board decision?
Do you disagree with the Board's decision and have new and relevant evidence that supports your case?
Yes, you could say that.
Then you can file a Supplemental Claim. You can also appeal to the U.S. Court of Appeals for Veterans Claims.
================
I figured that. Can I submit new evidence then?
Yes, you have the option to add new evidence for a judge to review. You'll have to submit this evidence within 90 days from the date VA receives your Board Appeal form.
================
What if I want another hearing?
You have the option to request a hearing with a judge. A video conference hearing will take place at a VA location near you.
