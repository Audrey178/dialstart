What happens after I apply for health care benefits for a Veteran?
After you have applied for VA health care, you will receive a letter in the mail to let you know if your application is approved and what to do next.
================
What will I do after you approve my application?
You will receive a welcome call from the VA welcoming you to the VA health care program.  They will also help you with scheduling your first doctor's appointment.
================
How do I find out about VA health care benefits, health care team, and where I go for care?
We will help you learn all about your VA health care coverage, health care team, and where to go for care.
================
How do I get ahold of the Veteran's Health Benefits Handbook?
We will send the Handbook by mail and it will explain your specific health care benefits, how much copay, and other important information.
================
