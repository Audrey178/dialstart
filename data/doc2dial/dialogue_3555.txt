Can you tell me a little bit about Supplemental Security Income benefits?
Supplemental Security Income or SSI is a program for disabled adults and children who have limited income and resources.
================
Is there a number I could call because I am hard of hearing?

Is there an online application for children with disability?
At this time Social Security does not have an online application for Children's benefits; but you can always visit Apply For Disability Benefits Under Age 18 to apply for you child.
