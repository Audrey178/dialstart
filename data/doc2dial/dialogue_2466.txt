How do I go about transferring my IRP registration?
Do you need to transfer an IRP registration and apportioned plates?
No, not at this time.
================
Is it true I can't transfer an IRP registration at the DMV office?
Yes, it is true you cannot transfer them at the DMV.
================
What is the address of International Registration Bureau NYS?
It is International Registration Bureau NYS DMV Room 1366 Empire State Plaza, Albany, NY 12228.
================
When will I know how much a transfer costs?
Fees are calculated by the jurisdiction you are in and the NY State IRB does not calculate them until your transfer has been processed.
================
