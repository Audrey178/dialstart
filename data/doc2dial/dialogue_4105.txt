I need to know if I am eligible for federal student loan forgiveness.
I can assist you with this. The type of loan forgiveness that you are referring to is known as borrower defense to repayment.
================
I defaulted on my federal student loan after entering a rehabilitation plan. Can I still qualify for loan forgiveness?
In your case, you should contact the collection agency that helped you set up the plan. They will be able to tell you whether or not, there would be any negative consequences if you chose to enter stopped collection status.
================
What about Direct Loans disbursed before July 1st, 2017?
If your school engaged in acts that would rise to a cause of action against the school under state law, you could be eligible for a discharge forgiveness of part or all of your Direct Loan.
================
When will I receive a notification about the Sweet v. DeVos Lawsuit?
If you are a potential member of the group of borrowers to which the lawsuit applies, you should receive an email and mail notification between June and early July 2020.
Do you have any other questions about the Sweet V DeVos lawsuit?
No.
================
Are FFEL Program Loans eligible?
No, these loans are not eligible for discharge under the Borrower Defense to Repayment law.
