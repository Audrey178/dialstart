Morning, I would like to have some information regarding my taxes and fees. I got a car today, do I have to pay an use tax or fees?.
================
If you are a resident of New York, you MUST pay a vehicle use tax when you register your car.
================
I'm in a county outside of NY, could you tell me the use tax and fee that I have to pay?
To determine the use tax you should be paying in counties outside of NY state please use this chart: www.chartfortaxesoutsidenewyorkstate.com, find your location in the chart and then add 3.25% to the fee listed in the chart.
Ok, I have the chart. But I don't know understand it. Could you help me out with that?
The chart shows you a list of counties: find the one you are living in and see the amount of the use tax.You also have one chart for the registration fee depending on how much your vehicle weights.
================
I have some questions regarding those taxes, with whom should I talk?
You can ask the city or county where you reside. They will help you.
Ok, because it seems that I am exempt from the vehicle use tax in my county.
If you are exempt from the vehicle use tax, please complete the form UT-11C.
