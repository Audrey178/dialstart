Hello I need information about whether the New York State DMV began issuing REAL ID licenses, permits and ID cards on October 30, 2017
================
You can get a REAL ID at any time, but you will have to go to a DMV office. You cannot obtain a REAL ID online, by phone, or by mail.
================
and what documents should i bring to the office?
Proof of identity, such as a valid license, birth certificate, or passport, with your full name, middle name if applicable, and last name.
================
and those under 18 can get it?
Currently, the TSA does not require children under the age of 18 to provide identification when traveling with a partner within the United States. The partner will need acceptable identification.
================
Obtaining a REAL ID is mandatory but what do you recommend?
A REAL ID is optional and is not required for the following:    Have a driver's license   Vote or register to vote
don't want a REAL ID document?
do not
================
and how will my name appear?
As it appears on your proof of identity, it will be shown on your card as required by federal law.
