I can tell you more about it. Could you tell me if you have passed all the tests required for this certificate?
Yes I have.
As you have passed all the tests the Bureau of Driver Training Programs will issue you with a Driving School Instructor Certificate (MV-524). The certificate will be mailed to the driving school where you are employed.
================
I am looking to change my career and would like to qualify to become a certified driving school instructor, how do I go about becoming an instructor?
To become a driving school instructor you will first have to find employment at a licensed driving school. Once you are employed by a driving school you will have to complete an Application for Driving School Instructor Certificate (4). When completing the application be sure to answer all questions and provide any explanations/information needed.
================
I am thinking of becoming a driving school instructor what are the requirements for this career?
Firstly, you will have to be employed by a driving school licensed by DMV. You must also meet several requirements identified in Commissioner's Regulations Part 76.16 (2).
