 What is the feedback center for?
 You can use the feedback center to give us feedback regarding any complaint, issue, concern, suspicious activity, or scam that you are aware of or simply to giving us positive feedback or to review an already submitted case.
What if I don't agree with the response to my submitted complaint?
In that case, you should follow our procedure for resolving disputes before contacting the Federal Student Aid Ombudsman Group.
