I need help to determine the fees of my commercial vehicle.
To determine the correct fee you have to use both the registration fee and county use tax charts.
================
What fees apply if my vehicle is registered in Manhattan, New York.
For the renewal or original registration of all passenger vehicles, the vehicle use tax is 80$ for 2 years and for livery and for-hire vehicles in these registration classes, the fee is 400$ for 1 year.
What about the supplemental MCTD fee?
The supplemental MCTD fee is 50$ for 2 years.
================
And for the use tax for counties outside of New York City?
You can see the use taxes and supplemental fees for all original registrations and renewals on the chart. The sales tax depends on the purchase price and your locality. You can go to the Department of Taxation and Finance website to see the current sales tax rates per jurisdiction.
================
Where can I know more about these taxes?
You can ask the city or county where you reside or have a business.
================
What charts should I use to determine the fees for commercial vehicles?
You have to use the registration fee and the county use tax charts.
