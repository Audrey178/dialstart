Am I able to mail documents pertaining to my disability benefits?
================
Yes you can mail documents to us as long as you follow the specified criteria.
Yes, I can follow the criteria that was specified.
The documents must include your social security number. Do not write anything on the document and please write down you social security number on separate sheet of paper and include it in the envelope with the other documents.
Why do you need my social security number?
We need your social security number so that we can match you with the correct application.
What other options are available if I don't want to mail the documents?
You can bring the documents to a social security office.
================
Is there a number I can call if I am hard of hearing?
Yes you may call us at TTY 1-800-325-0778 or visit us in person at your local social security office.
Do you have specified hours for phone calls?
Yes, our agents are available by phone 7am to 7pm Monday through Friday.
================
Are there advantages of applying online for disability?
Yes, applying online is quicker as it allows you to start your disability claim immediately.
