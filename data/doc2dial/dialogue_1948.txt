How do I replace a title?
You can apply at a DMV office.
Will I get a new one while there.
No, we mail them to you.
================
Where will you mail them to.
To the address on file. You can replace them online.
================
So i can do it at the DMV also?
Yes but the fastest way to do it is online.
================
How do I apply for a title only?
Use the PDF file and follow instructions carefully.
================
Can I have them forwarded to a new address?
There is no guarantee the DMV will forward them. Please contact them with updated info.
================
Where are certificates printed?
In a secure facility in Albany.
================
Can I change the address online?
No, you have to contact the Title Service Bureau.
