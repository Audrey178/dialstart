are you planning to attend college or vocational school?
yes
in that case financial aid is essential. We'll explain how financial aid works, resources to pay for college, and loan repayment options.
================
And how can I do if I have been accepted in several universities?
In that case you should compare costs and offers of help. Accept help from the school that is best for you and inform them of other sources of help, such as the scholarships you hope to receive.
================
And would you recommend that I pay the student loans?
As you prepare to graduate, prepare to pay off your student loans. Good news! Federal student loan borrowers have a six-month grace period before payments begin.
================
and how to determine if I am eligible for help?
Your college uses your FAFSA data to determine your eligibility for federal aid.
================
and how do i keep that help?
don't forget to fill out a FAFSA form every year!
