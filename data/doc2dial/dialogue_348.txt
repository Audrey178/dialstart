Hi, I applied for benefits but my application was denied. Are there benefits that members of my family can apply for?
================
Yes even if you don't qualify for benefits on someone else's record some of your family may qualify for benefits on your record.
================
How do I go about applying for benefits?
You can apply in person. All you have to do is visit your local Social Security office. I suggest that you call them first and make an appointment.
================
What is the maximum benefits my family can receive?
There is a limit to the amount that is paid to family members. The total depends on your spouse's benefit amount and the number of family members who also qualify on the same record. The total varies but generally the total amount you and your family can receive is about 150 to 180 percent of your full retirement benefit.
================
As the main applicant for retirement benefits how much will I receive?
Benefits paid to your spouse will not decrease your retirement benefits.
================
