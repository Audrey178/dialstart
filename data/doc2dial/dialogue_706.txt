I'm a future benefits receiver and would like information about Direct Deposit.
================
So you don't get Social Security or SSI benefits yet?
Not at the moment, no.
Do you already have an account with us?
No I don't.
================
Do you need a bank account to receive benefits?
We offer a Direct Express card which is a debit card you can use to access your benefits and you don't need a bank account.
What do I need to do for this?
You must open an account before you can sign up for Direct Deposit. Shop around in your area to find an account that has the features you want at a price you can afford.
================
And what information do I need to create an account?
It would be best to have your checkbook or a copy of your bank statement with you.
