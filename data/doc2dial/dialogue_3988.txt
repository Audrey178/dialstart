How can I find out about transferring a vehicle?
================
You can look at Buy or sell a vehicle transfer ownership.
Do you not have the proof we need?
Yes, I do not have the proof you need.
Then you would need to look at the other kinds of proof you can use on our guides.
What proof can I use?
You can use a NY State driver license or learner permit.
Can I use an expired driver ID card?
Yes, you can use a expired less than 2 years driver ID card as proof of identity and age.
Can more than one vehicle have the same registration?
Yes, two vehicles can if you have the same type of registration.
My car is model year 1972 what do I do?
You would have to have a NY State Transferable Registration original or certified copy and not a photocopy, and bill of sale.
What if my car model is 1973 or older?
You would need a NY State Certificate of Title original or certified copy with the transfer ownership section completed by the seller.
