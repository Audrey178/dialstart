If my SSI application was denied am I allowed to dispute it?
================
Yes, you have the right to appeal the denial.
Have we denied one of your SSI applications recently?
Yes, you guys denied my last SSI application that I sent in, what should I do next?
You can file an SSI appeal online.
Is there somewhere where I can read more information regarding this?
Yes, you click on the link titled "How To Appeal A Decision" to learn more.
================
I need to call you guys because I have a lot of questions. What number can I reach you at?
You can call us at 1-800-772-1213.
================
How will I know if my online application for disability benefits went through?
A social security agent will contact you for additional information needed for the application.
Are there other ways to apply besides doing it online?
Yes, you can schedule an appointment with a local social security office or call us at 1-800-772-1213 Monday through Friday from 7am to 7pm.
================
Who does the supplemental Security Income pay out to?
SSI pays benefits to disabled adults and children who have limited income and resources.
