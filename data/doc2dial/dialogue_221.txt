How do I start the online application?
================
Click on the Apply for Retirement Benefits to begin the application.
Are you stuck on a screen?
Yes, I don't know the answer to some of them.
You can skip it and go back to it later.
================
How far in advance can I do the application?
You can apply at any time, you just won't be able to check the status until 1 month before benefits start.
I answered all the questions, now what do I do?
Click on Submit Now
================
Is online the only way to apply?
You can also apply over the phone.
================
Can I check to see if I'm eligible under my spouse?
We will let you know if you are eligible under your spouse's record.
