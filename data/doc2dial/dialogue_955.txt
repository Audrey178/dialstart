What information would I need to change by GI bill?
================
You would need your Social Security number and your VA file number to complete your change.
Are you a dependent of a Veteran or service member?
Yes I am a dependent of someone that is a Veteran.
Then, you will need to include that person's Social Security or VA file number and basic service history information along with your information.
================
If I am a Veteran what do I need to do to make a change of program?
You will need to submit a Request for Change of Program or Place of Training VA Form 22-1995 and you can complete the form online at this time.
================
I would like to change my GI Bill school, what can I do?
We will help you with the information you need so you can keep getting funding.
================
What information do I need to get this change finished?
All you need is basic information about your service history and education or training plans and goals.
================
What takes place if I am a dependent of a Veteran?
You will need to submit a Dependent's Request for Change of Program and it is possible to complete online right now.  Just choose VA Form 22-5495.
