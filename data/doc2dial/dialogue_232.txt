Does social security provide a benefits planner?
Yes, social security can provide you and other eligible family members benefits and a benefit planner.
================
Am I able to apply for benefits online?
Do you want to apply for retirement or disability benefits?
Yes, I want to apply for disability benefits online.
You can complete your application online, all you need to do is agree to a Terms of Service Agreement and create or log in to your personal Social Security Account.
Are there other places I can find more information about ways to apply?
Yes, there are.
Will I be notified if you weren't able to process my request?
Yes, you will receive information on how to contact us to discuss it by phone or by appointment.
As the spouse of a disabled worker, how old do I have to be to fill out a benefits application?
You must be 65 years old or within 3 months of that age.
