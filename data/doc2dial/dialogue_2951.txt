I have questions about how financial aid works.
Are you planning to attend college or a career school?
No, my daughter will be.
================
What is an aid offer?
Your aid offer will explain the types and amounts of student aid a college is offering your daughter. It also covers the expected costs for the year.
Has your daughter been accepted to more than one college?
No, but that could change.
================
How can I plan early for my daughter's college expenses?
To start planning, you should ask your daughter's school counselors and the college financial aid office about different state, college, and non-profit grants and scholarships that she can apply for.
================
When should my daughter fill out her FAFSA form?
Your daughter should apply for federal grants, work-study, and loans with the free application for FAFSA forms before each year of college.
