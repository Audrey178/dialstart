can I have my lifetime sporting stuff added to my license?
You can request the icons be added to your DMV photo document in two ways ; online using the New York State website or by visiting a NY State Department of Motor Vehicles office and presenting the required proof.

could I also have an icon removed if I don't want it on there anymore?
