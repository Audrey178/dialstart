What if I am in crisis as a Veteran?
You can connect with our caring and qualified Veterans Crisis Line responders for confidential help 24/7 for free.
================
Can I find out if I am eligible for vocational rehab?
If you have a service-connected disability that prevents you from working, then you can find out if you are eligible.
What career and employment assistance could I get?
You can get help through programs that teach new skills, help find a job, start a business, get educational counseling for Veterans.
================
Is there some type of VA transition assistance?
Yes, VA transition assistance is available as you separate from the military.  You can explore your career options by joining us at Transition Assistance Program briefing.
Is there help for job searching?
Yes, we can help by searching through Department of Labor Search, help with translating your military skills and experience to civilian jobs, and other resources.
