Wonderful. I had a bad sexual experience. Do you provide help related to such traumas?
Yes, we do. You can get help through VA. You don't need to have a service-connected disability rating, and you may be able to get MST-related care even if you don't qualify for other VA services. You also don't need to have reported the MST or have other proof that it happened.
================
That's a start. Where can I find women's health services near my location?
You can look for women's health services specific to your nearest VA medical center. Simply go to the website of the VA health care system that the medical center is part of.
================
And where can I find more information?
Our women's health website gives you the latest information about how we help women Veterans stay well and our Center for Women Veterans website tells you about health care and other benefits and programs for women Veterans.
