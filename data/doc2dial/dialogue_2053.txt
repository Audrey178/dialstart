I need help with a non-titled vehicle
================
Do you wish to replace a NY State transferable registration?
Yes
Is the registration lost?
No
================
What happens if I can't get any proof of ownership?
You must contact the person that sold you the vehicle , boat or snowmobile to you and get a receipt of sale.
================
Can I apply for a duplcate registration?
The current owner can apply for a duplicate registration document at a DMV office.
================
Can I use an old registration?
You can show an old registration , an original Manufacturer 's Certificate of Origin , or an original Manufacturer 's Statement of Origin
================
What forms do I need to apply for a transferable registration?
Use the Application for Registration or Title PDF MV-82 and the instructions for Registering / Titling a Vehicle in New York State PDF MV-82.1
================
What do I need to transfer titled vehicles?
You must get a title certificate that displays the name of the seller and use the Application for Title Only PDF MV-82TON form
