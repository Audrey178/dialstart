Please, tell me about replacing a title certificate.
will you apply for a replacement title at a DMV office?
yes, I need to apply for a replacement of a title certificate.
In that case, the DMV office won't hand you the new title while you are there. The DMV prints the title certificates in Albany and then mails them to the vehicle owner.
================
what items do I need to bring to the DMV office?
first, you need a completed Application for Duplicate Title form (PDF MV-902)
================
Can I get the replacement title faster using the express mail?
Yes, we can send you the replacement title via express mail but you'll have to pay extra for it and you'll need to sign for the delivery.
================
What can I do if I don't have a NY State Driver License Permit?
You still can provide other kinds of proof.
================
Can I cancel an online transaction after submitting it?
Yes, but you can only cancel an online transaction the same day it was ordered.
================
For the mailing of the new title, what if the registration for my vehicle is in my name.
In that case, you can add or change your address online and then replace your title.
================
what are the limitations of ordering online?
You can't order online if there was a title certificate processed in the past 15 days or the title certificate is a duplicate title requesting removal of a lien.
