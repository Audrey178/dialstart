Do you have any information on what Contract Solicitations are?
Contract solicitations, active acquisition synopses, and other information is available at FedBizOpps, the U.S. Government 's single point-of-entry for federal business opportunities.
================
Where can I find information on future contract opportunities?
Federal Student Aid prepares an annual forecast, with periodic updates, of expected contract opportunities for each fiscal year. Information on this forecast, as well as other U.S. Department of Education acquisitions, can be found at Forecast of ED Contract Opportunities.
================
What ballpark figure does FedBizOpps generally offer?
FedBizOpps contains copies of requests for quotations for proposed contract actions expected to exceed $ 10,000 but not to exceed $ 25,000.
