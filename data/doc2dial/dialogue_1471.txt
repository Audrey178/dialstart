I want help with PTSD matters
Posttraumatic stress can happen after someone goes through a traumatic event such as combat , an assault , or a disaster . Most people have some stress reactions following trauma , but if the reactions don't go away over time or they disrupt your life you may have posttraumatic stress disorder.
