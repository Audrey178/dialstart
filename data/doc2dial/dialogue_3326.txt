I am completing the forms for Federal Student Aid and my unable to provide my parents information. Will this affect my application?
================
In certain circumstances if you are unable to provide parent information you may be able to submit your FAFSA without parent information despite being considered a dependent student. Are you considered a dependent student?
Yes I am considered a dependent student.
Are your parents incarcerated?
No they are not.
Have you left home as a result of an abusive family environment?
No that is not the case.
Do you know where your parents are and are unable to contact them?
No I know where my parents are.
Are you older than 21 but not yet 24, unaccompanied and either homeless or self-supporting and at risk of being homeless?
No that does not apply to me.
Seeing as none of the reasons for not being able to provide parent information apply to you you should provide the said information on your application.
