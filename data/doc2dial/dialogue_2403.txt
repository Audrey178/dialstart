Will the DMV suspend the driver's license and all the records of a person who has an unsatisfied judgment against them?
Yes. The DMV will suspend the license and registrations if the person fails to pay or does not comply with a judgment of more than $ 1,000 and the failure is related to a car accident, collision or crash that causes property damage, personal injury or death.

What should I do If I was notified that there is an unsatisfied suspension of judgment in my record?
In that case you must contact the lawyer or court that issued the judgment. Information about the suspension order will tell you the name of the court and the court's index number for the judgment. The DMV cannot remove the suspension without court proof.
