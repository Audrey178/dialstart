We can help you learn more about the grant programs that are available from the U.S. Department of Education
================
who will collect if I have to pay a grant back?
If you enter into a satisfactory repayment arrangement, the school may assign the debt to ED for collection or may keep the debt and allow you to make payments directly to the school.
================
will I for sure have to pay my grant back since I dropped out?
You might have to repay all or part of a federal grant if you withdrew early from the program for which the grant was given to you
================
who can get a grant?
Most of our grants are awarded only to students with financial need

what are these grants about?
The federal government provides grants for students attending college or career school. Most types of grants are sources of free money that generally do not have to be repaid.
