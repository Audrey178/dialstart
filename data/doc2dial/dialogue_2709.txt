Please, tell me about replace or transfer personalized plates
================
Do you want to know how you can replace or transfer personalized plates?
Yes, please
There are several ways you can do it.
================
What if my plates are damaged? Can I replace them?
Call to the Custom Plates Unit at 1-518-402-4838. Office hours are 8:00am - 4:00pm, Monday through Friday
================
I have a custom image plate and want to replace it with a different image plate. Can I keep my current custom combination and use it on my new imaging plate?
Have you been told if the plate category you want allows custom combinations that contain the number of characters on your current plates?
Yes, I have heard that.
If that is so, you may have the combination on your new custom plates. Most image plates require a $ 31.25 annual custom fee in addition to the $ 31.25 custom license plate fee, and you have also pay a plate fee for the new plates.
================
What if my plates are old? Can I replace them too?
Sure. Same that damaged plates. Call to the  Custom Plates Unit at 1-518-402-4838. Office hours are 8:00am - 4:00pm, Monday through Friday
