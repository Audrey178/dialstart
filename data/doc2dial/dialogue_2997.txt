How much loan forgiveness could I possibly get?
================
The most loan forgiveness you could get is either$17,500 or $5,000 and it is determined by what subject area you taught.
What if I have eligible loans under both Direct Loan and FFEL Program?
You still can only receive a combined maximum forgiveness of $17,500 or $5,000.
================
Do I need to submit a seperate application for both?
Yes, you would need to submit a seperate application for both.
================

Did you teach as a highly qualified full-time math or science teacher at the secondary level.
No, I did not teach those subjects or at that level.
Are you a highly qualified special ed teacher at the elementary or secondary level whose primary responsibility was to provide special ed to children with disabilities?
No, I am not a special ed teacher.
