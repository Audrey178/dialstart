What if I can't get one of the required documents?

Is there a limit to how many times I can get a replacement card, and, are there exceptions to the limits?
yes, there are limits. You are limited to only 3 replacements a year and 10 replacement during your lifetime. There are exceptions like changes in immigration status, legal name changes or if you can prove that you need the replacement to prevent a significant hardship.
