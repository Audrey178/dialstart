What should I do when I complete the application?

================
What if I need help?
A veterans services organization or a VA-accredited attorney or agent can help you request a decision review.
================
What if I have more questions?
Get answers to frequently asked questions about decision reviews. Go to frequently asked questions
================
Can I add new evidence?
of course You have the option to add new evidence for a judge to review. You must submit this evidence within 90 days from the date VA receives your Board Appeal form.
================
What happens if I make an appeal?
At your hearing, you and a Veteran Law Judge will have a conversation, and ask questions about your appeal.
