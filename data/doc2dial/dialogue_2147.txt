What is the cost of a personalized plate for a passenger vehicle?
================
The plate for a passenger vehicle has an initial cost of $60 plus an annual fee of $31.25 in addition to your regular vehicle registration fee.
You do need plates for a regular passenger vehicle, correct?  It is not considered a commercial vehicle?
My vehicle is a regular passenger vehicle just used as a retirement vehicle for shopping, errands and vacation, but I would like to have a personalized plate for it.
Yes, then as stated previously, your cost would initially be $60 plus the annual fee of $31.25 in addition to your regular vehicle registration fee.

What is the maximum amount of characters I can use if I want one of he picture plates?
For the picture plates, the maximum characters usable is six.
================
Can my personalized plate be all numbers?
Combinations that do not have one letter are considered a restricted combination and will not be approved.
================
What are the different categories of picture plates?
Picture plate categories are; I Love NY Adventure, Sports, Military and Veterans, Counties and Regions of NY State, Colleges, Fraternities, Sororities, Causes, Professions, Organization, and Motorcycle Plates.
================
How often is the renewal on the personalized plates?
A registration renewal is for two years and will include two annual fees in addition to your vehicle registration renewal fee and will be paid when you renew your registration.
