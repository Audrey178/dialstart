I want to know what to do if I received a suspension order for an unsatisfied judgment related to a motor vehicle accident
Do you want to remove the suspension?
Of course I want
You must resolve the judgment
What if I was notified that there is an unsatisfied judgment suspension on my record?
You have to contact the attorney or court that issued the judgment. The information on the suspension order will advise you of the name of the court and the court index number for the judgment. The DMV can't clear the suspension without proof from the court.
================
And what if I get an unsatisfied judgment against a motorist as a result of a motor vehicle accident?
You need to file a Supporting Affirmation SR-60 and the other documents listed on the affirmation SR-60.
================
Can you repeat what if I was notified that there is an unsatisfied judgment suspension on my record?
Sure. You have to contact the attorney or court that issued the judgment. The information on the suspension order will advise you of the name of the court and the court index number for the judgment. The DMV can't clear the suspension without proof from the court.
================
Could the DMV suspend the driver's license and all the records of a person who has an unsatisfied judgment against them?
Yes, the DMV will suspend the license and registrations if the person fails to pay or satisfy a judgment of more than $1,000 and the judgment is related to a motor vehicle accident, crash which causes property damage, bodily injury or death...
================
I don't know which court to contact, what can I do?
Please, call the NY State DMV Insurance Services Bureau
