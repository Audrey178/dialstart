I feel terrible, I got a traffic ticket. What do I do?
================
To confirm, you have received the ticket yourself?
Yes I did.
Was the ticket for a non-criminal moving violation in one of the five boroughs of New York?
No, not in New York.
================
Can someone come with me to the hearing? My English isn't great.
Yes just tell the ALJ before the hearing starts.
================
Who will handle my case?
The courts in the area will handle your criminal case.
================
How do I figure out what to do?
There are instructions on the ticket for how to enter your plea. Make sure you act promptly.
================
What happens if I'm found guilty?
You will have to pay a fine set by the judge. Depending on your driving record and the charge your license may be revoked.
Can I plead guilty by mail?
