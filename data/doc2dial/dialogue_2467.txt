I need to transfer an IRP registration.
I can assist you with that. Will you be transferring an IRP registration and apportioned plates?
No, just the IRP registration.
================
Can I transfer the IRP registration online?
Yes. You can apply to transfer an IRP registration online by using the New York State One Stop Credentialing and Registration system.
================
Can I request that the IRB transfer the registration when I buy a new vehicle?
Yes. When you buy or register your new vehicle, you can request that the IRB transfer the registration.
================
Can I get the IRP registration transferred at the customer service counter at the Albany DMV?
Yes. You can transfer an IRP registration there. Be sure to bring all items required to make the transfer.
================
What would I need if I transfer the IRP registration by mail?
You would need proof of ownership and proof of insurance form FS-20 for the new vehicle you will be adding to your account. You will also need a proof of identity.
