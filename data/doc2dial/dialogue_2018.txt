If I want to get a license to drive a taxi or livery vehicle, what can I do if I don't have a New York State driver license?
================
Do you have a current valid driver's license?
No, I don't
What did I do if I plan to drive a taxi or livery vehicle in New York City and don't have a New York State driver license?
You must contact the Taxi and Limousine Commission.
What do I need to do to apply for a valid driver license in New York State?
You must apply for a driver license. Please note to drive a taxi or livery vehicle, you must apply for a Class E driver license instead of a Class D license. Both licenses require the same vision, written and road tests
================
How do I change from a Class D to a Class E license?
You must initially bring your existing NY State license as proof of identity to the DMV centre
================
What are the requirements needed to drive a taxi or livery vehicle?
You must be at least 18 years of age and have a driver license from one of the classes of Class A or Class B
================
What changes are there in relation to travelling and using my drivers license as identification?
It is recommended that you attend a DMV office to get an Enhanced or REAL ID as this will be needed shortly to board a flight
