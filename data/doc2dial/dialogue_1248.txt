Can you help with more information and resources for VA health care?
================
We can show you the different rates for VA health care copays for different test and medications.
================
Do you need to know about the other VA benefits and services available?
No I do not need to know about them at this time.
================
Is there a way to update my information for health benefits?
Yes, you can update your benefits with the VA Form 10-10EZR and the things to update are personal, financial, or insurance.
================
What if I am homeless, how do I get help?
We actually offer programs and services that can help such as free health care and in some instances free limited dental care and we can help link you with resources in your community like homeless shelters.
================
How can I find out about my refills on my VA prescriptions?
You can refill and track your VA prescriptions online and track your shipment there too.
================
How do I get care for special needs and conditions?
You can get care for special needs and conditions online like women health needs and service-related health concerns like agent orange.
