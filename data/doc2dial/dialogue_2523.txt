Hello I need information on How do I close my account?
================
Do you want to close your escrow account?
Yes
To close your escrow account, submit a request to close your account by mail, fax, or email to the DMV Income Accounts. Email scoring to DMV income accounts. Application must be on letterhead, must include your account number, and must be signed and dated.
And what should I do when I close the account?
Be sure to let us know what you want the remaining balance, if any, to do to your account.
What if I don't have letterhead?
You can send the request on your monthly statement.
and can I write a check with the outstanding balance?
of course If there is a remaining balance in your account, you can write a refund check, or you can transfer the balance to another escrow account you have with the DMV.
