How do i get replacement card for a noncitizen child?
You can replace your child's Social Security card for free if it is lost or stolen.

My child has permission to work.

What original documents do i need to prove my child's immigration status?
Need to show your child's current immigation status by using lawful permanent resident card, machine readable immigrant visa or employment authorization document.
Are you sure that is what I need to show as original documents?
Yes, you need to show your child's U.S. immigration document such as Lawful Permanent Resident Card or EAD.
Can I use the non-work number to work?
The non-work number is for other purposes; but not for working.
What can I use as proof of identity for social security?
You can always use your U.S. driver's license, state-issued non-driver identification.
What do I need if I am an F-1 student authorized to work in curricular practical training?
You will have to provide us with Form I-20 with the employment page completed and signed by your designated school official.
What documents do you need to see for children with work-authorization?
We only need to see an I-94 Arrival/Departure Record and some children must show their I-766 EAD.
