What are the reasons involved in the 2011 Informational Rates not being intended to make any specific assumptions or determinations about specific GE Programs?
Because they are Informational Rates, no general or specific assumptions or determinations can be made for any specific GE Program, or even to make a projection about future years' rates
What was done by the Government after the publication of Information Rates for GE Programs in 2011?
The Department stated that the Median Title IV Loans amounts posted were reflected in the student debt information, but that this information did not include debt information for previous years. An updated spreadsheet was posted by the Department on October 25, 2012
What is the purpose of FY 2011 GE Informational Rates?
These are for informational purposes only. They do not state any regulatory requirements, or sanctions or any other adverse actions
