================
of course. For manufactured home from the model year 1995 and newer, you can use as proof of ownership the Certificate of Title, any acceptable proof of ownership from other states, among other documents.

What about proof of ownership for a new ATV?
for new ATVs, you can use as acceptable proof of ownership the Manufacturer's Certificate of Origin (MCO) or the Manufacturer's Statement of Origin (MSO).
================
And what can I use as proof of ownership for a trailer model 1976?
for that case, you can use either the NY State Certificate of Title (original or a certified copy) or a acceptable proof of ownership from any other state.
================
Ok, now I need to know about the proof of ownership for snowmobiles purchased from a dealer.
for snowmobiles purchased from a dealer, you can use as proof of ownership the Manufacturer's Certificate of Origin or the Manufacturer's Statement of Origin. There are some more options.
================
What in case the owner dies. How does the transfer go?
In case the owner is deceased, the Estates , Powers & Trust Law of New York State takes care of providing for the transfer but only through the property distribution of an estate set up by a Surrogate Court. The surviving Spouse, minor shield or next kin can't assume the ownership automatically.
