How long will it take to get my new photo document with the icons?
It may take approximately 2 weeks for you to receive your photo document in the mail.
================
And then Can I remove these icons from my DMV photo document if I want to?
Yes. You can contact the Parks or DEC agency that issued the lifetime license, privilege, or credential to request that they notify DMV to remove the icons. There are no fees associated with these requests.
================
What happens if you chose to print the icons at the next renewal?
If you chose to print the icons at the next renewal of your document, you will receive your photo document with the icons after you renew your photo document.
================
Are there any additional charges for adding icons to my DMV photo document?
There are no additional fees if you request that the icons be added at the time of the renewal of your photographic document.
