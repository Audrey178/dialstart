I need some info about how to replace or transfer personalized plates
================
Do you want to replace personalized plates?
Yes, please
For that, there are a variety of circumstances in which you can replace or transfer personalized plates
================
Can I to replace old personalized plates?
Of course you can. Please, call the Custom Plates Unit at 1-518-402-4838 . Office hours are 8:00am-4:00pm, Monday through Friday
================
Should I do the replacement at a DMV office?
No, you can't replace personalized plates at the DMV
================
I'm in a DMV office. What now?
You will receive a FS-6 T receipt when you deliver your custom license plates. Verify the information on the receipt and make sure the receipt shows the words "Stored at the local office" and keep this receipt. After that, we will indicate on the registration record that the vehicle plates have been stored
================
Can I ask at the DMV to transfer my personalized plates when I register the vehicle?
Yes, you can do that.
