I need to know how to appeal a TVB ticket, please.
================
How can I appeal a TVB ticket?
You must file your appeal within 30 days of the date of conviction and pay a $10 non refundable appeal fee for each conviction appealed. This is the way you can appeal.
================
What are the deadlines to submit an additional Appeal Argument?
The Additional Argument must be submitted within 30 days after the transcript you ordered has been sent to you from the Transcriber. To send an additional argument after receiving your transcript, you must access the transaction again and enter the ticket number.
================
Is it possible to order a transcript of my appeal?
Yes, you can. When the transcript you ordered has been sent to you, you must mail the additional arguments to the Appeals Board within 30 days.
================
What happens if my traffic ticket does not have the words "Traffic Violations Bureau"?
In that case, you can't file an appeal with the DMV.
================
I just sent my appeal by mail, was it already accepted?
No,That does not mean that your appeal has already been accepted. This is determinated by the DMV Appeals Board after check the legal requirements of your appeal.
