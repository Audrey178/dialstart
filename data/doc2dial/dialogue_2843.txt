Can you tell me about college and career school costs?
Yes, we can help you with learning about college costs and give you tips to lower costs when you go to school.

What tools are I use?
You can use our college search tool and College Navigator to help you.
When will colleges and career schools offer discounts on tuition?
Did one your parents go to a college you are applying to?.
Yes, one of my parents are an alumnus or alumna of the college I am applying at.
Then there is a possibility that you will get a discount at that college.

You can find info about whether the cost of a college is low, medium, or high by using the College Scorecard.
