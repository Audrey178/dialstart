================
How do I qualify for benefits from my spouse?
You may qualify for spouse's benefits even if you never worked under Social Security. One of the criteria is if you are the spouse or divorced spouse of a retired worker
================
How do I find out what benefits I may qualify for?
You can use the planners we have provided to better understand your Social Security protection benefits. You can then choose our benefit calculator to get estimates of the benefits you may qualify for
================
What is the process of applying for benefits online?
You complete your application online where you are asked to agree to the Terms of Service Agreement. You then either create or log into your personal Social Security account
