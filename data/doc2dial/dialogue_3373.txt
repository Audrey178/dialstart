I came here to learn more about work-study jobs. Can you please tell me more about those?
Of course.Undergraduate and graduate students with work-study jobs will work part-time on or off campus while enrolled.
What else can you tell me about work-study jobs?
They are administered by schools participating in the Federal Work-Study Program. Check with your school's financial aid office to find out if your school participates.
================
What kinds of jobs exist?
The Federal Work-Study Program emphasizes employment in civic education and work related to your course of study whenever possible.
================
Are those jobs on campus or off campus?
Both. They can be on and off campus.
Do you plan to work on campus?
Yes, it just seems to be more practical.
If you work on campus, you'll usually work for your school.
================
What if I'm a graduate or professional student?
Then you're paid by the hour or by salary, depending on the work you do.
