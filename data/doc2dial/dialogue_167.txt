So what kind of documents do I need to prove my citizenship?
We can only accept certain documents to prove citizenship, including a U.S. passport or U.S. birth certificate.
Do I need any other paperwork?
You will need proof of your identity, such as a U.S. driver's license, State-issued identification card or U. S. passport.
