I need information about the direct PLUS loans.
The direct PLUS Loans are federal loans that can help you pay for education expenses not covered by other financial aid.
Do you want to apply?
Not at the moment.
================
How can I get a public service loan forgiveness?
To get one you need to repay your loans under an income-driven repayment plan.
================
What is the interest rate for Direct PLUS Loans?
for loans disbursed on or after July 1, 2019, the interest rate is 7.08% which is a fixed interest rate for the life of the loan.
================
Can you tell me where can I get info about the parent PLUS loans I've received?
You can visit My Aid to get information about all the federal student loans and other financial aid you've received.
================
What should I be aware of after receiving a parent PLUS loan?
After you get the loan you'll be contacted by your loan servicer. You will repay your loan to your loan servicer and they will contact you to provide you with regular updates regarding your loan.
