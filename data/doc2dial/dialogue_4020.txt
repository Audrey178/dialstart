What are Direct PLUs Loans?
PLUS loans are federal loans that parents get to help their dependent pay for education expenses.
Could I apply online?
Yes, you can fill out the online application for a parent PLUS loan.
================
How much am I allowed to borrow with a PLUS loan?
The most you can borrow with a PLUS loan is the cost of attendance at the school your child would attend minus any other financial assistance and the school determines what the cost of attending there is.
================
What must I do to receive a parent PLUS loan?
Are you the biological or adoptive parent or a step-parent of a dependent undergraduate student who would be enrolled at least half time?
Yes, I am a parent of a dependent undergraduate student that will be attending full time.
