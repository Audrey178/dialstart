I have questions about getting my driver record abstract.
================
I can help you with that. Do you need help with understanding what a driver record abstract is?
Yes.
You can find an explanation of Standard driving record abstracts issued by NY DMV on our website. Here you will also find a sample abstract.
What if I need a record of all information that DMV has?
To obtain a full record, you will need your lifetime driving record.
================
Where can I get more information about this topic?
You can go to our website and search under DMV Records for more information.
================
Will an abstract that I order online be the same as one that I obtain at a DMV office?
Yes. Your abstract will be the same whether you order it online, by mail, or at an office.
================
How much does it cost to get a driving record abstract at the Albany DMV office?
You will need to pay a $10.00 search fee and provide a proof of your identity. You must also certify that you have DPPA permissible use.
