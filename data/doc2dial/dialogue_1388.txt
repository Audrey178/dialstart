I want to appeal a VA decision and need guidance
================
Would you like to know if you can request a Higher-Level Review?
yes
You can request a Higher-Level Review of an initial claim or Supplemental Claim decision.
can I get a higher level review of another higher level review?
No, this option isn’t available after a previous Higher-Level Review
================
can I select a combo of benefits?
You can not select multiple benefit types
so I just pick what?
The most common benefit type is compensation, you can check your VA decision for more help on this
================
does this review allow me to send evidence?
If you have evidence to submit you should select another review option.  We can help you learn more about the review options
