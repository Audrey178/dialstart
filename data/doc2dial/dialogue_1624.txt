Hello, can you tell me how to access the services?
================
Of course. You may be able to use one or more of these services if you meet all of the requirements.
Oh. What are the requirements?
Are you signed up for VA health care?
No, not yet.
I'm afraid that you can't use our services then.
================
But what if I sign up for your VA health care? Can I use more than one of the services you offer?
Yes. In that case you may be able to use more than one service at the same time.
Provided I sign up, what services can you offer me?
You can get help managing your disability or health condition while living at home, for example. The services you qualify for will depend on your needs and whether a service is available in your area.
================
And how can I pay for those services?
You may be able to pay through Medicaid, Medicare, or your own private insurance.
