I need help with my disability benefits application.
================
Are you applying for a child?
Yes, for my disabled daughter.
In that case you will need to complete an Application for Supplemental Security Income (SSI) AND a Child Disability Report. The report collects information about the child's disabling condition and how it affects his/her ability to function.
Do I need to schedule an appointment for my SSI application?
Yes, you do. Please contact us by phone or in person to schedule an appointment to complete the SSI application. We will help you in person or by phone.
Is there a way to do the whole application online?
At this time, only the Child Disability Report can be completed online.
================
Ah, darn it. And what if I need additional help?
Then you can call us toll-free at 1-800-772-1213.
Alright, and what should I do with my application after I've contact social security?
You should fill out the online Child Disability Report. At the end of the report, we will ask you to sign a form that gives the child's doctor permission to give us information about his/her disability.
