Can you please tell me if I can apply for a renewal registration by mail?
================
Of course. You'll need to send us a completed Application for Duplicate / Renewal Registration (MV-82D) and a photocopy of your NY State driver license, permit, non-driver ID, or other proof of identity.

================
Are there any cases in which I can't replace registration documents online?
Yes. For vehicles older than model year 1973, boats, snowmobiles or All-Terrain Vehicles ATVs.
================
I see. Is there a way to get a temporary registration?
Yes, you can download and print a temporary registration in PDF format that is valid for 10 days. We also attach a copy to your confirmation email. You will get your new registration document by mail within 10 days.
================
Will do. What should I do if my address has changed?
Your mailing address on DMV files must be current. You can not change your address during the transaction. You can first change your address online and then renew.

Can I also apply by visiting a DMV office instead?
Yes. You can use the Application for Duplicate / Renewal Registration MV-82D, and bring everything that is needed to a DMV office. For example, a completed Application for Duplicate / Renewal Registration (MV-82D).
