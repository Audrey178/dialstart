tell me about tax benefits for school purposes
Tax benefits can be used to get back some of the money you spend on tuition or loan interest or to maximize your college savings.
================
how much can I claim on the lifetime learning credit?
It allows you to claim up to $2,000 per student per year for tuition and fees, as well as for books, supplies, and equipment that were required for the course and had to be purchased from the school.
is that for the lifetime learning credit?
Yes
================
what can you tell me about savings through the Coverdell savings?
The Coverdell Education Savings Account  allows up to $2,000 a year to be put aside for a student’s education expenses.
================
what other tax credits do you have?
Two tax credits help offset the costs of college by reducing the amount of your income tax; The American Opportunity Credit  and The Lifetime Learning Credit.
