What original documents do I need to obtain a social security card?
================
Anything pertaining to your child's immigration status.
Do you have documents to prove your child's immigration status?
Yes I have documentation for their status.
You must show us any current immigration documents on your child such as a work permit, a lawful permanent resident card, or an immigration visa.
If I use a document for work related purposes, who will be informed of that?
We will inform DHS if you use it for work purposes.
What documentation do I need if my child is a F-1 or M-1 student?
We would need to see your child's I-20 Certificate of Eligibility for Nonimmigrant Student Status.
If my child doesn't have permission to work, would they still get benefits?
As long as they entered the country lawfully, they can conduct business with banks and schools and sign up for private health insurance and apply for subsidized housing and school lunch programs.
Am I able to use an I-766 Employment Authorization Document as proof of my child's immigration status?
Yes, that is a document that would suffice for proving their status.
