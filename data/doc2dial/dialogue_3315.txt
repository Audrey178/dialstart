Are there a defined set of admission requirements that cover off all schools?
Each school has their own set of admission requirements. You can visit the relevant school's website to find out what exactly these are

Is there any way that I can get a refund if I do not complete course work?
It is possible that you might be able to get some of your money back, this will depend on the circumstances and on the school
================
Are there any lists that I can refer to that highlight any complaints that have been made against a school?
You could check with your local Better Business Bureau. You could also check with your state higher education agency or the consumer protection division of your state attorney general's office. You could also check with the agency that accredits the school, to see if the school meets the required standards
