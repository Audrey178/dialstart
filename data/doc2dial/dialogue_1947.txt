How to replace a title certificate?
================
There are few ways to do this but if you choose to do this by mail, you need to send the completed Application for Duplicate Title PDF [ 4 ] MV-902, proof of identity and a personal check or money order of $ 20.00 payable to Commissioner of Motor Vehicles.
Where should I mail all those documents?
All of those documents including the Application for Duplicate Title PDF [ 4 ] MV-902 should be mail to Title Bureau NYS DMV PO Box 2750 Albany , NY 12220 - 0750.
================
If i choose to apply for a replacement title at a DMV office, will i get the new ones directly there?
You will not. The certificates will only be printed at Albany in a secure facility and mailed to the owner afterwards. Therefore, doing the application online saves your time and it is the easiest way to do it.
================
If i do it by mail, what document is suitable as my proof of identity?
The document could be a photocopy of your NY State Driver License, Learner Permit or you can refer to Proofs of Identify for Registration and Title PDF [ 5 ] ID-82 for other acceptable proof that is Non - Driver ID.
================
Can i order online if my title certificate was processed last week?
No. If it has been processed within the last 15 days, you could not proceed your order online.
================
What if i do not have any Driver ID proof of identity?
You can still proceed using other kinds of proof which is Non - Driver ID proof.
================
Is it possible change the address on my title record online?
No. It is not possible. To update this, you need to contact Title Services Bureau at 1 - 518 - 486 - 4714.
