I need to know how many social security credits I need to qualify for benefits.
You must earn at least 40 Social Security credits to qualify for Social Security benefits. You earn Social Security credits when you work and pay Social Security taxes.
================
What if I work and pay social security taxes?
In that case you earn up to a maximum of four credits per year.
================

================
What if I earn more credits than I need?
These extra credits do not increase your benefit amount. The average of your earnings over your working years counts, not the total number of credits you earn.
