================
Do I need a valid NY registration of my car for that?
Yes. You must have a valid New York State vehicle registration in the passenger, commercial or motorcycle registration class that does not expire within 60 days. The mailing address on your vehicle registration record must be your current mailing address and your vehicle registration record must match your current name if your name has changed.
================
I see. Should I dispose of my old plates when I get the new ones?
Yes. Once you receive your new plates you will want to destroy your old plates to protect against the fraudulent use. Mark each vehicle plate with a permanent marker.
