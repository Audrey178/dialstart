What happens in the event that a lienholder has my title certificate?
In a number of states, a lienholder is in possession of the title certificate until the lien has been satisfied in full. When the lien has been satisfied, they then send the title certificate to the owner
Is your vehicle registered in NYS but the out-of-state title certificate is received by you from the lienholder?
Yes, this is the case
In this instance, you must send the title certificate to the Title Services Bureau. The DMV then send you a NYS title certificate. There is no fee applicable to this as you already paid for the title certificate fee when registering your vehicle
