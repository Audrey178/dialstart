It is a request for supplemental information in connection with your application for a Federal Direct PLUS Loan Direct PLUS Loan through the William D. Ford Federal Direct Loan Direct Loan Program. Some schools may have a different process for obtaining the additional information needed to process your Direct PLUS Loan application.
What if I place a security freeze in place on my credit?
Then you must lift or remove the freeze at each credit bureau before you continue. Your application will not be processed if you have a security freeze.

And what happens to the information I provide? Is it kept safe?
The information that you provide will be sent to the school that you select. The school you select will use the information collected to determine your eligibility for a Direct PLUS Loan and process your application.
Do I need to give the school my permission to use my funds to cover the costs?
The Direct PLUS Loan Application allows you to authorize the school to use your loan funds to satisfy other educationally related charges after tuition and fees, and room and board have been paid. You are not required to provide this authorization.
