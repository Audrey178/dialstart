Can you tell me some of the organizations that are exempt from the search fees?
Some of the exempted organizations are any public organization, its officers, a volunteer fire company, volunteer ambulance service, etc. These organizations are exempt from the search fee.
================
What to do in case none of the users performed a search that the DMV contacted me for?
You should contact the DMV immediately.
Why would the DMV contact me about a search?
The DMV may contact you to ask you about a search to make sure you comply with the Dial-In Terms of Service.
What if the search is done on behalf of a client?
In that case, you'll have to give the permissible use of your client and a proof of your client-agent relationship.
