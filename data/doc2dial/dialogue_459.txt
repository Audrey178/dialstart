What can I learn about SSI?
The Supplemental Security Income or SSI program pays benefits to disabled adults and children who have limited income and resources.
================

Can I be married to apply for SSI?

What if I am deaf or hard of hearing?
If you are deaf or hard of hearing, then you can call use at TTY 1-800-325-0778 or visit your local Social Security office.
