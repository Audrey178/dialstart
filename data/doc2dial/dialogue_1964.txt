If my vehicle is recovered and is damaged, what should I do?
================
If there is any damage, you will need to contact your insurance company.
What if my plates are still missing?
Bring a copy of form MV-78B to the DMV office to apply for new ones.
Will the recovery be listed on my title record?
When the stolen vehicle alarm is cancelled, it will show recovered on your title.
What happens if my car is totaled?
Contact your insurance company to resolve the matter.
Can I get new plates?
Yes, bring a copy of MV-78B to apply for new plates.
Can I go ahead and get new plates before the insurance company finishes investigating?
No, resolve the issue with the insurance company first, the apply for new plates.
================
What do I do if my car is stolen?
Report it to both the police and your insurance company immediately.
