What can I do if I can't find some of the required documents?
You'll get asked for any other suitable document. Any submitted document should be current, contain all the needed information to identify you and have a recent photograph. For example, you could use your employee identification card.

You can use your U.S. passport, state-issued non-driver ID card or your U.S. driver's license.
