What kind of questions do I need to fill out when I use FAFSA4caster?
You answer financial and other questions that are used to estimate your federal student aid eligibility. You may be able to answer most of the questions easily, but some of the questions may ask you to reference your personal records for instance, your federal tax information or your bank statements.

What happens when I select "calculate"?
Once you select "calculate", FAFSA4caster summarizes the cost, the total aid entered, and the difference the net cost of attending college. our estimated Expected Family Contribution (EFC) also appears.
