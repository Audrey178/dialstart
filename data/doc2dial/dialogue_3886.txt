Hello, I would like to know how I do the inspections if I bought a vehicle from a private seller
================
buy a vehicle from someone other than a dealer?
Yes
You will receive an inspection extension that will last 10 days from the date of registration.
================
Do I need to replace the inspection tag?
Yes You can have your vehicle re-inspected or you can request a replacement sticker using the PDF VS-118 Replacement Inspection Sticker Request form. Complete the form and mail it to the address on the form.
Has your vehicle's inspection tag expired in the last 60 days?
Yes
You may be fined $ 25 to $ 50.
================
If the tag cannot be used?
You can have your vehicle re-inspected or you can request a replacement label using the PDF VS-118 Replacement Inspection Label Request Form. Complete the form and mail it to the address on the form.
================
and who to send the inspections?
Inspection stations electronically send a record of all emissions inspections to the DMV.
If the inspection tag expires more than 60 days?
the fine is between $ 50 and $ 100.
================
When do you need a security inspection?
All vehicles registered in New York State must obtain a safety inspection at a DMV - licensed from the inspection station   every 12 months   when vehicle ownership is transferred
