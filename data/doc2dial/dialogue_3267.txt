How do I make a payment to a Direct Loan?
Yes there is, you will receive a 0.25% interest rate deduction if you set up an automatic direct debit to your Direct Loan
================
Is there a way to make a payment to a Great Lakes or Nelnet Loan without going through the FSA website?
It could happen that we cannot accept a payment to this loan due to your account status. If you'd prefer, you can always make a payment on your servicer's website, all you need to do is log in to your Great Lakes or Nelnet account online
================
