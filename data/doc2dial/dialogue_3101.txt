I have questions about the TEACH Grant Agreement To Serve.
================
Are you interested in completing the TEACH Grant ATS?
Yes, I am.
Great. You will first need to log into your account.
================
How do I know if I qualify for this grant?
This grant is for students who are completing or plan to complete any coursework that is required to begin a career in teaching. To qualify for this grant, you also need to agree to teach full time for at least 4 years.
================
How do I know if I should complete this?
If you are a student receiving a TEACH grant and have completed TEACH Initial and Subsequent Counseling in the same Award Year, you should complete this.
================
What information will I need to complete this?
You will need your verified FSA ID.
================
How long does it take to complete the ATS?
Most individuals complete the ATS in 20-30 minutes. Please be advised that this must be completed in one session.
