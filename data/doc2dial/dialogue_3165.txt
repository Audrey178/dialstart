What if I have questions about Public Service Loan Forgiveness?
================
Do you have more questions?
No, I want to know in case I have questions.
What if my questions are not covered on the FAQ page?
You can call the FedLoan Servicing at 1-855-265-4038.
================
What is one organization that is not qualifying employment?
If you work for a Partisan political organization.
================
Are there any alerts I should be aware of that are important?
Yes, if you don't periodically submit the Employment Certification Form at the time you apply for forgiveness you will be required to submit a form for each empolyer worked.
================
Is there anything I need to know about Income-Driven Repayment plans before I decide to change to one?
Yes, you should be aware that your payment may increase under IDR plans depending on your income and the amount you owe.
