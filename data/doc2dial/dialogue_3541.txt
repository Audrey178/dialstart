I need help with the retirement benefits planner.
================
Do you want information about being divorced and getting benefits?
No, I do not need information about an ex-spouse.
Can I receive spousal benefits if I am divorced?
Yes, you will receive benefits on your ex-spouse's record even if they are remarried.
Will the retirement benefit limit still apply if I continue to work?
Yes, the retirement benefit earnings limit still applies if you continue to work.
What happens if i have benefits and I am eligible under my ex-spouse's records?
You will get another amount on your ex-spouse's record so that the combination of benefits equals the higher amount.
Will my earnings affect my benefits payments?
You can use our earnings test calculator to see how your earnings will affect your payments.
What will I need to use to figure out the amount of benefits?
You can use the earning calculator to see how it would affect the benefits payment.
