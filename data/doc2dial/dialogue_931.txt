Can you give me any tips to help me figure the rating out?
Yes, look for your highest disability rating in the left column and your lowest rating in the top column and your combined rating is the number where the two intersect , rounded to the nearest 10%.
================
In step 4 how do you figure out the combined disability rating?
We will round combined values ending in 1 to 4 down, and those ending in 5 to 9 up.
================
What do you base my ratings on if I had a disability before I entered the service and the service made it worse?
We essentially base your monthly compensation on the level of aggravation or how much worse your preservice disability got after your military service.
