can you give me more information about renewing the non-driver ID card?
================
My status is Temporary Visitor.
You'll be able to renew your non-driver ID card only if your Temporary Visitor status is not expired at the DMV.
So how can I renew my non-driver ID card?
You can do it online, via mail or at a DMV office.
================
I got my non-driver ID on February 12, 2917. It's not enhanced. What kind of ID do I have?
In your case, you have a Standard document.
================
Can I renew more than 41 days before my 21st birthday?
You can but your ID will have a "UNDER 21" message on it.
What if my renewal is processed 40 days before my birthday?
We'll send you your non-driver ID card with the "UNDER 21" message on or near your birth date.
================
In case my non-driver ID is expired for over 2 years can I still renew it?
you have to apply for an original non-driver ID card.
================
My Temporary Visitor status was recently extended, what should I do now?
Go to a DMV office with your updated paperwork from the DHS. We'll use your updated paperwork to update our records to show that your status has been extended so you can renew your non-driver ID card. Your new card will show the new temporary visitor expiration date.
