Can you explain to me what VA Vocational Rehabilitation is ?
First off, do you have a service-connected disability that limits your ability to work or even prevents you from working?
Yes, I am not able to work because of a service-connected disability.
The VA Vocational Rehabilitation and Employment program will be able to help you and you can find out if you are eligible.
================
Can family members of service members and Veterans get help with these resources?
Yes, dependent family members can get counseling services, training, and education benefits.
================
How can I access VR&E?
You can contact VR&E through Integrated Disability Evaluation System or IDES if you are wounded, injured, or become sick during your service time and can't perform duties.
================
Are there any more resources I could use at this time?
You can use VetSuccess on Campus and find out if our counselors can help you transition from military to college life.
================
What is available through VR&E?
VR&E programs for service members and Veterans Explore help you learn new skills, find a new job, starting a business, and getting educational counseling or returning to a former job.
How can I find out if I can get VR&E benefits and services as a service member?
Yes, you can find out if you are eligible.
