Hello I need information about How to renew a registration
You must renew your registration before it expires. Your new registration documents will arrive in the mail in approximately 10 days. Early renewal will not change the new expiration date of your new registration.
================
but I have a situation, I don't know the correct amount that I should put in the check to renew, how do I do?
You can send a check payable to the 'Commissioner of Motor Vehicles', but do not write the amount
I don't know the correct amount to put in the check to renew, how do I do it?
As I said earlier, send a check made payable to the 'Commissioner of Motor Vehicles', but do not write the amount.
================
and how do I renew if My registration is about to expire?
It is very easy. You can renew online up to one year after the expiration date. You can download and print a temporary registration that is valid for 10 days on the day you renew.
================
And how can I do if my address is not the same as when I gave the data?
Your email address on DMV files must be current. You cannot change it during the transaction. First change your address online and then renew.
