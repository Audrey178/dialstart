I need help changing the school I'm using my GI Bill on
We can help you make GI Bill changes. You can find out when and how to request changes to your GI Bill school, program, or other VA education benefits so you can keep getting funding to help pay for your education or training.
================
Do I have to make changes to my benefits or do they happen automatically?
Yes, you need to request changes to your benefits when you make certain education changes.
================
ok and how do I request those changes?
================
are you a Veteran or service member?
I was in the Army
To request changes to your GI BIll you will need to submit a Request for Change of Program or Place of Training, VA Form 22-1995. You can complete the form online
================
and I request changes if I want to pursue a new plan or program?
Yes, you need to request changes if you are changing your educational , professional , or vocational goal
ok,  so I request a change for my new school too?
Yes, if you are transferring to a different school you must request changes to your GI Bill.
================
what if I was a dependent, would I use the same form to make changes?
You would need to submit a Dependent’s Request for Change of Program or Place of Training , VA Form 22-5495. The form can be completed online.
