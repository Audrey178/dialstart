Hello there, I have problems with substance use and need help.
================
Of course. The VA health care program covers services to treat substance use problems.
Do you wish to access these services?
No, it's fine. I just wanted to ask some questions first.
I served in Operation New Dawn and wanted to know what to do when suffering from substance use problems?
If you served in Operation New Dawn, call your local VA medical center, and ask to speak to the OND coordinator.
So all I have to do is to call there?
Yes, just call your local VA medical center and they will help you.
I already have a VA primary care provider, can I talk to him?
Of course. You can talk to him about your substance use. Your provider can help you get screened for substance use problems and related issues like PTSD or depression and can offer treatment and support as needed.
================
I have to admit, all this substance use made me a little broke. I might not be able to pay my mortgage. What if I'm at the risk of becoming homeless? What then?
Please visit our website to learn about VA programs for Veterans who are homeless and learn about our homelessness programs. Contact the National Call Center for Homeless Veterans at 877-424-3838 for help 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
