In case I have a moving violation before I get my driver's license while I have the problem driver restriction, what could happen?
If you get convicted with a moving violation, like tailgating or speeding, the approval of a driver license would be withdrawn.
================
what if I have a DUI violation in my driving history?
in that case, you could be required to install an Ignition interlock device on all of the vehicles you own or operate. Also, you would be subject to the problem driving restrictions.
What could happen if I get convicted from driving out of restriction?
If you get convicted then your driver's license will be revoked.
