You can help me on how to replace my registration by mail please.
================
When replacing your registration by mail you must complete and send a completed Application for Duplicate / Renewal Registration PDF MV-82Ds and a photocopy of your NY State driver license, permit, non - driver ID, or other proof of identity.

================
What do I need to show if I got to the DMV in person?
You will fill out the same Application for Duplicate / Renewal Registration MV-82D and bring the same identification papers.
================
What registration information do I need to show?
Your vehicle plate or registration numbers, registration class and the ZIP Code for the address on your registration.
================
Is it the same if my registration was stolen?
You must apply for a replacement by mail or at a DMV office. Click on the link here for lost or stolen plates [ 2 ] for more information.
