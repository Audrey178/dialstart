Are you allowed to use other health insurance along with the VA health care insurance?
Do you have other health insurance plans like Medicaid, Medicare, or TRICARE?
Yes, I have medicare at this time.
You can use your Medicare as well as your VA health care benefits; we can help you learn more about this.
================
Why does the VA make me provide my health insurance coverage information?
The VA needs to bill your private health insurance for any care, supplies, or medicine we use to treat your non-service connected conditions, illnesses or injuries.
================
What do I need to do to use VA benefits for care?
You will need to get care from a VA medical center and we will cover care if we pre-authorize you for a non-VA hospital or care setting.
Will I need to choose which benefits to use?
Yes, you will need to choose which benefits to use based on your situation.
What if I do not want to keep my private insurance?
You would be without health coverage at that time.
Could I save money if I drop my private health insurance?
You would absolutely save money; but there are risks involved when dropping your private health insurance.
