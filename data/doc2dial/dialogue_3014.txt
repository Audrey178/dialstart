exit counseling info
================
Did you want to know what Exit Counseling is?
yes
Exit counseling provides important information to prepare you to repay your federal student loan
Have you received a subsidized, unsubsidized or PLUS loan under the Direct Loan Program or the FFEL Program?
no, just wanted to be prepared
================
what will I need to have to do this counseling?
You will need a verified FSA ID
================
who needs to complete the exit counseling?
Students who have received a subsidized, unsubsidized or PLUS loan under the Direct Loan Program or the FFEL Program, must complete exit counseling each time they drop below half-time enrollment, graduate, or leave school.
================
will I need this if I drop out without finishing or I transfer?
 Yes, you must complete exit counseling each time you leave school.
