Can you order a photo or professional plates online?
You must have a valid New York State vehicle registration in the passenger, commercial, or motorcycle registration class that does not expire within 60 days.   The mailing address in your vehicle registration record must be your current mailing address.   Your vehicle registration record must match your current name if your name has changed.
What if I want the plates for the disabled?
If you would like to purchase Plaques for People with Disabilities that have the Universal Access Symbol, Chase Plates for People with Disabilities that have the Universal Access Symbol, you should contact the Custom License Plate Unit.
