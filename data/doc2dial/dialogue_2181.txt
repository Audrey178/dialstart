How to know that my PIRP course completion shows on my driving record?
================
Request a copy of your driving license to verify that.
Is there any other way to verify my course completion on the DMV record?
================
Does point reduction protects me from future violations or points?
No. It does not. It is only for violations occurred eighteen months before course completion and does not affect earlier violations points.
Can point reduction affect a scheduled hearing?
It will not affect a scheduled hearing.
================
How will the DMV knows about my PIRP course?
After you complete the course, the course sponsor will notify the DMV within 10 weeks. 4 active points will then be deducted from your driving record.
================
Can the point reduction help if my license has already been revoked or suspended?
No. It will not affect that.
How about Driver Responsibility Assessment? Could it affect that?
No. It can not prevent or reduce the calculation points for that.
