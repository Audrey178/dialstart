Am I able to get benefits if I was a volunteer in the research for chemical and biological testing?
================
You were one of the 60,000 Veterans who volunteered for research for the U.S.?
Yes, I was one of 60,000 Veterans.
Then it was the Biological and Chemical Programs.
What needs to happen for me to get benefits as a volunteer in the chemical and biological programs?
Did you volunteer for research during 1942 through 1972?
Yes, I was a volunteer between 1942 and 1972.
Do you have an injury or disease from the participation in the testing?
No, I do not have an injury or disease caused from the testing programs.
It looks like you are not eligible for medical care through the U.S. Army because you do not meet the requirements.
So what happens, now that I was part of the research?
You can only get medical care through the U.S. Army if you were a volunteer during 1942 and 1975 and have a disease because of it.
