I am looking to get a card for my child as a foreign born US citizen. What documents do you need?
================
Make sure all documents are current. You will need a consular report of birth abroad.
If your child does not have a foreign birth certificate, you can submit your child's passport. We need proof of age.
What if I do not have a foreign birth certificate?
You should submit your child's passport or any other DHS form to prove your child's age.
Can I use an old passport or school records?
Yes, either of those will work.
Are there any other documents I can use?
You can use your child's passport or a DHS form to prove your child's age.
What if I cannot find that document?
You can use a state ID card (not a driver's license).
================
Is this how I get a social security card for my child born out of the US?
Yes. Make sure you submit the required documents.
