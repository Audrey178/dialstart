I don't know what an ignition interlock device is.
================
It measures the alcohol level in your breath and keeps the car from starting if the levels are too high.
Was it ordered by the court?
No, not a court ordered device.
================
How do I get the interlock restriction off of my license?
Your monitor can fill out a form saying you don't have to have it anymore.  Take the form to the DMV.
================
What is Leandra's Law?
It created a new felony for driving while under the influence with children in the vehicle.
What happens if someone is convicted?
Probation and a minimum of 12 months using an ignition interlock device.
================
Why is it called Leandra's Law?
It's named after a girl who died while riding with the intoxicated mother of her friend.
================
What does the law say?
No one can operate a motor vehicle while under the influence of alcohol or drugs while a child 15 or under is present.
================
How do you get an ignition interlock device installed?
The court will give you a list of vendors who install them.
