How do I change some information on my VA direct deposit please?
================
You'll need to create a Premium DS Logon account and sign in to eBenefits.
I can't do it online, is there another way?
Can I change the type of account I have to Premium?
Yes just go to eBenefits to upgrade your DS Logon account to Premium.
================
What if I've had a recent setback and my condition is worse, can I prolong my benefits?
In the event that your disability gets worse, you can file a claim for an increase in VA benefits.
================
So if I can't do this online, what other ways are there to update my information?
Besides the online route, you can change your information by mail or phone.
================
What form do I send by mail?
Fill out a VA Direct Deposit Enrollment VA Form 24 - 0296.
================
I'm moving soon and would like to eventually change my address, is that possible?
Yes it is very important to update your contact information so we send information about your benefits and services as well as any prescriptions, lab test results, or appointment reminders you may need to the right location.
