Hello, I want to know about county use taxes and supplemental fees.
================
these are taxes that all commercial vehicles must pay as residents of New York City and other counties in New York. This is a vehicle use tax that the DMV collects for the city and the counties.

You can ask the city or county in which you reside or have your business.
Does this tax apply for the renewable of commercial vehicles?
Yes, the vehicle use tax applies for commercial vehicles and the renewal of commercial vehicle registration.
In the county I currently reside, I'm exempt from the vehicle use tax. What should I do?
You have to complete the form UT-11C

What are the fees for vehicles  from Manhattan
For all passenger vehicle registrations and renewals, the fees are 80$ for 2 years of Vehicle use tax and 400$ for 1 year for livery and for-hire vehicles.

for that case, get the form UT-11 from a local DMV office or a DMV call center.
