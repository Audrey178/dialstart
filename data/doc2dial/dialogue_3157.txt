If I want to continue receiving federal student aid, what should I be sure of?
You must continue to meet the basic eligibility criteria, make satisfactory academic progress, and complete the FAFSA every year.
================
What are the basic eligibility criteria?
The basic eligibility criteria that allow you to get federal student aid continue to apply the entire time you receive aid, not just when you first fill out the FAFSA and receive aid.
