I'm from New Jersey and need information about obtaining a restricted use license in New York.
================
Do you meet the requirements to receive a restricted driving privilege in NY?
Yes, I do.
Yes, you can obtain a restricted driving privilege to drive in NY. The restrictions on your license will be the same as those for a driver with a NY driver's license.
================
What exactly is a restricted license?
Restricted use licenses are issued for drivers who qualify and have had their license suspended or revoked for violations not related to alcohol or drugs.
================
How is a conditional license different than a restricted use license?
Conditional licenses are issued to qualified drivers who have had their license suspended or revoked for alcohol or drug related violations.
================
Can I apply for a restricted license online?
No, you will need to apply at a DMV office.
IIs this available at all DMV offices?
No. Not all offices, but many provide this service. You can contact your local DMV office to see where to apply.
================
Are there any requirements for getting a conditional license?
Yes, those seeking to obtain a conditional license need to complete an Impaired Driver Program approved by the DMV.
