and once I pay, will my refund be restored?
Your payment does not guarantee that the DMV will restore your registration or close any other suspensions or revocations you have. Follow the instructions in your suspension order. Send the bottom of the order along with a certified check, personal check, or money order made payable to the Motor Vehicle Commissioner for the fine
