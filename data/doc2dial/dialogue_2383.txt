I need help with driving school instructor certificate matters
================
Have you passed all tests?
yes , so what happens next
The Bureau of Driver Training Programs will issue a Driving School Instructor Certificate and mail it to the driving school where you are employed.
================
does everyone have to pass those same tests?
Yes, you must pass the vision test, road sign test, written test, and the driving school instructor driving test
================
if I want to get permission to work at another school do I still need my driving record for my New Jersey license?
Yes, if your driver license was issued from a state other than New York, you must include a certified copy of your driving record from the state agency that issued your license.
do I need to have my certificate with me when I work?
It should be shown if a customer or DMV employee wants to see the certificate.
================
would I have needed to pay again if I had failed a test?
Once you submit an application you are allowed to take each of the Road Sign Test and Written Test twice, and the Behind-The-Wheel Instructor’s Driving Test once.  If you do not pass these tests, you will have to submit a new application and pay another $10 fee.
