Can my husband use my Social Security account?
================
No, sorry. Only you are allowed to create and use your mySocial Security account.
Would you like more information about this security measure?
No, thank you.
================
What happens if I have a freeze on my account?
Once you request a temporary lift, your ISP will automatically reinstate it after the temporary lift expires.
Can I still open an account with a freeze on my credit report?
Yes, you can still open an account by doing a temporary lift. You will need to contact your identity services provider and request them to temporary lift the freeze.
So, as long as I request a temporary lift, my identity services provider will reinstate it after it expires?
Yes, that is correct.
================
What will I be asked to do to create an account?
First, you will be asked to provide personal information and answer security questions to prove your identity. After you prove your identity, you will then create a username and password for your account.
