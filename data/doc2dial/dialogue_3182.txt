What do I have to do to receive the Direct PLUS Loan?
Before you can receive a Direct PLUS Loan, you must complete a Direct PLUS promissory note Master Promissory Direct PLUS Loan MPN, which explains all the terms and conditions of Direct PLUS loans and constitutes your legally binding agreement to pay all Direct PLUS loans that receive under the Direct PLUS MPN loan.
and when can I complete the Direct PLUS Loan MPN?
You will have the opportunity to complete the Direct PLUS Loan MPN after completing the Direct PLUS Loan Application.
and can the funds be used for other things?
The Direct PLUS Loan Application allows you to authorize the school to use your loan funds to satisfy other charges related to education after tuition and fees have been paid, and room and board.
