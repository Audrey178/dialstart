Hi, what happens if I fail the course? Can I get a refund of the course fee?
================
Do you meet the minimum test score requirements?
Yes
If you do not meet all the requirements to complete the course or do not meet the minimum test score requirements, you cannot receive credit for completing the course and you cannot receive points and insurance reduction for the course.
================
And what else is there besides the knowledge tests about the course content?
In addition to the knowledge tests about the course content, there will be questions to help verify your identity and participation in the course.
================
and the sponsor can't repeat the course for me?
A sponsor may offer you to retake the same course at no additional cost. The sponsor is not obligated to offer this.
================
Do I receive points and insurance reduction if I take an I-PIRP course?
Yes
================
What is the I-PIRP / ADM program?
I - PIRP / ADM is an acronym for Internet Point and Insurance Reduction Program / Alternative Delivery Method. This program allows approved sponsors to provide defensive driving / accident prevention courses over the Internet or other alternative delivery methods, such as DVD or digital cable.
