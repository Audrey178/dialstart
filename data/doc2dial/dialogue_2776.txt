What does PIRP do for me, if I'm the principal operator of a motor vehicle?
In that case you will receive a minimum 10 % reduction in the base rate of your automobile and motorcycle liability and collision insurance premiums each year for three years.
================
Regarding the point system, what if I accumulate more points than allowed in a certain time?
Then you will be called to a DMV hearing, after which your license may be suspended or revoked. You will be offered the option of waiving the hearing or accepting a definite period of suspension.
