I want to know about the pre-licensing course.
================
This is a DMV-approved and standardized classroom course that teaches you all you need to know as a new driver, such as: driving within the highway transportation system.
================
What should I do in case I encounter stop lines or crosswalks?
You should stop before crossing them.
What should I take into consideration for my road test?
Remember to steer smoothly in all circumstances, accelerate in a controlled and smooth way and don't force the engine or cause it to stall.
================
Is there anything I need to do before I take my road test?
Once you get your learner permit you should always obey the permit restrictions on where and when you may drive. Then you have to complete a 5-hour pre-licensing course.
================
What should I do in case I lost my certificate?
You have to contact the school that issued the certificate to obtain another one.
