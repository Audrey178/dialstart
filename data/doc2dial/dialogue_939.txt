Are you the spouse, surviving spouse, dependent child, or family caregiver of a Veteran?
None of those apply to me.
================
I am a biological child of a woman Vietnam War Veteran and have birth defects, can I get Health care benefits?
Then you may qualify for VA health care benefits which help pay for services needed to treat your covered birth defect and related medical conditions.
================
What if I am a current or surviving spouse or child of a Veteran with disabilities or a service member who died in the line of duty?
You may be able to get health insurance through Civilian Health and Medical Program of the Department of Veterans Affairs or CHAMPVA; which will cover the cost of some of the health care services and supplies.
Will it cover all the costs of the health care services and supplies I need?
it will cover some of the costs of the health care services and supplies.
================
What is the Camp Lejeune Family Member Program?
If you lived at U.S. marine Corps Base Camp LeJeune in North Carolina you may have had contact with contaminants in the drinking water and could cause diseases which is covered under this program.
