Are direct unsubsidized loans available to undergraduate students?
Yes, Direct Unsubsidized Loans are available to undergraduate and graduate students; there is no requirement to demonstrate financial need.
================
Do I need to sign a loan contract for my first direct loan?
Yes, you'll need to sign a loan contract called a Master Promissory Note , agreeing to the terms of the loan.
