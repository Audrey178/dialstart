Yes an Enhanced driver license and valid U.S. Passport are already REAL ID compliant and can be used to board a flight.

Isn't a passport good enough to board a plane?
Yes it can be used for identification, boarding a plane for domestic and international flights, and also for entry into a federal building or military base.
================
So it's interchangeable with this Enhanced driver license?
Yes they are both REAL ID compliant and can be used to board a flight.
================
Do my kids need identification when they are travelling with me?
The TSA does not currently require children under 18 years old to provide identification when traveling with a companion within the United States. The companion will need acceptable identification though.
