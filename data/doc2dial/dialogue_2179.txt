How do I know my driving record shows my PIRP course completion?
================
Do you need to verify that your course completion is on your DMV record?
Yes
You can request a copy of your driving record
================
Will my point reduction effect my record?
If your license has already been revoked or suspended the point reduction will not affect that action
================
What is a points reduction?
" Point reduction " means the number of points on your driving record used to calculate the number of total points received within 18 months will be reduced by 4 points and may help you avoid a suspension
================
How long will it take for the DMV to be notified?
The PIRP course sponsor will notify the DMV within 10 weeks after you complete the course. DMV will then reduce the active points on your driving record by up to 4 points
================
Will this course affect my points on the driver responsibility assessment?
Completion of a course under the Point & Insurance Reduction Program also can not prevent or reduce the calculation of points affecting a Driver Responsibility Assessment
How low can point reduction go?
Point reduction can not reduce your point total lower than zero
