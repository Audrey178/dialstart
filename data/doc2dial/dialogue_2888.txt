What is the Discretionary Income Rate?
Discretionary Income Rates of 20% or less are considered passing rates, rates higher than 20%, but less than or equal to 30% are called zone rates, and rates greater than 30% are failing rates.

What is a D/E rate?
A D/E rate is based on the typical loan debt and earnings of a cohort of the program's former students who completed that program.
