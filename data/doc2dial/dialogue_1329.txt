I'm here for answers on the GI Bill and its usage
Okay, we can help you find out if you can use GI Bill benefits for college as a Veteran, service member, or qualified dependent. You can learn about payment rates for undergraduate and graduate degree courses at institutions of higher learning.
================
I want to know if I can use the GI Bill for two degrees?
Yes, you can
Have you gotten your first degree?
yes
You can use any GI Bill benefits you have left over to pay for classes for another degree
================
could I have gone to another school at the same time I was working on my first degree?
You could have as long as the classes at both schools count toward your degree, and the school from where you are getting your degree considers the classes at the second school as required in order to get your degree
================
how do you decide how much I will get?
We decide payment based on the number of classes you go to and the hours spent in those classes
================
Does the Post 9-11 GI Bill pay the same way?
The Post-9/11 GI Bill pays tuition and fees directly to the school, and the student receives money for books at the beginning of the term.
