I have 6 months with my Pre-licensing Course Certificate, how much longer is it valid?
================
It is valid for 1 year since the certificate is issued, while a Student Certificate of Completion is valid for 2 years.
================
What should I know about speed and following distance when practicing for my road test?
Always respect the posted speed limit and adjust your speed taking into account factors such as weather, road, visibility and traffic conditions. Maintain adequate "cushion" space between your vehicle and the ones you are following. Always keep in mind the "two second rule" described in the Driver's Manual.
Is it okay to cross a crosswalk during my road test?
No, you must stop before crossing the crosswalk.
================
Can I take my road test without having supervised practice driving?
Can I take my road test if I am under 18?
You must have at least 50 hours of supervised practice driving before you take your road test.
================
How many hours of supervised practice driving do I need if I have a Class DJ or MJ learner permit?
A minimum of 50 hours that includes at least 15 hours of driving after sunset before you take a road test, and you must bring to the road test a completed Certification of Supervised Driving MV-262 signed by your parent or guardian.
================
What do I need to change the class of my license?
You need just a valid NY State driver license and you apply to amend to another license Class.
