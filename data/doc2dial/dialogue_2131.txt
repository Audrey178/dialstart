I want to know about operating ATVs on private land
================
Do you want to operate your ATV only on private lands?
Yes, that's correct.
First of all, you must have the permission of the land owner or lessee.
Anything else I need to know?
after getting the permission, you should not drive your ATV beyond the boundaries of the property and respect andy restriction or request from the owner. You could lose your permission to ride on private property by littering, causing damage to the property or driving carelessly.
================
What should I do if I have an accident with my ATV?
In case of an accident, you must give your name and address, and the name and address of the ATV owner and its plate number, to any injured person or the owner of any damaged property or the police. You also must show the proof of insurance to the person claiming to have suffered damage or injuries.
What if I can't locate the owner of the damaged property?
In that case, you must provide the information to the police as soon as possible.
Do I have to report any damage to the property immediately, or there is a limit?
In case the estimated damage is at least 600$, you have to report the damage immediately to the police.
================
Ok now, what is the required equipment for an ATV?
two of the required equipment are to have the brakes in good condition and that the ATV is in good operating condition and meets federal standards.
================
can I cross a highway with my ATV?
That is not recommended by the DMV unleast is absolutely necessary. If you have to cross a highway you must do it at an approximate angle of 90 degrees at a place free from obstructions to ensure a safe and quick crossing.
