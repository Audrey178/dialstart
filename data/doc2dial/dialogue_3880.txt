and all vehicles need inspection?
Yes, all vehicles registered in New York State must obtain a safety inspection at a DMV - licensed from the inspection station every 12 months, when vehicle ownership is transferred.
================
What if there is no inspection tag?
If there is no inspection tag, the fine is between $ 50 and $ 100. A mandatory state surcharge of $ 88 $ 93 is added to the fine in city and town courts.
================
And what other inspections should the vehicle have?
Most vehicles must also obtain an emissions inspection or a diesel emissions inspection at the time of the safety inspection....
================
What if I have an inspection of the previous applicant, does it work?
An inspection issued to the previous applicant is invalid.
================
and when do inspections expire?
A valid inspection tag must be displayed on your vehicle. Inspections expire on the last day of the month marked on the inspection label.
