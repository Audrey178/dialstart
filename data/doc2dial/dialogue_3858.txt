Where do I apply for escort driver certification?
================
Most DMV offices offer the test, it will be listed with their Office Hours.
Does your local office offer it?
No, the certification test is not listed for my location.
Can I take the test online?
It isn't offered online yet, but there are several offices close to you.
================
Will there be paperwork I need to fill out to take the test?
Yes, you need to fill out the Vehicle Escort Driver Application.
================
How old do you have to be to apply?
You need to be at least 21.
================
What do I need to bring with me?
You need your driver's license and additional ID.  The DMV Document Guide shows the whole list of acceptablr ID.
