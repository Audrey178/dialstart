I'm looking for information as to what constitutes a co-signer in relation to a spouse's income-driven repayment plan application form within the FSA,  can you help me with this?
A co-signer is defined as being the spouse of an applicant who initiated an Income-Driven Repayment Plan Request
Have you a concern or worry about being a co-signer on an application?
Yes, I'm concerned that I may be obligated to repay the loan by signing this application
What can I do if I do not have a Reference Number /Co-sign Code for the particular application?
You should contact the IDR applicant, who should have received an email containing the Reference Number/Co-sign Code. The IDR applicant can also access this information by logging into StudentAid.gov and clicking on the relevant link
================
What exactly do I need to complete my role as a co-signer within the FSA?
Initially, you will need to be logged in with your own personal FSA ID in order to co-sign the applicant's IDR request
================
How long should it normally take to complete my role as a co-signer?
Your role is normally completed in one session, which most people manage to complete in 10 minutes
================
How does my spouse provide me with a co-sign code during the Income-Driven Repayment Plan Request?
Your spouse needs to click on the "Direct Consolidation Loan Applications" button which is located under "My Loan Documents" on the website.
