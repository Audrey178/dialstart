I would not want to commit a driver Violation Point System, so I would like to know what it is.
This system is for the state of New York to identify high-risk drivers. The DMV assigns points to these drivers each time they commit certain infractions.
Do you have 11 or more points in an 18-month period?
No, I don't have that many points.
================
I'm not sure how many points I have, how are those points calculated?
You must be convicted of the traffic violation before points are added to your driving record. Your point total is calculated based on the date of the violation , not the date of the conviction. The points for violations that all occurred within the last 18 months are added to calculate your point total.
================
Are there any violations that I could commit that are not worthy of points?
Yes... bicycle violations, pedestrian violations and parking violations.
================
What happens if I am in New York and receive more than 6 points in 18 months?
You must pay a Driver Responsibility Assessment fee.
================
A few months ago I was in another country and was convicted of a traffic violation, will I have problems here for that?
No, unless the violation you committed was in Ontario or Quebec.
