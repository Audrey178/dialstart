I have passed my road test, now what?
================
You will receive an interim license that allows you to drive. Keep the interim license with your photo learner permit. You will receive your photo driver license in the mail  in about 2 weeks.
What should I do if I am 17 years old and I have a DJ Class driver's license?
You must obey the restrictions for junior drivers. Please, see "Graduated Driver License GDL Law and restrictions on drivers under age 18" for more information.

How can I get one a Pre-Licensing Course Certificate MV-278?
First of all, see "Prepare for your road test". Then the fee for up to two road tests for a non-commercial license is included in your $10 driver license application fee. The earliest date is within three to five weeks.
My temporary learner permit is without a photo... is this a problem?
 You must show a NY State photo driver license or a NY State non-driver ID Card, Photo documents from other states are not acceptable. The vehicle must have functional seat belts, headlights, windshield wipers and other equipment, a front passenger area and a seat belt free of debris, clutter or debris that may stain or damage the license examiner's uniform, and a driver's door front passenger that can be opened from inside the vehicle.
