Is it possible to access accident reports I already purchased online?
================
Yes you can access accidents reports you all ready purchased.
Did you purchase accident reports online in the last 7 days?
Yes, I did I purchased them 3 days ago.
You can access the reports again and you need to enter the report confirmation number displayed during the report purchase transaction and emailed to you in the transaction confirmation and enter the search confirmation number.
================
What topics can I look under online?
You would be able to look under the topic DMV Records online.
================
What if I want my accident report by mail?
You must pay a required search fee of $10 plus an additional fee of $15 for each accident report you order my mail; but the cost is less if you order it online.
================
How do I order a copy of a motorist accident report in NY State?
You would have to be named on the accident report or represent a person named on the report or be a party to a civil action that arose from the accident.
================
What happens if I search by county and date but do not select any reports?
You would not be charged a fee if you did not select any reports.
