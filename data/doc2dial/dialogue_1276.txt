I need help getting VA health care for PTSD
================
We can help, the VA health care program covers PTSD health services .
Would you like more help accessing these services?
Yes, please
Okay, to use these services you must first apply for VA health care. We can help you apply for health care
================
what if I need help right at this moment?
You can talk with our Veterans Crisis Line responders for private free help.  Many of them are Veterans and are available 24/7.
================
can I also get disability benefits for my PTSD?
Yes, if you have PTSD linked to your military service you may be able to get disability benefits. We can help you find out if you can get disability benefits.
================
I don't have health benefits, but I was in combat when in the military. so what should I do for help?
You can get free counseling and support at one of our 300 community Vet Centers.  We can help you locate a Vet Center near you.
================
I haven't used the PTSD help before so where do I start?
You can contact a VA PTSD program near you or get in touch with your local VA medical center.  You can also call our general information hotline at 800-827-1000
