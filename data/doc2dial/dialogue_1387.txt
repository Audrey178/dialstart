I requested a decision review and I need to know what to expect now.
================
If you have requested a decision review, you don't need to do anything until you receive a letter from the VA asking you for any information that they might need.
Please be aware that the VA might schedule more exams for you.
They haven't asked me to have any more exams yet.
================
When will I find out the results of my request?
Once the VA processes your request, a reviewer will call you within 2-4 weeks.
Is there anyone I can talk to so I can tell them why they should review my decision and change it?
You and/or your representative can call your reviewer on the phone and talk to them about the review.
================
This all seems like a lot to figure out. Is there anyone I can talk to for more help?
You can speak to an attorney or agent who is accredited by the VA or Veterans Service Organization to get more help.
================
I have more questions, where can I find more information?
Go to our FAQs page to read more questions and answers about decision reviews.
