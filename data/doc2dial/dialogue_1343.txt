Is it possible that they can give me a 100% disability if my condition worsens?
Yes For Example: A veteran had a left knee injury that resulted in a disability rating of 30%. As the knee problem worsened, the veteran VA doctor recommended surgery to prevent further damage from occurring. Since the doctor would not know if the surgery was successful until 3 months after the 1 week stay in the Veteran's hospital, we gave the Veteran a temporary disability rating of 100% for 3 months.
