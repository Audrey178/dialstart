I need help with replacement card for a noncitizen adult.
You can replace your Social Security card for free if it is lost or stolen; but you may also not need to replace your Social Security card.
Do you not have permission to work but need a Social Security number for other purposes?
No I do not need the Social Security number for other purposes.
================
Do I need to have by Form I-20 with employment page completed and signed?
Yes you need to have your Form I-20 with employment page completed and signed by your designated school official.
What if I need a number to meet my state or local requirements?
You will need to bring us a letter from the government agency and it must be on letterhead stationery but not on form letters or cannot be photocopied.
What do I need to do if I am an F-1 or M-1 student?
You must show us your I-20 Certificate of Eligibility for Nonimmigrant Student Status.
What happens if I am a J-1 student, student intern or international visitor?
You must provide a letter from your sponsor and the letter must be on letterhead with an original signature that authorizes your employment.
