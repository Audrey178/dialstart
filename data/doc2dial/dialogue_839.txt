Hello. I would like to file an appeal to a veterans law judge and need some information. Can you help me?
================
Of course. If you need help with filing an appeal, you can download VA Form 10182: Board Appeal PDF.
================
Would you like to know if you qualify for a board appeal?
Yes, that'd be really nice.
You can request a board appeal after an initial claim, supplemental claim, or higher-level review decision. You can't request 2 board appeals in a row.
================
Alright, I get that. Who is going to review my appeal once I filed it?
A judge at the Board of Veterans Appeals in Washington, D.C., will review your case.
================
I see. And what if I want them to review a decision? What is the time limit to request a review?
You have 1 year from the date on your decision to request a decision review, unless you have a fiduciary claim or a multiple party claim.
================
Thanks, that was helpful. So if I disagree with a decision of the board, I can contest it, right?
Yes, if you have new and relevant evidence that supports your case, you can file a Supplemental Claim. You can also appeal to the U.S. Court of Appeals for Veterans Claims.
================
What do I need to consider if I want to present new evidence?
For more information on this, you can download VA Form 20 - 0995: Supplemental Claim PDF.
================
Will do. One more question, if VA schedules an exam for me, do I need to attend it?
Yes, please be sure not to miss it.
