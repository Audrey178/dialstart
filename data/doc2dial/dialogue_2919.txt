What about scholarships?
Unlike financial aid, scholarships provide free money for college expenses. Scholarships are usually given to students based on their academic merits.
================
How do student loans work?
When a person takes out a student loan, they are borrowing money for college that will be need to be paid back later. Like other loans, student loans accrue interest over time.
