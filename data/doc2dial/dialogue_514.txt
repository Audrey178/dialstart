================
Do you want to know about benefits based on year of birth?
Yes, I would like to know about Benefits Planning for Retirement.
Full retirement age is the age at which a person may first be eligible for full or unreduced retirement benefits.
================
What happens if I delay my benefits until after full retirement age?
You may be eligible for delayed retirement credits that would increase your monthly benefit.  There are also other things you should consider to make the decision.
================
What will happen if I retire early?
You can retire early at between age 62 and full retirement age.
Would you like to know what takes place if you take benefits early?
No, I am not going to start benefits early.
What age does early retirement start?
Early retirement starts between age 62 and full retirement age.
Are there any charts to help me figure out how much retirement benefits I would receive if i take them early?
Yes, there is a chart that lists age 62 reduction amounts and includes examples based on estimated monthly benefits at $1000 at full retirement age.  Just select your year of birth to find out how much your benefit will be reduced if you took them early.
