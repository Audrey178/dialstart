and, what about those that can't get enough credits to qualify for Social Security disability benefits?
In case people don't have enough credits for the disability benefits, they could qualify for the Supplemental Security Income (SSI) if they have limited income and resources.
How can I appeal a decision if my application gets rejected?
If your application is denied you have the right to ask us to review your application again and in the notice, you receive from us, that says that you don't qualify, you'll find instructions on how to appeal.

What personal information do I have to give to apply for the benefits?
You'll be asked for your social security number and proof of age, the name, address and phone numbers of any professional medic or medical center that took care of your case, the names and dosages of any medication you are currently taking, etc.
