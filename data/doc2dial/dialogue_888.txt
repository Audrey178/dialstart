Where can I access my military records?
You can submit a military records request through the milConnect website to get your DD214 or other military service records.
================
What do I need to do?
Do you have a pre-existing account there?
No I don't, can you guide me through the process?
It's quite easy. First you have to set up a Premium DS Logon account. To do so go to milConnect to register.
================
If I'm trying to access my uncle's records who was discharged in 1982, what conditions apply?
If you are not their next of kin, you may only be able to request limited information from their Military Personnel File.
================
I'm trying to access my uncle's record so that he can have a military burial, where do I call?
Call our National Cemetery Scheduling Office at 800-535-1117. They will be able to help you get access to his DD214 or other discharge documents.
================
What other types of records can be accessed on milConnect?
You can access documents from your Official Military Personnel File to view and download. Connect today to find out.
