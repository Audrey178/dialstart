Hi, what if I am not currently receiving aid?
================
If you do not get the benefits, you can apply for such a plan.
What are the steps to apply?
It is important that you inform the Social Security representative.
================
What level of difficulty does it present when registering on the card?
It is fast and easy, you can also call 1 - 800 - 333 - 1795 to facilitate the process.
================
Do I have to have an account?
It is important that you carry your checkbook or a copy of your bank statement.
================
I wonder if I have to have an account before I can register?
Of course, in addition to being able to register for Direct Deposit.
What benefits does the Direct Express card have?
Mainly it has a goal that you can use to access your benefits.
