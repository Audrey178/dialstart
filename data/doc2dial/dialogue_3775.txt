How does the Work study for Veterans work?
Our Veteran work -study program can help you earn money while enrolled in a college, a vocational school, or professional training program.
================
Do you know how much will l earn?
They may pay you the difference between the amount we pay and their usual rate.
================
What kind of a job can I get?
You can get a job that is related to VA work, meets your interests, and fills a need.  As an example you could work for a VA facility.
================
Do you know when and how much can I work?
You are able to work work during or between enrollment periods.
Are you enrolled for a semester that lasts for 15 weeks?
if you are enrolled for at least 15 weeks then you can work up to 375 hours for that enrollment period.
================
What must be true for me to take part in the Veterans work-study?
Are you enrolled at least for three-quarter time in a college degree?
No, I am not enrolled at least three-quarter time in a college degree right now.
Then you cannot take part in our work-study program.
