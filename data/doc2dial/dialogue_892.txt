Am I able to find out what happens after I apply for GI Bill benefits?
================
Yes, you can find out what happens after you apply.
Have we asked you for any documents?
Yes, you have asked for documents.
You will need to upload the documents through the GI Bill website.
================
How do I apply through the mail after I filled the application out?
As long as you have filled the application for GI Bill benefits out you can then mail it to the VA regional claims processing office that is in the same location as your school.
Is there a list of regional claims processing offices for me to look at?
Yes, you can see the list of regional claims processing offices.
================
Can you tell me what happens after I apply?
Actually yes, you cannot make changes but if you have questions about VA education benefits, please call 888-GI-Bill or 1-888-442-4551, Monday thru Friday, 8 a.m. to 7 p.m. ET
================
Is there anything I need to prepare before completing my application?
First of all, you will need to find out if you are eligible for VA education benefits, gather all your documents and info, and see what benefits you will get at the school you will attend.
