Do you need to find out who can use the Retirement Estimator?
Yes please
You are able to use the Retirement Estimator if you have enough Social Security credits built up at this time to qualify for benefits
What happens if I am only in receipt of Medicare benefits at this time?
You are still able to get an estimate from the Retirement Estimator
================
Are there any time restraints, for security reasons, that I can stay on any page of the Retirement Estimator for?
Each page of the Estimator has a time limit. If you are inactive on any page for 25 minutes, you will receive a warning, but you can at this stage extend your time on the page
================
What do I do if I cannot use the Retirement Estimator or if I'm looking for a survivors or disability benefit estimate?
In this instance, you can use one of our other calculators
