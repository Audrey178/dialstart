Can I restore a licence after revocation by mail?
================
Yes, but It can take up to 12 weeks for DIU to process a request received by mail
What if I request approval by mail?
Yes, I want it
In that case, you must wait until 30 days before the revocation period ends to send your request
What if I need to request approval to apply for a new license?
In that case, you need to complete an Application for Permit, Driver License or Non-Driver ID Card MV-44, and please, leave the I am applying for a boxes blank
================
What must I do if  the conditions of my probation do not include driving restriction?
You have to provide a copy of the conditions. Please, mail or fax this information to DIU.
What happens if I owe any DMV fees suspension termination fee?
In that case, they have to be paid before applying for a license or the processing of your application will be delayed
================
What do I need to apply for a new license after revocation?
You have to submit a completed Application for Permit, Driver License or Non-Driver ID Card MV-44, a non-refundable $100 fee and any pertinent items below with your application.
