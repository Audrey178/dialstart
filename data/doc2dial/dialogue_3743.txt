What does service-disable veterans life insurance provide?
It provides low cost coverage to eligible service members.
If I have a service-related disability what is the form called that I have to apply for?
You would be applying on an S-DVI form either online or by mail.
================
If I have a severe service-connected disability, would I be eligible for VMLI?
Yes, if we conclude that your disability was caused or made worse by your service, you may qualify for VMLI.
Who will notify me if I qualify for VMLI?
Your Loan Guaranty Agent will let you know if you have been accepted.
================
What requirements must be met to receive S-DVI?
Were you honorably discharged from active duty on or after April 25th, 1951?
No, I was discharged in March of 1951.
Then you would not be eligible to receive S-DVI.
================
Is there any way to waive the premium fees?
Yes, some basic S-DVI policies provides a waiver of premiums for veterans who are totally disabled.
================
Do I have to apply for S-DVI online? I don't have a computer.
No, you can also apply by mail.
