What does the Direct PLUS Loan Request allow me to do?
The Direct PLUS Loan Request allows you to authorize the school to use your loan funds to for other educationally related charges after all the tuition and fees and room and board have been paid.
What is the William D. Ford Federal Direct Loan Program request for supplemental information used for?
It is a request for supplemental info connected to your application for Federal Direct PLUS Loan Direct PLUS Loan through the William D. Ford Federal Direct Loan Program.
What must I do before I receive my Direct PLUS Loan?
Actually, you have to complete a Direct PLUS Loan Master Promissory Note that explains the terms and conditions of Direct PLUS Loans and is your legally binding agreement to repay all your loans.
