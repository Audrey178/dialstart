What can I learn about my student loan servicer?
You can learn about the student loan servicers and what role they play in the repayment process.
================
What will happen if my loan is transferred to another loan servicer?
You should receive an email or letter from your assigned servicer to tell you about the transfer.
================
What if I have FFEL Program loans that are not owned by ED?
Then you would need to contact your servicer for details about repayment options and tools.
================
What if my federal student loan was transferred from one servicer to a different one?
The transfer just means that a new servicer will provide the support you need to repay your loans; but is still owned by ED.
================
