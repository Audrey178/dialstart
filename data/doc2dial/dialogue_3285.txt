Should I consider applying for as many scholarships as I want because it is free money for college?
Yes, start researching early and meet the deadlines and you could have scholarship success.
================
If I have any more questions you can't answer who do I talk to?
You should ask your financial aid office those questions.
================
Are there different ways to find scholarships?
Yes there are different ways to find scholarships such as contacting the financial aid office at the school you want to attend and also checking info at the library or online.
================
Will my scholarships affect my other student aid?
Yes, a scholarship would affect your other student aid because you student aid added together with scholarships should not be more than the cost of attendance.
================
How would my scholarship money get to me?
It actually relies on the scholarship and sometimes the money goes directly to your college and distributed to tuition and fees and then leftover funds given to you or it could be sent to you in a check.
Has your scholarship provider not tell you what to expect?
Yes, they have told me what to expect and did not not tell me.
