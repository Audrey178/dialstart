Hello, I would like to know what kind of equipment and technology do I need to take an I-PIRP course?
================
The answer depends on the alternative ADM delivery method of the course. For example, if the course is an Internet course, you must have a computer and an acceptable Internet connection.
if the course is an internet course?
You must have a computer and an acceptable Internet connection.
================
Are I-PIRP websites safe?
Yes The DMV requires sponsors and their delivery agents to provide secure websites that protect the personal information of those taking the courses.
================
What is done to ensure that the person enrolling in the course is the person taking the course?
Each course sponsor has different methods to validate that the person who registered for the course is the same person who takes the course. Anyone who attempts to defraud the system or who accepts a certificate of completion of the course and its associated benefits without having personally completed the entire course has committed a crime and may be subject to prosecution, fines, and imprisonment.

The course sponsor must issue the course completion certificates within 14 business days after the course ends. Some sponsors offer express delivery options at an additional cost.
