I have some questions about what it means to be a co-signer.
I can help you with that. A co-signer will be the spouse of the applicant who initiated an Income-Driven Repayment Plan request.
Were you aware that the co-signer isn't obligated to repay the loan?
No, I didn't know that.
================
How long does it usually take to go through the co-sign process?
The majority of people complete the process in just 10 minutes.
================
Who can complete the co-sign process?
The spouse of the applicant.
================
It is saying that I need a co-sign code, but I don't think I have one. What should I do?
If you are the co-signer, you should contact the IDR applicant for the code. If you are the applicant, you should have received an email containing the code.
I can't find the code and my IDR is part of a Direct Consolidation Loan Application. Where can I find the co-sign code?
Once you log into your account, you will need to click on "Direct Consolidation Loan Applications" under My Loan Documents to locate the code.
