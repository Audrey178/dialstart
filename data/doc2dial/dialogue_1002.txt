I think I need to see someone who can counsel me on my next career move.
You might be eligible to get free educational and career counseling as a recently discharged Veteran or a dependent family member.
================
I tried but didn't qualify for the Post-9/11 GI Bill, are there other options out there for me?
You might be able to get education benefits through other VA programs.
================
I don't have anywhere to live now, can I get help with that?
Yes, we can help you connect with resources in your community, like homeless shelters or faith - based organizations.
Where do I call and is it free?
You can talk privately with a trained VA counselor for free at the National Call Center for Homeless Veterans at 877-424-3838. This service is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
