How do I renew?
================
Your application for renewal of a Driving School License must be submitted between 30 and 60 days before the license expires.
Are you in the process of renewing?
No not at this time.
How do I get my expiration date?
It is printed on your license.
What if my school uses vehicles for instruction?
Yes my school uses vehicles for instruction.
You must attach a List of Driving School Vehicles-MV-527
What about pre-licensing?
You must attach a Signature Authorization List-Pre-Licensing Course, MV-278.6
================
How long are licenses renewed for?
Licenses are renewed for two years.
