Is the REAP program still in effect?
The REAP program ended on November 25th, 2015. However, under the National Defense Authorization Act of 2016, some REAP benefits will remain in place for 3 more years.
================
Will this affect me now that the program has ended?
Yes, below are some descriptions that best fit your personal situation and the steps that you can take next.
================
What if I was using REAP benefits before November 24th of 2015?
You will have until November 25th, 2019 to continue using the benefits.
Does that count if I was already attending school on November 24th, 2015?
Yes, you would still apply.
================
Will I receive REAP benefits if I didn't start school until after November 24th, 2015?
No, you are no longer able to receive REAP benefits.
================
When can I credit my REAP eligibility toward my post-9/11 GI bill?
You can do this if you established REAP eligibility before November 25th, 2015, does that apply to you?
No, I established Reap benefits after that date.
You would not be able to credit your REAP eligibility toward your post-9/11 GI Bill then.
