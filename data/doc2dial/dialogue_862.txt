Do you have information on help I can get while I'm in a VA employment program?
================
We have a subsistence allowance which helps you pay for basic needs like food and clothing if you're an eligible Veteran participating in the Voc Rehab program.
================

can you help with GI BIll benefits?
Yes we can help you go over your options for using GI Bill benefits to help pay for school or training , and find out what benefits you can get .

how do I know how much I can get in subsistence allowance?
You can view the current subsistence allowance rates to find out the amount we will provide to help you pay for basic needs like food and clothing. I can give you the link so you can get an idea of what you'll get

We offer many programs and services that may help. We can also help you connect with resources in your community like homeless shelters or faith-based organizations .

and do you have other help if we don't have the GI Bill?
Yes, If you are a Veteran , Reservist , or National Guard Soldier who doesn't qualify for the Post-9/11 GI Bill we can help you find out if you can get education benefits through other VA programs
