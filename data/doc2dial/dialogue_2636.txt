Do you know which vehicles are considered all terrain vehicle or ATV?
Yes, I know which vehicles are ATVs.
That is great that you know ATV is any self-propelled vehicle, including off road motorcycle, that is manufactured for use on off-road trails or in off-road competitions and they are less than 70 inches wide and weigh less than 1000 pounds.

================
What do I need to show the DMV when registering my ATV?
You would need to complete the Document Guide to make sure you bring the correct documents with you to the DMV.
================
Do I need to register my ATV with the DMV?
Yes, you need a valid NY State registration to ride your ATV in NY State unless you are going to use it for snow removal or agricultural puproses on your own property.
What if I purchase my ATV from a NY State ATV dealer?
Your dealer is required to register it before it is delivered to you.
================
What if the ATV is transferred by a private person?
You would still need acceptable proof of ownership which is a NY State or out of state transferable registration or title certificate signed by the previous owner to transfer to you.
