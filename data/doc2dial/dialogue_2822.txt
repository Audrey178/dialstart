What loan types are eligible for Deferment?
================
Deferments are available for Direct Loan, FFEL Program Loan, and Perkins Loans.
Do you have a Perkins Loan?
No I have not received a Perkins Loan.
What will happen if I stop paying and my deferment is not approved?
Your loan will be delinquent and you could go into default.
================
What if I am in the military and want a deferment?
You will have to complete the Military Service and Post-Active Duty Student Deferment Request.
================
What if I have an economic hardship?
You will need to complete the Economic Hardship Deferment Request.
================
Could income-driven plans provide loan forgiveness?
Yes, Income-Driven plans can provide loan forgiveness if the loan is not paid in full after 20 or 25 years.
