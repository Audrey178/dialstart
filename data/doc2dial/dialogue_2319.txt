What if I had a change name?
You would need to read the instructions to change your name or other information on your registration.
================
What do I need to bring to the DMV office?
Complete the Application for Duplicate/Renewal Registration and bring it and the items to the DMV with you.
================
What if I want to mail the items can I?
You would need to send in a completed Application for Duplicate/Renewal Registration and a photocopy of your NY State driver license, permit, no-driver ID or other proof of identity.
