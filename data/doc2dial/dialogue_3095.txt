What if I discover that my living expenses are not going to be as high as has been estimated by my school, do I have to accept all the loan that has been given to me?
You are entitled to turn down any loan that is provided or even to request a lower amount of borrowing. Your school will be able to help you with this query, should it arise

Do schools have specific financial plans in place for students that re attending the school?
Yes, a lot of schools have tuition payment plans, these are monthly interest-free payments towards tuition. These plans can help in reducing the amount of borrowing you need. The financial aid office in your school will have more details
================
How do I let my school know what type of financial aid I will be accepting?
Any offer of student aid that you receive will include instructions on how to accpt the aid. You will need to follow these instructions carefully. You may have to enter amounts that you are accepting in an online form and then submit the form
