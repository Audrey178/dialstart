How do I organise a temporary certificate of emissions waiver?
================
Have you an existing diesel emissions inspection certificate that is expired?
No, not at the moment
Where is a temporary certificate of emissions waiver issued and when is it needed?
If your existing diesel emissions inspection certificate expires while outside the DEMA, you may apply to the Department of Motor Vehicles for a temporary certificate. This certificate is only available at the DMV Issuing Offices. This temporary certificate allows the vehicle to be operated when displayed on the vehicle
================
What types of vehicles must undergo a diesel emissions inspection?
All non-exempt vehicles in the NYMA area or DEMA area with an MGW over 8500 pounds must get an annual diesel emissions inspection. This inspection has to occur at th same time as the annual safety inspection.
================
Can vehicles that are due for a diesel emissions inspection be carried out at a non-diesel emissions inspection station?
Non-diesel emissions inspection stations cannot inspect vehicles that are due a diesel emissions inspection.
================
What vehicles are subject to the diesel emissions inspection?
All vehicles from 1996 or older with a GVWR less than 8501 pounds are not subject to the OBDII emissions inspection, low-enhanced emissions inspection or the diesel emissions inspection
================
