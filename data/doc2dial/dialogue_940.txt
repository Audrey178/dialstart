What happens after I apply for health care benefits?
After you have applied for VA health care, we will send you a letter in the mail to let you know if your application has been approved.
I am not a veteran, but am trying to get information for my husband.
================
What happens if he only wants his VA health care for a short amount of time to deal with one issue with one doctor and then does not desire to keep the benefit?
You always have the right to appeal our decision at any time.
Who else can I go to with any questions on my husband's health care benefit?
You can contact your local VA medical center or clinic and ask for the enrollment coordinator or the urgent care clinic.  You can also seek help from the emergency room.
What else will he have the ability to do once he is signed up?
He can manage his VA health and benefits online, refill prescriptions, send secure messages to health care providers and check the status of any disability or pension claims.
