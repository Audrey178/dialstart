What are fiduciary claims?
================
Did you mark you calendar for one year to request a decision review?
No, I did not know I needed to have it filed within a year.
================
How can I file a new fiduciary claim?
The only way to file a new claim is if you have new evidence.
================
How do I get answers to more questions?
You can get answers to frequently asked questions about decision reviews by going to FAQs.
================
Is there a form to download to ask for a new look from a senior reviewer?
Yes, you can download VA Form 20-0996-Higher Level Review pdf.
================
What should I do if the VA schedules an exam for me?
It is very important that you do not miss the exams the VA sets up for you.
================
What can I do if I disagree with the board decision and have new relevant evidence?
Then it is in your best interest to file a Supplemental Claim and also appeal to the U.S. Court of Appeals for Veterans Claims.
