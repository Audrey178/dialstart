I need more info on self-certification
================
Do you need to self-certify?
No
================
Do you want to know the self-certification driving types?
No
================
Do you want to know what happens if your driving type changes?
Yes
If your driving type changes you must go into your local DMV office to change your self - certification driving type and have your license amended to add or delete the appropriate restrictions
What do I do next if my driving type changes?
 You must go into your local DMV office to change your self - certification driving type and have your license amended to add or delete the appropriate restrictions. A transaction fee will apply, if you are self - certifying to a non - excepted driving type NA or NI
================
When will I know if the DMV has received my self-certification?
DMV is entering commercial driver self - certification and medical certification data daily, however due to the volume of responses it could take a period of time for a driver record to be fully updated
================
What happens if my CDL was downgraded because of your " not certified " medical status
You will need to visit a local DMV office to upgrade your license back to a CDL
================
Do I need a medical examiners certificate?
 If you expect to operate in an excepted driving type EA or EI then a Medical Examiner s Certificate is not required. You will need to have the appropriate restrictions added to your driver s license A3 and/or K
