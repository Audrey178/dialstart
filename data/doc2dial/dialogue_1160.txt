How do I know which life insurance program I am eligible for?
The different programs cover VGLI veterans, SGLI service members, and FSGLI family members. You can also get short-term financial coverage through TSGLI to help you recover from a serious traumatic injury.
Will you end your military shift soon?
Yes
If your military duty shift ends soon, you will need to get new coverage quickly. In some cases, you must act within 120 days of leaving the military to make sure there are no breaks in your coverage.
