Please, talk me about my VA care team.
================
Your team's goal is to plan all the care you need to help you stay healthy throughout your life.
Do you want to change to a different primary care doctor?
No, that is not among my plans.
What if I am re-enrolled in VA health care?
In that case, find out how you can receive care from a provider in your local community through community care.
================
Can you talk me about my VA primary care provider and PACT team?
Of course. at VA, we take a team approach to health care with you as the principal figure. Research shows that this type of approach leads to better quality care, more satisfied patients, and fewer hospital visits.
================
What can you do to take care of me??
We can help you get care in the way that works best for you. This may include personal visits with your primary care provider, group clinics, and 24/7 phone support. You can also get educational information online and secure messages with your healthcare team through "My HealtheVet" portal.
Anything else you can tell me about my Va care team?
Your VA care team can provide or organize preventive care, such as flu shots to prevent disease, and screenings to help find diseases like cancer in its early stages, when treatment is most likely to be successful.
