What happens if I have been told I can't get federal student aid any more?
It is possible to get back your eligibility for financial aid.
================
My grades slipped and the financial aid office told me I am not able to get federal student aid, why?
In order to get financial aid you need to have satisfactory academic progession to keep your federal student aid and it is best to talk to your school and see if you can appeal the decision that made you unable to continue getting federal student aid.
================
I was convicted of a drug offense but now want to get more federal student aid, is this possible?
Have you lost eligibility due to conviction of the drug offense?
No, I have not lost eligibility yet.
================
What happens if I was an eligible noncitizen but my status has been expired or revoked?
It isn't bad at all, all you have to do is get the status reinstated that made you an eligible noncitizen or become a permanent resident to receive the federal student aid.
================
What if I happened to be convicted of or pled guilty to a crime involving fraud to receive federal student aid funds?
You would need to repay the funds to the U.S. Department of Education or to the loan holder or servicer.
