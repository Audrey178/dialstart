hi i need help about VA benefits for service members
Are you serving on active duty in the United States Uniformed Services, including the National Guard and Active Reserve with Federal Pay?
Yes
then you may be eligible for VA benefits both during service and after separation or retirement.
================
And what is the Home Aid and Assistance program or subsidy?
Home Aid and Assistance or Subsidy If you need help with your daily activities or are confined to your home, check to see if you are eligible to increase the aid added to your monthly Veterans Pension payments. Open only to active duty veterans
================
What do I need to do To connect with other resources for veterans and transitioning service members?
Visit the Department of Defense website DoD Military OneSource  Visit our mental health site for service members in transition  Read about our commitment to supporting veterans in transition
and what should I do if there is a mental health problem?
Get care now     If you need help with a specific mental health problem or are having trouble sleeping, control your anger, or adjust to civilian life, you are not alone.
