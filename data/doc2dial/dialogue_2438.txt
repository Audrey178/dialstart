I want to get an ID card that has the word VETERAN on it, so what do I have to show to let you know I'm a veteran?
================
We accept a U.S. Department of Defense Form DD-214 that indicates an honorable discharge.  If you don't have one I can tell you what other documents we accept as proof.
================
will I have to keep proving my service everytime I have to do business with the DMV?
After you receive the veteran designation, it will automatically be on all of your future DMV documents when you replace or renew.
