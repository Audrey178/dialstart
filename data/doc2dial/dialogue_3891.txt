What can I do when my license is suspended because I forgot and let my insurance lapse?
================
Is your registration expired?
No, my registration has not expired.
What if I am eligible to pay the optional civil penalty?
Then it would help you to pay the optional civil penalty for the entire length of the lapse.
================
What happens if the registration suspension has been over 90 days?
You will have to surrender your vehicle registration and plates and then the DMV will suspend your driver license.
================
How should I avoid an insurance lapse?
There is a simple rule to go by which is no insurance, no plates; always keep your address current with the DMV; and have liability coverage from a company licensed in NYS.
================
How many days will be drivers license be suspended?
It will be suspended for the same number of days your registration is suspended.
