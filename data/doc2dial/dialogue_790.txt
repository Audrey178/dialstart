Can you tell me about social security credits?
You will have to earn at least 40 social security credits to get benefits for social security.
================
How are many credits are earned each year?
You can earn up to a maximum of four credits per year as you earn and pay social security taxes.

What happens if I do not get enough credits?
We will not be able to give you benefits when you don't make enough credit and we use the amount of credits you earned to determine your eligibility for retirement or disability benefits.
