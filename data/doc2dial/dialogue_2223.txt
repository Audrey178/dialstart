What is the procedure for course cancellation or refunds?
================
Was you sponsor not able to finish the course?
Yes, my sponsor did not finish teaching the course.
They will have to give you the option to receive a full refund of all course fees or reschedule the course.
================
Is there any special equipment or technology I need to take an I-PIRP course?
It depends on the alternate delivery method such as if you need the internet then you would need a computer to finish the course.
What happens if I have an internet course?
You definitely would need to have a computer and an acceptable connection to the internet.
================
Will my automobile insurance company receive the completion certificate from the DMV?
We will send your certificate to you and you must supply the information to your insurance company for reduction benefits.
================
What personal information do I have to provide to take the course?
The sponsors will collect your name and date of birth and it needs to be exactly as it is on your NY State driver license and do not use nicknames.
