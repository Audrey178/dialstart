I am looking for more information and resources pertaining to careers and employment.
================
We are able to help you manage your career in several ways.  VA transition assistance can help as you separate from the military.  You can explore your career options by attending a Transition Assistance Program briefing as the first step.
================
Would you also be looking for information about subsistence allowance rates?
Yes, I would like information on that.
We can discuss that as well as other VA benefits and services such as exploring your options for using GI Bill benefits to help pay for school or training and finding out what benefits you can get.
================
Is there one single point of contact that I could use to gather information on all benefits?
You can reach the VA benefits hotline at 1-800-827-1000.
================
Unfortunately, I am fearing that I may need help to avoid becoming homeless.
We offer many programs and services that may help including free health care and, in some cases, free limited dental care.  We can also help you connect with resources in your community, like homeless shelters or faith-based organizations.
How do I get help if I am at risk of becoming homeless?
We can connect you with resources in your community, like homeless shelters or faith-based organizations.
================
What other benefits might I possibly get?
You can always get updates on benefits by e-mail.
