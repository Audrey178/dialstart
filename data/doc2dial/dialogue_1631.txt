Will my benefits change every year on January 1st?
================
No, not always.
If my school has an approved agreement with you and continues in the program, do they still have to offer Yellow Ribbon Benefits?
Yes, they must continue to offer Yellow Ribbon benefits to you the next year as long as you meet all of the requirements.
================
How much will I have to pay if I already receive financial aid?
We will deduct any financial aid then your school's funding through the Yellow Ribbon Program can cover part or all of the remaining unmet charges.
================
What is the purpose of the Yellow Ribbon Program?
The Yellow Ribbon Program can help you pay for higher out-of-state, private school, or graduate school tuition that the Post-9/11 GI Bill doesn't cover .
================
Can I attend school part-time and still be in the program?
Yes, you do not have to be in school full-time.
================
Do I have to meet certain criteria to remain in the program?
Are you making acceptable progress toward completing your program according to your school?
