can you tell me more of the fully developed claim for a pension
The Fully Developed Claim program is the fastest way to get your VA pension claim processed , and there is no risk to your taking part in it .Just send in all your evidence , or supporting documents , when you file your claim .
what if I don't have all the documents I need?
If that occurs we will remove your claim from the FDC program and process it as a standard claim .
================
How do I start off filing a claim?
Send us all the supporting documents you have and provide information about supporting documents that you don't have. Fill out an Application for Pension, Application for Pension VA Form 21P-527EZ
what else needs to be done?
We will request relevant information and records that you identify and authorize us to get from a federal facility , like a VA medical centers
================
