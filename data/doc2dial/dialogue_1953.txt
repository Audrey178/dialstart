To qualify, you need to be 21 years old or older, have a valid driver license and pass the Escort Driver's Certification Test.

What kind of violation would result in the cancellation of my certificate?
Yes, that is all. You certification can be canceled when you violate safe escort procedures, provide fraudulent information during certification application, subject to fines and penalties, become unable to drive and other things depend on the nature of violation.

No. It will not be valid when that happens.
