What is a TEMPORARY VISITOR
================
A Temporary Visitor is anyone who is not immigrating to or not residing living in the U.S. and they have documentation from Homeland Security DHS that supports their legal status.

Do I need a NY State Driver license if I am just studying in New York State?
No,  you do not need a NY State drivers license because you are not a permanent resident.
