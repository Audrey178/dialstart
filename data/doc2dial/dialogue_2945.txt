Can you tell me how Financial Aid for students works?
Are you planning to attend college or career school?
Yes, I am.
Then you should know that you can get essential help through our financial aid and we can teach you how it works.
================
What if I'm accepted to multiple colleges?
Then evaluate and compare the costs and aid offers and accept the aid from the school that you choose. Also, don't forget to inform them of other sources of aid, such as scholarships, you expect to receive.
================
And what can I do if it starts to get difficult to make my payments?
Just contact your loan servicer to talk about repayment options.
================
How can I know what kind of help each school offers?
You can check their aid offer, there it should be explained the types and amounts of aid they are offering to you.
================
Ok, thanks for the information. Anything else I should know about how to maintain my aid?
Yes, don't forget to complete your FAFSA form each academic year.
