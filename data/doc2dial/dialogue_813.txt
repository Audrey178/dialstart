Hello, I would like you to help me know how to apply for retirement benefits.
================
Of course Applying online is the easiest way to complete your application in a time that works for you, without having to go to the Social Security office. You can also file an application over the phone or at a Social Security office. This section will inform you about the different options.
================
Do you want to apply online?
no
================
Do you want to apply at your local office?
yes
It is important to know that you can do most of your business with Social Security online.
And how can I do if I can't use these services online?
If you can't use these services online, your local Social Security office can help you apply. Although our offices are closed to the public, the employees of those offices are helping people over the phone.
================
and what are you going to do when I complete everything?
Once you have completed everything, we will ask you to confirm that your answers are true to the best of your knowledge.
================
And how will you know when they make a decision?
You will receive a letter in the mail with our decision
