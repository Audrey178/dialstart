I want to become a driving school instructor and I have questions about the Driving School Instructor Certificate.
================
Have you passed all the required tests?
Yes, I have.
Great. You will be issued a Driving School Instructor Certificate MV-524 by the Bureau of Driver Training Programs. Your certificate will be mailed to the driving school where you work.
================
Do you need to be hired at a driving school, before you can complete the Application for Driving School Instructor Certificate?
Yes. You will need to be employed at a licensed driving school.
================
Do I need to tell them that my driver license was issued in a different state in the application?
Yes. You will need to include a certified copy of your driving record from the state who issued your license.
Do I need to show a customer or DMV employee my certificate, if they ask to see it?
Yes. Your certificate needs to be with you and you must be able to present it when asked.
================
What is the name of the application?
Application for Driving School Instructor Certificate.
Okay. Where does the application need to be mailed?
The application needs to be mailed to the New York State Department of Motor Vehicles Bureau of Driver Training Programs, 6 Empire State Plaza, Room 327, Albany, NY 12228 473-7174.
================
Can you qualify for the certificate, if you have been convicted of a felony?
No. Those who have been convicted of a felony or another crime involving violence, dishonesty, deceit, indecency, degeneracy, or moral turpitude do not qualify.
