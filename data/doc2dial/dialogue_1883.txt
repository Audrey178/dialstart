Is there a minimum amount of proven supervised practice driving that I must have before I can take the raod test?
================
What age are you? The reason I ask is because there are different rules that relate to applicants that are aged under 18 and those that are aged over 18
I am under 18
As you are under 18 years of age, you must have a minimum of 50 hours supervised driving practice before you are eligible to take the road test
================
What happens if I lose my Certificate of Supervised Driving before taking the road test?
If you have lost your Certificate of Supervised Driving, you should contact the office that issued this certificate to you, who will be able to replace it for you
What should I do in the event of stoplines or crosswalks being present during my road test?
In this case, you must come to a full stop before crossing them
================
How long exactly is my Pre-licensing Course Certificate valid for?
Your Pre-licensing course Certificate is valid for one year from the date of issue, and in the case of a Student Certificate of Completion, this is valid for two years
================
What things should I know in relation to travelling speed of my vehicle and the correct distances that should be kept between myself and other vehicles during the road test
Applicants should abide by the relevant speed limits, being mindful of the weather conditions/road conditions/visibility and traffic conditions that are prevalent. An adequate space "cushion" should be kept between yourself and any traffic in front of you. The "two second rule" that is described in the Driver's Manual should be referred to and used
What should I do if my view is obscured after stopping at a crosswalk during the road test?
If your view is obstructed while stopped at a crosswalk, you should move until your view is not obscured and if necessary, you should stop again
