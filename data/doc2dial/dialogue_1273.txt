What is some information I can find out about VA health care?
================
You can learn about VA health benefits and how VA health care works and which Veterans health care service we will cover.
How do I apply for VA health care online?
You can apply online with VA Form 10-10EZ.
================
What can I do if I as a Veteran am in crisis?
You can contact our qualified Veterans Crisis Line which is staffed with some Veterans and other qualified people 24/7 and is free.
================
What are some ways to manage my health and benefits online?
You would be able to refill and track your VA prescriptions online.
================
Are there any other resources or information that would be helpful?
We have VA health care copay rates for such things as different types of care, test, and medication online that would be helpful.
================
Would you like to learn about other VA benefits and services?
Yes, I would like to know about other VA benefits and services.
You can learn how to file a claim for disability compensation and mange your benefits online.
================
When I hear from the VA about my application decision, what will happen?
You will then find out what to do next after the decision is made.
