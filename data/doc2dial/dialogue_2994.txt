If I have a defaulted loan, am I eligible for teacher loan forgiveness?
================
Are you currently in default with your loan?
Yes, unfortunately, I am
You will not be eligible for forgiveness of that loan unless you make satisfactory arrangements with the holder of the defaulted loan
================
What is one of the additional requirements of teacher loan forgiveness for elementary, middle or secondary school teachers who are not new to the profession?
One of the requirements is that the individual must be made available to the public upon request
================
What way do I go about applying for teacher loan forgiveness?
You need to submit a completed Teacher Loan Forgiveness Application to your loan servicer. You must have completed the required five consecutive years of qualifying teaching
I have loans with different loan servicers and am applying for forgiveness, will that cause a problem?
You will need to submit a separate form to each loan servicer
================
Is it possible to receive teacher loan forgiveness on PLUS loans or Federal Perkins Loans?
Federal Perkins Loans are not eligible for this type of forgiveness. There is a possibility that you could have a portion or all of the loan canceled based on your employment or volunteer service or even discharged under certain conditions
