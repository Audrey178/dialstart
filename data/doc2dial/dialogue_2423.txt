Am I required to have the adventure icons added to my DMV photo document?
================
It is optional, not a requirement to have the icons displayed on your NY State DMV photo document.
Would you like to have the icons on your DMV photo document?
I was asking whether the icons were optional to have or if they were required.
You must have the Lifetime Empire Passport displayed on you NY State DMV photo document as it is the only proof that you have this credential.  The other icons are indeed optional.

What if I do not receive my new photo document with the icons if I do choose to have them added after I complete my boating safety certificate?
If it has been more than two weeks since you requested your photo document with DEC or Parks, you can contact Parks through their website - www.parks.ny.gov.

How long should it actually take to get my new photo document with the icons added onto it?
If you paid the fee to request the icons be added to your photo document you can get them immediately.
