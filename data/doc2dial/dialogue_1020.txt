What happens after a board decision that I disagree with?
================
Do you disagree with the Board's decision and have new and relevant evidence that will support your case?
Yes, I have new evidence and disagree with their decision.
Then you need file a Supplemental Claim and you can appeal to the U.S. Court of Appeals for Veterans Claims.
================
Can I get help with my appeal?
A Veterans Service Organization or VA-accredited attorney or agent can help you request an appeal.
================
Is there a way to find more answers to my questions?
You can look at frequently asked questions about decision review or just go to FAQs.
================
What happens after my request for decision review?
You find out what happens after the request decision review is over and you wait for a letter.
What do I need to do if VA schedules an exam for me?
You must be sure not to miss any of the scheduled exams.
