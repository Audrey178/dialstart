What do I need to know about the Income-Sensitive Repayment Plan?
The payments under this plan increase or decrease based on your annual income.
Do you need to make lower payments on your Federal Family Education Loan Program at this time?
Yes, I lost my job and need to make lower payments.
Then the Income-Sensitive Repayment Plan could be the perfect plan for you.
================
What FFEL Program loans are eligible?
Subsidized, Unsubsidized Federal Stafford Loans; FFEL Plus Loans, and FFEL Consolidation Loans are all eligible for the Income-Sensitive Repayment Plan.
================
What if I have FFEL Program loans owned by the US Deparment of Education?
You would be best to contact your loan servicer.
How do I find info for all the federal student loans I have received?
You should visit My Federal Student Aid to view any info about the federal student loans you have gotten and to find out any info for the loan servicer.
What if  have FFEL Program loans not owned by the ED?
Then you should contact your lender.
