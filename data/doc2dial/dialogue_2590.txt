What type of plates can I order picture and professional plates with?
You are allowed to order picture or professional plates for passenger vehicles, commercial vehicles and motorcycles.
================
What happens if my registration expires in the next 60 days and want picture plates?
Renew your registration and change your address online before ordering picture plates and you must visit a DMV office to do it.
