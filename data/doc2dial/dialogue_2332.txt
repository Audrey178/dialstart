I'm leasing a vehicle and need to know whether or not I have to pay sales tax at the DMV.
================
Have you already paid your NY sales tax to the leasing company?
Yes, I have.
Since you have already paid your sales tax at the leasing company, you will need to provide your lease agreement that shows you have already paid the NY sales tax. You will also need to complete the Claim for Sales Tax Exemption { Sales Tax Form } PDF [ 5 ] at NY State Department of Tax and Finance DTF-803.
================
Where do I register my vehicle at?
Your vehicle must be registered at the DMV office. Be sure to complete the Document Guide to find out which documents that you will need to bring with you.
================
Do I need to provide proof of insurance?
Yes, you will need to provide your current NY State Insurance ID card.
What do I need to provide proof of authorization by the leasing company to register my vehicle in NY?
You will need to provide a letter from the leasing company that gives you permission to register the vehicle in NY, a power of attorney from the leasing company that authorizes you to register the vehicle, and the leasing company officer's signature in box 3 on the Vehicle Registration/Title Application.
================
