================
Do you want to remove a CORRECTIVE LENS restriction?
No, not that kind of restriction
What if the Vision Test Report MV-619 has a print date of 9/03?
The form is valid for 6 months after the date of the eye test
And what if the form has a print date of 3/04 or later?
In that case, the vision care professional marks a box on the form to indicate if the form is valid for 6 months or for 12 months after the date of the eye test
How long is the form valid for?
For 6 months after the date of the eye test
Can you repeat what if the form has a print date of 3/04 or later?
Sure. The vision care professional marks a box on the form to indicate if the form is valid for 6 months or for 12 months after the date of the eye test
