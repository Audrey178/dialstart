health care
VA health care covers you for regular checkups with your primary care provider and appointments with specialists . You can access Veterans health care services like home health and geriatric care.
================
can I get help if I am homeless?
We offer many services that may help you including free health care. We can also help you connect with resources in your community
================
can you also help me with benefits?
Yes, we can help you manage your benefits
================
do you have a number I can call to get more help?
You can talk privately with a trained VA counselor for free, just call the National Call Center for Homeless Veterans at 877-424-3838.  they are available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week
================
do you help with mental health too?
Yes, with VA health care you are covered for regular checkups with mental health providers.
