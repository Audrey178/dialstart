================
Eligible payment plans include all income-driven IDR payment plans that base your monthly payment on your income.
and what are the qualified payments?
Although the payments made under the Standard 10-Year Payment Plan are qualified payments, you would have to switch to an IDR plan to benefit from the PSLF.
