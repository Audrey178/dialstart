I need some information on VA life insurance.
================
Do you know how much life insurance you'll need?
No. I don't know.
================
Can I update my beneficiaries on my policy?
Yes. It's very important to keep your beneficiaries information up to date, so that your loved ones can file a claim more easily when needed.
================
How can I learn more about other VA benefits?
You can learn more about other VA benefits such as burial benefits on our website. You can also apply for survivor or dependent benefits.
================
I eceived a denial letter for Traumatic Injury Protection Life Insurance benefits. Can I appeal it?
Absolutely. You can file a TSGLI appeal VA Form SGLV 8600A. Be sure to file your appeal by the date given in your denial letter.
================
I have Diabetes. Will this make it more difficult for me to obtain private life insurance?
Some medical conditions can make it harder for you to get private life insurance. A list of these conditions can be found on our website.
