Good morning. I'm preparing for my road test, I'ld like to know how much supervised practice driving I must get before taking my road test.
================
I'm 17 years old, can you help me with that please?
Morning! Before taking your road test (as you are 16) you must have a minimum of 50 hours of supervised practice driving.
================
I am currently practicing some stopping positions for my test... but I'm not quite sure what to do when my view is blocked after stopping behind the crosswalk, do you know?
You have to move up until you can clearly see your way, remember to stop again if it's necessary
I am aiming for a MJ license because I'm into motorcyles, does the 50 hours of supervised practice applies to MJ as well?
Pretty much the same. You must have a minimum of 50 hours of supervised pratice driving, and that includes at least 15 hours after sunset. So driving 15 hours at night is important.
================
Thanks!!! That's really helpful. BTW, if I want to change my license from one class to another, would I need a  Pre-Licensing Course Certificate?
No, you don't need a Pre-Licensing Course Certificate or a SCC, you can apply for changing your license class with just a Valid NY state driver license.
================
What if I lose my certificate?
You should contact the school that issued it to obtain a replacement.
================

Yes, there are additional requirements. You can visit www.getamotorcylelicense.com to see them.
