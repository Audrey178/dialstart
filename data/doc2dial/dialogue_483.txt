Can my husband open up a mySocial Security account for me?
================
No, sorry. To protect your personal information, you are the only one who can create your mySocial Security account.
Do you have any questions about our protection measures?
Yes, I do.
Allowing others to make an account for you could compromise the security of your information, so we don't allow anyone but you to create a personal account for yourself.
================
What kind of information is needed to create an account?
First, we will need to verify your identity by asking you to provide personal information about yourself and answering security questions. After you have proven your identity, you will then create a username and password for your account.
What are the security questions for?
These questions are just an added security measure that we take. The questions we ask will be about information you are only likely to know.
================
Are there any other ways to open an account?
Yes. You can also open an account by visiting your local Social Security office.
What does a freeze on my account mean?
You can learn more about security freezes by reading the Federal Trade Commissions Credit Freeze FAQs on our website.
