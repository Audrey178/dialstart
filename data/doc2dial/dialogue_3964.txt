Hello, I would like more information about licenses for Drivers from other states
================
Do you have a driver's license issued in another state?
Yes
If you have a driver's license issued in another state and meet the other requirements, you may receive a conditional driving privilege or a restricted driving privilege to drive in New York State. The conditions or restrictions are the same as the conditions or restrictions for a driver with a New York State driver's license.
================
and can a restricted use license be issued?
The DMV may issue a restricted use license to a qualifying driver whose license is suspended or revoked due to violations or incidents that are not related to alcohol or drugs, or due to some drug related violations.
================
and can you issue a conditional license?
The DMV may issue a conditional license to a qualified driver whose New York State license has been suspended or revoked due to an alcohol or drug related violation. The driver must attend a Disability Drivers Program IDP, formerly known as the DMV-approved Drinking Driving Program DDP.
================
and where do you request these licenses?
You must apply for a conditional or restricted use license in person at a DMV office.
and can I do it in any office?
You can do this at most, but not at all Motor Vehicle offices.
