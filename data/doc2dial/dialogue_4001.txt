================

Are your personalized plates old or damaged?
Yes, my plates are old
To replace them, you can call to the Custom Plates Unit at 1-518-402-4838. Office hours are 8:00am - 4:00pm, Monday through Friday
What do I need to replace personalized plates?
You need to pay a fee. Call to the Custom Plates Unit at 1-518-402-4838
================
How can I keep personalized plates ?
If you have IMJOE standard series personalized plates , with an annual fee of $ 31.25 $62.50 per two - year registration. You order IMJOE on a picture plate e.g., NASCAR , Yankees and pay the $62.50 annual custom plate fee $125 at the next two-year renewal in addition to other renewal fees, plus plate and document fees. The online custom plate ordering system cannot credit you for the custom license plate fees you have already paid in your current registration
================
What if I purchase the vehicle from a private person?
In that case, ask to the DMV to transfer your personalized plates when you register the vehicle
