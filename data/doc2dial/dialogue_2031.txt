What are the consequences of not responding to a Notice of non-compliance and license downgrade that I have received?
================
If you have a Commercial Driver License, this will be automatically downgraded to a Non-CDL class and/or your Commercial Learner Permit being cancelled. It is strongly recommended that you submit the required information to the Medical Certification Unit, at least 20 days before the downgrade comes into force, as this will allow it to be processed in time
