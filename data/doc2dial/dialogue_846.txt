================
What if I need help with the process?
A Veterans Service Organization or VA-accredited attorney can help you request a decision review.
================
What if I have more questions?
You can get answers to frequently asked questions about decision reviews by going to FAQs.
================
Is it necessary to go to the VA scheduled exam?
Yes, it is necessary and please do not miss the scheduled exams.
================
Is it possible for me to request a Board Appeal?
You can request a Board Appeal after an initial claim, Supplemental Claim, or Higher-Level Review Decision and you can request 2 board appeals in a row.
