I want to know more about transfer credit.
================
Like, what if I transfer my remaining registration to another vehicle?.
In that case, your new registration will expire the same date as your old one, and you can only do this for an new original registration. You can not use it to renew a registration.
================
If I'm eligible for a refund, how can I get one?
I also wants to request a transfer receipt.
When not eligible for a refund or wanting a transfer receipt, the DMV office can give you a Receipt FS-6 T for you to use to transfer the registration to another vehicle.
================
What do I need to apply for a full 2-year refund?
you have to surrender the vehicle plate and the unused registration sticker and stop at your local DMV office and request a 2-year refund.
My registration sticker is attached to the vehicle windshield, is that a problem?
yes, you can't remove the sticker and can't apply for a full refund.
