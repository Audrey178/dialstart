I would like information on registering a leased vehicle?
================
do you need to register a leased vehicle?
No, I do not need to register a lease vehicle at this time.
================
Do you need to know how to show proof of sales tax payment of your leased vehicle?
No, not right now.
================
Can I register at the DMV?
You would need to bring in your completed registration application and any additional documents to your local DMV office.
================
What Topic would I look at online?
You would need to look under the Topic of Registration.
================
What proofs of ID can I use?
You are able to use a NY State Driver License, Learner Permit, or Non-Driver ID card that is current or has not been expired for more than 2 years.
