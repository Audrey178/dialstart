How would I go about applying for a Total and Permanent Disability Discharge (TPD)?
================
You would need to complete a TPD discharge application and send it in along with any required documents to Nelnet, the TPD discharge servicer.
How can I learn more about applying for this TPD discharge and obtain an application?
You should take the time to visit our TPD Discharge website at disabilitydischarge.com and select "Application Process".
================
If I have SSDI or SSI could I qualify for TPD discharge?
Yes and you would have to send a copy of the reward letter or Benefits Planning Query showing that you your next scheduled disability review would be five to seven years or more from date of your last SSA disability determination.
