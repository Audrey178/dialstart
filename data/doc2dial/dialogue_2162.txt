How do I change users?
================
Is someone taking on another user's duties?
Yes another person is taking on the duties,
You will need to request additional user IDs.  Do not share the former user's ID and passwod with the new user.
================
Does the applicant get a personal ID and password?
Yes, when the DMV creates a LENS roster, we issue a personal ID and password to the applicant.
================
How do I register for LENS?
Log in to LEN.S and provide ID informatio
Will you assist if someone is using another person's ID?
No we will not provide support to any person who is using another person's user ID.
================
What happens if someone leaves my organization?
We will disable the departed user's access to your LENS roster.
What other information do you need?
You will need to identify the user and your LENS customer number.
