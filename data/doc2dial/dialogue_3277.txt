What if I have a dramatic change in my family's financial situation since filed taxes?
Complete the FAFSA questions as required and submit the form and then get in contact with the school and talk to the financial aid about your situation.
================
Do I need to report mine and my parents information if I am a dependent student?
Yes you would need to report yours and your parents information on the application.
================
If I am a dependent student do I report my parents info on the FAFSA form also?
Yes, you will need to report your parent info and you can visit our page on reporting parent information to find out who counts as your parent and what to do if you don't live with your parents, and what to do if you do not have access to their financial info.
================
How many schools can I list on my FAFSA and will each school receive my info?
Yes, each school will receive your info that is listed on your FAFSA form and you can only add 10 schools on the form and can add more later.
