Yes, this type of debt forgiveness is called borrower defense to repayment, which is also shortened to borrower defense. There is more information available on the website

Is there a way for me to apply for a Direct Consolidation Loan if I want to have my borrower defense discharge apply to my FFEL?
You can apply by logging on to StudentAid.gov/app/launchConsolidation.action. Once your Direct Consolidation Loan is approved, you can then contact the borrower defense hotline on 1-855-279-6207 from 8am to 8pm
Where can I go for reconsideration of a decison regarding a borrower defense?
We have a dedicated borrower defense hotline at 1-855-279-6207 from 8am to 8pm
