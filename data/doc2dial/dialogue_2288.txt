I need help with getting a military skills test waiver.
Do you have any military experience driving commercial vehicles?
Yes, I do.
Great. You can apply for the New York CDL skills road test waiver when you apply for your CDL.
What are the the requirements that I need?
If you meet the requirements, you'll be issued a CDL. Any class, endorsements, and restrictions placed on your CDL will depend on the type of Commercial Motor Vehicle you drove in the military.
================
How can I find more information about this topic?
You can find more information about Commercial Drivers on our website.
================
If I qualify for the waiver, do I still need to do the written test?
Yes, you will need to complete and pass all your written tests to get your CDL. You will also need to pay a written test fee, permit fee, and license fee.
How do I find information on the different CDL classes?
You can find that information on our website under Driver License Class Descriptions.
