Can you help me with the process of renewing a registration, please?
Of course. You need to renew your registration before it expires. Your new registration documents will arrive in the mail in about 10 days. Renewing early will not change the new expiration date for your new registration.
================
Makes sense. Do you have any additional information for me?
================
Is your registration about to expire or already expired?
No, it is still valid.
================
Has your address changed?
No, it's still the same.
================
Has your name changed?
Yes, my family name did. I recently married a very wonderful man.
In that case you can still renew online but the new document will display your previous name. You can not change your name during the online transaction. See how to change your name or other information on a registration or title on our website.
What if I qualify for a registration without fees?
Then you will need to renew at an office. You will need to bring documentation to prove you are eligible for exempt status.
================
What if I don't have the MV-3 or OP-3 listed on the website and I can't bring it to the DMV office?
Then you need to complete the Application for Duplicate/Renewal Registration (MV-82D).
