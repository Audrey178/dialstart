What form do I need to use for a Snowmobile Dealer?
================
Did you  apply for a snowmobile dealer certificate form
No, I have not applied yet for a snowmobile dealer certifcate.
================
What is the on-site investigation about?
The inspector arrives to inspect your facility and will be checking your dealer starter kit, permanently mounted signs that are able to be seen from the street.
================
Does the DMV endorse the companies?
The DMV does not endorse the companies.
================
What if my signs are not visible from the street?
If your signs are not visible, then the AFI will deny your application.
================
What if I have not gotten my Certificate of Authority yet?
Please, do not submit your application with the DMV yet.
