so how does the grant work that I can't buy my books yet?
Usually the school first applies your grant money toward your tuition, fees, and room and board. Money left over is paid to you directly for other education expenses like the books you need.

Your school may have negotiated a deal with the publisher that will allow you online access at a fraction of the cost you would pay for a physical book.
