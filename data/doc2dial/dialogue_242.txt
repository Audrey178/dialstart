Can you tell me about retirement benefits?
================
Do you need to know how to apply for retirement benefits?
Yes, I need to apply for Retirement Benefits.
Social Security offers to fill out the application online that only takes about 15 minutes and is very easy.
Can i take my application in another language other than English?
The online application will give you the option to make an appointment to apply for benefits in your preferred language.
================
Can I return to a saved application later and how do I know if their are other questions for me to answer?
We will let you know if any other questions need to be answered and you can save your application if you need to leave it for any reason.
================
What languages is the application online available?
Currently it is only available in English online; but you can schedule an appointment to apply for benefits in your preferred language.
