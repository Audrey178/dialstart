Can you explain to me how to request a higher-level review?
================
Do you need to request a higher-level review?
Yes, I need a higher-level review.
You can fill out the Decision Review Request: Higher-Level Review VA Form 20-0996.
================
What happens if I want an informal conference with a senior reviewer?
They will call the phone number that you have given them from the Higher-Level Review form to schedule a time to discuss your case with you.
What if I don't answer?
Then a voice mail for be left.
================
What happens when I request a high level review?
You are allowed to request a Higher-Level Review of an initial claim or Supplemental Claim decision and is not available after a Higher-Level Review or Board Appeal.
================
How do I submit the insurance form?
You would need to refer to your decision for the instructions about submitting the form.
================
What happens if I need help?
A Veterans Service Organization or VA-accredited attorney or agent can help you request a decion review.
