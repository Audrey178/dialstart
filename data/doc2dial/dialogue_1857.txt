I need help to renew my non-driver ID card
Ok. You can renew your non-driver ID card only 1 year before and up to 2 years after your card expires.
================
My ID card is an Enhanced non-driver ID, is the cost the same?
the additional 30$ still applies to the renewal.
================
In case I'm overseas what can I do to renew my non-driver ID card?
When overseas you can renew your non-driver ID card via mail.
================
If my Temporary Visitor status is extended can I renew my non-driver ID card?
for that case, you have to go to a DMV office with your updated paperwork from the DHS and we'll update our records to show your new status so you can renew your non-driver ID card.
================
My father is 64 years old, can he get a reduced fee benefit when renewing his non-driver ID card?
Yes, he is eligible to get a reduced fee or even "No Fee" ID card.
================
What would apply to him? he also receives SSI.
Well, for his case, he receives SSI and is over 62 years old, he can get the "No Fee" renewal. The fee for those under 62 is $6.50.
