What is the procedure if I do not have enough proof of name identification to bring with me to the DMV?
================
Fortunately, you may have more proof than you think or may be able to get the one you needs.
================
I can't find my original U.S. birth certificate or marriage record what can I do?
The NY State Department of Health Vital Records office will help you get it or you can order your birth certificate online from NY and many other states through VitalChek Network at vitalchek.com.
================
Is it possible for me to use proof of residence documents in my spouse's name?
Do you and your spouse have the same last name?
No, not at this time.
================
Can i use GED certificate or U.S. college transcripts?
Yes, you would be able to use your GED certificate by gettng a certified copy from them and use your college transcripts.
What examples of bills or documents can I use for proof of name for DMV?
Some examples are a phone, electric, gas, water bill; also, divorce papers.
