Hello, I'm on the GI Bill and was wondering if you have information about changing something.
Yes we there is information available to help with this.
================
If I want to change program do I need to make a request?
Yes if certain situations are applicable to you.
================
Do I need to show any of my wife's information as well?
You will be required to show that person's Social Security number or VA file numbers and also Basic service history information.
================
Besides doing this online can I mail in the information?
Yes you must download VA Form 22 - 1995 for Veterans and service members PDF and VA Form 22 - 5495 for dependents PDF.
================
What if I stopped going to school but have returned recently?
Yes you must inform GI Bill of this information.
================
