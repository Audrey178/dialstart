Am I able to use the VA Appointments tools?
================
Yes, you can use these tools if you meet all the requirements.
================
How would I know if my VA health facility uses online scheduling?
You can check our list of VA facilities that use online scheduling tool and we hope to expand in the future.
================
Can I use secure messaging to schedule my appointments?
Yes, as long you use Secure Messaging with you VA health care team you maybe able to use this service to schedule and cancel appointments.
================
What must be true for me to use the VA appointments tools?
Are you enrolled in VA health care?
No, I am not enrolled in VA health care.
Then you are not able to use the VA appointments tools at this time.
================
What types of VA medical appointments are able to be scheduled online?
it all depends on your VA health facility has it?

What is the fasted way to make all VA appointments?
You should usually call to make all VA appointments because it is the fastest.
