Who are the I LOVE NY Adventure Custom Plates available to?
================
You can get the I LOVE NY Adventure Custom Plates if you are an individual that holds a currently valid NYS Department of Environmental Conservation DEC issued Sporting License and/or a NYS Office of Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation Parks issued Empire Passport.
How much do I have to pay to get an I LOVE NY Adventure Custom Plate?
The fee noted for each renewal is the $15 annual I LOVE NY fee plus the personalization fee if applicable and does not include the regular registration renewal fees due upon each renewal.  As the $15 I LOVE NY fee is an annual fee, if your registration is renewed every two years passenger and commercial class, the fee is $30 at renewal.  Motorcycle classes renew every one year, therefore a $15 I LOVE NY fee will be due at each renewal.
================
Are fees for the I LOVE NY Adventure Custom Plates waived if I purchased a LIFETIME NYS Parks issued Empire Passport in 2014?
If your purchase of the LIFETIME NYS Parks issued Empire Passport was in 2014, then, yes, indeed, the fees except for the personalization fees, if applicable, are waived for your first plate purchase and first renewal only.
What are the personalization fees for the I LOVE NY Adventure Custom Plates?
Personalization fees are $31.25 for passenger and commercial registration classes and $18.75 for motorcycle class.
Can I order an I LOVE NY Adventure Custom Plate online?
Can I order an I LOVE NY Adventure Custom Plate online?
You can order your plate online at I LOVE NY Adventure Custom Plates Gallery.  To order your plate online, you must attach a copy of your driver license and a copy of your sporting license or Empire Passport with the application form.
================
What is the fee for choosing to get my new driver's license with the icons immediately?
If you hold a driver's license the feel will be $12.50 or it is only $5.00 if you hold a non-driver ID, or you can wait for your next license renewal at no charge.
================
How can I request the icons to be added to my driver's license if I have a safe boating certificate?
You may request the icons be added in two ways;  online using the Online Sporting License System at the NYS website or by visiting a NYS Department of Motor Vehicles office and presenting the required proof.
