================
Is this a situation where you can not get the title certificate for a vehicle you sold and the temporary registration expires soon?
no
================
can you also check on what the title certificate will have on it for a rebuilt 09 Mustang that we salvaged?
 If a title certificate is issued it will receive a brand that displays "REBUILT SALVAGE:NY".
================
How do we get a surety bond?
You will have to contact a surety insurance company.
================
another thing I wanted to ask is how do we know how much our bond will be?
Bond amount depends on the number of vehicles the dealer sold in the previous calendar year, or if the dealer is licensed as a franchised new dealer.
================
also, what should we put down for price on a car we sold wholesale when we're doing the retail certificate of sale?
You don't need the selling price on form MV-50 when a vehicle is sold wholesale.
