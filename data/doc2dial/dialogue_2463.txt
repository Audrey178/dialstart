Then you should know that there are pro-rated registration fees for the difference in weight, administrative fees for the vehicle plate transfer and fees for any fee increases that may have occurred since the original registration was issued.
================
What if I am not going to transfer the registration immediately?
Then you should surrender the apportioned plates and cab card to the IRB. Your vehicle registration will be suspended if there is a lapse of liability insurance and the apportioned plates were not surrendered. The driver license of the registrant can also be suspended.
