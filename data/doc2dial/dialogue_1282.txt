Will treatment help my PTSD after I return from deployment?
Yes, treatment for PTSD is helpful even after 40 years of being home or when you first return from deployment; it is never too late to get help.

Do you offer other forms of treatment and support?
We offer other PTSD specialists that provide regular outpatient care to Veterans with PTSD in all VA medical centers.
I don't live by a VA medical center or clinic, what will happen?
The good news is that our mental health providers can counsel you over the phone which we call telemental health care.
