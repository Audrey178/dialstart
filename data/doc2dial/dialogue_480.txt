I want to create a MySocial Securtiy account.
Do you want to know what's needed to create your account?
Yes, please.
When you create your account you'll be asked for personal information about yourself to verify your identity and then you'll be asked to create your username and password.
================
Can I lift a security freeze on my credit report to create an account?
Yes, you can ask our Identity Services Provider to temporarily lift your security freeze so you can create your account.
================
Is it possible to let other people use my account?.
No, your account must be used only by you.
================
Will my security freeze be reinstated after I create my account?
The Identity Service Provider may reinstate the security freeze after the temporary lift expires.
Where can I get more info about security freezes?
You can read the Federal Trade Commission's Credit Freeze FAQs
