Is there a situation whereby I will have to give a refund of the financial aid that I receive?
If you withdraw from school, some of the monies may have to be returned to the source of the funds, either by you or the school
================
Should I pay much attention to Enrollment Contracts as published by schools?
You should read these carefully before you sign them. The contract explains to you what you will get from the school for your money, so it is an important document
================
I would like to see if there are any useful statistics available for my research, where could I find this information?
There are statistics available, and these are designed to help you in your choice of school
================
Will I have to refund any financial aid if I leave before the course work is completed?
There are situations whereby you can receive some of your money back, this is listed in the refund policy of the school in question
