Are other people allowed to access my social security account?
================
No, you are the only person who can create and access your social security account.
Are you asking this to see if your account is protected or because you want to allow someone else to access your account?
Yes, I just wanted to make sure that my account was secure.
Yes, we will only allow you exclusive use of your account.
================
What will I be asked to do to create a social security account?
You will be asked to provide personal information that only you would know to verify your identity. You will also be asked to create and username and password to access your account.
================
Is there more information concerning security freezes and fraud alerts?
Yes, for more information read the Federal Trade Commission's Credit Freeze FAQs.
Can I still open my social security account if I have a fraud alert on my credit report?
Yes, you can have it temporarily lifted to access your account by contacting our Identity Services Provider.
Do I have to do this online or can I do it another way?
You can visit your local social security office to open an account in person.
On what subject is your FAQs section on?
The FAQs section makes reference to security freezes and fraud alerts.
