Can I connect with the Veterans Benefits Administration through social media?
Yes, you can connect with the Veterans Benefits Administration through many social media channels including Facebook, Instagram, and LinkedIn.

I was thinking about enrolling for SGLI. How can I get in touch with them?
You can use their SGLI Online Enrollment System to ask any questions that you may have.
================
Do they have a number I could call?
Yes, for questions about SGLI, you can call 1-800-419-1473.
================
What if I get a rejection letter for Traumatic Injury Protection Life Insurance?
In the event that you receive a denial letter for TSGLI, you can file an appeal VA Form SGLV 8600A, by the date given in your letter.
================
I suffer from hearing loss, is there a TTY number available?
Yes, you can call TTY: 711 for help.
