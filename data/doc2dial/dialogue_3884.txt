I moved to New York and registered my vehicle here. Do I need to do an inspection again?
================
Was your vehicle registered in another state that issues an inspection tag?
Yes
In that case you don't need to be inspected immediately. Your out-of-state inspection remains valid until it expires or for one year after the registration date, whichever comes first.
================
What if I leave New York and my inspection expires, what should I do?
Wait 14 days for the DMV to process and ship your extension decal. You will need it before returning your vehicle to New York
================
and which program is in charge of this?
The DMV brochure, New York State Vehicle Safety and Emissions Inspection Program for Automobiles and Light Trucks [3] C-50 lists the items of equipment reviewed during a safety inspection, and a description of the inspection of security
================
If the inspection tag expires more than 60 days, how much is the fine?
the fine is between $ 50 and $ 100
I need to replace the inspection tag, how do I do it?
You can have your vehicle re-inspected or you can request a replacement sticker using the Request for Replacement Inspection Sticker PDF [8] VS-118 form.
and what if my vehicle's inspection tag has expired in the last 60 days
You may be fined $ 25 to $ 50.
