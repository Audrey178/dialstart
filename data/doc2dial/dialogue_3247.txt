how do I report school loans I want taken into consideration when my repayment period is worked out for a direct consolidation loan?
You need to list those loans on your consolidation application in the section for listing loans that you do not want to consolidate, but want to be considered in the determination of your repayment period.
what kind of loans can I list on there?
Any federal student loans that are not included in the consolidation loan, as well as private education loans that are not eligible for consolidation.
