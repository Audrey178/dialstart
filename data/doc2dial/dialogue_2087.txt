What is shown on my lifetime driving record?
A lifetime driving record contains all the information DMV possesses about you.
Is there any specific information you are seeking on your driving record such as suspensions and revocations?
Yes, will all suspensions on my license be shown on my driving record?
On your standard driving record, most suspensions will be removed 4 years from the suspension date. However, all suspensions that you have received since you've obtained your license will show up on your lifetime driving record.
================
What do I do if I think that suspension information is wrong in my driving record abstract?
IPlease contact the DMV Call Center. They will be able to assist you with this problem.
================
Will suspensions that I obtained out of state show up on my lifetime driving record?
Yes. The address and phone number of the court that issued your suspension will be recorded in your lifetime driving record.
================
What information about my current license is recorded in my lifetime driving record?
Your license class and current expiration date will be included in your lifetime driving record.
================
How will suspensions be shown in my lifetime driving record?
Your suspensions will be divided into two sections. One for Non-Scofflaws and one for Scofflaws.
