I need help with my Student Aid Report.
Your Student Aid Report(SAR) is a summary of your FAFSA form, so it's highly important that you make sure your information is correct and complete.
================
Why does the FAFSA form ask for my email?
By providing your email on your FAFSA form, you get access to an online copy of your SAR.
What if I don't provide my email?
You will receive a paper copy of you SAR.
================
What type of information does my SAR contain?
Your SAR will contain basic information about your eligibility for federal student aid and answers you gave to FAFSA questions.
================
How do I get another copy of my SAR if I need it?
You can contact the Federal Student Aid Information Center at 1-800-4-FED-AID to request a copy of your SAR by mail.
================
