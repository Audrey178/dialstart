I need to have a corrective lens restriction removed from my license and need to know if I can do it by mail.
================
Do you want to remove the corrective lens restriction before you renew your driver license?
Yes, I'd like to have this removed right away.
Okay. To remove the restriction by mail, you will need to provide proof that you no longer need the corrective lenses and order a replacement license.
My vision test report MV-619 has a print date before 9/03. Is it still valid?
Yes, your form is valid for 6 months after the date of your eye test.
Where do I mail the form and documents to have the restriction removed from my license?
You need to mail these to your local DMV office.
================
What does a teloscopic lenses restriction mean?
Teloscopic lenses are much more powerful than normal glasses or contact lenses. If you need these type of lenses, the restriction will be placed on your license.
================
Why does the print date of the vision test report matter?
The print date is important because vision test reports with a print date of 9/03 or earlier are valid for 6 months after the date of the test. Whereas those with a print date of 9/03 or later, may be valid for 6 months or 12 months.
Can I provide proof that I don't need corrective lenses online?
Yes. Your proof can be submitted electronically by your vision care provider, if they are enrolled in the DMV Online Vision Registry.
================
Can my Vision Test Report be done in Spanish?
No, sorry. The form must be completed in English.
