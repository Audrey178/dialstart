Will I need any particular documents to apply?
================
You will need proof of citizenship.
Have you already established the child's U.S. citizenship with us?
Yes, I already proved her citizenship.
We need certain documents to prove citizenship like a U.S. birth certificate.
================
Can I replace a stolen Social Security card?
Yes, you can replace a stolen or lost card for free.
================
What is acceptable as proof of identity for a child?
You can use a state issued ID card or an adoption decree.
Do I need proof of identity as well?
Yes, you can use your driver's license or state issued ID card.
What can I bring that covers citizenship and identity in one document?
A U.S. passport will work for both.
================
Can I get a replacement card for my child?
Yes, up to 3 in one year and 10 over a lifetime.
