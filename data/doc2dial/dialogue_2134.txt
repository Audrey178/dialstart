When will it expire?
your registration will expire each year on August 31
================
What if it is registered out of state?
 You must obtain a New York State registration before operating your ATV in this state
================
Who can use an ATV?
you may operate an ATV only under adult supervision
================
Do I have to have a certain type of insurance for an ATV?
You may not operate any ATV anywhere in New York State , except on your own property , unless it is covered by liability insurance
