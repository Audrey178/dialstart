I need to renew my truck's registration.
I can help you with that. Please be advised that you must renew your registration before the expiration date.
================
Do I need a MV-3 or OP-3 to renew my registration at DMV?
It's best that you bring your renewal notice MV-3 or OP-3 with you when you renew, but if you don't have one, you can still renew your registration by completing the Application for Duplicate/Renewal Registration form.
================
Can I renew my registration by mail?
Absolutely. You'll need to mail the bottom part of your registration renewal notice MV-3 or OP-3 to the address listed on that notice, as well as a personal check or money order payable to the Commissioner of Motor Vehicles for the fee.
Do you have your MV-3 or OP-3?
Yes, I do.
Excellent. For future reference, you would need to complete the Application for Duplicate/Renewal Registration form that I mentioned earlier, if you did not have your MV-3 or OP-3.
================
How long will it take for me to receive my new registration documents?
Your new registration documents will arrive in the mail about 10 days after you renew.
================
Where do I need to mail my information and payment to, if I choose to renew by mail?
You'll need to mail your form and payment to the New York State Department of Motor Vehicles Utica Processing Center, P.O. Box 359, Utica, NY 13503.
