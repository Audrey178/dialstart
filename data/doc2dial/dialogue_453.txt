Can I apply online for an adult with a disability?
================
It is possible for you to file Supplemental Security Income or SSI online but you really have to meet certain requirements.
================
Are SSI benefits payable to people 65 and older?
Yes, SSI benefits are payable to people 65 and older without disabilities who meet the financial limits.
================
Can I file a child's disability claim online?
At this time Social Security for Children's benefits are not yet allowed to apply online.
================
If you recently denied by SSI application can I file an appeal?
Yes, you can file an SSI appeal online.
================
