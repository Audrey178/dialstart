Hi, there. I'm looking for information on housing assistance.
================
You can find information of how to apply for housing grant as a veteran or as a service member.
================
You can also check for others VA benefits and services.
And which benefits or services would this be?
================
There are benefits for women and medical services?
Yes, there are. You can check on veteran women program manager and also you can see the nearest VA medical center to you.
================
What about if I got a disability that limits my ability to work?
In that case you need to check if your disability makes you apply to vocational rehabilitation, helping you to explore employment options.
================
There is the possibility to get a home loan as a veteran?
Yes, as a veteran you can apply for a home loan, also as a surviving spouse of a veteran or the spouse of a missing in action veteran.
================
Which kind of program there are?
we offer many programs and services that may help including free health care and free limited dental care. Also we can help you to connect with some resources in your community.
