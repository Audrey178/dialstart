Do I need to register my car in New York if it's registered in a different state?
================
Yes, you need to register any vehicle you own at the DMV.
================
What do I need to  bring to get it registered?
Complete the DMV's Document Guide to make sure you bring everything you need.
================
Can I renew my registration online?
In most cases, but some vehicles can not be renewed online.
================
What else can I complete online instead of going to the office?
You have the ability to change your name or address and check your registration status online.
================
Where can I register my car?
You can register at any DMV office.
================
Do any special situations apply to you, such as farm vehicles, no title or more than one owner?
Yes, it's a farm vehicle.
The procedure for registering that will be slightly different, so come to the office and we can walk you through it.
