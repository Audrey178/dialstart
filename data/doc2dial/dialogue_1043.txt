Can I find out if dependent family member of a Veteran  can get employment benefits?
You can find out if you are eligible and how to apply for career and educational counseling through VR&E as a dependent family member of a Veteran with service-connected disability.
================
What particular VR&E benefits are available?
You can gt education and vocation counseling and help with figuring out your abilities and interests.
================
Does VR&E offer career counseling?
Yes, VR&E offer career counseling as the dependent family member of a service member.
================
Are there other benefits and education benefits for dependents?
Yes, in some cases you can get other education and employment benefits and services for dependents such as being a child of a Vietnam or Korean Veteran and have certain birth defects.
