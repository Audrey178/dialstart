What happens to my benefits if I stop working? Will I lose them?
You will lose your paycheck but you also may lose your employer provided health insurance although there are some exceptions to this.
What happens to my benefits if I have additional earnings on my record?
If you have additional earnings recorded, we will check to see if it increases your monthly benefit. If they do, we'll send you a letter alerting you to your new benefit amount.
What happens when I reach full retirement age?
When you reach full retirement age we calculate your benefit amount to give you credit for any months you did not receive a benefit due to your earnings.
What should I do about my benefits if I am in poor health?
If you are in poor health, you may want to consider starting your benefits at an earlier time.
