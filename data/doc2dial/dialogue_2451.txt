Should I bring the bill of sale from the dealer?
If you purchased the vehicle from a New York State auto dealer or an out-of-state dealer who is authorized to collect New York State sales tax, please bring the dealer's bill of sale showing that you paid to the state New York sales tax. Also bring a bill of sale from the MV-50 NY State dealer only or a complete sales tax exemption {Sales Tax Form} pdf at the New York State Department of Taxation and Finance DTF-803 for a departure from - state dealer.
