How much is it to replace my registration?
The fee to replace your vehicle registration document and windshield sticker is $ 3.00
Was your loss of registration and/or plates the result of a crime??
Yes to both the registration and plates.
In that case you must apply by mail or at a DMV office.  There is more information in the following section called lost or stolen plates [ 2 ].
================
My address has changed since I got my last registration, will this cause a problem?
If your are sending it in by mail, you can just write your new address on the application.
================
Am I taking a chance if my mail is being forwarded to my new address?
There is no guarantee that the USPS will forward all DMV documents to your new address.
Is there a faster way to change my address?
Yes, you can first change your address online and then renew the information.
================
It will be too long to send my application by mail and I don't have the time to go by the office, can't I get a registration like now?
Yes you can replace registration documents online for most vehicles with the condition that your computer must accept cookies.
================
Would you like to know the type of vehicles that are ineligible for online registration replacement?
Yes that would be helpful.
Amongst others, there are vehicles older than model year 1973, boats, snowmobiles or ATVs.
