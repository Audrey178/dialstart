If you disagree with the Board's decision and have new and relevant evidence that supports your case you can file a Supplemental Claim .You can also appeal to the U.S. Court of Appeals for Veterans Claims .

ok, what if I want to have a senior reviewer look at my case. can you give me some info on that ?
With a senior reviewer you are asking for another review of the same evidence . A senior reviewer will take another look at your case and determine whether the decision can be changed based on a difference of opinion or an error . You can learn how to request a Higher - Level Review from a senior reviewer.
================
Is one of my options going to court?
Yes, you also have the option to bring an action in a United States district court . You have 6 years to bring such an action after accrual of the right for which the claim is made . You may want to speak with an attorney about this option .

can I ask about filing a supplemental claim?
 When you choose to file a Supplemental Claim you are adding new evidence that supports your case or identifying evidence for review .A reviewer will determine whether the new evidence changes the decision .
