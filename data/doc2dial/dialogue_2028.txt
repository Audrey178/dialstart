What is a mandated ignition interlock device?
================
Have you had alcohol related violations?
Yes.
You may be required to install one on your vehicle.
================
What if I have a problem while on driver restriction?
You license will be revoked for at least 30 days.
================
Is there an installation fee?
You can visit the Ignition Interlock page.
Can I be convicted?
If you are your driver license will be revoked.
================
Can I drive thru New York with an out of state license while on driver restriction?
Yes but you must carry attachment and abide by the rules.
================
What are the restrictions?
They will be outlined on an attachment issued to you by the DMV.
