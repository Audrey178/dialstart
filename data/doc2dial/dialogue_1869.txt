Tell me about rounds and transfer credit.
================
I want to transfer my remaining registration to another vehicle.
When transferring your remaining registration to another vehicle, take into consideration that the new registration will expire the same day as the old one, and you can only use the transfer credit for a new original registration, you can't use it for a renewal.
================
How can I know if am eligible for a refund?
After you surrender your plates, your Receipt FS-6 T indicates if you are eligible for a refund. If you are and you do not request a transfer receipt, your refund will be processed automatically and you'll get it in your mail after some weeks.
================
Can I still apply for a refund if the registration sticker was attached to the plate?
No, in that case, the sticker can't be removed and you won't be able to apply for a full refund.
What is needed to apply for the 2-year refund?
The 2-year registration fee refund can only be applied if the registration was never used and it hasn't been more than 60 days from the date the registration was issued.
So if I applied for a registration but never used the registration sticker, can I get a 2-year refund?
yes, you can apply for a refund of the 2-year fee you paid when you renewed the registration.
