Yes, this could happen for a variety of reasons, such as if you indicated that you plan to live on campus when you submitted your FAFSA but then decided to live off campus or if you are selected for verification.

Because your financial aid package is created for you and is based on the cost of attending a particular school, your aid amounts will vary from school to school.
