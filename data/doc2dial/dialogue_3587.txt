I would like to know what happens if the deceased receives monthly benefits?
================
Is your deceased receiving Social Security benefits?
yes
in this case you must return the benefit received for the month of death and any subsequent month.
================

not

If I mentioned continuing to receive benefits after death. For example, if the person died in July, when should they return the benefits?
must return benefits paid in August

and can benefits be paid to the worker's children and the surviving spouse who cares for the children, even if the worker does not have the required amount of credits?
Yes, they can obtain benefits if the worker has credit for a year and a half of work 6 credits in the three years just before his death. Everyone's situation is different and you should talk to a Social Security claims representative about your options.
