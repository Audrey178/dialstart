Are there any other VA benefits and services that I am not aware of?
Yes there are other benefits that you might not be aware of. There are burial benefits and memorial items. You can get help planning a burial, order a headstone or other memorial items and apply for survivor and dependent benefits.
================
What are the benefits of having VA life insurance?
VA life insurance can offer financial security for Veterans, service members, and their spouses and dependent children.
================
What can I do if my request for a policy was denied?
If you application for a policy was denied you can file a TSGLI appeal VA Form SGLV 8600A if you've received a denial letter for Traumatic Injury Protection Life Insurance benefits that instructs you to use this form. Remember you must file your appeal by the date provided in the letter.
What about my beneficiaries, why do I have to keep updating this information?
You should keep information about your beneficiaries up to date. This will make it easier for your family members to file a claim in their time of need.
