Does a scholarship affect my student aid and if it does, to what extent?
If you are awarded a scholarship, you need to inform your school of this. They will then subtract the amount of the scholarship from the financial aid that you have been offered. Any amount of funding that is left over can be covered by other financial aid, which you will still be eligible for
Where does the funding from a scholarship go to?
The scholarship funds are used to pay for the cost of attendance and also take over what other financial aid would cover, for example, loans that you may have been offered
Am I eligible to apply for a scholarship along with student aid, or is it possible to get both?
A scholarship does affect your student aid as you cannot earn more in financial aid than the cost of your attendance at college or career chool

Are there different types of scholarships that are available or are they all just basically the same?
Some scholarships are merit-based, where the scholarship giver sets standards and the applicant meets or exceeds these standards. These merit scholarships can be awarded based on academic success or a combination of academia and a special talent, trait or interest
