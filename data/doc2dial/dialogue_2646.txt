How can I answer these types of traffic tickets?
You have to contact the court directly. A suspension due to a traffic ticket is an 'indefinite' suspension.
What is an indefinite suspension?
An indefinite suspension means your driver license or privilege to drive is taken away until you pay a suspension termination fee of $ 70 and have valid driver license.
