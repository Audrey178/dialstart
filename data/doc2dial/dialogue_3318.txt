You can consider such things as cost, location and accreditation when you are trying to decide where to go.
================
what is accreditation about?
An accredited school meets certain standards set by an independent agency. It helps ensure the training or education you get meets employer standards in a specific field.
================
how can I learn more about schools?
You can attend college fairs.  It will give you the chance to look at different schools by talking to representatives.
================
are the SATs a must?
For most undergraduate programs you will have to take either the SAT or the ACT test.
================

We have some useful statistics you can look at to help you evaluate schools.
