Are spouse's able to claim benefits?
Yes, a spouse can claim benefits if they are the spouse or divorced spouse of a retired worker or a disabled worker or the widow/widower of a deceased worker.
================
What does a benefit calculator do?
A benefit calculator helps you to get an estimate of the benefits that you and your family may be eligible for.
================
If I'm the spouse of a retired worker, can I still claim benefits?
Yes, as long as you are 62 or older now or within 3 months.
