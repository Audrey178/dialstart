hi i need help on how do i apply?
================
You can apply online right now.
What if the service member or veteran in your family is not yet using VR&E benefits and services?
If your family member's service or veteran is not yet using VR&E benefits and services, they can also apply online through eBenefits.
================
What kind of VR&E benefits can I get?
You can get educational and vocational advice, plus help with: Exploring your skills and interests Charting a path to employment
And in which cases, for example, is it possible to obtain the benefit?
In certain cases, you may be able to get other dependent education and employment benefits and services. For example, you may qualify if you are the child of a Vietnam or Korean war veteran and have certain birth defects, or if you are a dependent child or spouse of a veteran who has died or is permanently and totally disabled as a result of active duty.
================
What happens if I comply with The Post-9/11 GI Bill Chapter 33?
if you meet those requirements and others you may be Eligible for a VA educational benefit
