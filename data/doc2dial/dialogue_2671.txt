What exactly is a TNC?
The TNC is actually the transportation network company and is a business know as a rideshare company, licensed by DMV.
What types of trips are TNC licensed through the DMV?
TNC companies are licensed by the DMV to use a digital network to connect passengers to the drivers for prearranged trips.
