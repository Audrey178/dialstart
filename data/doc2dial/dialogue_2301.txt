What will happen if my NY registration expires while I'm on Active Duty?
Your registration will be extended for up to 60 days once you return to NY. The extension only applies to vehicles registered to military personnel, so you will need to show proof of military service or separation.
================
How can I get more information about being on Active Duty and having a lapse of insurance coverage?
Contact the DMV Call Center. They will be able to assist you with any questions that you have about that topic.
What if I'm not able to surrender my plates after letting my insurance lapse?
DMV has a procedure in place to exempt you from administrative actions once you return. You will need to provide a notarized statement that your vehicle was not used during the time in question, a copy of your military ID, and signed and dated copies of deployment and return papers. You will also need to provide proof of current insurance. If you cannot, you will need to surrender your plates.
================
Who issued these extensions for military personnel?
NY Governor Andrew M. Cuomo issued these extensions under Executive Order No. 2.
