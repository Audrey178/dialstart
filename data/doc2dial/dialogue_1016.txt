================
Do you disagree with the Board's decision and do you have new and relevant evidence to support your case?
Yes
You can file a Supplemental Claim. You can also appeal to the United States Court of Appeals for veteran claims. Learn more about your options after a Board decision
================
Can I request a higher level review?
You can request a higher level review of an initial complaint or a supplemental complaint decision. This option is not available after a higher level review or Board decision.
================
and who will review my case?
A judge from the Board of Veterans Appeals in Washington, DC will review your case. Learn more about how to request a Board Appeal.
