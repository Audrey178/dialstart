Can I apply for SS benefits online?
================
Yes you can apply for retirement benefits, disablility benefits or just medicare and you meet certain criteria
I need just medicare
You will be asked to agree to the terms of service agreement and create or login to your personal SS account.
I need help with how to apply as a divorced spouse of a retired worker
YOu can fill out your medicare benefits online if you are the divorced spouse of a retired worker and  within 3 months of age 62 or older and you meet certain criteria
