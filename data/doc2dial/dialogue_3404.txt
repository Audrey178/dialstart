How will I receive my federal student aid?
Your college not the U.S. Department of Education will distribute your federal student aid.
================
When should I receive financial aid?
It all depends on what type of aid you have accepted.
================
What happens with the disbursements of grants and student loans?
Ultimately your school will dispurse your grant or loan money in at least two payments and must give you your grant or loan money at least once per semester, trimester, or quarter.
What is a dispursement?
A disbursement is when you receive your grant or loan money.
================
