hi i need help about VA Decision Review and Appeals
================
Do you have a calendar?
do not
Do you have 1 year to request the review?
If you have 1 year from the date of your decision to request a review of the decision, unless you have a fiduciary claim or a multi-party claim.
================
What if I have more questions?
Get answers to frequently asked questions about decision reviews. Go to frequently asked questions
================
Can I request a Board Appeal?
You can request a Board Appeal after an initial complaint, a supplemental complaint, or a higher level review decision. You cannot request 2 Board Appeals in a row.
================
What should I do after I make the request?
You don't need to do anything while you wait, unless VA sends you a letter asking for more information.
================
And for the Appeal before a veterans law judge, which form should I fill out?
Must Download VA Form 10182: Appeal Panel PDF
