What if i all ready have Medicare?
================
Do you all ready have Medicare?
Yes, I all ready have Medicare.
You can still use the application to apply for your retirement benefits.
================
Can other family members receive benefits under my work record?
n/a
What happens after I apply?
Once we get your application we will review it.
================
What happens if I don't have Medicare and three months within age 65?
You can use the application to apply for both Medicare and retirement benefits within 3 months of age 65.
================
What are examples i cannot do when entering my address?
You are not allowed to use a period as part of your address.
================
What if I have trouble answering questions?
If you have trouble answering questions you can find additional information in our more info links.
