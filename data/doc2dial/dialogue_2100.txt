What are the employer responsibilities with regards to CLP?
Section 140 of the New York State Transportation Law is amended to provide that no employer will allow an employee to operate a commercial motor vehicle during any period in which the operator does not have a valid CLP or CDL or does not have those with a proper class or endorsement
================
What if I have a CDL with an L restriction?
You will be upgraded from an L to a Z restriction
================
