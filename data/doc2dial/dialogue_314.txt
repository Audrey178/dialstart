Am I able to mail my disability benefits documents?
================
Yes, you are able to mail those documents to us, do you have your social security number?
Yes, I know my social security number.
Great, you can mail your documents if you include your social security number on a separate sheet of paper and as long as you don't write anything on the original documentation.
================
Are there other ways, besides online, I can apply for disability benefits?
Yes, you can apply by calling us at 1-800-772-1213 from 7am to 7pm Monday through Friday.
================
Should I still apply for benefits even if I don't have all the correct paperwork?
Yes, you should still apply, we can always help you get the correct documentation that you need.
================
Will the application process go quicker if I have my information on hand?
Yes, the process will be quicker and smoother if you have your information ready to go.
What kind of information do I need to have on hand?
You would need to have your personal info such as date of birth and social security number.
================
What happens after I apply for my disability benefits?
After we receive your application we will confirm with either by email or mail that it was received.
