I am looking to remove a lienholder from a title certificate, how do I go about this?
================
Is there a lienholder currently appearing on your title certificate?
Not on mine, no
================
To remove a lienholder from a certificate you must have proof that a lien is satisfied, do you have any proof of this?
What is acceptable proof that a lien on a title is satisfied?
There are currently two types of proof, the first proof is to provide the DMV with what's known as a Recorded Lien MV-901
What happens if the lienholder present on the title certificate is a person and not a loan company?
In this instance, the letter that is sent to the DMV must be notarized
================
I have a lien on my title certificate, but my certificate is lost, is there anything I can do in this instance?
You must send an application for a Duplicate Certificate of Title and original proof which shows that a lien is satisfied to the address that is listed or alternatively, bring them to a DMV office. The DMV will issue you with a duplicate certificate but this does not list the lien. The certificate is mailed to you for a fee of $20.00
