I want to renew my non-driver ID card, please, help me with info about it.
You can renew 1 year before and up to 2 years after your non-driver ID expires.
Can I renew my non-driver ID card if it expired 3 years ago?
No, just 1 year before and up to 2 years after your non - driver ID expires.
================
I am going out of the country for a while and would like information regarding the renewal of my non-driver ID, can you help me with this, please?
Before your trip I recommend that you go through a DMV office and get an Enhanced or REAL ID , and avoid a second office visit later.
================
Do I need to know something important before going to a DMV office?
You must bring an original copy of proof of your eligibility to get a reduced fee.
What is my fee if I receive Supplemental Security Income SSI and I am 60 years old?
If you are 60 years old,, your fee is $ 6.50.
================
I am somewhat busy and prefer to renew my Standard by mail. There is a problem with that?
If you renew by mail, your new ID will show: "NOT FOR FEDERAL PURPOSES".
