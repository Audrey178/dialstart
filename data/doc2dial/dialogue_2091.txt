Do I need to turn in in to DMV?
================
Do you have these documents available?
Yes, why do I need to mail a copy of his death certificate to DMV?
Providing copies of these documents will ensure that your deceased loved one will not become a target of identity theft.
================
What about registration? My father had just registered his truck shortly before he passed away. Is it possible to receive a refund?
After the criteria is met to receive a refund, DMV can issue a refund to the name of the estate.
================
What about his vehicle title and license plates?
DMV can assist you with these issues.
================
So, I need a copy of his death certficate and license to give to DMV?
Yes. This ensures that you will not receive any more mailings for the deceased loved one.
================
What criteria needs to be met to receive a registration refund?
You need to fill out and sign the Request for Refund MV-215 with "deceased" wriiten on the form. You will also need a copy of his death certificate.
================
Can I transfer my deceased father's truck into my name?
Yes. To help you transfer the ownership of the truck, you can review the MV-843 checklist.
