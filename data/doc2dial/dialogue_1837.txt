Can you please tell me more about the county use taxes?
================
This is the tax that applies to all residents of New York City and several counties in New York for the registration or renewals of a commercial vehicle
================
Can you tell me how to determine the fees for a commercial vehicle?
For that, you can use the registration fee and the county use tax charts.
================
How should I calculate the correct fee?
You have to add 3.25% to the fee listed in the chart and then change the result you get to the nearest 0.25$.
================
What form should I use If I'm from outside New York City and exempt from the vehicle use tax in my county?
For your case, you have to complete form UT-11C
================
What can I use the use tax chart for counties outside New York City?
This chart shows the use taxes and supplemental fees for all the original registrations and renewals.
================
And what if I'm from New York City and exempt from the NYC vehicle use tax?
Go and get the form UT-11 from a local DMV or from a DMV Call center
