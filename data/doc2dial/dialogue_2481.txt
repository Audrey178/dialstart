Where can I plea to or pay a TVB ticket or schedule a hearing?
You can find a TVB near you and plead there; but you may not plead to or pay a TVB ticket, or schedule a hearing at a regular DMV issuing office.
================
What information will i need?
You will need your full name and date of birth as they happen to appear on the traffic ticket.
================
What if all my questions cannot be answered?
You would need to call the Traffic Violations Bureau at 718-488-5710.
What will I need to do if I have a new address and plead Not Guilty to one or more traffic tickets?
Since your address has changed you would need to provide your new address so we can send the Notice of Hearing in the mail; but it will not update your other DMV records.
