Hi there. I'm looking information related with drivers from others states.
================
No problem. Is your driver license from another state?
Not exactly.
================
If the license is from another state this can be restricted?
Yes, a license can be restricted in some cases, in others can be suspended or revoked because of violations or incidents.
================
And how can I get a license?
The dmv can issue a conditional license to a qualified driver whose ny state license has been suspended or revoked because of a alcohol or drug- related violation.
================
In that case I can still receive then a license?
Yes, you can receive a conditional driving privilege or a restricted driving privilege to drive in NY state.
================
And how do I get access of this conditional license?
You need to be a qualified driver and must attend a impaired driver program previously know as drinking driving program approved by the dmv.
================
And what I need to do to apply for it?
To apply for this kind of license you need to go in person at a dmv office.
The whole process must be done at the office?
Yes. You can do this at most, but not all, motor vehicles office. You need to contact your nearest office to find out where you can apply.
