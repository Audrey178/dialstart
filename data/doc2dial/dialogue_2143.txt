Can picture plates be personalized?
================
Yes, some picture plates can be personalized for an additional fee in addition to your picture plate and regular registration fee

Can I use personalized plates on any type of vehicle?
You can use personalized plates on passenger vehicles , commercial vehicles , motorcycles and trailers
================
What plate combinations are restricted?
Restrictions include: Personalized plates that give the appearance of an official plate or the configuration of special registration classes. Combinations that are considered obscene , derogatory or offensive, and combinations that do not have one letter
================
What are personalized plates?
Personalized plates are standard series plates that have a combination of numbers and letters that you select , for an additional fee in addition to your regular vehicle registration fee
