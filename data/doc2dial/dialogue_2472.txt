Hello, there are a few things I need to know about replacing IRP registration items. Can you help me out?
Of course. Do you wish to replace IRP registration items that are lost or stolen?
Yes, I do.
Then you must apply to the International Registration Bureau (IRB).
================
Can I apply by fax?
Yes. You can apply to replace IRP registration items by fax at 518-486-6579.
================
What about the customer service counter? Can I apply at the one in Albany?
Yes, you can apply to replace IRP registration items there.
================
Alright. And how about applying by mail? Is that possible too?
Of course. You can also apply to replace IRP registration items by mail.
================
But I can't replace IRP registration items at a DMV local office, right?
Yes, you can't replace IRP registration items at a DMV local office.
