I already know that it is, but what should I do if I qualify for both the fry scholarship and DEA? Apply for both?
In that case, you'll need to choose which program you want to use. But beware: once you choose, you can't change your mind.
================
I understand, I have to choose one. Well, where do I apply for benefits then?
You can apply online now, or fill out a Dependents Application for VA Education Benefits VA Form 22-5490 and send it to the VA regional benefit office where you want to go to school.
But what if I've already started my educational program? Can I still get the benefits?
Of course, just ask a certifying official from your school or your employer to fill out a VA Enrollment Certification VA Form 22-1999. They'll need to submit it online using VA Online Certification VA-ONCE.
================
My dad died on a combat mission, 12 years ago. I've read that this qualifies me for several support programs, do you know which?
Yes, you can use both the Fry Scholarship and DEA. You can get up to 81 months of full-time training, but you can use only one program at a time.
