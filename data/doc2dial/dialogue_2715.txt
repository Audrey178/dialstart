Hey, I have some questions regarding the voter registration. Can you answer them?
================
Of course. Are you wondering if you are eligible to register to vote in New York State?
Yes!
To register to vote in New York State you must be a U.S. Citizen and at least 18 years old by the end of this year.
================
How do I know what my voter registration status is right now?
The NYS Board of Elections provides a website for New York State residents to check their voter registration status.
================
Ah, perfect. And do I have to put my signature on my voter registration application?
Do you agree to the New York State Department of Motor Vehicles Attestation in the voter registration application?
No, I haven't agreed with it.
================
So what happens next, after my voter registration application is submitted?
The electronic voter registration application is transferred from DMV to your County or City Board of Elections for review. Once processed, your County will notify you either that you are registered to vote or additional information is needed to complete your application.
