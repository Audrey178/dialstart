I'm here for registration renewal
Okay, you need to renew your registration before it expires. Your new registration documents will arrive in the mail in about 10 days. Renewing early will not change the new expiration date for your new registration
================
can I do it online?
Some registrations are not eligible. Don't renew online if you are eligible for a 'no fee' renewal. The fee is on your renewal notice , and displayed on your computer screen when you renew online.
================
can I get my new last name on the registration online?
You can not change your name during the online transaction. You can still renew online but the new document will display your previous name.
Are you eligible for a 'no fee' registration?
yes I am
Okay, you will need to renew at an office, and you will need to bring documentation to prove you are eligible for exempt status.  You can also use this visit to make the name change you wanted.
================
will you guys get me my stuff if I've had an address change and let the postal office know?
We will try but there is no guarantee that the USPS will forward all DMV documents to your new address
================
what will I need besides my personal info when I renew the registration online?
You will need your registration plate number and your registration class.
