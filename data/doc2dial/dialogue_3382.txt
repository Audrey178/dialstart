================
Most types of forbearance are not automatic. You need to submit a request to your student loan servicer, often using a form.
Are there any other procedures I have to go through for certain types of forbearances?
Yes. For some types of forbearance, you must provide your student loan servicer with documentation to show that you meet the eligibility requirements for the forbearance you are requesting.
