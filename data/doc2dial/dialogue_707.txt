I have a few questions about my social security benefits. It'd be great if you had the time to answer them.
Of course. Do you already receive Social Security or SSI benefits and you have a bank account?
Yes, to both.
Then you can sign up for Direct Deposit.
================
My brother is not receiving benefits yet, but he is planning to apply and has a bank account. Can he apply for direct deposit, too?
Yes. Please tell him to take his checkbook or a copy of his bank statement with him.
================
Will do. And how can I sign up for direct deposit?
You can sign up by contacting your bank, credit union or savings and loan association, for example.
================
Great. What if I already have a bank account? What do I need to consider during my application?
Just have your checkbook or a copy of your bank statement with you.
================
And what would someone without an account do?
If you don't have an account, you must open an account before you can sign up for Direct Deposit. You should shop around in your area to find an account that has the features you want at a price you can afford.
