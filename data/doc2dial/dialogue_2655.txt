================
Of course. Do you want to update the address on your license, permit, or ID, vehicle registration and title?
Yes, I do.
Are you moving?
Yes, that's the reason why I want to change the address.
Then you must report a change of residence address on your license, permit, non-driver ID or vehicle records to the DMV within 10 days.
================
What else should I consider if I'm changing my address?
You can write your new address on the back of your driver license, non-driver ID card or learner permit, and on the front of the registration document carefully draw lines through the old address. Do not write the new address on a title certificate.
================
What if I rather want to replace my documents for up-to-date ones?
In that case, be sure to include payment. You can find payment information on the Change Address (MV-232) form.
Can I also change my address by mail?
Sure. To change your license, permit, non-driver ID and/or the address on your registration, simply mail a completed Address Change (MV-232) form to us.
