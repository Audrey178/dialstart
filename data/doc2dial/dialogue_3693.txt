Where can I learn more about VA education and training benefits?
You're in the right place. VA education benefits help Veterans, service members, and their qualified family members with needs like paying college tuition, finding the right school or training program, and getting career counseling.
================
Would you like to know what you can do on our page?
Yes, by all means.
You can get the GI Bill and other education benefits, manage your Veterans education benefits or find more information and resources.

That's quite informative, thank you. Are there any other benefits I should be aware of?
You can explore options for Veterans home loans, apply for your Certificate of Eligibility COE, and get help if you're having trouble making your mortgage payments.
================
About the GI Bill you mentioned before: my brother used that service, but his school closed. Does that mean his benefits are gone for good?
You can apply to restore your GI Bill benefits, if your school closed or your program ended because of a change in VA regulations or a new law. Simply apply to have your GI Bill benefits restored.
================
And are there any other options or programs beside GI Bill?
Of course. If you're a Guardsman, Reservist, or Veteran who doesn't qualify for the Post-9/11 GI Bill, you may be eligible for other VA education benefits.
================
