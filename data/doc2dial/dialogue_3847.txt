How do I surrender New York State plates to the DMV?
================
Do you need to surrender plates from another state?
Yes they are from another state.
They need to be surrendered to the state's DMV they are from then.
================
Will I get a refund once I surrender the plates?
It depends.  You need to go to registrations refunds for surrendered plates to get more information.
================
Do I need to surrender motorcycle plates also?
No you do not need to surrender motorcycle plates.
================
Can someone else turn in the plates for me?
Yes they can do that.  Just make sure that the person gives you the FS-6 receipt for your records.
================
Am I able to surrender my plates just temporarily?
Yes, you can do that.  You can look under special procedures to surrender plates for a season or temporary surrender of plates.
================
How are we notified that they were received if we mail them in?
We  will mail you a FS-6 T receipt if we receive them.
