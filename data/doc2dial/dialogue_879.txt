I would like to file a sexual harassment complaint is this the right place?
================
Are you a Veteran?
Yes.
What if I haven't reported the incident through proper channels.
Generally you can get help through the VA even if you haven't reported the incident and you don't necessarily need proof that it happened.
Who do I contact at VA?
Find a VA medical center near you and ask to speak to the MST coordinator.   You can and should ask for a provider of a specific gender if it makes you feel more comfortable.
I don't want to go to a clinic is that possible?
You can get free confidential counselling , alcohol and drug assessment , and other support at one of our 300 community Vet Centers.
