I went to a mall and stole my car plates in the parking lot, what should I do?
================
I'm sorry for that. Did you surrender your registration?
Yes, I did it.
You must report the theft of your plates and surrender the registration in order to obtain new plates and registration from the DMV.
If the plates were stolen from a trailer without liability insurance, would I have problems requesting new plates? Will I have problems with my license if fail to surrender a stolen plates?
Normally a trailer does not need liability insurance, and no, the DMV will not suspend your driver license if you fail to surrender the plates.
================
I want to give my plates to a friend, is that possible?
Just if that friend will surrender the plates for you.
================
What form should I fill to surrender my plates?
The Plate Surrender Application PD-7 for each set of plates you are surrendering, you must to bring the plates and the form PD-7 to a DMV office.
