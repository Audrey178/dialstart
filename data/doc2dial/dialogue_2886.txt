What is gainful employment information mean?
Gainful employment means in order to get federal student aid a student must take an educational degree at a non-profit or public institution that will prepare them for gainful employment in a recognized occupation.
Is there an special act that states this?
Yes, the Higher Education Act Title IV student assistance programs.
