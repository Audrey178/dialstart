I cannot provide my parents info what can I do?
================
Did you know you could submit your FAFSA form without your parents info?
Yes, I did know there are some situations that I could send without my parents info.
Are your parents incarcerated?
No, my parents are not incarcerated.
Did you leave because of an abusive relationship?
No, my parents were not abusive to me.
Are you not able to get in contact with your parents?
No, I know where they live and could get in contact.
Are you older than 21 but not yet 24 and are homeless?
Yes, I am homeless and not 24 yet.
Then you are able to send in the FAFSA form without filling in your parents info.
================
What should I consider if not including my parents info?
Some state or school based aid programs look at the EFC  in order to determine your eligibility for their funds.  You could be giving up a chance at many resources of aid.
