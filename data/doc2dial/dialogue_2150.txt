Can picture plates be personalized?
================
Yes, some picture plates can be personalized for an additional fee.
Which picture plate are you interested in?
Any of them.
You can browse our collection of picture plates online.
What fees are associated with personalized plates?
There is an annual fee in addition to your vehicle registration renewal fee and will be paid when you renew your registration.  If you renew for two years, there will be two annual fee.
================
How much do personalized plates cost?
Passenger and Commercial plates initial cost is $60 and the annual fee is $31.25.
================
What is considered a personalized plate?
Personalized plates are standard series plates that have a combination of numbers and letters that you select.
================
Are there restrictions?
Yes, plates can not give the appearance of official plates, be obscene, derogatory, or offensive or be combinations that do not have one letter.
