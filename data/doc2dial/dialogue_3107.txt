What payments are available through the Income-Sensitive Repayment Plan who have Federal Family Education Loan Program loans?
The payments under the Income-Sensitive Repayment Plan may increase or decrease based on your income.

Are the FFEL Consolidation Loans eligible?
Yes, they are available for the Income-Sensitive Plan.
================
How can I get more information about FFEL Program loans?
You should contact your loans servicer for more information.
Where should I go to find out about federal student loans I have received?
You should visit My Federal Student aid to view information about all of the federal student loans you have received and to find contact information for the loan servicer or lender for you loans.
================
Are unsubsidized Federal Stafford Loans eligible for Income-Sensitive Repayment?
Yes, they are eligible for Income-Sensitive Repayment.
