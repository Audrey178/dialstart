I am looking for information regarding removing a lienholder from my title certificate
================
Is there a lienholder currently on your title certificate?
Yes there is
Have you recently sold a vehicle?
Yes I have
After selling a vehicle, you don't have to remove a lienholder from the title certificate, you give the original title and lien release to the buyer
================
Can a lienholder remove the lien from my title certificate?
They can apply to have the lien removed electronically
================
I have lost my title certificate and need to remove a lien, what can I do?
You must complete and Application for a Duplicate Certificate of Title and proof that the original lien is satisfied to the address online or bring these to a DMV office. A duplicate title certificate is processed by the DMV and the certificate is mailed to you for a fee of $20.00
Can I request the DMV to remove a lien when I apply for a duplicate title certificate?
You can apply for this by way of mail or at a DMV office
================
Is there any way that I can transfer a title certificate that has a lien listed?
When the DMV processes the title certificate of a new owner, the lien is removed
================
What happens in the case of a lienholder being in possession of my title certificate?
In a n umber of states, the lienholder keeps the certificate until the lien has been satisfied. The lienholder then sends the title certificate to the owner
