Can I use VA health care with other forms of insurance?
Do you have a private insurance plan through an employer?
Yes, I have private insurance through my job.
You can use your private insurance in conjunction with your VA health care.
================
Will I have to pay out of pocket for services that aren't covered?
No, you won't have to pay any unpaid balance that is not covered by your health insurance provider.
Were you assigned to a priority group?
No, not to my knowledge.
================
Will I lose my VA health care coverage if I'm in one of the lower priority groups?
Yes, you may lose these benefits in the future.
Will my family still have coverage if I drop my private insurance plan?
No, they may not have coverage because we don't know if Congress will provide future funding to care for all of the veterans who are signed up for VA health care.
Is all of my health information kept private?
Yes, your private health information is protected.
