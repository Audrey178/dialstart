want to know What is a driver responsibility evaluation?
Yes
The Driver Responsibility Assessment is a fee that you must pay the DMV over a three-year period if you are convicted of certain traffic violations in New York State or accumulate 6 or more points on your driving record within 18 months. .
================
What do I do if I have to pay for a driver responsibility evaluation?
DMV will send you a statement with the amount you owe. You must pay at least the minimum amount by the payment date or your driver's license will be suspended. You can also pay for the full evaluation.
If you receive more than 6 points on your driving record during an 18-month period, what would the annual assessment be?
The annual evaluation is $ 25 for each point, in addition to the original six points. The minimum amount you must pay each year is the annual evaluation.
