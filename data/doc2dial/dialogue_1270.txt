what does VA health care cover?
You are covered for regular checkups with your primary care provider and appointments with specialists like cardiologists , gynecologists , and mental health providers
================
am I still covered if I become homeless and have to move around?
Yes, we offer many programs and services that may help including free health care. We can also help you connect with resources in your community, like homeless shelters or faith-based organizations.
================
do you have a wellness program?
Yes, we have Veterans programs for health and wellness
================
who do we talk to if we need help with a problem?
If you are a Veteran in crisis you can talk to our Veterans Crisis Line responders for confidential help. Many of them are Veterans themselves. This service is free and available 24/7.
================
do you have home care services?
Yes, you can access Veterans health care services like home care
