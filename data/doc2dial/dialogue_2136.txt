Why do you need proof of identity and my date of birth?
================
Do you wish to register an ATV?
Yes, I wish to register an ATV.
In order to register an ATV with us, you must first provide proof of identity and your date of birth.
================
What should I do with my ATV plates if I'm permanently moving it out of state?
You should surrender the plates and all registration documents for the ATV to a local BMV.
================
Can a child under 10 operate an ATV?
A child under 10 can only operate an ATV under adult supervision or without supervision on land that is owned by the parent or guardian.
================
Who writes a report if I crash an ATV while incapacitated?
A report will be written by someone who witnessed the accident  or by an investigating police officer.
When do I have to report an  ATV accident?
You must report an accident if it causes over $1,000 worth of property damages or if it harms or kills someone.
================
Can I drive my ATV on private owned land?
You can drive your ATV on private owned land with permission from the owner.
