Yes I would like some information about changing my out of state driver's license.
Yes we have that information. You must do so within 30 days of becoming a resident of New York.
================
Am I still considered as living in my new state even though my taxes are paid in another state?
Technically, if you pay taxes or your children attend school in another state, a judge considers these facts to decide if your intent is to make NYS a "fixed and permanent" residence.
================
Do I need to show my driver's license when I go through this process?
Yes you do.
================
If I'm still living in 2 states do I need to change my driver's license?
No, you do not need to apply for a New York State driver license until you become a New York State resident.

If my daughter becomes a student at a college in New York, does she need a driver's license from there?
Students from other states, countries or nations who attend school in New York State are normally not considered residents of New York State, so no she doesn't need one specifically from there.
