================
do contractors qualify for the loan forgiveness?
You have to be directly employed by a qualifying employer for your employment to count toward PSLF
================
what can I do if I missed sending in some employment certification forms?
When you apply for forgiveness, you will be asked to submit an Employment Certification Form for each employer you worked for while making the required 120 qualifying monthly payments.
do we need to be employed at a federal job when we apply?
You must be working for a qualifying employer at the time you submit the application for forgiveness and at the time the remaining balance on your loan is forgiven.
