I need help with asking for a review on a fiduciary claim
================
Do you want to add new and relevant evidence to your claim?
yeah in a supplemental claim
The Supplemental Claim option isn’t available for fiduciary claims. You can file a new claim if you have new evidence.
can you help me with what options I have?
Yes, we can help you with the options available
================
do you have the forms for a higher level review?
Yes, we can help you download VA Form 20-0996: Higher-Level Review (PDF)
================
also can you give me the form for a veterans law judge appeal?
You will need to download VA Form 10182 : Board Appeal PDF
================
can I see where the process is once I have asked for one of the reviews?
Yes, you will have the opportunity to track the status of your appeal
================
can I still add the new evidence I have after a decision that doesn't go my way?
You can file a Supplemental Claim or you can appeal to the U.S. Court of Appeals for Veterans Claims.
