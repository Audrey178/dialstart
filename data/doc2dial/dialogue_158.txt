What original documents do I need to get a social security card if any?
================
Yes, you will need original documents that prove your citizenship.
Are you able to verify that you are a U.S. born citizen?
No, I do not have documentation on that, what steps would I need to take?
Do you need to show proof of the child's identity?
Yes, we will need to verify the child's identity in order to issue a social security card.
If my child lives outside the U.S. for extended periods of time what documents would you need to see?
We would need to see a current/previous passport or school/employment records for the child.
Is there other steps to take if I don't have the necessary documentation?
Yes, we can ask to see other current documents that display your date of birth, name, or a recent photo.
What forms of proof does social security need to see?
They would need to verify your identity through id forms such as a U.S. driver's license or a state issued id card.
