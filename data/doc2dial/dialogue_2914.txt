What do I do with my FAFSA student aid report?
You should review your Student Aid Report to make sure everything is correct and complete.
================
What do I need to do if there is a mistake on my SAR?
Did you review your SAR and find a mistake?
No, I did not find any mistakes on my Student Aid Report.
================
Where do I find the SAR Data Release Number?
The DRN appears on the first page in the upper right corner of the paper SAR and it is below the EFC on the electronic SAR.
================
What does the SAR list?
The SAR lists your FAFSA info and provides space for any corrections you need to make and you will receive a paper SAR if you file a paper FAFSA and do not provide an email address.
================
Will the SAR tell me if I have been selected for verification?
Yes, your SAR could contain a note that you have been selected for verification.
