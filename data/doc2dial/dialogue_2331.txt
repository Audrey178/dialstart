hi i want to know how to register a leased vehicle
================
do you want to register a leased vehicle?
Yes
leased the vehicle from a dealer in New York State?
Yes
If you leased the vehicle from a dealer in New York State, the dealer normally registers the vehicle for you by providing all the evidence and your registration request to the DMV. In this case, you don't need to do anything else
================
and can you be a minor?
Must be at least 16 years old, except for ATV records.
================
and what proof do i need?
proof of payment of sales tax, tax exemption, or purchase price see below for instructions to demonstrate proof
================
What if I made the payment in another state?
This process can reduce the amount of your New York State sales tax if the State of New York and the other state have a sales tax agreement.
