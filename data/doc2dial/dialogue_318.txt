What information do I need to know about mailing documents?
================
Do you want to know what happens if mail documents to us?
Yes, I would like to know the information about mailing documents.
If mailing documents, then you must include the Social Security number so that we can match them with the correct application.  Also, do not write anything on the original documents and please write the number on a separate sheet of paper and include it in the mailing envelope with the documents.
================
Will I be able to schedule an appointment to apply for benefits in my preferred language?
Yes, the online application will give you the option to schedule an appointment to apply for benefits in the language you need.
================
What happens after i apply?
After we receive your online application, we will provide confirmation of your application electronically or by mail.
================
What happens if I do not want to mail documents?
You can bring the documents to your local Social Security office.  Please do not mail foreign birth records or any documents from the Department of Homeland Security DHS.
================
What documents do I need to provide?
We may ask you to provide birth certificate or other proof of birth; proof of U.S. citizenship or lawful alien status if not born in the U.S.
================
What are the other ways I can apply?
You can also apply by phone at 1-800-772-1213 from 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. Monday through Friday.
