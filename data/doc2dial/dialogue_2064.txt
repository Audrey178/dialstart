I would like to know how much I need to pay for fees and taxes for my car.
You will need to look at the attached charts. One is for registration fees and one is for taxes.
This is a new car, so it is my first time paying the registration fee.
Okay. You will need to pay for your plate, which is $25, and you will need to pay for your title certificate, which is $50.
================
Do I have to pay any money to the county?
Yes. This is based on how much your car weighs. Also, if you live in one of the MCTD counties, you will have an additional fee.
================
When do I need to pay the vehicle use tax?
You will need to pay the tax when you register a car or renew your registration.
What if I don't live in New York City?
You may be exempt from the vehicle use tax in the county where you live.
================
What if I have an electric vehicle?
You will have a minimum 2 year fee of $32.50.
