Will do. By the way, can I change my address through my VA.gov profile?
Of course. You can sign in to VA.gov and change your address or other contact information in your VA.gov profile. This will update your information across disability compensation, pension benefits, claims and appeals, Vocational Rehabilitation & Employment VR&E, and VA health care.
================
What if I have a disability rating that is higher than 25%?
If you have a disability rating of 30% or higher, you may be able to add eligible dependents to your VA disability compensation to get a higher payment also called a benefit rate.
Can you help me find out how to add dependents that qualify?
Yes, I can help you find out how to add eligible dependents.
================
I recently moved and my address has changed. Does this matter?
It does. Make sure to update your contact information so we send information about your benefits and services as well as any prescriptions, lab test results, or appointment reminders you may need to the right location.
