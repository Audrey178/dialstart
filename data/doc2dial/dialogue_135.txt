I need to get a card for my son who is not currently a citizen.
Currently, only noncitizens who have permission to work by the DHS can apply for a card.
Does your child have permission to work here?
He does not have permission to work. Is there any way else I can get a card?
In this case, more information will be needed to obtain a card.
================
What if he is an F-1 student?
You will need to submit your son's I-20 Certificate of Eligibility for Nonimmigrant Student Status.
I don't have a current DHS document for him. Is there anything else I can use?
Yes, other documents may be accepted, but they need to contain your son's name, his date of birth, and his biological parents' names. We also like it to have a recent photo, but we can accept it without a photo if it has enough information to verify his identity.
Is there any way my son can legally work without DHS authorization?
Yes, you can apply for an Individual Taxpayer ID Number for your son at the IRS. You can visit your local IRS office or call them toll free at 1-800-TAXFORM and request a Form W-7A for Taxpayer ID Number for Pending U.S. adoptions.
Is there any way I can prove his U.S. immigration status?
To prove his status, you need a current U.S. immigration document. For example, an I-551 Lawful Permanent Resident Card or an I-766 Employment Authorization Document.
