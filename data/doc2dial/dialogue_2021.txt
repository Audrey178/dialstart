I have a query regarding problem driver restrictions. How long is the restriction in effect for and is there any way to remove the restriction earlier?
================
Was the license revoked due to an alcohol-related incident?
Yes, it was
If this is the case, the restriction is in effect for five years from the date you receive your driver license
================
Where can I find more information regarding a problem driver restriction with the DMV mandated ignition interlock device?
You can visit the Ignition Interlock page at the NYS Division of Criminal Justice Services website, these device must be fitted in all vehicles you own or operate
What happens if I receive a conviction for driving out of restriction?
If you are convicted of an offense while driving out of restriction, your driver license will be revoked
================
Is there anything I can do if I have a moving violation conviction before I get my driver license or permit with the problem restriction?
If you have been convicted of any of the following violations: following too closely, tailgating or speeding, the approval for a driver license or permit with the problem driver restriction will be withdrawn
================
What if my revocation of my license was not alcohol related?
In this instance, the restriction remains in place for two years from the date you received your driver license or permit
