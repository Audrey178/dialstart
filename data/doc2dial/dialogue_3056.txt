What happens with entrance counseling?
================
Did you do entrance counseling for you Direct Loan?
No, I have not received Entrance Counseling yet.
================
Do you know who should complete the entrance counseling?
Yes, I was told I had to because I received a Direct Loan.
================
How long will this counseling take?
The whole counseling process usually takes from 20-30 minutes and should be completed in one session.
================
Would my school have different requirements?
Yes, your school may have alternate entrance counseling requirements just check with your school's financial aid office to be our website satisfies its requirements for entrance counseling.
================
What do I need to complete the entrance counseling?
You need a verified FSA ID.
================
Where would I go at my school for entrance counseling?
You would go to your financial aid office.
