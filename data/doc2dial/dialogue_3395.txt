What about closed school loan discharges?
================
Do you need to know the benefits you have by receiving a closed school loan discharge?
No, I do not need to know this.
================
Do you have a nonfederal private loan that you want a closed school discharge?
Yes, I have a nonfederal private loan that I want to know about closed school discharge.
You would need to get into contact with that private lender to find out any information about a closed school discharge through them.
================
I received several loans for different programs of study at the same school and was unable to complete any of the programs or transfer credits before the school closed, is it possible that all the loans would be discharged?
The only loans eligible for discharge are the ones for the program you didn't get to complete because of the school closure; not the ones you decided not to complete on your own and moved to another program.
Did you start a program and decide not to continue and move on to another field of study?
No, that is not what happened.
================
Are there certain criteria I need to meet to be eligible for a discharge of my federal student loan?
Yes, certain eligible requirements are needed to get a closed school discharge  and you must apply for it.
