Hi, i was told something about a Driver Responsability fee, what's that?
================
The Driver Responsability Assessment fee is a special fee that you must pay if you receive 6 or more points on your NYS driving record in the last 18 months.
Yeah, that's my case, i have 8 points in my license.
Then you definitely must pay the fee.

I was wondering, about a month ago i was convicted for a violation but it wasn't me, what can i do about it? Is there any resource i can use?
You can use the hearing resource for case revision. But if you're looking for a non-guilty outcome, i'm afraid DMV hearing isn't for that.
================
Is there any way i can put down my points account?
There is. You have the Point and Insurance Reduction Program course. It's a DMV-approved course that will help to prevent you from losing your license in the event you accrued 11 or more points in your record.
================
Isn't that great, huh?.  Now please, if happens i'm out of the state and i'm convicted for any traffic violation, that will affect my record in NYS?
Don't you worry. Unless that violation occurred on Ontario or Quebec, any out-of-state conviction won't be adding any points to your driving record.
