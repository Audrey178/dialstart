What would happen if my obligation to repay my loans or satisfy my TEACH grant obligation is reinstated?
================
You would again be responsible to repay your loans with the terms of your promissory note that you signed.
Did you apply for a discharge of loans?
Yes, I applied for a discharge.
Your status of your loans would go back to the status they were before you applied for the discharge.
================
What if I am a veteran that proved I am totally and permanently disabled?
You would be considered to have received the discharge for a federal tax purposes on the date the discharge was approved.
================
Do I need to resume repayment on my previously discharged loans?
Yes, you must resume repayment of your previously discharged loans or acknowledge that you are once again responsible to meet the terms and conditions of your TEACH grant service obligation.
I received a TPD discharge but would like to go back to school, could I get more student loans or TEACH Grants?
Actually you would be able to but some conditions would need to be met.
What if I received a TPD discharge based on VA documentation?
You would have to give your school a letter from a physician stating you are able to engage in gainful activity, sign a statement that you can't get another TPD discharge.
