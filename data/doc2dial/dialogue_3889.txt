I need to get an extension so I don't get into problems when I'm away
================
Will you be out of state when your inspection expires?
yes
You can apply for an extension. You should also find out if an expired out-of-state inspection is a violation of law in the state you will be in.
================
what day of the month will my inspection expire?
The inspection will expire the last day of the month punched on the inspection sticker
================
do you have a list of what will be checked during the inspection?
You can look at the DMV brochure, New York State Vehicle Safety/Emissions Inspection Program for Cars and Light Trucks, it  lists the equipment items checked during a safety inspection.
================
can you also tell me what to do when an inspection sticker comes off?
You can reattach it to the windshield with clear tape or a small amount of clear adhesive.
what if the sticker is all messed up?
You could have your vehicle reinspected or you can order a replacement sticker.
