What must be true to get disability benefits caused by Agent Orange?
You would need to have an illness we believe is caused by Agent Orange exposure.
I do have a presumptive disease caused from Agent Orange exposure.
Then you are able to get disability benefits from the VA.
================
What will I need to do if I have an illness that is not on the list of Agent Orange related diseases, but think it was caused by Agent Orange?
All you need to do is provide scientific and medical evidence that the condition is related to exposure to Agent Orange or show the problem started during or got worse because of your military service.
