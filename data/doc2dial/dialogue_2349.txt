When must someone renew there registration for a vehicle?
You need to renew your registration before it expires and they will arrive in the mail within ten days; and renewing early will not change the new expiration date.
================
How do I renew at DMV office?
Please bring your registration renewal notice MV-3 or OP-3 along with your payment to the DMV office.
Do you know what to if you don't have your MV-3 or OP-3?
No, I don't know what to do.
================
Are all registrations able to be filed online?
Some registrations are but you cannot renew online if you are eligible for a no fee renewal
How do I find the fee?
The fee is on your renewal notice and also displayed on your computer screen if you decide to renew online.
================
What should I do if my address has changed?
You should always have your current address on file with the DMV and you are not able to change it during the transaction; you would have to change your address online; then make a renewal transaction.
