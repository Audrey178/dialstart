Can you tell me about SSI Benefits?
SSI or Supplemental Security Income program pay benefits to disabled adults and children who have limited income and resources.
================
Can I apply online for SSI and Disability benefits online?
Yes, you can apply online for SSI and Disability benefits.
Is it necessary to apply for benefits online?
Yes, you can apply for Disability and SSI benefits online on your computer.
What is the age a person needs to be to apply for SSI online?
You can file for SSI online but you are eligible if you are 18-65 years old.
