What original documents are needed?
================
We would need to have information about immigration status.
Do you need to know what documents needed to prove your child's U.S. immigration status?
No, I all ready have information to prove my child's U.S. immigration status.
What happens if I do not have the specific documents in the time period?
We will ask to see others documents that are not expired, have to show your name, and birth age and recent photograph.
What do I need if child does not have permission to work?
Lawfully admitted noncitizens can get many benefits and services without social security numbers.  You do not need a number to conduct business with a bank, register for school, or apply for educational test.
What happens if you are a J-1 student?
If you are a J-1 student you must provide a letter from your sponsor and should be on the letterhead of the sponsor with original signature that authorize employment.
What documents do I need to show child's U.S. immigration status?
You must provide your child's current U.S. immigration document such as I-551 Lawful Permanent Resident Card, Machine Readable Immigrant Visa or I-766 Employment Authorization Document, work permit.
