Is out-of-state insurance valid for a vehicle registered in New York?
Not at all.
================
What can I do after receiving a letter from the DMV about a lapse of insurance on my vehicle?
You must send us a copy of your out of state registration in your name along with a copy of your out of state insurance
================
What if i What happens if I register my vehicle in another state and deliver the New York license plates to the New York DMV?
In that case, you can get insurance in the other state. Your insurance must meet the requirements of the state where your vehicle is registered
================
What data should the proof have?
All proof must include the name and address of the registrant and the year, make and vehicle identification number VIN of the vehicle. We will apply your proof to the New York registry to reduce or delete a lapse of insurance coverage reported by your insurance company.
