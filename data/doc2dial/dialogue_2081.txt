Do you sell our personal information to companies without our consent?
We do for a fee. That includes contract sales, a pay-per-search, and over-the-counter.
================
Would you like to know if your information is being sold under contract sales?
Yes, I would like to know if my information is being sold via contract sales.
We sell registration and vehicle data every two years and the information can only be used for purposes such as warranties or safety recalls.
================
What happens if insurance companies cannot access my information through their searches?
The insurance company would need you to seed them a copy of your driver record in order for them to send you a quote. This makes it harder for customers to get affordable rates.
================
How will I be made aware of any safety recalls on my used car?
Manufacturers will be able to contact you about safety recalls by the data they have through their DMV contract sales program.
================
How are insurance companies able to provide quotes so quickly?
They can provide quick quotes because they are able to quickly and easily access your driving records.
================
Is contract sales legal?
Contract sales are legal. They follow New York State Law and federal law.
================
Is anyone able to access the pay-per-search feature?
Yes, to access pay-per-search you must apply at the DMV and sign an agreement form which must then be verified and approved.
