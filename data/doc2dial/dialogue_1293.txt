I have new evidence to add for my claim and want to know what to do
================
Would you like to file a Supplemental Claim?
yes
You need to fill out the Decision Review Request: Supplemental Claim, VA Form 20-0995. You can download the form as a PDF file
================
and I add the evidence in there?
The evidence must be new evidence that the VA didn't have before that is relevant to your case
================
can I select any benefit type on the claim?
The most common benefit type is compensation, but if you’re unsure, check your VA decision. You can’t select multiple benefit types.
================
and what is the address to where I send it?
Department of Veterans AffairsClaims Intake CenterPO Box 4444Janesville, WI 53547-4444
================
what can I send as evidence with my claim?
You can submit evidence you have gotten or ask VA to get evidence, like medical records. VA can’t accept your Supplemental Claim without new and relevant evidence.
