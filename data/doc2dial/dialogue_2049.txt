I need to know what kind of fees I'm going to have to pay when I register.
Is this the first time you've registered your vehicle?
Yes, it's a new car.
First time registrations need to pay the registration and vehicle plate fees.
================
Can I get an estimate of how much it will cost?
Yes, you can estimate the fees online.
================
Do trailer fees work the same way?
No, but there is an online estimator for trailers as well.
What about boat registration?
Boats are like trailers and use their own estimate calculator.
================
Can I estimate the registration taxes online?
Yes, you can estimate the taxes and fees online.
================
Do I have to pay the plate fees again if I transfer them to a different vehicle?
No, you won't have to pay plate fees again.
Is there a fee for the plates for my car?
Yes, there is a vehicle plate fee as well as a registration fee.
