What can I do if I don't agree with a board decision?
================
Do you have new and relevant evidence to support your case?
Yes, I have new and relevant information that will support my case.
Then you may file a Supplemental Claim or appeal to the U.S. Court of Appeals for Veterans Claims.
================
When can I request a Board Appeal?
You can request a Board Appeal after an initial claim, Supplemental Claim, or Higher-Level Review decision; and you can't request to appeals in a row.
================
Is there help available with a decision review?
A Veterans Service Organization or VA-accredited attorney can help you request a decision review.
================
I have more questions, how do I find the answers?
You can look at frequently asked questions about decision reviews under FAQs.
================
If VA schedules an exam what do I do?
It is in your best interest not to miss the scheduled exams.
