What kind of documents are required to get a card?
================
You need an original proof of citizenship.
If it's for a child, you need a State issued birth certificate recorded before the child was 5.
What if her birth certificate wasn't issued when she was born?
Does your child have a passport?
No, she doesn't have a passport.
What if I can't get a hold of the documents?
There are other documents we can consider, such as an employee ID card if it has your name, age, and is not expired.
What can I use for a child without a passport?
You can submit an adoption decree or state issued ID card.
My older child used to live in another country, how can I get him ma Social Security card?
Submit his passport or school records as proof he was never issued a SSN.
Will you need proof of my identity?
Yes, you can use your driver's license or state ID card.
