What should I expect after taking my photo?
After taking your photo we will process your VHIC and mail it to you. You should receive your card within 10 days.

What if you change your medical center during the trip since you received your original card?
You can contact the medical center where you currently receive care. Or, call us at 877 - 222 - 8387, Monday through Friday, 8:00 a.m. at 8:00 p.m.

and what do these new cards offer?
It also offers other useful new features. This includes "VA" printed in braille to help low vision Veterans quickly recognize the card, as well as an display of their latest branch of service emblem and a list of special awards they may have received.

and can I use this card as a credit card?
Sorry. You cannot use your VHIC as a credit card or insurance card. You do not pay copays or authorize care in non-VA health facilities.
