I need help with retirement benefits.
================
Are you and your spouse divorced?
Not currently.
Is there a limit if I'm still working?
Yes, the benefit earnings limit still applies.
If I'm eligible for benefits for me and my ex-spouse, which do I get?
Your retirement benefits would be paid first.
What if my ex's benefits are higher?
You'll get the difference, so the combination is equal to the higher amount.
What if my ex re-married?
There ways to qualify even if they re-married.
