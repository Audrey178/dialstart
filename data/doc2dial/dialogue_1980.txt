I would like to register a moped and I have a few questions.
================
Just to make sure, you are certain your motorcycle is classified as a moped, correct?
Not quite, what is the legal definition of a moped?
New York law defines a "moped" or a"motor scooter" as a limited use vehicle with two or three wheels
================
What should I do if the county I'm in doesn't have online reservation?
You would need to make a reservation through one of our office locations, you can check the locations and hours.
What If I don't have one of these required documents?
You must provide other kinds of proof of both identity and age.
================
Should I pay attention to any special requirements to operate a moped?
The requirements are the same as those for motorcycles. You must have a driver license and register your mopen to drive it on streets and highways. There are some exceptions for these requirements though.
