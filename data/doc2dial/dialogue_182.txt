Is it possible for me to apply for benefits online?
================
Do you want to file for retirement benefits, disability benefits, or Medicare?
Yes, I would like to apply for at least one of those benefits.
Then you can always complete your application online.
What if you cannot process my request?
You would be able to contact us by phone or schedule an appointment if you cannot process your request.
What if I am the spouse of a retired or disabled worker?
You should fill out your application for monthly benefits or just Medicare online within 3 months of age 62 or older and must meet certain criteria.
