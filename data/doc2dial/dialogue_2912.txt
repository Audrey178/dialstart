I have a few questions about FAFSA and the Student Aid Report.
I can help you with that. Your Student Aid report summarizes the information in your FAFSA form, so be sure to check that your information is both correct and complete.
================
What if my information is incorrect on my SAR?
There will be a space on your SAR where you can write in any corrections.
================
Do I need to correct any mistakes on my SAR?
Yes. It is very important that you correct or update any incorrect information on your FAFSA form.
================
How do I receive my SAR?
You will receive your SAR on paper. If you provide an email address, you will also be able to view your SAR online.
Did you provide an email?
Yes, I did.
Great. You will receive an email from noreply@FAFSA.gov. This email will provide you with instructions to get an online copy of your SAR.
Will I receive a paper SAR if I get the online copy?
Yes. Whether you provide an email or not, you will still receive a paper SAR copy.
