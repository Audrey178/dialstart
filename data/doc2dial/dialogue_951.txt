How do I change my GI Bill school or program?
You can find out when and how to request changes to your GI Bill school, program, or other VA education benefits to keep getting funding.
================
Do you need to request changes to my GI Bill or other VA education benefits?
Yes, I need to know what situations I need to request changes to my GI Bill.
You will need to request changes to your benefits if things change.
When do I need to request changes?
When your schooling changes or changing professions.
================
What if I am a dependent of a Veteran or service member?
You will need to submit a Dependents Request for Change of Program or Place of Training VA Form 22-5495 online.
================
What if I need to receive benefits while active duty?
n/a

What do I need to do with form when mailing?
Just fill out the form and mail lit to VA regional claims processing office that is by your school.
