How can I qualify for disability benefits?
are you looking to qualify for Social Security disability benefits?
yes, that's correct.
The first requirement you must meet is to have worked in jobs covered by Social Security and then you must have a medical condition that meets Social Security's definition of disability.
================
Could I work on my previous job if I receive the benefits?
If after reviewing your case we decide that despite your condition you still can work then you wouldn't qualify for the benefits.
================
Is there a way to make the application process faster?
For disabled people, they can file an Adult Disability Report to speed up the process.
================
How should a survivor widow apply for disability benefits?
They can't do it online but they can contact Social Security at 1-800-772-1213 to apply for disability benefits.
================
And for an adult child with a disability?
We must evaluate his condition to determine if it meets our definition of disability.
