I registered my vehicle out of state, and then received a letter from the DMV about an insurance lapse in the vehicle. What I can do?
Send us a copy of your out-of-state record in your name along with a copy of your out-of-state insurance if available
================
What happens if my valid insurance coverage expires?
We will take action against your New York State registration and driver's license, even if you move from New York. This can affect your ability to have a driver's license in your new state.
