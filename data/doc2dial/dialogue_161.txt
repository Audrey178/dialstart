================
For starters you would need your proof of citizenship.

Is there a website where I can get more information?
Yes, we have a Social Security Number and Card website.
================
What if don't have the specific documents that are required?
If we don't receive the documents within 10 days we can look at other documents as long as they aren't expired and show your name and identifying information such as a birthday or a photo.
What counts as proof of identity?
Any document that isn't expired that has information about you such as a driver's license or passport.
What specific cards show proof of identity?
You can use a U.S. driver's license, a state ID card, or a U.S. passport.
================
Am I able to request a replacement card online?
Yes, ordering online is a fast and secure way to get a replacement card.
