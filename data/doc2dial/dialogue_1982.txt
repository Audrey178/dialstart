Hello, I would like to register my moped. How do I proceed?
================
Is your vehicle a moped or a motorcycle?
It is a moped not a motorcycle.
================
Do you have a driver's license and is your moped registered?
I'm about to receive my driver's license but my moped is not registered.
================
Once you get your driver's license, you need to come register your moped at the DMV.
Okay, I will do that.
Before going to the DMV, please complete the Document Guide. It will inform you of the correct documents to bring with you.
================
I still haven't received my driver's license, can I use my learner's permit from another state?
Yes as long as it is from one of the Tri-States.
Can I see a list of these eligible counties?
They are listed on the website on the office locations page.
Are the offices open on Saturdays?
Yes some of our offices are open on Saturdays.
================
If I want to eventually get a moped that has three wheels, do I need a different kind of driver's license?
All new vehicles must be registered again at the DMV.
