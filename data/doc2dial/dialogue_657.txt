How is the amount of money earned per credit calculated?
How the system works is:You earn one Social Security or Medicare credit for every $ 1,410 in covered earnings each year and you must earn $ 5,640 to get the maximum four credits for the year. Please remember that the amount of earnings it takes to earn a credit may change each year.
