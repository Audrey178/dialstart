Do I have to get insurance to register my vehicle?
================
Yes, you have to. DMV requires auto liability insurance to be able to register a vehicle in New York.
================
I have a vehicle registered in New York, where should I get the insurance.
for vehicles registered in New York, you have to get a New York State auto liability insurance coverage.
================
Should the copy of my Insurance Identification Card be perfectly legible?
Yes. The DMV will not accept a card if our barcode reader can't read the barcode.
================
Should I get insurance for a motorcycle?
Yes. If you plan to ride your motorcycle on any public road or highway, you must have insurance coverage.
================
So can I use a copy or fax of my Insurance Identification Card to give it to the DMV?
We do accept copies and faxes of Insurance identification cards, just make sure that the barcode is perfectly legible or it will get rejected if the DMV barcode reader can't scan it.
