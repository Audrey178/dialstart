Could forgiveness of my federal student loan be available if the school I attended misled me or engaged in misconduct in violation of certain laws?
Yes, this is available and the loan forgiveness is called borrower defense to repayment and you can learn more.
================
What if I accidentally used two email addresses associated with my borrower defense application?
The ED will send a notification email to both of the email addresses on file.
================
What are some common questions that are answered regarding forbearance or stopped collections status?
Some common questions are what happens while completing and submitting a borrower defense application and the answer is you may have all your federal student loans in repayment placed into forbearance and collections stopped; but interest will still accrue.
Has your application for borrower defense been denied?
No, my application has not been denied.
================
Where would I find more info about forgiveness of federal student loan I used to attend Corinthian Colleges?
You can go to info about Debt Relief for Corinthian Colleges Student page.
================
Can I have my borrower defense discharge applied to my FFEL or Federal Perkins loans?
Yes, you are able to consolidate your loans into a Direct Consolidation Loan and obtain a discharge.
