What are Direct Plus Loans?
Direct Plus Loans are federal loans that parents of dependent undergraduate students can use to help pay for college or career school which help pay for education expenses not covered by financial aid.

When can I can my loan after it is disbursed?
You may cancel all or part of the loan within certain time frames and the information you receive along with your promissory note will explain the procedures and the time frames for cancelling your loans.
