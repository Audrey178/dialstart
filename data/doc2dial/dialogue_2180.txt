Good morning. I would like to know how I know if my driving record shows my PIRP course completion?
================
Good morning! Do you need to verify that your course is completed?
Yes, if it is on my DMV record.
No problem, you can request a copy of your driving record.
================
When will the DMV know if I have completed the course?
They will know within 10 weeks.
================
If my license has been revoked what point effect does that have on my driving record?
If your license has been revoked, the point reduction will not affect it.
================
Ok, where can I see more information?
You can check out the NY State Point System for more information about how points are calculated.
================
What if I completed the course, but it is not on my driving record after 10 weeks?
Then you should contact the course sponsor.
So I would contact the course sponsor? Then what?
Yes, they would have to tell you what to do next.
================
What does point reduction refer to?
 Point reduction applies to points assessed for violations that occurred within the eighteen months immediately before course completion.
