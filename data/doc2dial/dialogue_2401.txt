I received a suspension order for an unsatisfied fault related to a car accident, collision or crash. What I need to do?
If you were notified that there is an unsatisfied suspension of judgment on your record, you should contact the attorney or court that issued the judgment. The information on the suspension order will tell you the name of the court and the court's index number for the judgment. The DMV cannot erase the suspension without proof from the court.
