How do I get an insurance premium reduction?
================
The course sponsor will mail you a completion certificate within 45 business days after you complete the classroom course or 14 business days after you complete an online course
Have you presented your certificate to your insurance company within 90 days after you completed the course?
No
Why not?
I presented it after 90 days
In that case the insurer may issue the premium discount effective from the date presented
================
How can I reduce driving record points?
Contact your insurance company or agent to learn if both reductions will be given at the same time
================
Do you need any more information on reducing driving record points?
Yes
================
Any other ways to reduce driving record points?
Any insured driver who is the principal operator and completes a Point & Insurance Reduction Program course will receive the 10 % reduction
================
What if more than one person named on a policy completes the PIRP course?
Only the principal operator can receive the 10 % reduction
