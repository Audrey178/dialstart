I have some questions about obtaining a REAL ID.
================
Will you be adding a veteran status to your REAL ID?
Yes, I will be.
Great. You will have to apply for this at your local DMV office.
What do I need to bring with me?
You will need to bring your proof of honorable discharge and proofs of identification.
================
I was charged a document fee when I applied to have the Veteran Indicator put on my license, do I have to pay again?
No. Not only will you not have to pay another fee, you qualify for a refund of the original fee.
Can I apply by mail?
Yes. You will need to complete an Application for Permit, Driver License, or Non-Driver ID Card form.
================
What proofs do I need?
There is a guide on our website that will help you find the proofs of identification that you need.
