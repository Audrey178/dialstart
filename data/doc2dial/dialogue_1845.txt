Can you tell me when do I need to surrender my plate¡
You should surrender your vehicle's plate before you end the vehicle's liability insurance coverage.
================
I lost my plate, what should I do?
You have to report your lost plate and surrender the registration to the DMV. The DMV will issue you a new registration and plate.
================
Can another person surrender the plate for me and what would I need after?
you just have to make sure that the other person gives you the FS-6 T receipt for your records.
================
What about refunds. will I get a refund for my surrenderd plate?
That depends. You can check the section "registrations refunds for surrendered plates" for more info about that
================
In case I need to surrender my plate temporarily, can I do it?
Yes, you can. There's more info about this in the section " special procedures to surrender plates for a season - temporary surrender of plates"
================
How should I mail my plate so I can get the FS-6 T receipt?
You should send the plate via registered or certified mail and request a return receipt, that way you can be notified by the US Postal Service that we received the plate.
