Tell me about county taxes and supplemental fees
================
This are taxes that all residents of New York City and several counties in New York must pay for the registration of a commercial vehicle or the renewal of any commercial vehicle registration. These taxes are collected by the DMV for the city and the counties.
================
What about the supplemental MCTD fee for taxis?
In the case of a passenger vehicle like a taxi, the supplemental MCTD fee is $50 for two years. All registrants for TC vehicles must pay the tax,
How much is the vehicle use tax?
It's $80 for 2 years for all passenger vehicle registrations and renewals. For Livery and for-hire vehicles, the tax is $400 for one year.
My vehicle registration is from Manhattan, New York. Does the tax apply to my vehicle?
Yes, your location apply to the vehicle use tax for all passenger registrations and renewals.
================
