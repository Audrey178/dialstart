What do I need to do before I take my road test?
Once you have a learner permit you must obey the learner permit restrictions on where and when you may drive.
================
What should I do if my view is blocked after stopping at a crosswalk?
Move up until you can clearly see your way, then stop again if necessary.
================
Are there restrictions on a learner permit?
Yes, there are restrictions on where and when you may drive.
================
How do I find a pre-licensing course?
To find one in your area look online or in your telephone directory.
What is covered in the Pre licensing course?
The course covers many important topics such as driving within the highway transportation system.
