You can have your vehicle re-inspected or you can request a replacement sticker using the PDF VS-118 Replacement Inspection Sticker Request Form. Complete the form and mail it to the address on the form.
If my vehicle's inspection tag has expired, will I be fined?
If your vehicle's inspection tag has expired in the past 60 days, you may be fined $ 25 to $ 50.
