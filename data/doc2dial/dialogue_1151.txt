How would I get help filing a claim or appeal?
Do you need help to file your claim?
Yes, I need help filing my claim.
The best way to file your claim would to work with an accredited attorney, claims agent, or a Veterans Service Officer or VSO because they are trained and certified in VA claims and appeals processes.
================
How does one become an accredited representative or a VSO?
Each accredited representative or a VSO needs to pass an exam and a background check.
================
Why is it best to use an accredited attorney?
It is best to use an accredited attorney because they are specifically trained and certified in teh VA claims process.
What do the VSOs do?
The VSOs work for the Veterans and service members as well as their dependents.
================
Does it cost alot to use an accredited representative or a VSO?
Actually, you are not charged a fee to help file your initial application for benefit; but can for unusual expenses.
