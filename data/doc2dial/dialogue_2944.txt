In that situation you would have to contact your loan servicer to discuss repayment options.

How can I get a jump on the application process?
You can ask your current school counselors and the college financial aid office about state, college, and nonprofit grants and scholarships that you can apply for.
================
I was accepted at 3 colleges, which do I consult?
It would be wise to compare their costs and aid offers. Accept the aid from the school that's best for you and inform them of other sources of aid such as scholarships you expect to receive.
What is an aid offer?
An aid offer explains the types and amounts of aid a college is offering you, and includes your expected costs for the year.
