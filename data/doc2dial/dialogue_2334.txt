Let's say I leased a vehicle from a dealer located in NY, do I have to register it?
The dealer normally registers the vehicle for you by providing all your proofs and your registration application to the DMV. In this case, you don't need to do anything more.

Yes, you can bring your completed registration application and additional documents to a local DMV office.
