What if I have an endorsement of H or X?
In that case, you must pass the hazardous materials knowledge test at a DMV office within 2 years of your renewal and pay an additional $5 fee for the test. If you are a resident from Bronx , Kings Brooklyn , New York Manhattan , Queens , Richmond Staten Island ; Dutchess , Nassau , Orange , Putnam , Rockland , Suffolk or Westchester, you must pay a supplemental Metropolitan Commuter Transportation District (MCTD) fee. The MCTD fee is $1.00 for every 6 months the permit or driver license will be in effect.

Where should I renew?
You can renew a commercial driver license (CDL) or commercial license permit (CLP)  at a DMV office. You must complete a guide to make sure you have the proofs of identification you need before coming.
