what if my license is already revoked though?!
if your license has already been revoked or suspended [22] the point reduction will not affect that action

[24] the point reduction will not affect that action.
================
will the PIRP course sponsor actually notify the DMV??
The PIRP course sponsor will notify the DMV within 10 weeks after you complete the courses [11] DMV will then reduce the active points on your driving record by up to 4 points
