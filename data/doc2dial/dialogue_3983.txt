================
of course you can see the section Buy or sell a vehicle transfer property
don't you have some of these tests found there?
Yes
look at other types of tests you can use.
Can I bring only the copy of the Certificate of Title?
Must carry original New York State Certificate of Title or certified copy, not a photocopy with property transfer section completed by seller
================
and should I deliver the plates?
If you do not want to transfer the registration, you must deliver the vehicle plates to the DMV. You may be eligible for a refund if you turn in the plates before registration expires.
================
And what should I bring if the vehicle is a 1972 model year or older?
You must bring an original or certified copy of the New York State transferable record, not a photocopy and a bill of sale
and what vehicles can be transferred?
Cars, trucks, trailers and motorcycles
