Do I need to meet certain requirements to get federal student aid?
Yes there are basic eligibility criteria to receive student aid.
================
What is federal student aid used for?
FSA must only be used for educational purposes.
What amount of enrollment should I be in?
 To get Direct Loan Program funds, you must be enrolled at least half-time.
Along with being registered with selective service at age 18-25 what are is another criteria?
You must be enrolled or accepted for enrollment as a regular student in a eligible degree or certificate program to get student aid.

Can I sign the form saying I am not in default on a federal student loan?
Yes you would have to sign the certification on the FAFSA form stating that you are not in default.
