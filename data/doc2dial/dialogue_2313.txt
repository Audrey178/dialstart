I got married and changed my name and wanted to know how to get it on my replacement registration
================
You can't change your name while asking for a replacement registration.  If you get a replacement registration it will show your old name.
Did you change your name recently?
yes

can I get my replacement online?
You can replace registration documents online for most vehicles but you cannot replace registration documents online for vehicles older than model year 1973.

We occasionally use "session cookies" to enhance or customize your use of our website.
