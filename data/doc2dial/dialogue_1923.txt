What exactly does Self-Certification mean in relation to driving?
================
Is there any way that I can change my self-certification driving type online?
Unfortunately, you must go to a DMV office to change your self-certification driving type
================
Is there any way for me to know that the DMV received my self-certification/Medical Examiner's Certificate Information?
What action do I follow if I am self-certified to driving types NI & NA?
To confirm that you are self-certified to driving types NI & NA, you should have a medical certification status of either "Certified" or "Not Certified"
================
I received my Medical Examiner's Certificate back in the mail from the DMV along with a rejection letter, why is this?
The rejection letter explains why this was returned to you
Does the rejection letter mention anything about missing or illegible or incorrect information?
No, all information that I supplied was correct
================
What can I do if my CDL gets downgraded because of my "not certified" medical status?
In this instance, you will need to attend your local DMV office to upgrade your license back to a CDL
