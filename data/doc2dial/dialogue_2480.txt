I'm getting ready to move. Will that effect the hearing?
No, you will just need to provide your new address, so that a Notice of Hearing can be mailed to you. Be advised that this doesn't update your address on other DMV records. You will need to do that separately.
================
What if it's a bicycle-related offense?
Any convictions for bicycle offenses do not require the surcharge payment and no points will be applied to your driving record.
