How do I remove a lienholder from a title?
================
Do you want to remove a lienholder from a title certificate?
Yes

No

Yes

================
Should I keep a copy for my own records after I transfer it?
ou can transfer the title certificate and give the new owner the original proof that any lien listed on the title certificate was satisfied. Keep a copy of the proof for your records
