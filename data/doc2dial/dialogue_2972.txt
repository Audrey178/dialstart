I need info on a loan for a school I dropped out of
Okay, we can help you learn more about the unpaid refund discharge to see whether you might qualify
Did you withdraw from school after receiving a loan made under the William D. Ford Federal Direct Loan Direct Loan Program or Federal Family Education Loan  Program?
yes
The school may have been required under federal regulations to return some or all of the loan money to your loan servicer
What if the school didn't return anything after I left?
You could be eligible for a discharge of the portion of your loan that the school failed to return
================
what do I do to see about that discharge for the loan?
You will need to complete the Loan Discharge Application: Unpaid Refund,  and send the completed form to your loan servicer
================
is the discharge the same if you are the parent and you got the loan?
Yes,  the amount of the loan that the school should have returned will be discharged. You can contact your loan servicer for more information.
================
can I just go to the school and ask for the loan back?
You can contact that school and attempt to resolve the issue with the school before applying for an unpaid refund discharge.
