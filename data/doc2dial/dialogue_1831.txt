Hello, good morning. I recently moved here to New York and I want to get an Insurance for my new car. I was hoping you could help me with the requirements, and also telling me if my out of state insurance document would work?
================
For the requirements you just need your ID, your car, and the minimum wave coverage which is $10000, go to a DMV and you can begin your process. Regarding out of states documents: No. We will NEVER accept out of states insurance documents/coverages
But what can I do with my insurance from another state?
If your coverage time haven't lapse you can still use it there. Whenever you go to a different state, that you have an insurance in, DMV will cover you there. So, if you ever go back to that state your insurance will still work.
If my vehicle is registered here in new york, would that be a problem when I visit another state I have a insurance in?
If your vehicle is registered in New York, it MUST have a New York insurance. If you go to texas (where you have a Insurance coverage) you'll have to first register the vehicle there, in order to be able to use their insurance.
================
 What if I don't use the vehicle?
As long as your registration is valid, your insurance will remain in effect.
Does that work for motorcycles too?
Motorcycles are the black sheep in the family:Your motorcycle must have insurance coverage any time it is operated on a public road or highway. Unlike most other motor vehicles, motorcycles are registered for one year rather than two. By any means your motorcycle should operate on public highways without liability coverage in effect.
================
When do my motorcyle registration expire?
On April 30.
