Is there an additional fee to have icons added to my DMV photo document?
================
There are no additional fees if you request the icons be added at the time of your photo document renewal.
What if I go ahead and go into the DMV now wanting the icons added onto my license?
If you wish to have the icons added prior to renewal, you will be required to pay the amendment fee as required by the Vehicle and Traffic Law, which is either $12.50 for driver licenses or $5.00 for non-driver identification cards.
================
What should I do if I do not receive my new photo document with the icons?
For Lifetime Sportsman, Small/Big Game, Bow Hunting, Trapping, Muzzle Loading, or Fishing, you can contact DEC via their website, which is www.dec.ny.gov.
================
Where is my document number located for my DMV issued photo document?
It is now located on the back of the photo document.
