I've temporarily taken one of my vehicles off the road. Do I need to return the professional plate?
================
No, you can keep your plate and store it at most DMV offices.
How do I register my vehicle in the future?
Yeah, I plan to register it again later on.
When you do register your vehicle again, you need to visit the DMV office that has your plates. Don't forget to bring you receipt form FS-6 T and other documents needed to register your vehicle.
================
Where can I get more information about professional plates?
Your local police or parking enforcement agency can give you more information about parking regulations.
Do I need to pay a custom plate fee when I renew?
No. If your registration is in the first year of a two year registration, you'll need to submit a request for refund for motor vehicles and drivers license to get a refund.
================
Is picture plate and professional plate the same thing?
Yes, they are.
================
Can I change my professional plates to standard plates for a vehicle?
Yes, if you return your professional plates, you will be issued standard plates for your vehicle.
