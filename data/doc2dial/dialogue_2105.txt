I need help with plate storage
================
The DMV does not store standard vehicle plates
Are you wishing to surrender standard plates?
No
================
What do I need to do to the plates before surrendering them?
Be sure to remove plate frames and fasteners before you surrender them, we will not accept plates with frames or fasteners attached
================
How long do I have to reinstate my registration before it explires?
You can renew the registration and pay the renewal fees
================
How do I surrender personalised license plates?
The DMV office will store them, you must then apply for the reinstatement of the registration at the same DMV office where you surrendered your personalized plates or custom plates
================
What form do I need to get back surrendered plates?
Bring Application for Registration or Title pdf MV-82 to a DMV office.
