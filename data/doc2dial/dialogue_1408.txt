Can you tell me about applying for VA Voc Rehab adn Employment?
I can help you find out about VA Voc Rehab and Employment also known as Vocational Rehabilitation and Employment, VR&E, or Chapter 31 benefits.
================
Is there a way to apply even though I already have a VA disability rating?
Yes, you can sign into your eBenefits account.
================
What happens once an entitlement decision has been made?
You and your counselor will work together to develop a plan for rehabilitation which will outline rehabilitation and other services that VR&E will provide to you.
================
What is the process after VRC creates my rehabilitation plan?
Your VRC will help put your plan into action with you and the will provide ongoing counseling and help get tutorial assistance, job training, work-readiness support, and the medical referrals you will need.
================
What happens after I apply?
A meeting will be scheduled for you with a VRC to find out if you have an employment handicap and if you are eligible VR&E benefits and services.
================
