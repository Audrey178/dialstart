Hello, I have an issue. I withdrew from school and I'm wondering about some things regarding the return of my loan funds.
You're in the right place then. Here you can learn more about the unpaid refund discharge to see whether you might qualify.
Did you withdraw from school after receiving a loan made under the William D. Ford Federal Direct Loan Direct Loan Program or Federal Family Education Loan FFEL Program?
No, that isn't the case.
Did the school NOT make a required return of the loan funds after you withdrew?
No, they returned the loan funds.
================
So how much of my loan will be discharged if I was approved for that?
Only the portion of your loan that your school should have returned will be discharged. Contact your loan servicer for more information.
================
What if I'm ready to apply for the unpaid refund discharge?
Then you must complete the Loan Discharge Application: Unpaid Refund and send the completed form to your loan servicer.
What if I want to apply for an unpaid refund discharge, but my school has closed down?
In that case you should first determine if you may be eligible for a closed school discharge instead. Contact your loan servicer for more information. Find out who your loan servicer is by logging in to My Federal Student Aid.
