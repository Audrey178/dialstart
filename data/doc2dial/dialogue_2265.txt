Tell about the investigation process
================
The AFI will conduct a formal investigation. The result may not be any action, an investigation notification warning letter placed in your file, or a formal administrative hearing before an administrative law judge (ALJ).
Did the vehicle safety start the hearing process?
No, not yet
Was the waiver accepted?
Yes, it was
In that case, no hearing is required
================
What if I am revoked?
Then you need to surrender all supplies and cease to operate
================
What if the offer is rejected?
A hearing must be held
================
And what happens if the ALJ only imposes civil penalties?
To avoid losing your operating privilege, you must pay all fines and penalties. You will receive a letter from the DMV after the hearing.
