Excuse me, I need some help with the social security direct deposit.
================
Of course. Are you already receiving benefits?
Yes, I am.
Do you already receive Social Security or SSI benefits and you have a bank account?
No, I don't have an account yet.
How can I sign up for direct deposit?
You can sign up by contacting your bank, credit union or savings and loan association.
What if I don't have an account?
Then you must open an account before you can sign up for Direct Deposit. You should shop around in your area to find an account that has the features you want at a price you can afford.
================
What if I didn't receive any benefits yet, but I was planning to apply?
Then you should tell the Social Security representative when you apply for benefits that you want to sign up for Direct Deposit.
