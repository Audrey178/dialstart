My vehicle is already registered in New York
================
What transactions can I do online?
Some of the transactions you can do online are: Change your address, change the name or other information and check the status of your registration.
================
Tell me about registering a vehicle in New York
You have to register any vehicle owned by you or your business at a New York DMV office
================
Would there be any problem if a don't answer a parking ticket?
yes. The locality may contact the DMV about it and this can prevent the renewal of your vehicle and even suspend your vehicle registration.
================
Ok, can you tell me now what do I need to register a vehicle?
You should first complete the DMV's Document Guide to make sure that you have all the proofs of identification and documents you need before going to the DMV office.
================
where should I register my business vehicle?
If your business is located in New York then you should register it at the lock DMV office.
