How do I obtain a social security card for a child not born in the U.S.?
Typically, you can only get a card if you have permission to work from the DHS.
What if my child does not have permission to work but would still like to apply for a card?
Please find the section titled "If your child doesn't have permission to work" for more information.
Is the DHS the department that gives out permission to work?
Yes, the DHS deems which noncitizens have permission to work.
