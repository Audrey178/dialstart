How can I manage my VA benefits and health care?
================
Do you want to know about how to access and manage your health care?
Yes, I would like to know what I would be able to access.
You can refill and track your prescriptions, send a secure message to your healthcare team, and view appointments and schedule them.
================
Does the VA have an official podcast?
Yes, it is called Borne the Battle and it contains stories of inspiring Veterans, important resources, offices, and VA benefits.
================
What service member benefits would I find out about?
You will find out about eligibility during service and which time-sensitive benefits to consider when you are down serving.
================
Can family members get benefits?
Yes, your spouse, dependent, or survivor can get them.
================
What can I check about education benefits?
You will be ablel to check your Post-9/11 GI Bill benefits; view payment history; or change your current education benefits.
================
What about my disability benefits can I check on VA.gov?
You can check your claim or appeal status, view payment history, or upload evidence to support your claim.
