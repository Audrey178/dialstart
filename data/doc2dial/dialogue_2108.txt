Hello, I stored my plates before last winter, how do I reinstate them.
================
You must apply for reinstatement at the DMV and bring a completed Application for Registration or TitleMV-82.
Is your registration still active?
Yes but it is going to expire in 3 months. Is that okay?
Yes that complies with the rules.
Will there be a fee to renew?
Yes renewal and fee payment will ensure that your registration is valid.
================
Which DMV do I have to go to to retrieve my plates?
You must return to the same DMV office where you surrendered your plates.
Were my plates stored there?
Yes they were.
What if the plates that I stored have been destroyed?
You can apply with the DMV for the reinstatement of the registration at any office that processes these types of registration transactions. A new set of vehicle plates will be issued and can charge a  fee of up to $ 25.00.
