I need some info about how to renew a registration, please
You have to renew your registration before it expires. Your new registration documents will arrive in about 10 days, check your mail. Renewing early will not change the new expiration date for your new registration.
================
Can you give me more information?
================
Is your registration about to expire or has it expired?
Yes, it has expired.
In that case, you an renew online up to one year past the expiration date. And you can download and print a temporary registration that's valid for 10 days the day you renew, if you wish.
================
What if I don't know the correct amount to renew?
You can send a check payable to 'Commissioner of Motor Vehicles', but do not write the amount, please.
================
Is there another way to renew?
Yes, you can do it online.
================
What if my address has changed?
In that case, your mailing address on DMV files must be current. You can't change it during the transaction. Please, you need change your address online first and then renew
