How long will driver restriction be in effect?
================
Is your revocation due to an alcohol-related incident?
Yes
The restriction remains in effect for five years from the date you receive your driver license or permit
================
What happens if I have a moving violation conviction before I get my driver license or permit with the problem driver restriction?
The approval for a driver license or permit with the problem driver restriction will be withdrawn if you are convicted of any of these violations: following too closely tailgatings or speeding , speed contest
================
What if I have been convicted?
Your driver license will be revoked
================
Can I operate a commercial vehicle in another state if I have a CDL?
A driver with a CDL issued in another state can not operate a commercial motor vehicle in New York State with a problem driver restriction on their New York State record
================
How long with my license be revoked?
Your driver license will be revoked for a minimum of 30 days. You may then apply for to the DIU for a new license and must pay a non - refundable $ 100 application fee
================
Where can I get more info on installation fees?
Visit the Ignition Interlock page at the NYS Division of Criminal Justice Services website
Do I need to install an ignition lock device?
You may be required to install an ignition interlock device on any and all vehicles you own and/or operate. You are also subject to the problem driving restrictions
