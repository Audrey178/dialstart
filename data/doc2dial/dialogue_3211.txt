Is there certain requirements I need to qualify for federal student aid?
Yes we can go over the basic eligibility criteria.
================
Is it necessary for me to register for Selective Service if I am a male to receive federal student aid?
Yes since you are a male you must register with Selective Service to receive federal student aid.
================
How many credits must I take to be eligible for Direct Loan Program funds?
You must be enrolled at the college for at least half-time to be eligible for Direct Loan Program funds.
What is another criteria beside being registered with Selective Service ?
Another criteria is to be enrolled or accepted for enrollment as a regular student in an eligible degree or certificate program.
================
Why must I register for Selective Service if I want federal student aid?
You must register with Selective Service if you are a male and want to receive federal student aid.
