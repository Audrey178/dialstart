How can I get a Surety Bond?
DMV doesn't provide Surety Bonds. You must contact a surety insurance company and have them fill out Dealer Bond Under NY State Vehicle and Traffic Law Section 415 6-B VS-3. Please, make sure that the business name and mail address of your bond match exactly those that appear on your application.
================
Which are the starter kit components?
Book of Registry Police Book, Bills of sale (Retail Certificate of Sale (MV-50), Odometer Statements except ATV and Boat Dealers
================
What about Snowmobile Dealer?
The DMV doesn't endorse these companies nor does it declare that the DMV has any association or supervision of these companies.
================
What if the on-site investigation is approved?
In that case, the DMV will issue you a certificate of registration , MV-50's Retail Certificates of Sale and a dealer plate letter if applicable.
