I would like to know how I can surrender IRP registration and plates.
================
Specifically, what should I do to surrender IRP registration and plates?
You must complete and sign form Schedule A&C.
================
One of my plates was stolen, what should I do?
You must complete the MV-78B form from a police agency and send it with form IRP-6, surrender the apportioned plates only if no vehicle will replace the current vehicle.
And what if one of my plates is lost?
It is the same procedure as with a stolen plate. You must complete the MV-78B form from a police agency and send it with form IRP-6, surrender the apportioned plates only if no vehicle will replace the current vehicle.You must return the unused cab card, sticker and license plates distributed within 60 days after your original receipt or within 60 days after the effective date of registration renewal.
================
I don't want to renew my registration, how can I surrender an IRP registration in this way?
send the apportioned plates and the cab card to the address you see below.
================
Where should I send the surrendered items?
Send it to International Registration Bureau, PO Box 2850, Albany, NY 12220-0850.
================
How can I get a refund of the IRP registration fees I paid to the state of New York?
You only qualify for a refund if you surrender the apportioned plates to the NY State IRB and any of these listed conditions occurred:- The NY State IRB made an error and the error is on your invoice.- You paid duplicate apportioned registration fees for the same vehicle or vehicles.
