What is the purpose of a social security benefit verification letter?
This letter serves as proof of your retirement, disability, SSI, or Medicare benefits. It also serves as proof that you have applied for benefits or that you have never received SSI.
Can I use it as proof that I have never received Social Security benefits?
Yes, you can.
