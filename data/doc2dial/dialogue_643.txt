I need some guidance with my application for disability benefits. Can you assist me?
================
Of course. Are you applying for a child?
Yes, for my son.
================
Do you want me to tell you more about the necessary steps to apply?
Yes, please.
Well, first you should review the Child Disability Starter Kit. This kit answers common questions about applying for Supplemental Security Income (SSI) benefits for children, and includes a worksheet that will help you gather the information you need.
What if I don't want to do a child disability report online?
Then you call us toll-free at 1-800-772-1213.
Do you also have a number for deaf people? That is part of my disability.
Yes, simply call our toll-free TTY number, 1-800-325-0778.
Would you recommend me to contact social security during my application phase?
Yes, you should contact Social Security right away to find out whether the income and resources of the parents and the child are within the allowed limits, and to start the SSI application process.
