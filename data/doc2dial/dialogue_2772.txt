The following table shows the point values assigned to moving traffic violations. The "points" are evaluated based on your driving record based on the date you committed the violation, not the date you were convicted in court.
What if I accumulate 11 or more points in 18 months?
You will be called to a DMV hearing, after which your license can be suspended or revoked. You will be offered the option to either waive the hearing or accept a defined period of suspension.
