Can you tell me what years of FAFSA submission and completion data are available to check?
Currently, the Federal Student Aid releases data for the 2018-19 cycle and provides a side-by-side comparison of the previous 2017-18 cycle. There's also an archive that contains data files from the 2017-18 cycle comparing it to the past 2016-17 cycle.
What kind of information are available through these files?
Only the number of submitted and completed FAFSA forms. No personally identifiable information for any FAFSA filer is shared.
