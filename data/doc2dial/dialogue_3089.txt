I am concerned because I have been told that I can no longer get federal student aid
Don't worry, there are things you can do to get back your eligibility for financial aid
================
What if I don't have a high school diploma
In that case, you need to talk to the financial aid office at your school to find out what to do
================
I'm incarcerated.Is that a problem to get aid?
Yes, your incarceration affects your eligibility for different types of aid in different ways
Have you been informed that you are ineligible for a particular type of student aid due to your incarceration?
Yes, indeed
Well, you need to wait until you're released before you can get that type of aid
================
What can I do?
You need talk to your financial aid office about the circumstances that cause you to lose eligibility. Read our tips below.
================
What if my grades are low or I have not completed enough credits and my financial aid office told me that I cannot get federal student aid?
In that case, you need to make satisfactory academic progress in college to keep getting federal student aid.  And you can talk to your school too about whether you can appeal the decision that made you ineligible to continue receiving federal student aid
