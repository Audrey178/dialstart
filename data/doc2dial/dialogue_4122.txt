What if I live somewhere besides with my parents?
It does not matter if you live with your parents; you would still have to report info pertaining to them.
================
What if my parents refuse to provide info needed for the FAFSA, how do I provide the info?
You would have to choose the one that says I am unable to provide info about my parent.
================
Why would I use my stepparents info?
Using your stepparents info on the FAFSA form gives an accurate picture of your family's financial ability.
