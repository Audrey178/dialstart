I was wondering if I can get help with my dental insurance, becauce I can't get VA dental care benefits.
================
You may be able to buy dental insurance at a reduced cost through the VA Dental Insurance Program, VADIP, if you meet at least one of the requirements.
And what are those requirements, if I may ask?
Are you signed up for VA health care?
No, I am not.
Are you signed up for our Civilian Health and Medical Program also called CHAMPVA, a health insurance program for dependents of Veterans?
No, I'm not.
Unfortunately, you won't be able to buy dental insurance at a reduced cost through the VA Dental Insurance Program then. I'm sorry.
================
That's ok. Is there anything else I may qualify for?
You may qualify for a one-time course of dental care that a VA dental care provider concludes.
Do I need the dental care for a specific goal?
Yes, the dental care must be needed to make it possible for you to be in the vocational rehab program and make it possible for you to reach the goals of your vocational rehab program.
Is there anything else I should know?
Yes, you should know that a Dental Trauma Rating VA Form 10 - 564-D or VA Regional Office Rating Decision letter VA Form 10 - 7131 will tell you the tooth/teeth/conditions that are trauma-rated.
