What happens after I have applied for VA health care benefits?
After your application, we will send you a letter to let you know if your application has been approved
================
How do I get my Veterans Health Identification Card?
You can contact your local VA medical center and speak with the enrolment coordinator. This coordinator can help you arrange to have your picture taken for your new VHIC. You can, alternatively, request your VHIC at your next VA health care appointment
================

Where do I go if I need help accessing VA services?
You can contact our toll free number at 877-222-8387, Monday through Friday from 8.00 a.m. to 8.00 p.m. ET
Can I cancel my enrolment in VA healthcare at any stage?
You can cancel your enrolment but this may impact your health care coverage requirements under the Affordable Care Act, if you don't have other health care
What can I do if I disagree with a decision that is made on my VA healthcare application?
In this instance, you can file an appeal. If your application is not approved, you will receive a letter outlining the reasons why, and this letter will also include instructions on how to appeal the decision
