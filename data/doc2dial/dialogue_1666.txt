How can my father get VA disability benefits?
Has your father been diagnosed with a presumptive disease believed to have been caused by Agent Orange exposure?
No, he hasn't.
In this case, your father doesn't qualify for disability benefits due to Agent Orange exposure.
Is there any other way that he could qualify for disability benefits?
Your father has to be diagnosed with an illness related to Agent Orange exposure to qualify.
================
How would he file a claim for disability compensation?
To file a claim, he will need to submit his medical record that shows he has an Agent Orange related illness.
================
