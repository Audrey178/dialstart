I want to know about Direct PLUS loans.
Direct PLUS loans are federal loans that you can use to help pay for education expenses not covered by other financial aid.
 Are you looking to apply for a Direct PLUS Loan?
Not right now.
================
 What I want to know is where to find information about the parent PLUS loans I've received.
You can visit My Aid to get information about all of the federal student loans and other financial aid you've received and also get contact information for your loan servicer.

Can I cancel a loan?
Yes, you can cancel all or part of the loan before the money is disbursed. You just need to notify the school.
You can also do it after the money is disbursed. Does this interest you?
