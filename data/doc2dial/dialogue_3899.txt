How is it possible to avoid an lapse in insurance?
================
Always remember that if you have no insurance you have no plates and keep your address up to date for your vehicle registration and driver license with the DMV.
Do you have valid auto liability insurance coverage?
Yes, I have valid auto liability insurance coverage for my vehicle.
It is a good thing you have valid liability coverage because otherwise you would have to surrender your registration and vehicle plates.
================
How can I determine the lapse period?
You can figure it out by when the plate were reported lost/stolen/confiscated; or the registration expires.
What can I do when my license is suspended for a lapse of insurance?
Has your registration expired?
Yes, it has expired.
You then have to surrender your plates.
================
Can another person register the vehicle if my registration is suspended for a lapse of insurance?
No, the DMV will not issue new registration if they determine that the purpose of the new registration is to avoid the suspension.
