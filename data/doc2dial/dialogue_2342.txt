I need help getting a learner permit.
I can help you with that. You need to be 16 or older to obtain a permit and you must apply in person at a DMV office.
================
Where do I go if I need to find more information about this?
You can find a list of various learner permit topics and information for each online at our website. These topics include getting a Certification of Supervised Driving form and special requirements needed for motorcycle and commercial learner permits.
================
How can I find out the operating hours at my local DMV?
You can find a list of locations and their hours online at the DMV website.
================
What should I do to prepare for the road test?
Before you take your driving test, you will need to have supervised driving practice. You will also need to take a pre-licensing or driver education course.
Are you under the age of 18?
Yes. I just turned 16.
Okay. Due to your age, you will have to wait 6 months from the date you received your learner permit before you can schedule to road test.
================
How do I pass the written permit test?
It's very important to study before taking the written test. You will need to get at least 14 of the 20 multiple choice questions correct to pass.
