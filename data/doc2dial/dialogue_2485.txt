What happens if I have a Traffic Violation Bureau traffic ticket that was issued in New York City?
You would be able to plead guilty and pay tickets answerable to TVB 1 or plead not guilty and schedule a TVB hearing online, over the phone or in a TVB office.
Do you handle parking violations?
The TVB does not handle traffic violations, red light camera tickets, criminal driving violations.
