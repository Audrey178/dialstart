What happens if I request a hearing with the DMV?
If you request a hearing with the DMV, the suspension is put on hold pending the outcome of the hearing.
================
What is the purpose of a DMV hearing?
The purpose of the hearing is to determine if the toll authority notified you that it did not pay tolls, fees, or other charges related to or more violations of the Authority's toll collection regulations, all such violations committed within a period of 5 years period, or did not pay $ 200 in tolls accumulated within 5 years, if the violations occurred in a commercial vehicle, or were not discarded or transferred. At the hearing, you can NOT dispute whether you evaded a toll and / or did not pay tolls, fees, or other charges.
================
What would happen if I request a hearing with the DMV and my affidavit does not appear or present?
In that case your registration will be suspended immediately.
