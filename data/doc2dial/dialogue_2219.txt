do all online PIRP classes cost the same?
The cost is set by the course sponsor and it could vary from one course to another course.
================
how soon after we finish a class do we get the proof that we took the class?
The sponsor will mail the course completion certificate to you. Please note, that an email copy of the certificate is not valid and will not be accepted by your insurance company.
================
can I do the classes through DVD?
Yes, you will need a DVD player and a television, or a computer that plays DVDs.
================
are the online PIRPS any different from the on location PIRPS?
They both have very similar content and information, and both courses include 320 minutes of instruction. The classroom course is given in one daytime session or two evening sessions, you can take the I-PIRP course from home at a convenient time for you.
================
what kind of tests or quizzes do you get for the online classes and how do you know the real person is doing the exams?
In addition to knowledge tests about course content, there will be questions to help verify your identity and participation in the course.
