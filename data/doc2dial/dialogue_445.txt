How do I apply for SSI if I'm over 65 years of age?
================
For people age 65 and older, you can call at 1-800-772-1213.
Is this the number to apply for SSI?
Yes, that is correct.
================
What do I do if my application is denied?
If this is the case, you are able to appeal it.
Has your SSI application been denied?
Yes, it has.
To begin the appeal process, go online and file an SSI appeal.
================
Does it have to be done online?
No, you can also schedule an appointment at your local Social Security office.
How do I know if I'm eligible for SSI?
Adults with a disability can now file for SSI online, if you meet the requirements.
