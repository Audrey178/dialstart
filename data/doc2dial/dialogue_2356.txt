I need help with getting my driving record abstract.
================
I can help you with that. Would you like more information about what your standard driving record abstract entails?
Yes, this would be very helpful.
You can see a sample of a driving record abstract under Description of Abstract of Driving Record PDF [ 3 ] DS-242.1 on our website.
Do you happen to need all information that DMV has on file for you?
No, I just need to see the last few years.
For future reference, how would I obtain this information?
To get a record of all your information, you'll need a lifetime driving record.
Can I get my abstract online?
Absolutely, and rest assured that no matter whether you order online, at an office, or by mail, there will be no difference in your abstract. They are all certified DMV records that come with a certification statement and the signature of the Commissioner of Motor Vehicles.
================
What do I need if I decide to obtain one by mail?
If you wish to obtain your abstract by mail, you'll need a completed Request for DMV Records PDF [ 11 ] MV-15 and a photocopy of your proof of identity.
What if I just wait and get it at the DMV, when I renew my license?
In this case, just mark the blue box on the renewal notice. Be advised, there will be an extra $10 search fee added to your renewal fee.
