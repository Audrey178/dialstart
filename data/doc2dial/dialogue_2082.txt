Why should I share DMV information?
================
The DMV information shared with bus companies , employers , insurance companies , researchers , and motor vehicle manufacturers , through each of the three sharing methods , makes our lives safer and more convenient
If this information were not made available traffic safety and commerce would be negatively impacted, do you need more infromation on this?
Yes
A few examples of how DMV s information sharing programs benefit all of us are provided below
================
How much does the DMV get from pay-per-search sales?
$ 58 million per year
================
How much does it get from over the counter sales?
Approximately $ 3.0 million per year
================
How long does a quote take?
You can expect a rate quote within minutes. Insurance companies are able to provide this convenient service because they have direct pay - per - search access to DMV information
================
How much does the DMV get from contract sales?
Approximately $ 2.2 million per year
================
