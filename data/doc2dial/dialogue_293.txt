What are disability benefits and how do I receive them?
Disability benefits are paid through social security to people who can't work due to a medical condition that is expected to last more than one year or result in death.  You can find more information online under Manage your Benefits.

Who can receive disability benefits?
People can receive Social Security disability benefits if they can have a medical condition that keeps them from working for more than a year.
