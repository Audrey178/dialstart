I have a disability from a combat mission that prevents me from working and I don't know how to make my payments. Can't I get some benefits?
You might. If your service-connected disability limits your ability to work or prevents you from working, find out if you can get VR&E benefits and services like help exploring employment options and getting more training if required.
================

On our page you can find out how to apply for a housing grant as a Veteran or service member with a service-connected disability.

You can tell me one more thing though. What if I can't pay my mortgage and I become homeless after losing my house? Can you help me then?
Of course. You can find support by calling the National Call Center for Homeless Veterans at 877-424-3838 for help 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. You'll talk privately with a trained VA counselor for free.
