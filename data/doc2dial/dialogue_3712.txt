Is it possible for getting more than one VA education benefit at the same time?
================
It is possible to qualify for more than one at once.
You should know, however, that if you qualify for more than one, you cannot use both at the same time. They must be used separately.
I did find out that I qualify for two different ones. How do I decide what to do now?
We can help you make the right decision for you to help maximize the benefits in your situation.
