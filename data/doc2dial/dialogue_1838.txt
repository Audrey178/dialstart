I want to know about the fees for commercial vehicles.
You can use the registration fee and county use tax charts to know your registration fee and county use fee.
================
Where can I get the UT-11 form?
You can get the form from a local DMV office or from a DMV call center.
================
So what is the chart for county use tax?
The chart have the same name, county use tax chart.
================
Where can I know more about these taxes?
You can ask your city or county about these taxes.
================
Help me determine the correct fee.
ok, you have to add 3.25% to the fee listed in the chart and then change the value you get to the nearest 0.25$.
