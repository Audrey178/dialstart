What do VA dental insurance programs offer?
The VA Dental Insurance Program or VADIP offers discounted private dental insurance for Veterans and family members who meet certain requirements and you can find out if you qualify today>
================
How do i find out if I am eligible for the VA Dental Insurance Program?
You may be eligible for VADIP if you meet our requirement.
What requirements need to be true?
Are you a Veteran who is enrolled in VA health care?
No, I am not a Veteran enrolled in VA health care.
Is your current or surviving spouse or dependent child of a Veteran or service member?
Yes, my current or surviving spouse or dependent child is a Veteran.
Where is VADIP coverage?
VADIP provides coverage throughout the United States and its territories, including Puerto Rico, Guam, U.S. Virgin Islands, American Samoa, and the Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands.
================
Do I need to enroll in the VADIP program if I was in the pilot program?
Yes, you will need to enroll again to get new coverage because some of the plan options  and fees may have changed.
