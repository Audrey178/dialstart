Am I able to get  federal student aid any more, if I was told I can't get it?
It could be possible if you do the things that will make you eligible again for financial aid.
================
What will happen if I have property subject to a judgment lien?
Do you have property subject to a judgment lien for a debt owed t the United States?
Yes, I have property subject to a judgment lien for debt owed to the United States.
You would have to pay your debt in full or you can make payment arrangements to pay the debt.
================
What is the best thing to do if I have been told I was not able to get federal student aid any more?
It is in your best interest to talk to your financial aid office and we also can provide some tips on how to get your eligibility back.
================
Who should I talk to regarding repaying aid or getting additional aid?
You should talk to the financial aid office at your school.
================
Who should I be in contact with for info about reinstating my status of noncitizen eligibility of student aid?
You should contact US Citizenship and Immigration Services.
