Perfect, and in relation to retiree benefits?
================
It is recommended to apply online, the process is easier.
Of course, you can also file an application over the phone or at a Social Security office.
================
What is the process to withdraw the application?
It is noteworthy that you have up to 12 months to withdraw your request, of course if you change your mind.
================
Where can you obtain more information?
You can visit Appeal a decision, to further expand the information.
================
What if you don't live in the United States?
It is important that you visit the Federal Benefits Unit of your country of residence.
