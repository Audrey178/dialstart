I am interested in the Student Loan Interest Deduction scheme, can you give me further information about this please?
If you took out a student loan for yourself, your spouse or your dependent, you can avail of a tax deduction for any interest that was paid on these loans. This tax benefit applied to all loans that were taken out for education, not just federal student loans. The maximum deduction that you can avail of is $2,500 per year
================
