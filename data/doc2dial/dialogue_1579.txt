What life insurance benefits can I get with VGLI?
 $ 10,000 to $ 400,000 in life insurance benefits , based on the amount of SGLI coverage you had when you left the military
When I leave the army, what should I do?
You can enroll through VGLI to get coverage up to the amount you had through SGLI. You can also increase your coverage by $ 25,000 every 5 years to $ 400,000 until you are 60 years old.
