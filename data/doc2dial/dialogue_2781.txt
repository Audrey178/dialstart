I'd like some pointers on how to register my vehicle.
================
Is it an out-of-state vehicle?
Yes it's presently registered in Jersey.
It's dependent on certain criteria.
================
If the car isn't in my name what needs to be done?
For a new vehicle purchased from an out-of-state dealer there are some documents that you will need to provide like the Manufacturer s Certificate of Origin MCO and the dealer's bill of sale.
================
Okay I'll come down to the DMV with them. Is there a special time for this sort of thing?
You will need to make a reservation online before visiting DMV offices in New York City, Westchester, Nassau, Suffolk, Rockland, Onondaga or Monroe Counties.
================
Do the same documents apply for a used car?
It depends if the vehicle was bought new or used.
================
I'd like to remove the lien on the car too, is that possible?
In that case, you must provide proof that the lien has been satisfied and that you paid your vehicle loan off. For more information see remove or add a lienholder from a title [ 5 ].
