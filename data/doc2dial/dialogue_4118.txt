How can I regain eligibility for federal student aid?
================
You can check on our website.
Were you informed that you no longer qualify for federal student aid?
Yes, that's correct.
We have resources online that teach you how to regain your eligibility back.
================
Are non-citizens eligible for the aid?
 Non-citizens that meet the requirements can also be eligible for federal student aid.
================
Can you tell me some resources that I can use to know how to help homeless students?
You can check on our website the page Identifying and Supporting Students Experiencing Homelessness from Pre-School to Post-Secondary Ages. There you can find useful information in this regard.
================
 What is it taken into consideration when evaluating an student eligibility for federal student aid?
First, there's no income cut-off to qualify, and many factors such as the size of the student's family and their year in school are taken into consideration.
================
lastly, can you tell me some of the eligibility requirements?
Some of the general eligibility requirements are that the student is a U.S citizen or eligible noncitizen with financial need, and is enrolled in an eligible degree or certificate program at their college or career school.
