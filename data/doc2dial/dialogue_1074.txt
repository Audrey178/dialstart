How do I get education and career counseling through the VA?
================
You can get these benefits by applying for Vocational Rehabilitation and Employment benefits.
================
How do I apply by mail?
You would need to fill out an Educational/Vocational Counseling Application VA Form 28-8832 after you download it.
Do you know what happens after you qualify?
No, I do not know.
How do I apply by mail?
Mail the completed form to the nearest VA regional benefit office.
================
How do I get more information about the VR&E services?
You can watch a video we provide about VR&E Chapter 36 services.
================
How do we find out if we are eligible for Educational and Career Counseling?
You can get the services if you will be discharged from active duty within six months.
