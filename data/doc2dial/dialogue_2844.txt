What are the cost of college schools?
It can vary significantly from school to school but we can give you tips on how to lower the costs when you go to school.

Can you give me a good part-time job recommendation that is compatible with my studies?
One good option is the Federal Work-Study Program that provides the opportunity to earn money while going to school. You can ask schools if they participate in the program.
