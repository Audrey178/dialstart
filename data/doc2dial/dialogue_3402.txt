Could my federal student loan possibly qualify for discharge if the school in which I am currently enrolled is closing?
I can help you to learn about the eligibility requirements for discharge and how you can apply.
================
Ok what are the criteria for a possible discharge?
You may be eligible for a 100 percent discharge if you were unable to complete your program because your school closed.
I was enrolled when my school closed and did not complete my desired courses.
In that even you may indeed be eligible for a 100% discharge of your William D. Ford Federal Direct Loan Direct Program loans, Federal Family Education Loan FFEL Program loans, or Federal Perkins Loans.
================
How do I obtain this discharge of my loan?
It is very important for you to obtain your academic and financial aid records if your school closes since you might need those records if you plan to attend another school or want your student loans discharged.
What would I need from this school if I am planning on completing my education elsewhere?
You will need to obtain your academic and financial records if you plan to attend another school.
================
Are you able to answer questions about benefits I may have by receiving a closed school loan discharge?
================
Are you inquiring about the benefits you would receive by obtaining a closed school loan discharge?
Yes, I am.
If you receive the closed school loan discharge, you have no further obligation to repay that loan, you will receive reimbursement of payments made voluntarily or through forced collection, and the record of the loan and all repayment history associated with the loan, including any adverse history, will be deleted from your credit report.
