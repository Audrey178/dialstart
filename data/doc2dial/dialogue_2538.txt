Hello, I would like to know If I remove a vehicle from the road, can I keep my photo or my professional license plates?
================
Yes You can store your photograph or professional license plates at most DMV offices.
Do you want to use your stored photo or professional license plates to register another vehicle?
Yes
To register the vehicle again, or to use your stored photo or professional license plates to register another vehicle, you must visit the office that has your vehicle plates. Be sure to bring the FS-6 T receipt and the other documents necessary to register your vehicle.
================
Where can i get more information?
For more information, contact your local police or parking enforcement agency, or the agency responsible for parking regulations.
================
And how can I register my license plates in another vehicle?
You should visit the office that has the license plates on your vehicle. Be sure to bring the FS-6 T receipt and the other documents necessary to register your vehicle.
================
And won't I pay the custom license plate fees when I renew my registration?
If your registration is in the first year of a two-year registration, you must submit a Refund Request or Fee Paid for Motor Vehicle Registrations and Driver's Licenses pdf MV-215 to request a refund of the fee balance. A $ 1.00 processing fee is subtracted from your refund. You will not receive a refund if the registration expires in less than a year.
and the plates can give me some benefit?
Professional license plates do not entitle you to any special parking privileges unless the city, town, or village where you park your vehicle has granted you those privileges.
