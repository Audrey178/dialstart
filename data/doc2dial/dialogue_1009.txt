================
No disrespect, sir or ma'am, but I'd rather talk to another Veteran. They might have more experience and understand my point of view better. Can you please forward me to a Veteran or someone who currently serves?
Of course. You can connect with our caring, qualified Veterans Crisis Line responders for confidential help. Many of them are Veterans themselves. This service is private, free, and available 24/7.
I see. Does this apply to female Veterans as well?
Of course. If you are a female Veteran, just ask for the Women Veterans Program Manager or find the nearest VA medical center.
================
One more thing I've been meaning to ask: if a natural disaster occurs, what support can I expect?
Should a natural disaster occur, we can help you find out if your home was damaged by that natural disaster.
================

Of course. You can find out how to file a claim for compensation for conditions related to your military service on our page.
