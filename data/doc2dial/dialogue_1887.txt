Ok, in case you want to file the appeal online, remember that the traffic ticket must display the words "Traffic Violations Bureau" at the bottom of the ticket.
Yes, the traffic ticket must have the words "Traffic Violations Bureau" at the bottom.
================
Do I have to submit all the exhibits and documents to the hearing officer?
Yes, otherwise any of the exhibits and documents may not be filed with the appeal.
================
Can I avoid to pay the fine after filing an appeal?
no, you have to pay the fine, filing an appeal won't change your obligation to pay the fine.
================
What if my traffic ticket does not show the words "Traffic Violations Bureau" at the bottom?
It has to display the words "Traffic Violations Bureau" at the bottom of the ticket, if not then you can't file an appeal with the Department of Motor Vehicles.
