Is there a difference between the Iraq and Afghanistan Service Grant and the Pell Grant?
The maximum amount awarded by the Iraq and Afghanistan Service Grant is the same as that awarded by the Pell Grant. With both of these grants, your EFC is not affected which means that your eligibility for any need-based federal student aid will also not be affected
================
How do I further my complaint with my school over my belief that they have mishandled the administration of funding of my financial aid?
The next steps you take depends on the particular situation. If you have tried to solve the problem between the school and yourself but to no avail, then you may contact the Federal Student Aid Feedback Center or call 1-844-651-0077. If you suspect that your school has given your funding to someone else, then you should familiarise yourself with identity theft and how to report it to our Office of Inspector General Hotline
