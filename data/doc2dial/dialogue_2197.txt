Hello I would like to become an instructor at a driving school. Would you tell me a bit about the requirements?
================
Certainly. In order to be certified you must be employed by a licensed driving school and be at least 21 years of age.
Additional information is available as well. Would you be interested in receiving it?
Yes please tell me about this information.
You must contact DTP or Drier Training Program for said information.
Is there additional training if I want to be a fully qualified instructor?
Yes there is a 30-hour Teaching Techniques and Methodology course for those that wish to teach the Pre - licensing Course.
================
Are driving schools investigated on a regular basis?
Yes the DMV conducts inspections and audits that require driving schools to follow requirements and typically the regulations of the municipality in which it is operating.
================
Will I receive an official certificate once I pass all the required exams?
Yes and you must possess this certificate at all times while providing driving instructions.
