do you want to know how to transfer an IRP registration and distributed plates?
Yes
To transfer an IRP registration and distributed license plates, request that the IRB transfer the registration when you purchase or register the new vehicle.
================
Can I transfer the IRP registration online?
You can request the transfer of an IRP registration online through the New York State OSCAR Credentialing and One Stop Registration system.
================
When will the new registration expire?
The new registration expires on the same date as the original registration.
================
and can i request by mail?
of course If you apply by mail, send photocopies of your proof of identity.
What happens if I don't transfer the registration right away?
If you do not transfer the registration immediately, turn in the assigned license plates and taxi card to the IRB. Registration of your vehicle will be suspended if there is an expiration of liability insurance and the plates provided were not delivered. The registrant's driver's license may also be suspended. See Section 9.0 of the New York State IRP Handbook pdf IRP-8 for complete instructions on registration transfers.
