Need help with the Direct Plus Loan Application for graduate students.
================
Would you like to learn what a Direct Plus Loan Application for graduate students is?
No, I know what the Direct Plus Loan application is.
================
Do you want to apply for a PLUS Loan?
No, I do not need to apply for a PLUS Loan at this time.
================
When do I complete the Direct PLUS Loan MPN?
You have the chance to complete the Direct PLUS Loan MPN after you have completed the Direct PLUS Loan Request.
What happens if I have placed a security freeze on my credit file?
You definitely have to lift or remove the freeze at each credit bureau before you will be able to continue because your application will not be processed otherwise.
================
Can I use my computer to apply for a PLUS Loan?
Yes, you must LOG IN to start.
