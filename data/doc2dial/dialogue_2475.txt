I need help with getting replacements for IRP registration items.
Do you need to replace IRP registration items that have been lost or stolen?
Yes, I believe they were stolen.
Okay. You will need to apply to the International Registration Bureau to get replacements.
================
Is it possible to do this by fax?
Absolutely. You can apply to replace IRP registration items by using fax number 518-486-6579.
================
What if I decide to do it by phone?
It's not possible to apply for replacements by phone, but you can call IRB at 1-518-473-5834 for any questions you have.
================
Can it be done by mail?
Yes. You will need to complete and sign Schedule A & C in Part 1. Then check the box(es) for Duplicate Cab Card, Replacement Plate, and/or Replacement Sticker.
Both of my plates were stolen. What should I do?
Contact a police agency and get form Report of Lost or Stolen Motor Vehicle Items.
