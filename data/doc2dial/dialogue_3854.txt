What do I need to do before my road test
Read the manual given to you when you got your permit.
================
Is there anything I should practice?
I would recommend practicing and studying it all.
================
Is there any way to speed this up?
No, you must go through every step as outlined by the state.
================
Where can I find a pre-licensing course?
There are numerous agencies and companies that do these, please refer to the state in order to find ones that are com;iant with the state.
================
What should I do if my view is blocked when at a crosswalk?
You should make sure to observe safety at all times and refer to the manual for proper driving "right of way" materials.
What do I need to do if I have a DJ/MJ permit?
Make sure to have supervised driving time that is logged and study the permit licensiing materials.
================
How many hours do I need?
15 hours after sunset, 50 hours total.
What if I'm under 18?
The same, but everything must be done in a month.
