How do I enter my registration renewal for my vehicle?
================
You should enter the vehicle plate number from your registration notice or document and should only enter letters and numbers.
Do you need to file a boat registration number?

What happens when I submit an address change online?
Make sure you check the ones you want because the changes to DMV records are only for ones you checked online and it will show the drivers license, permit or non-driver ID DMV photo document and registration records to locate under your name.
What if there are registrations in my name I can't find or not shown?
All you need to do is enter the plate or registration number to include them to the list and change the address for those.
What happens after my address change is processed?
There are links provided on the website to order replacement duplicate documents that show the new address and fees will apply for the duplicates.
What if I select "No Change" for any photo documents or registrations?
Then those DMV records will not be changed with the new address.
