How many social security credits do I need for benefits?
You must earn at least 40 Social Security credits to qualify for Social Security benefits.
Alright, and how do I get those credits?
You earn Social Security credits when you work and pay Social Security taxes.
================
What about retirement benefits? How many credits do I require to retire?
Were you born after 1928?
Well, yes.
Then you need 40 credits to qualify for retirement.
================
How many credits can I earn per year?
You can earn up to a maximum of four credits per year.
If I earn more credits than needed, do I get a higher amount of benefits?
Your extra credits do not increase your benefit amount. The average of your earnings over your working years counts, not the total number of credits you earn.
