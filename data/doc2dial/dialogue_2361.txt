================
What will happen during an on-site investigation when the automotive facilities inspector arrives?
The AFI will be checking that you have permanently mounted signs visible from the street and if not visible they will deny your application; and you will need proof of identification for everyone named on the application with a driver license or government issued photo ID.
================
What is required to become a licensed Inspection Station in the state of New York?
If you want to become a licensed Inspection Station by the DMV in New York state then you should consult the DMV Commissioner's Motor Vehicle Inspection Regulations.
================
What topic should I look for?
You should look for the topic under Inspection.
================
Is the DMV Commissioner's Motor Vehicle Inspection Regulations able to be downloaded?
Yes, you can download them and read Facility Requirements Inspection Stations.
================
What happens if I do not have proof of Workers Compensation Insurance at this time?
Since you don't have your proof of Workers Compensation Insurance, then it is in your best interest not to submit an application with DMV yet.
