Hello, Can I work and study?
Yes, our Veterans work-study program can help you earn money while you re-enrolled in a college, a vocational school, or professional training program.
================
Where do I request program information?
You can contact the VA regional processing office that handles your claim.
================
How can I participate in the program?
Are you enrolled at least three - quarter time in a college degree, vocational, or professional program?
Yes, I am enrolled in a vocational program.
Do you have an open job either at a nearby VA facility or in a VA - related role at your school?
No, I don't.
I'm sorry. You cannot take part in our work-study program because you don't meet all of the requirements.
================
Can I get paid to work a part-time job while you study?
Yes, you can.
================
How much can I work?
You can work during or between enrollment periods.
