Please tell me more about the county use tax chart for counties outside New York City,
You can use the chart to determine the use taxes and supplemental fees for all original registrations and renewals. For example, the amount of sales tax depends on your locality and what was the purchase price. You can know more about the current rates by jurisdiction at the Department of Taxation and Finance website.
================
When does the county use tax applies to me?
This tax is for all the residents of New York City and several counties in New York, and it applies to all the registrations and renewals of commercial vehicles.
I reside in New York City and I'm exempt from the NYC vehicle use tax, what should I do.
In your case, you should get form UT-11 from a local DMV office or a DMV call center.
I want to know more about these taxes.
You can ask the city or county where you currently reside or have your business.

I want to know more about the registration fee chart.
The fees on this chart are for commercial vehicles that are less than 18000 lbs in weight. For diesel commercial vehicles that are 8501 pounds or more in weight, there is an additional fee.
